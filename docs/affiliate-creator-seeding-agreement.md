# Creator Seeding Agreement Template

Effective Date: 2026-05-20  
Version: v5.0.0 (matches `docs/DECISIONS/locked_override_2026-05-20.md`)  
Status: draft business template for attorney review. Do not send as a final
contract until counsel reviews governing law, remedies, platform disclosure
rules, and product-specific compliance language.

## Parties

This Creator Seeding Agreement is entered into by and between VialChem Labs
LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company ("Company"), and the creator
identified in the signed order or campaign brief ("Creator").

## Sample Order

Company may provide Creator with a one-time sample order of up to two sealed
research-product vials at no purchase price. Samples remain subject to all
research-use-only restrictions, shipping restrictions, and product disclaimers
published on the VialChem Labs site (https://vialchemlabs.net).

## Eligibility

Creator represents that, at the time of acceptance, Creator has:

- At least 1,000 relevant followers or subscribers.
- Typical recent video views of at least 2,000 per qualifying video.
- A content niche aligned with laboratory, analytical, research, or adjacent
  scientific education audiences.
- No history of making non-compliant health, dosing, therapeutic, clinical,
  disease, human-use, or veterinary-use claims about research compounds.

## Deliverables

Creator will publish the deliverables stated in the campaign brief. Unless a
separate brief states otherwise, the minimum deliverable is one original video
or post featuring the sample order and linking to the approved Company URL.

Deliverables must be published within 14 calendar days after confirmed delivery
of the sample order unless Company approves a written extension.

## Required Disclosures

Creator must clearly and conspicuously disclose the material connection with
Company in every related post, video, caption, landing page, or link placement.
Acceptable disclosure language includes "sample provided by VialChem Labs" or
another disclosure approved by Company in writing.

## Prohibited Claims

Creator may not make or imply:

- Human consumption, injection, ingestion, dosing, protocol, or administration.
- Therapeutic, health, wellness, cosmetic, performance, recovery, disease, or
  outcome claims.
- Product efficacy or safety claims outside the exact published product page
  and Certificate of Analysis language.
- Any statement suggesting Company products are drugs, medicines, supplements,
  or approved for human or veterinary use.

## Review And Takedown

Company may request removal or correction of any non-compliant deliverable.
Creator will remove or correct the content within 24 hours after written
notice.

## Failure To Publish

If Creator accepts the sample order and fails to publish the required
deliverable within the required period, Creator will either return unused,
sealed samples if legally and commercially acceptable, or reimburse Company
for the documented sample value and shipping cost. Company may withhold future
affiliate approval until resolved.

## Affiliate Commission

If Creator is separately approved as an affiliate, commissions are governed by
the affiliate program terms and the active commission tier in Company records.
Sample acceptance does not guarantee affiliate approval.

## No Employment Or Agency

Creator is an independent contractor and is not an employee, partner, agent, or
legal representative of Company.

## Governing Law

Governing law, venue, dispute resolution, and recoverable remedies must be
completed by counsel before execution.
