### Operational CS reply patterns (synthesized from observed practice)

- **Dosing question**: "We do not provide dosing information for our research compounds. Our products are sold for in-vitro laboratory research only and are not approved for human or animal administration." (Apply Apollo / Felix / NextChems-grade language; never quote a mg or mcg figure.)
- **Lost package**: vendors who rebooked WITHOUT changing language = Biotech Peptides ("we will send out a replacement FREE of charge"), Umbrella ("one-time, no-cost replacement"), GenX (re-ship, 50% discount on next order if international seizure). NEVER promise a "refund" without an "all sales final" preamble; even Limitless's permissive 10-day return is gated to "unopened."
- **COA request**: vendors point to the Lab Results / Independent Test Results / Test Reports page. Never email a COA as a personalized PDF — it must be available as a public on-site PDF or a third-party-portal verifiable link (Janoshik verify.janoshik.com is the gold standard; cited by Skye Peptides).
- **Refund**: "Our products are sold strictly for laboratory research use, we cannot guarantee research outcomes" (Blue Sky Peptide). "Due to regulations regarding the sale of our products, returns are prohibited" (Ascension). "All sales are final" (every other anchor).

## Section 5 — Top 10 Operational Compliance Rules (ranked by FDA enforcement signal strength)

1. **Never sell tirzepatide, retatrutide, semaglutide, or BAC water.** ITC GEO 337-TA-1377 (border-block since 2025-04-15) + 13 of 18 verbatim-retrieved warning letters list these compounds. Every 2024-12-10 and 2026-03-31 letter cites them. The 2026-03-31 wave specifically cited BAC water as itself a drug and the "Reconstitution Kit" as evidence of human-use intent. **Highest signal.**
2. **Never sell the GLP cryptic-letter convention** (GLP-1-S, GLP-2-T, GLP-3-R, Cag/Tirz blends). FDA's 2026-03-31 wave establishes letter-codes do NOT break the §201(g)(1) intended-use chain. Apollo Peptide Sciences uses this convention; Pink Pony, Mile High, PekCura, Prime Sciences, Gram all received warning letters citing it. **Very high signal.**
3. **Never publish dosing protocols, cheat sheets, or "research protocol" PDFs anywhere.** Synthetix/Helix 2024-02-07 letter cited a "Standard Dose Protocol for Tirzepatide Research"; Mile High Compounds 2026-03-31 letter cited a "Peptide Cheat Sheet" hosted on Skool. Even private-community PDFs are pulled into FDA evidence. **Very high signal.**
4. **Never run paid press releases, paid social, or vendor-account weight-loss content.** Limitless Biotech "Peptides for Weight Loss" press release Dec 2024 is the strongest crossover in corpus (paid distribution = vendor-authorized speech). Prime Peptides 2024-12-10 letter cited a YouTube video titled "Top 5 Peptides for Weight Loss." Distribute affiliate-attributed content only; vendor account stays clean. **High signal.**
5. **Never name a target disease anywhere on site.** Biotech Peptides PDP "prevent against irritable bowel syndrome (IBS), gastrointestinal cramps, and Crohn's disease" is the corpus exemplar of how named-disease language pierces RUO. Every FDA letter follows the "despite RUO labeling, evidence … establishes [drug intent]" structure. **High signal.**
6. **Never use personal pronouns in vendor-authorized speech (PDP, blog, vendor IG, vendor X, vendor email, vendor video description).** Affiliate creators may; vendor account may not. Influencer-vendor hybrid (@thepeptideguyy "Helping you heal & live longer") is a structural risk vector. **High signal.**
7. **Never accept Stripe / PayPal / Square / Apple Pay direct.** Stripe Restricted-Businesses Policy explicitly excludes research peptides. Warrior Labz 2023-06-12 letter quoted vendor's own social post: "Payment via: Zelle, Cash App, Venmo, Apple Pay and Pay Pal - Friends and Family." Vendors that accept mainstream rails are operating without category permission and will be terminated; build on crypto + ACH + e-check (Umbrella Labs BTCPay+Plaid model; Chemyo BTC 10%+e-check 5%; Swiss Chems explicit "we don't offer Paypal. Amazon pay or Stripe"). **High signal.**
8. **Always disclaim 503A/503B status in footer.** Verbatim template: "[Brand] is a chemical supplier. [Brand] is not a compounding pharmacy or chemical compounding facility as defined under 503A of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic act. [Brand] is not an outsourcing facility as defined under 503B of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic act." Used by 7+ vendors. GLP-1 Solution 2025-09-09 letter explicitly cited 503A/503B condition non-compliance. **High signal — defensive but works as a baseline prop.**
9. **Always 21+ self-attestation, not 18+.** Pink Pony / Mile High / PekCura / Prime Sciences / Gram all received letters; FDA never specifically cited age-gate weakness, but pattern across non-cited vendors (Peptide Sciences 21+, Swiss Chems 21+, Polaris 21+, Felix Chem 21+, Apollo 21+, Skye 21+) shows 21+ is the defensible posture; 18+ is rare among vendors that _also_ maintain other defenses. The rule reduces "minor" attack surface for any later litigation. **Medium signal.**
