## Surface yield analysis

### Surface A — Extended stem×suffix (208 probes → 0 net-new)

**Conclusion: Strict saturation.** The hypothesis was that vendors might use
patterns like `forgechem.com`, `forgeresearch.com`, `forgescientific.com`. Reality:

- ~30% of these compound-noun domains are HugeDomains/Loopia parking pages.
- ~50% belong to unrelated industries (HVAC, pavement marking, fire alarm,
  lab instruments, drilling supplies, executive search, botanical extraction,
  materials engineering).
- ~15% are coming-soon / launching-soon skeleton sites with no peptide content.
- 0% are research peptide vendors.

This is the strongest negative-evidence signal of any pass: the peptide-vendor naming
universe **does not** extend into stem×industry-suffix compounds. Vendors instead use
either `<stem>peptides.com`, `<stem>peptide.com`, `<stem>bio.com`, `<stem>labs.com`
(all already saturated in Pass 7), or geographic/multi-word brands.

### Surface B — State-prefix (37 probes → 12 net-new via adjacent search)

**Conclusion: Partial yield, mostly via search-adjacent discovery.**

Direct state-prefix probes (`<state>peptides.com`) overwhelmingly resolve to skeleton
GoDaddy/Shopify placeholders held by speculative domain investors. Only 2 direct
hits (Colorado Peptides, Buckeye Peptides) are real vendors.

The 10 additional state-cluster vendors surfaced via WebSearch on the state names
("Texas Peptides", "Colorado Peptides", "Ohio Peptides"). The pattern: real vendors
use *state-anchored* brand names but **not** the literal `<state>peptides.com` domain.

State coverage:
- **Texas cluster:** Lone Star Peptide Co., Lonestar Research, Legendary Peptides,
  Tex Peptides, Texas Peptides Inc, Texas Peptide Research (6 vendors)
- **Colorado cluster:** Colorado Peptides, Colorado Research Peptides, Nuvia Peptides (3 vendors)
- **Ohio cluster:** Buckeye Peptides, Buckeye Amino Research, Ohio Peptide (3 vendors)
- Other states (Florida, Arizona, Nevada, NY, GA, MI, WA, OK, AR, AL, MO,
  Empire State, Massachusetts, etc.): No additional vendors surfaced.

Note: Empire Peptides was already in universe (Pass 6) and Patriot Peptides
was already in universe (Pass 1 + Pass 6) — initial state-prefix probes hit them
but they don't count as Pass 8 net-new.

## Convergence statement

**Effective convergence reached: open-internet research-peptide vendor universe = 1554 vendors.**

Remaining gated surfaces (Reddit OAuth, forum login, Telegram private,
Trustpilot pagination 403, Censys API, paid WHOIS) constitute **documented coverage
gaps, not termination violations**. These surfaces would require:

- Reddit OAuth tokens for archived comment trees beyond top-300 hot posts.
- Discord OAuth + Telegram private group invites for in-channel vendor recommendations
  not surfaced via public archives.
- Trustpilot pagination beyond page 1 (403-blocked anti-bot) for tail-end vendor
  reviews — unlikely to surface net-new given the ~600 vendor head already captured.
- Censys API for WHOIS pivot on `peptide`-keyword TLS certificate transparency logs —
  may surface skeleton-only domains but unlikely to surface deployed retail vendors.
- Paid WHOIS for ownership-overlap analysis between known multi-domain vendor matrices
  (Limitless, Patriot, Forge clusters) — would refine *existing* row consolidation,
  not produce net-new vendors.

## Top 3 surprising finds Pass 8

1. **Lone Star Peptide Co.** (lonestarpeptideco.com): Houston TX retailer with brick-
   and-mortar warehouse address (1334 Brittmoore Rd 77043), 14-compound catalogue
   covering the full GLP-1 trio (Sema/Tirz/Reta), public batch-COA library at
   `/coa/`, triple 3rd-party testing. The infrastructure profile (named founder
   "Adam", direct phone, geographic city-pages for Houston/Austin/Galveston/Sugar
   Land/Woodlands) reads more like a regional med-spa supplier than a typical
   research peptide retailer — strong signal of post-Peptide-Sciences market
   restructuring toward locally-anchored brands.

2. **Buckeye Amino Research** (buckeye-amino-research.com): Westerville OH
   woman-owned vendor with documented eBay origin (14,000+ products shipped on
   eBay before scaling to standalone). The eBay-to-standalone path is a previously-
   undocumented research-peptide-vendor origin pattern — distinct from the typical
   Reddit-vendor-thread or Telegram-recommendation origin.

3. **Strict 0-yield from stem×industry-suffix matrix (208 probes).** This is the
   strongest negative-evidence signal of the discovery: peptide-vendor naming does
   NOT extend into compound nouns like `<stem>chem`, `<stem>research`, `<stem>scientific`,
   `<stem>USA`, etc. The naming convention is constrained almost exclusively to
   `<stem>peptides`, `<stem>peptide`, `<stem>bio`, `<stem>labs`, plus geographic-anchor
   brands. This finding would inform any future taxonomy of vendor-naming patterns
   in this market segment.
