# Refund policy

## **Return & Refund Policy**

### **Our Policy: All Sales Are Final**

Due to the nature of our products, **all sales are final.** To ensure the integrity, purity, and safety of the research compounds we supply, we cannot accept returns, exchanges, or refunds for any products once they have been shipped.

### **Exceptions: Damaged, Incorrect, or Missing Items**

We stand by the quality of our products and our commitment to customer satisfaction. If you receive an order that is **damaged during transit, contains an incorrect item, or is missing an item** , we are here to help.

  * **Timeframe:** You must contact our support team within **30 days** of the delivery date as indicated by your tracking number.

  * **How to Start a Claim:** To report an issue, please email us at **support@happypeptides.com**. Your email must include:

    1. Your **order number**.

    2. A clear **description of the issue**.

    3. For damaged or incorrect items, you must attach **clear photos** of the product(s) you received and the packaging.

  * **Resolution:** Once we have evaluated and verified the issue, we will be happy to offer a **refund or a reshipment** of the correct item at our sole discretion.

  * **Important:** Do not attempt to open or use a damaged or incorrect item. Doing so will void your eligibility for a refund or replacement.




Any issues reported after the 30-day period will not be eligible for a refund or reshipment.

### **Order Cancellations**

Most orders are processed and shipped the same day or within 24 business hours. If you need to cancel an order, please notify us immediately.

  * Orders that **have not yet been shipped** may be eligible for cancellation.

  * Cancelled orders that have already been paid are subject to a **10% cancellation fee** to cover non-refundable payment processing fees.

  * Orders that **have already been shipped** are not eligible for cancellation.




### **Shipping Fees**

Shipping fees are **non-refundable** under all circumstances, unless a package is confirmed lost in transit by the shipping carrier. If a package is returned to us because it was denied by the recipient, not retrieved from a designated location, or due to an incorrect address, the shipping fees will not be refunded.

### **Non-Returnable Items & Exchanges**

We do not accept returns or exchanges for any products, including gift cards. Please ensure your order is correct before purchasing.
