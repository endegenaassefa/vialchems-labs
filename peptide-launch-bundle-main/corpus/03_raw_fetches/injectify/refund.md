# Refund policy

At **INJ BIO LABS LLC** , operating under the trading name **Injectify** , we maintain a strict no-return policy due to the nature of our products. We're committed to ensuring your satisfaction through quality assurance and flexible resolution options.

### No Returns

We do not accept returns of any products once purchased and delivered. All sales are final.

### Refund Options

**Store Credit Only:**  
All approved refunds are issued as store credit to your Injectify account. Store credit never expires and can be applied to future purchases.

**  
No Refunds to Original Payment Method:**  
At this time, we do not offer refunds to your original payment method. This is a temporary policy while we transition payment processors.   
Currently, we accept payments via BTCPay (Bitcoin) and e-Transfer (Canada) only.

  
Once we integrate a traditional card processor, additional refund options may become available.

### Damaged or Defective Products

If you receive a damaged or defective product, contact us within 48 hours of delivery at [support@injectify.is](mailto:support@injectify.is). We'll review your case and may offer a replacement or store credit at our discretion. Please include:

  * Photos of the product

  * Photos of the packaging

  * Your order number

  * Description of the damage or defect
