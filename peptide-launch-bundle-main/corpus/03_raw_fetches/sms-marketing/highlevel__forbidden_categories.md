# HighLevel Forbidden 10DLC Categories — Verbatim relevant clauses

## Illegal Substances category (verbatim list)

The Illegal Substances row of the prohibited-categories table
contains the following bullets:

- "Prescription drugs"
- "Cannabis (United States)"
- "CBD (United States)"

## Definitional rule (verbatim)

"Offers for drugs that cannot be sold over the counter in the U.S./
Canada are forbidden."

## Other enumerated bans (top-level categories)

- "High-risk financial services"
- "Third-party lead generation services"
- "Debt collection or forgiveness"
- '"Get rich quick" schemes'
- "Illegal substances"
- "Gambling"
- "S. H.A.F.T. use cases"

## Investigator notes

This rule is the practical 10DLC gatekeeper for the peptide
vertical. The argument structure carriers and SMS aggregators
apply at registration:

1. The product (BPC-157, TB-500, MK-677, retatrutide, semaglutide,
   tesofensine, etc.) is not legal as an OTC supplement under
   DSHEA (FDA position: synthetic peptides not naturally found in
   food are not "dietary ingredients").
2. The product is also not an FDA-approved prescription drug for
   the use cases vendors imply.
3. Therefore "offers for drugs that cannot be sold over the counter"
   applies, and the campaign is forbidden.

The "research use only" labeling does not rescue the analysis —
FDA has explicitly rejected RUO as a defense in seven warning
letters issued March 31 2026 (cohort published April 7 2026 by
Health Law Alliance / Policy Canary), and 10DLC reviewers will
generally read the website end-to-end (not just the disclaimer)
when assessing the campaign.
