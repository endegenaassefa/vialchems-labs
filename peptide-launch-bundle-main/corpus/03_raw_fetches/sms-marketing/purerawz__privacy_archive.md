# Pure Rawz — Privacy Policy Telephone Numbers clause (verbatim)

## Telephone Numbers section (verbatim)

"Transparency is key to our operations. Hence, we have a strict policy
against trading, renting, or selling telephone numbers to any party.
Your telephone numbers are treated with the utmost care and are used
exclusively for communication related to your orders. We respect your
privacy and ensure that your contact information remains secure with
us."

## Investigator notes

- Phone is collected at checkout (the `phone_field` is present in
  the embedded WooCommerce/Elementor checkout JSON) but the privacy
  policy expressly forecloses any non-order use of those numbers
- No SMS-marketing opt-in language anywhere in the policy
- Combined with the absence of an SMS-platform script (Attentive /
  Postscript / Klaviyo SMS form) on the home and checkout pages,
  the observed posture is "phone for transactional only, no SMS
  acquisition channel"
