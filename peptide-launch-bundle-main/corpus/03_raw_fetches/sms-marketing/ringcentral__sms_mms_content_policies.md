# RingCentral SMS/MMS Content Policies — Verbatim relevant clauses

## Prohibited campaigns (verbatim line)

`<li>Pharmaceutical, vitamin, or other drug advertisements</li>`

## Prohibited business activities (verbatim lines)

`<li>Companies that promote, sell, or otherwise reference substances
defined as controlled substances under federal law, regardless of
legality, including marijuana (cannabis)</li>`

`<li>Messages that contain text or otherwise reference substances
defined as controlled substances under federal law, regardless of
legality, including marijuana (cannabis)&nbsp;</li>`

## Other enumerated activity bans (verbatim)

- "Indirect lending, short-term loans, payday loans, student loans"
- "Stocks, bonds, cryptocurrency, investment services"
- "Gambling or sweepstakes"
- "Credit repair, debt relief, debt collection, tax relief"
- "Work-from-home/multi-level marketing"
- "Alcohol, tobacco (including vape), firearms, or fireworks"
- "Kratom or similar substances, regardless of legality"
- "SMS opt-in/phone number sales to third parties"
- "Third-party affiliate/lead generation"

## Investigator notes

RingCentral's "Pharmaceutical, vitamin, or other drug
advertisements" line is the single broadest provider-level ban
encountered. It's substantially wider than the narrower CTIA rule
("abuse of controlled substances") because it captures a peptide
vendor's standard SMS use case — promotional messaging — even
when the products are legal as research chemicals. An applicant
in this vertical would not be approved on RingCentral.
