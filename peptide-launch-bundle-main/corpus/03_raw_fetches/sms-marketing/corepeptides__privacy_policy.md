# Core Peptides — Privacy Policy SMS clause (verbatim)

## Marketing section (verbatim, single paragraph)

"You may opt-in for marketing communications on our website. These
include emails and SMS messaging, which you can opt-out of at any
time. No purchase is necessary. Standard message and data rates may
apply."

## Investigator notes

The Marketing clause says SMS is offered, but no SMS-capture form
element, no SMS-platform vendor script (Attentive, Postscript,
Klaviyo SMS, etc.), and no `<input type="tel">` opt-in surface were
present on the homepage. The clause also lacks the specific
TCPA/CTIA-style consent language that Postscript, Klaviyo, and
Attentive instruct merchants to display at point of capture
(no "recurring auto-dialed marketing", no "Reply HELP for help",
no clear list of message frequency).
