# Postscript SMS-Marketing Compliance Overview — Verbatim relevant clauses

## SHAFT Compliance section (verbatim)

"SHAFT regulations protect consumers by prohibiting brands from
sending content containing sex, hate, alcohol, firearms, or tobacco
(CBD--included) to inappropriate audiences. These regulations are
industry-wide and affect every SMS platform, including Postscript.

While SHAFT content has been heavily regulated in SMS marketing for
years, the CTIA tightened its rules surrounding this content in May
2021. As a result, Postscript can no longer service brands that
offer or communicate about sex, hate, firearms, tobacco
(CBD--included) products.

Brands that sell alcohol and adhere to specific CTIA requirements—
including age-verifying both on the website and in the SMS thread—
are able to promote their brand via SMS marketing."

## TCPA-compliant opt-in language Postscript publishes (verbatim)

"By providing your number and clicking the button, you agree to
receive recurring auto-dialed marketing SMS (including cart
reminders; AI content; artificial or prerecorded voices) and our
TERMS OF SERVICE (including arbitration). Consent is not required
to purchase. Msg & data rates may apply. Msg frequency varies.
Reply HELP for help; STOP to opt-out. View PRIVACY POLICY."

## Cart-recovery rule (verbatim)

"In 2021, carriers enforced policies for abandoned shopping carts
and abandoned checkout flows to ensure that their end consumers
receive a consistent experience.

All automations related to abandoned shopping carts and abandoned
checkouts should:

* Be limited to only one message.
* Send the message within 48 hours of the trigger event."

## Penalty exposure (verbatim)

"Violations of the TCPA can lead to damages of $500-$1,500 per
message, while violations of CTIA guidelines can lead to being
prevented from using the wireless carrier networks."

## Investigator notes

- Postscript does not enumerate "supplements", "peptides",
  "research chemicals", "anabolic steroids", "SARMs", "hormones",
  or "weight loss" anywhere in this overview
- The platform's enforcement model is: (a) brand vetting at
  onboarding, (b) ongoing carrier feedback ("opt-out rates above 2%
  trigger violations" — see Klaviyo's parallel disclosure), (c)
  account suspension for any TCPA/CTIA breach
- The TCPA private-right-of-action damages ($500-$1,500/message)
  are why every SMS provider polices their merchants aggressively;
  it's the carriers' liability shield as well as their own
