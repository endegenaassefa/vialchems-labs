# Biotech Peptides — Privacy Policy SMS clauses (verbatim)

## Phone number collection

"User phone numbers are also collected upon purchasing from the website,
for shipping purposes."

## Marketing opt-in via SMS

"Users may receive promotional advertisements via SMS messaging if they
opt-in on our website."

## Mobile information third-party sharing

"No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for
marketing/promotional purposes."

## Originator opt-in carve-out (CTIA-derived)

"All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data
and consent; this information will not be shared with any third
parties."

## Opt-out

"For SMS messaging, users can opt-out at any time by replying STOP."

## Removal lag

"Please allow 10 business days for your email or phone number to be
removed from the mailing list."

## Standard rates disclosure

"Standard messaging rates may apply."

## Investigator notes

The privacy policy explicitly contemplates SMS marketing, but on the
public surfaces actually fetched (homepage, checkout) no SMS-capture
form, no Attentive / Postscript / Klaviyo SMS provider script, and
no `<input type="tel">` opt-in field were present. The policy
language is consistent with a Klaviyo-or-similar dual-channel posture
where SMS is technically supported in the privacy contract but not
actively run as an acquisition channel.
