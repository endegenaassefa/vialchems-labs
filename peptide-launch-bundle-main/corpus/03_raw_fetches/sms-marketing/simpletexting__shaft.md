# SimpleTexting Prohibited Content — Verbatim relevant clauses

## Controlled substances clause (verbatim, single sentence inside
## the age-gating section)

"Note that you're prohibited from sending messages about the abuse
of controlled substances."

## Cannabis clause (verbatim)

"Cannabis is a trickier topic to monitor because the legality of it
varies from state to state. It's impossible to filter by recipient
location, which makes it almost impossible to implement legally.
Because of this, at SimpleTexting, we monitor and prohibit messages
that include references to cannabis and marijuana (including CBD)."

## Other enumerated bans (verbatim short list)

## Investigator notes

SimpleTexting does not enumerate "supplements", "peptides",
"research chemicals", "anabolic steroids", "SARMs", "hormones", or
"weight loss" anywhere in this article. The "abuse of controlled
substances" clause is the verbatim CTIA Short Code Monitoring
Handbook v1.9 §3.5.1 language and would govern at the carrier
filtering layer regardless of the merchant's own SMS provider.
