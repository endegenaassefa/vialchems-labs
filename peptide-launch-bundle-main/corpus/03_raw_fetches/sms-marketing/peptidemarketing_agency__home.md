# Peptide Marketing (vertical-specific agency) — Service-menu observation

## Service offerings (verbatim list)

The agency lists exactly four services:

1. "Paid Advertising"
2. "E-mail Marketing"
3. "Search Engine Optimization"
4. "Web Development"

## Negative finding (verbatim absence)

The site does NOT mention SMS marketing, text-message marketing,
Klaviyo SMS, Postscript, Attentive, carrier restrictions, 10DLC, or
TCPA anywhere on the homepage.

## Investigator notes

A vertical-specific marketing agency that has chosen its core
service mix specifically for peptide / research-chemical brands
publishes email but not SMS. This is consistent with — and
strongly corroborates — the carrier / SMS-platform-policy reasons
documented elsewhere in the evidence file: SMS is not a viable
acquisition channel for the research-peptide vertical and the
agencies that work this vertical have priced that out of their
offering.
