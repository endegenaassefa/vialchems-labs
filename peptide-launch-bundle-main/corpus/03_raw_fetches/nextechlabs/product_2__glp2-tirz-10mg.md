## Description

TIRZ – 10mg is a laboratory-grade Tirzepatide peptide designed specifically for research purposes. This compound acts as a dual agonist for glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide (GIP) and glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) receptors, making it a significant focus in metabolic and endocrinology studies.

In ongoing research, Tirzepatide has demonstrated potential in modulating insulin secretion, improving glucose tolerance, and reducing appetite. It is being explored as a therapeutic candidate in the treatment of type 2 diabetes and obesity in experimental settings.

This 10mg formulation offers precise dosing for controlled lab experiments and is produced under strict quality standards to support reproducible results.

**Research Use Only**  
This product is not approved for human or veterinary use. It is intended strictly for in vitro and laboratory research applications by qualified professionals.
