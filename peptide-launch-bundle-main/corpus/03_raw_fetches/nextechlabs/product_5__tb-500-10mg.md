## Description

TB-500 10mg – Thymosin Beta 4 Recombinant Protein Peptide for Research

TB-500 is a synthetic analog of Thymosin Beta 4, a powerful recombinant protein found in nearly all human tissues. Known for its role in promoting cell migration, tissue regeneration, and inflammation modulation, TB-500 is a widely studied research peptide used in regenerative medicine models and biological studies.

Each 10mg vial contains highly purified, lyophilized Thymosin Beta 4 suitable for in-vitro research applications. Often referred to as a protein peptide, it is designed to replicate the activity of natural TB4 proteins within controlled laboratory environments.
