### My package says it's been delivered but I haven't received it

Although courrier issues are beyond our scope of our business, we recommend looking around your property for the package. Sometimes couriers leave parcels in inconspicuous areas for safety reasons or they may have delivered to a neighbor next door. In some instances packages may be delayed due to inclement weather. In rare cases we have also seen postal service online tracking system issues where a package says it has been delivered but may not have reached its final destination. 

We suggest contacting your local post office or carrier and providing them with the tracking number as they may be able to provide you with additional information regarding the delivery of your package. 

Should your local postal service be unable to assist you, please email us at [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection) . At this point we will have to file a lost package claim with the courier service. In order to file a claim, we will need a signed note from you stating you did not receive your package. In most instances, postal services consider domestic packages lost only after it has been 21 business days since the package has been in transit.

Once a claim is filed it can take up to 60 business days for them to come to a resolution. If you do not receive the package by the end of the 60 days or USPS decides to deny the claim, we will work with you as best we can to resolve the issue.
