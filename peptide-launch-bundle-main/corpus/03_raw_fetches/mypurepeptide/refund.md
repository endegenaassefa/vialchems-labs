## Refund and Returns Policy

Due to the specialized nature of our research materials and strict quality assurance protocols, all sales are final unless otherwise required by law or approved after review at our sole discretion. 

**Important:** If your order arrives damaged, defective, incorrect, or appears to be missing items, contact us within **48 hours of delivery** with your order number and clear supporting photo or video evidence.

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, please contact [support@mypurepeptide.com](mailto:support@mypurepeptide.com) within 48 hours of delivery with your order number, a description of the issue, and clear photo or video evidence of the package and contents. 

Submission of a request does not guarantee approval. All refund, replacement, or exchange decisions are subject to review and may be approved or denied at our sole discretion, except where otherwise required by law. 

## 2 Eligibility for Review

To be considered for review, your request should include:

  * Your order number
  * A clear description of the issue
  * Photos or unboxing video showing the package, shipping label, product condition, and contents
  * Submission within 48 hours of confirmed delivery



## 3 Non-Returnable Items

The following are generally not eligible for return, refund, replacement, or exchange unless required by law or approved at our sole discretion: 

  * Opened or used products
  * Products stored, handled, or maintained improperly after delivery
  * Orders submitted with incorrect or incomplete shipping information provided by the customer
  * Requests made outside the stated review window
  * Sale, promotional, discounted, or clearance items, unless they arrived damaged or incorrect
  * Orders where delivery issues resulted from carrier delays, failed delivery attempts, refusal, or failure to retrieve a package



## 4 Refunds

If your request is approved, any refund issued will be applied to the original method of payment within a reasonable number of business days. Processing times may vary depending on your bank, card issuer, payment processor, or financial institution. 

Shipping charges are generally non-refundable unless the issue resulted from our error. 

### Late or Missing Refunds

If you have not received a refund yet, first check your bank account again. Then contact your credit card company or bank, as it may take time before a refund is officially posted. 

If you have done this and still have not received your refund, contact [support@mypurepeptide.com](mailto:support@mypurepeptide.com).

We only replace items when approved after review of damage, defect, missing item, or fulfillment error. If you believe you received an eligible item, email [support@mypurepeptide.com](mailto:support@mypurepeptide.com) with your order number and supporting evidence.
