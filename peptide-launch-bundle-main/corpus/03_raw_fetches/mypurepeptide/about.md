# Research-focused quality, transparent documentation, and reliable support.

MyPurePeptide is built for customers who value clear labeling, independent lab documentation, reliable fulfillment, and a straightforward research-use-only purchasing experience.

## 1 Who we are

MyPurePeptide supplies research and laboratory-use peptides with a focus on consistency, documentation, and trust. Our goal is simple: provide a clean, transparent purchasing experience backed by clear product information and accessible documentation. 

We keep the catalog focused, maintain clear product labeling, publish available COAs for active lots, and prioritize reliable fulfillment and responsive customer support. 

## 2 How we operate

  * **Independent documentation:** We provide access to third-party lab documentation and COAs for supported lots.
  * **Clear labeling:** Product names, vial amounts, and lot information are presented clearly where available.
  * **Reliable fulfillment:** Orders are handled carefully and processed according to our posted shipping policy.
  * **Support-first experience:** Customers can contact us for order, account, shipping, and documentation questions.
  * **Research-use positioning:** Products are sold strictly for laboratory research purposes only.
