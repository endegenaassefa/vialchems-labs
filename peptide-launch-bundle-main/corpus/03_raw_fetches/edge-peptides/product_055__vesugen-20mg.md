## Description

Vesugen is a peptide bioregulator developed by Russian researcher Vladimir Khavinson, investigated for its interactions with vascular endothelial cell signaling pathways and extracellular matrix regulatory mechanisms. It has been studied for its observed effects on endothelial gene expression, atherogenic marker modulation, and vascular smooth muscle cell activity in experimental models.
