## Description

Melanotan 1 (MT-I), also known as afamelanotide, is a synthetic peptide analogue of alpha-melanocyte-stimulating hormone (α-MSH) investigated for its interactions with melanocortin receptor 1 (MC1R) signaling and downstream melanogenesis pathways. It has been studied for its observed effects on melanocyte pigmentation mechanisms, UV-induced DNA damage response pathways, and epidermal photoprotective signaling.
