## Description

Epitalon (also referred to as Epithalon) is a tetrapeptide bioregulator investigated for its interactions with telomerase activity and epigenetic regulatory mechanisms. It has been studied for its observed effects on telomere dynamics and DNA maintenance pathways, as well as its interactions with pineal gland signaling and melatonin biosynthesis regulation.
