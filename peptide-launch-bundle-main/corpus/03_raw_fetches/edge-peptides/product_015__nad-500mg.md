## Description

NAD+ (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide) is a naturally occurring coenzyme found in all living cells, investigated for its interactions with cellular energy metabolism pathways and DNA repair regulatory mechanisms. It has been studied for its observed effects on mitochondrial electron transport chain activity, sirtuin (SIRT1-7) deacetylase pathway modulation, and poly(ADP-ribose) polymerase (PARP) DNA repair signaling. Research has also examined its interactions with age-associated changes in endogenous NAD+ biosynthesis, CD38 NADase activity, and downstream effects on metabolic gene expression networks in experimental models.
