## Description

Survodutide is a synthetic dual-receptor agonist investigated for its interactions with glucagon receptor (GCGR) and glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor (GLP-1R) signaling pathways. It has been studied for its observed effects on hypothalamic neuropeptide signaling, hepatic glucose metabolism, and adipocyte lipid oxidation regulatory mechanisms in experimental models. Survodutide is currently undergoing Phase 3 clinical investigation as a pharmacological compound.
