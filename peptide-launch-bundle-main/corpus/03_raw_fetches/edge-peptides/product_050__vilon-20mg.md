## Description

Vilon is a Khavinson-derived bioregulator dipeptide investigated for its interactions with innate immune signaling pathways and epigenetic regulatory mechanisms. It has been studied for its observed effects on cytokine expression profiles, lymphocyte subset regulation, and DNA methylation pathway activity in experimental models.
