## Description

Selank is a synthetic heptapeptide analogue of tuftsin, a naturally occurring immunomodulatory peptide. Research has examined its observed effects on GABAergic and serotonergic neurotransmitter pathway modulation, neurotrophic factor expression, and neuropeptide receptor interactions. It has also been investigated for its interactions with innate immune signaling pathways and cytokine expression profiles. Selank was developed in Russia, where it has undergone clinical investigation as a pharmacological compound.
