## Description

Semaglutide is a synthetic GLP-1 receptor agonist investigated for its interactions with glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor (GLP-1R) signaling pathways and downstream pancreatic beta cell and hypothalamic regulatory mechanisms. Cagrilintide is a synthetic amylin analogue investigated for its interactions with amylin receptor (AMY1-3) signaling pathways via a mechanistically distinct receptor pathway. These compounds have been investigated in combination for their complementary and mechanistically distinct receptor pathway interactions, with research examining their combined effects on hypothalamic energy substrate regulation and pancreatic hormone secretion dynamics.
