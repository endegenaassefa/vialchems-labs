## Description

KLOW is a multi-compound peptide blend consisting of GHK-Cu (50 mg), TB-500 (10 mg), BPC-157 (10 mg), and KPV (10 mg), formulated for research examining overlapping tissue remodeling and cellular signaling pathways.

  1. **GHK-Cu** is a copper-binding tripeptide investigated for its interactions with extracellular matrix remodeling, inflammatory cytokine expression, angiogenic signaling, and collagen synthesis regulation.
  2. **TB-500 (TB-4)** is a synthetic Thymosin Beta-4 analogue studied for its interactions with actin sequestration, fibroblast migration, and extracellular matrix reorganization across muscle, tendon, and ligament tissue models.
  3. **KPV** is a tripeptide derived from alpha-MSH investigated for its interactions with melanocortin receptor signaling, inflammatory cytokine modulation, and dermal cellular pathway activity.
  4. **BPC-157** is a naturally occurring peptide studied for its observed effects on fibroblast proliferation, gastrointestinal epithelial integrity, tendon extracellular matrix remodeling, and inflammatory marker modulation.



These compounds are investigated in combination for their complementary mechanistic profiles across overlapping extracellular matrix, angiogenic, and inflammatory signaling pathways.
