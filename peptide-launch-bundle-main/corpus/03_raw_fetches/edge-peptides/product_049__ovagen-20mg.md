Ovagen is a Khavinson-derived bioregulator peptide investigated for its interactions with hepatic and gastrointestinal tissue signaling pathways. Research has examined its observed effects on hepatic stellate cell activity and fibrotic marker expression, as well as its interactions with gastrointestinal mucosal epithelial integrity mechanisms at the cellular level.
