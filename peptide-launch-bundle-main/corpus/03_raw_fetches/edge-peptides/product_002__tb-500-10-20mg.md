## Description

TB-500 (TB-4) is a synthetic analogue of the naturally occurring peptide Thymosin Beta-4, investigated for its interactions with actin sequestration mechanisms and cytoskeletal regulatory pathways. It has been studied for its observed effects on fibroblast migration, angiogenic signaling, extracellular matrix reorganization, and inflammatory cytokine modulation across muscle, tendon, and ligament tissue models in experimental settings.
