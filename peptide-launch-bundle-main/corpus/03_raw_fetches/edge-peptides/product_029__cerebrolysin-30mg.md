## Description

Cerebrolysin is a peptide-based nootropic compound investigated for its interactions with neurotrophic signaling pathways and neuropeptide receptor activity. Research has examined its observed effects on BDNF and NGF expression, neuroinflammatory marker modulation, and synaptic plasticity mechanisms across various experimental models involving neurodegenerative pathway dysregulation, cerebrovascular injury models, and traumatic neural stress conditions.

Our formulation is cerebroprotein hydrolysate, which can be reconstituted with bacteriostatic water for research use in the same manner as standard cerebrolysin preparations.
