## Description

Human Chorionic Gonadotropin (hCG) is a naturally occurring glycoprotein hormone composed of alpha and beta peptide subunits investigated for its interactions with luteinizing hormone receptors (LHR) and downstream gonadal steroidogenesis pathways. It has been studied for its observed effects on Leydig cell androgen biosynthesis, granulosa cell steroidogenic activity, and hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal (HPG) axis signaling dynamics. Research has examined its structural and functional homology with luteinizing hormone (LH) and its interactions with shared receptor binding mechanisms and intracellular cAMP signaling cascades.
