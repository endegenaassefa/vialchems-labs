## Description

CJC-1295 with DAC is a synthetic GHRH analogue incorporating a Drug Affinity Complex (DAC) technology investigated for its interactions with pituitary somatotroph GHRH receptor signaling and its observed effects on endogenous GH secretion dynamics and IGF-1 expression regulation. The DAC modification has been studied for its effects on albumin binding affinity and plasma half-life extension, enabling sustained GHRH receptor activation over extended timeframes relative to unmodified GHRH analogues. Research has examined its interactions with somatotropic axis regulatory mechanisms and GH pulse amplitude modulation in experimental models.
