## Description

Oxytocin is a naturally occurring peptide hormone biosynthesized in the hypothalamic paraventricular and supraoptic nuclei and released via the posterior pituitary gland. It has been investigated for its interactions with oxytocin receptor (OXTR) signaling pathways and observed effects on hypothalamic-limbic circuit activity, HPA axis modulation, and neuroendocrine signaling dynamics. Research has also examined its interactions with satellite cell activity, myogenic signaling pathways, and extracellular matrix remodeling mechanisms in musculoskeletal tissue models. Additionally, age-associated changes in hypothalamic oxytocin expression have been examined in experimental models.
