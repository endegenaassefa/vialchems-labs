## Description

Chonluten is a Khavinson-derived bioregulator peptide investigated for its interactions with pulmonary and gastrointestinal tissue signaling pathways. It has been studied for its observed effects on inflammatory cytokine gene expression, reactive oxygen species (ROS) modulation, and epithelial cell regulatory mechanisms in bronchial and gastrointestinal mucosal tissue models.
