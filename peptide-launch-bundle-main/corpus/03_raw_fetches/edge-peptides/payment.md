The apps listed above are the easiest to get started with using Bitcoin (BTC). You can also use any of the many reputable crypto exchanges, including Kraken, Crypto.com, Coinbase, and Binance.

New to crypto? No problem, here’s a step-by-step guide to get you started. While the steps might vary a bit from app to app, the basic process looks like this:

  1. Download your app of choice based on your location, or make an account on their website if using a computer. 
  2. Sign up, verify your account, and link/add funds from your bank (it may ask for ID verification).
  3. Make an order on our site with cryptocurrency (choose Bitcoin from the dropdown). you’ll see the exact bitcoin (BTC) amount needed.
  4. Go back to your app and buy slightly more bitcoin than you need (to cover small network fees).
  5. Inside the app, click withdraw/send bitcoin, and either copy paste the bitcoin address* or scan the QR code from our checkout page.
  6. Enter the exact checkout amount > Review fees > Confirm send.
  7. (Optional) send us an email with the transaction ID when you’ve made the payment so we can confirm that everything worked correctly. 



* **Important** : Double-check that the wallet address on your device matches the one shown at checkout before sending by verifying that the first and last 4 characters match exactly. Cryptocurrency sends are permanent and can’t be reversed.
