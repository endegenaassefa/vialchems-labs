## Description

N-Acetyl Semax Amidate is a chemically modified analogue of the synthetic peptide Semax, incorporating N-terminal acetylation and C-terminal amidation to enhance proteolytic stability and bioavailability. It has been investigated for its observed effects on BDNF expression, serotonergic and dopaminergic neurotransmitter pathway modulation, and neuropeptide receptor interactions. In experimental models, it has been examined for its effects on neurotrophic signaling cascades and neuroinflammatory marker expression under conditions of induced neural stress.
