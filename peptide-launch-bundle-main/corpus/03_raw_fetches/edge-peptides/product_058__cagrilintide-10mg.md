## Description

Cagrilintide is a synthetic long-acting amylin analogue investigated for its interactions with amylin receptor (AMY1-3) signaling pathways and hypothalamic energy homeostasis regulatory mechanisms. It has been studied for its observed effects on hypothalamic neuropeptide signaling, gastric motility regulation, and glucagon secretion modulation in experimental models. Research has examined its interactions with GLP-1 receptor agonist pathway activity, with investigation into combined mechanistic profiles when studied alongside semaglutide.
