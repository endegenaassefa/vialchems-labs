## Description

Glutathione is a naturally occurring tripeptide antioxidant composed of glutamate, cysteine, and glycine, investigated for its interactions with cellular redox signaling pathways and oxidative stress regulatory mechanisms. It has been studied for its observed effects on reactive oxygen species (ROS) modulation, phase II detoxification enzyme activity, and glutathione peroxidase and reductase pathway dynamics. Research has also examined its interactions with innate immune signaling pathways, melanogenesis regulatory mechanisms, and age-associated changes in endogenous glutathione biosynthesis in experimental models.
