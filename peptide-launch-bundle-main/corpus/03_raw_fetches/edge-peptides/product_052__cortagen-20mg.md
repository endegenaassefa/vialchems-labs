## Description

Cortagen is a Khavinson-derived bioregulator peptide investigated for its interactions with central and peripheral nervous system cellular signaling pathways. It has been studied for its observed effects on reactive oxygen species (ROS) modulation, neuroinflammatory cytokine expression, and DNA repair mechanism activity in neural tissue models. Research has also examined its interactions with innate immune signaling pathways and cerebrovascular regulatory mechanisms in experimental models.
