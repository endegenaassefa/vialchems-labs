## Description

Cartalax is a Khavinson-derived bioregulator peptide investigated for its interactions with connective tissue cellular signaling pathways across cartilage, bone, and tendon tissue models. It has been studied for its observed effects on chondrocyte and osteoblast proliferation mechanisms, reactive oxygen species (ROS) modulation, and extracellular matrix regulatory gene expression in experimental models.
