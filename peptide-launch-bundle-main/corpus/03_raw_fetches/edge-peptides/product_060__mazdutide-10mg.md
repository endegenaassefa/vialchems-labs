## Description

Mazdutide is a synthetic dual-receptor agonist investigated for its interactions with glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor (GLP-1R) and glucagon receptor (GCGR) signaling pathways. It has been studied for its observed effects on hypothalamic energy substrate regulation, hepatic glucose metabolism, and pancreatic beta cell signaling dynamics in experimental models. Mazdutide is the generic peptide equivalent of OXM3, a compound that has undergone clinical investigation as a pharmacological agent.
