## Description

Acetyl Octapeptide-3 (SNAP-8) is a synthetic octapeptide analogue of the SNAP-25 protein investigated for its interactions with SNARE complex formation and neuromuscular junction signaling mechanisms. It has been studied for its observed effects on acetylcholine vesicle release modulation and myosin contractile pathway activity at the dermal neuromuscular interface. Research has examined its interactions with facial muscle contractile signaling and extracellular matrix dynamics in dermal tissue models.
