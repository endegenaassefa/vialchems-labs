## Description

SS-31 (Elamipretide) is a synthetic tetrapeptide investigated for its interactions with inner mitochondrial membrane cardiolipin and its observed effects on electron transport chain efficiency and reactive oxygen species (ROS) modulation. It has been studied for its interactions with mitochondrial membrane potential dynamics, cristae structural integrity, and oxidative phosphorylation pathway activity. Research has examined its effects across various experimental models involving mitochondrial dysfunction and bioenergetic pathway dysregulation.
