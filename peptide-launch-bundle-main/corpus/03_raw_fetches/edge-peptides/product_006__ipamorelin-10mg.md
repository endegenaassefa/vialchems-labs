## Description

Ipamorelin is a synthetic pentapeptide growth hormone secretagogue investigated for its interactions with ghrelin receptor (GHSR-1a) signaling pathways and pituitary somatotroph cell activity. It has been studied for its observed effects on GH pulse amplitude modulation, IGF-1 expression dynamics, and somatostatin inhibition mechanisms in experimental models. Research has noted its selective receptor binding profile, with studies examining its interactions with GHSR-1a in the absence of significant corticotropic or prolactin axis pathway activation compared to other synthetic secretagogues.
