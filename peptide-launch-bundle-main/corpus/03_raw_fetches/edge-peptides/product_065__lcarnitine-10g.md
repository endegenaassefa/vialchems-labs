## Description

L-carnitine is a naturally occurring compound made in the body from certain amino acids and also found in some foods. It plays a key role in energy production by helping transport long-chain fatty acids into the mitochondria—the cell’s powerhouses—where they can be broken down and converted into usable energy. In research settings, it’s studied for its involvement in fatty acid metabolism and related cellular processes.

Our l-carnitine comes in 20 ml (500 mg/ml) liquid reconstituted vials with 10 grams l-carnitine per vial.
