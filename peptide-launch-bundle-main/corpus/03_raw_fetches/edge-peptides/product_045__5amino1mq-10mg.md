## Description

5-Amino-1MQ is a small-molecule NNMT (nicotinamide N-methyltransferase) inhibitor investigated for its interactions with NAD⁺ metabolic pathways and one-carbon metabolism. It has been studied for its observed effects on adipocyte lipid metabolism, methylation capacity, and intracellular energy substrate regulation at the cellular level. Research has examined its interactions with NNMT enzymatic activity and downstream effects on metabolic gene expression networks.
