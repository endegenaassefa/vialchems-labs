## Description

GHRP-6 is a synthetic hexapeptide growth hormone secretagogue investigated for its interactions with ghrelin receptor (GHSR-1a) signaling pathways and pituitary somatotroph cell activity. It has been studied for its observed effects on GH pulse amplitude modulation, IGF-1 expression dynamics, and hypothalamic neuropeptide Y (NPY) pathway interactions in experimental models.
