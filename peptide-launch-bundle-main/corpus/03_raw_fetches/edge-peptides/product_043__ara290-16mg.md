## Description

ARA-290 is a synthetic peptide derived from erythropoietin (EPO), engineered to interact selectively with the innate repair receptor (IRR) without activating erythropoietic signaling pathways. It has been investigated for its observed effects on inflammatory cytokine modulation, Schwann cell activity, and peripheral nerve fiber signaling mechanisms in experimental models involving neuroinflammatory and demyelinating pathway dysregulation.
