## Description

BPC-157 is a naturally occurring pentadecapeptide investigated for its interactions with tissue remodeling pathways and cellular repair mechanisms. It has been studied for its observed effects on fibroblast proliferation, tendon and muscle extracellular matrix remodeling, gastrointestinal epithelial integrity, and inflammatory cytokine modulation in experimental models.
