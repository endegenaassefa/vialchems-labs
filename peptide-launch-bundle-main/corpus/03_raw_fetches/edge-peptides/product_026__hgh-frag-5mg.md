## Description

HGH Fragment 176-191 is a synthetic peptide derived from the C-terminal region (amino acids 176–191) of human growth hormone (hGH), investigated for its interactions with beta-adrenergic receptor signaling pathways and adipocyte lipid metabolism mechanisms. It has been studied for its observed effects on lipolytic enzyme activity, fatty acid oxidation regulatory pathways, and adipogenesis inhibition mechanisms in experimental models, operating independently of IGF-1 axis activation.
