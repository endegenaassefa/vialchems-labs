## Description

Delta Sleep-Inducing Peptide (DSIP) is a naturally occurring neuropeptide investigated for its interactions with hypothalamic signaling pathways and circadian oscillator mechanisms. It has been studied for its observed effects on neurotransmitter modulation across serotonergic, dopaminergic, and opioidergic pathways, as well as its interactions with HPA axis signaling dynamics and nociceptive pathway activity in experimental models.
