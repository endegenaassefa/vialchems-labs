## Description

PE-22-28 is a synthetic peptide analogue modeled after the BDNF loop 4 domain, investigated for its interactions with TrkB (tropomyosin receptor kinase B) signaling pathways and downstream neurotrophic cascade activity. It has been studied for its observed effects on serotonergic receptor modulation, HPA axis signaling dynamics, and synaptic plasticity mechanisms in experimental models involving neurochemical pathway dysregulation.
