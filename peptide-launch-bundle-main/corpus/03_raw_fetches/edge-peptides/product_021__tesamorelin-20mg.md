## Description

Tesamorelin is a synthetic peptide analogue of growth hormone-releasing hormone (GHRH) investigated for its interactions with pituitary somatotroph GHRH receptor signaling and endogenous GH secretion dynamics. It has been studied for its observed effects on somatotropic axis regulation, IGF-1 expression, and lipid metabolism pathway modulation in experimental models. Tesamorelin is the generic peptide equivalent of Egrifta, a compound that has undergone clinical investigation as a pharmacological agent.
