# Third-Party Test Results 

We routinely send our research peptides for independent testing at trusted third-party laboratories like Janoshik, Chromate, and MZ Biolabs. These tests verify the potency and safety of our products. We test for both purity (% and peptide content) as well as biological safety tests (endotoxins, bacteria, heavy metals).

On each test certificate of analysis (CoA) document, you can see the test findings, testing date, and other helpful details. For tests from Janoshik and Chromate, you can also scan the QR code to verify the test online. See our latest third-party tests below. We’re always working on getting more of our peptides tested, as well as re-testing peptides. 

We also encourage customers to send our peptides for their own independent testing and post their test results. You can get free store credit for the cost of the tests (see more details below).

If you have any questions about tests, feel free to email us at [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#f29b9c949db297969597829782869b969781dc919d9f).

## Customer Third-Party Testing Store Credit

Want to independently test our products? We will issue store credit for the price of the test(s) at any reputable third-party lab like Janoshik or Chromate. To qualify for this credit, you must provide us with:

  * The test report with your name on it, as an image or a link to the report posted on the lab’s website
  * The tests you ordered and the price of the tests
  * The invoice or other payment slip, either as a document or a screenshot, with identifying information like the lab’s name
  * The test should be dated within 60 days of you sending us an email about third-party testing credit/ordering the tests



After this, we will give you store credit equivalent to the value of the tests in USD. We may want to share your test results on our website. If you don’t want us to share the tests, please let us know. We can also redact your last name if needed.
