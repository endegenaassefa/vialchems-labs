## Description

Pinealon is a short-chain Khavinson bioregulator peptide investigated for its interactions with gene expression regulatory mechanisms and protein synthesis pathways in neural tissue models. It has been studied for its observed effects on pineal gland signaling, circadian oscillator gene expression, and neuropeptide biosynthesis regulation. Research has examined its interactions with epigenetic regulatory mechanisms in neural cell models across various experimental conditions.
