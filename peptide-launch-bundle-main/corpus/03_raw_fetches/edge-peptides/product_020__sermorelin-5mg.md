## Description

Sermorelin is a synthetic 29-amino acid peptide analogue of growth hormone-releasing hormone (GHRH) investigated for its interactions with pituitary somatotroph GHRH receptor signaling and its observed effects on endogenous GH secretion dynamics. It has been studied for its interactions with somatotropic axis regulatory mechanisms, IGF-1 expression modulation, and GH pulse amplitude and frequency dynamics in experimental models. Sermorelin has undergone clinical investigation as a pharmacological compound for applications involving somatotropic axis dysregulation.
