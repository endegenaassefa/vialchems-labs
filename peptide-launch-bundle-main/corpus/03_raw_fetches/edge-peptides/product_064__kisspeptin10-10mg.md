## Description

Kisspeptin-10 is a peptide investigated for its interactions with hypothalamic KISS1 receptor (KISS1R) signaling and its role in gonadotropin-releasing hormone (GnRH) pulse regulation. It has been studied for its observed effects on luteinizing hormone (LH) and follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) secretion dynamics, and downstream androgenic and estrogenic steroidogenesis pathways. Research has examined its interactions with hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal (HPG) axis signaling mechanisms across various experimental models.
