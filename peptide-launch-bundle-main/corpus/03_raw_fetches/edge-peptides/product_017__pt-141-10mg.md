## Description

PT-141 (Bremelanotide) is a synthetic melanocortin receptor agonist investigated for its interactions with melanocortin receptor subtypes MC3R and MC4R within hypothalamic and limbic system signaling pathways. It has been studied for its observed effects on dopaminergic neurotransmitter pathway modulation and hypothalamic neuropeptide signaling mechanisms, operating via central nervous system receptor activity distinct from peripheral vascular signaling pathways.
