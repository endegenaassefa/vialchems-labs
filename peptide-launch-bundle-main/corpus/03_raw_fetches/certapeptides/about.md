About CertaPeptides

# Advancing Research Through Verified Quality

Founded in Romania in 2024, CertaPeptides provides high-purity research peptides backed by third-party verification to customers across the EU. Every compound we supply is a commitment to quality and transparency.

## Our Core Principles

### Purity First

Every batch undergoes rigorous HPLC analysis and mass spectrometry verification. We don't ship anything below ≥98% purity.

### Transparency

Full Certificate of Analysis with every order. No hidden data, no vague claims — just verifiable results.

### Customer-Focused

Built for you. From storage guides to reconstitution tools, we support your needs at every step.

### EU-Wide Delivery

Shipping to 24 EU member states with temperature-controlled logistics and full order tracking.

## Our Mission

The research peptide market is plagued by inconsistent quality, missing documentation, and opaque supply chains. We started CertaPeptides to change that.

Every peptide we sell undergoes rigorous third-party testing. Every order includes a Certificate of Analysis. Every claim we make is backed by data. We believe researchers deserve suppliers they can trust — not gamble on.

Based in Romania, we serve research institutions, universities, and independent laboratories across all 24 EU SEPA member states with fast, reliable shipping and responsive support.
