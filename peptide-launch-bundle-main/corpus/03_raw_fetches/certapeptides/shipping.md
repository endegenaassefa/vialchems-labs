### Policy Details

Quality Guarantee

If any product fails to meet the purity specifications stated on the COA, we will replace it or issue a full refund at no cost to you.

14-Day Return Window

Unopened products in original packaging may be returned within 14 days of delivery in accordance with EU consumer rights (Directive 2011/83/EU). Return shipping costs are the customer's responsibility.

Damaged Shipments

Report damaged shipments within 48 hours with photographic evidence. We will send a replacement at no additional cost via your original shipping method.

Non-Returnable Items

Opened products and items stored outside recommended conditions cannot be returned due to quality and safety regulations.
