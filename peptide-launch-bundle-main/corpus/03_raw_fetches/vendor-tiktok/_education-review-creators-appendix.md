## Pattern observed
The dominant vendor-acquisition pattern on TikTok is NOT vendor-owned brand accounts (which are either nonexistent, zero-follower placeholders, or constantly at risk of removal). It is INFLUENCER-PROXIED affiliate selling: third-party creators run the personal brand, hold the audience, and pipe traffic into vendor sites via Linktree/discount-code mechanics. The vendor does NOT need to operate the account; they need to operate the affiliate program and supply codes.
