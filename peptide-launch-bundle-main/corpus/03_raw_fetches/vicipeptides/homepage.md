1\. What purity are your peptides? 

Every Vici peptide is verified to be greater than 99% pure. Every batch must meet that threshold before it’s released for sale — no exceptions, no “average” purity claims. The exact purity of the batch you receive is printed on its Certificate of Analysis.

Every batch is tested by an independent ISO 17025 accredited laboratory that specializes in peptide analysis. Testing covers all five points of our protocol: Identification, Purity, Potency (Net Peptide Content), Endotoxin, and Heavy Metals. Most vendors run HPLC and stop there — we pay a specialized accredited lab to run all five, and the results go straight onto the COA. 

3\. Do you provide Certificates of Analysis (COA)? 

Yes — a full COA is available for every product and every batch. Unlike a basic purity summary, ours include full HPLC chromatograms and complete results for all five tests in our protocol. You can download a COA directly from each product page or request one from our support team.

Verification isn’t done by us — it’s done by an independent ISO 17025 accredited lab that specializes in peptide analysis. They confirm identity by mass spectrometry and purity by HPLC against reference standards, so what’s on the label is exactly what’s in the vial. That third-party documentation is what you get on every COA.

We partner exclusively with FDA-registered API manufacturers and maintain full batch tracking from synthesis to delivery. Every shipment is prepared and sent from our U.S. facility with complete documentation.
