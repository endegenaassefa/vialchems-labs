    **SEMAX**
    
     Semax peptide is a peptide fragment derived from adrenocorticotropic hormone fragment 4-7 (ACTH 4-7).(1) Published research indicates that Semax may influence specific cellular and tissue physiological systems:
      
        * A study reported that Semax induces BDNF.(2)
        * Regulation of certain neural pathways. (3)(4)
        * Amelioration of a cerebral hypoxia.(5)(6)
        * Modulation of the default mode network (7)(8)
        * Gene expression modulated in the hippocampus.(9)

     C37H51N9O10S

     813.9 g/mol
