    **SELANK**
    
     Selank is a seven amino acid peptide and is derived from the Tuftsin, which is a peptide derived from immunoglobulin G. (1) Researchers have investigated Tuftsin for its potential to modulate of immune cells.(2)
    
     
    
    Since then, various analogs of Tuftsin have been discovered.(3,4) Selank also has a Pro-Gly-Pro (PGP) domain. (5) This PGP domain may enhance Selank's bioavailability in the central nervous system.(6)
    
     
    
    **BDNF and Serotonin Signaling**
    
     Research suggests that Selank may influence the levels of BDNF, which is a protein that supports neuronal physiology.(3) Research indicates that Selank alters the expression of BDNF in the in the hippocampus.
    
     
    
    Selank may also potentially influence signaling pathways. Research utilizing murine models indicated that Selank might be able to regulate serotonin receptor levels.(7)
    
     
    
    **GABA Signaling**
    
     Studies suggest that Selank may act on the gamma-aminobutyric acid (GABA) signaling pathway. In one study, 84 neurotransmission genes were studied. The results indicate that Selank may modulate the GABAergic system.(7)
    
     
    
    **Enkephalin Signaling**
    
     One study examined the effects of Selank and found that it may modulate with tau leu-enkephalin.(9) Enkephalins have the highest activity with the delta-opioid receptor and the mu-opioid receptor and exhibit low affinity for the kappa-opioid receptor. This observation potentially provides on the signaling pathway associated with Selank. (11)
