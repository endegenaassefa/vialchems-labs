#  Shipping & Returns Policy 

Support & Common Questions

### Need Help or Have Questions?

Looking for answers to our most frequently asked questions? [Click here to visit our FAQ page](https://orosresearch.com/pages/faq).

Shipping Information

### Order Processing

At OROS Research, we make every effort to process and ship all orders within 1 business day or less. Orders placed and paid before **3:00 PM Eastern Time (Monday–Friday)** are typically shipped the same day.

Orders placed after 3:00 PM or on weekends/holidays will be processed the following business day.

Please allow for additional time during inclement weather, holidays, or unforeseen events.

### How is it shipped?

All orders are shipped via **UPS** , with your choice of **Ground** , **2-Day** , or **Express** services at checkout. We offer **free UPS 2-Day shipping** on all orders over **$150**. Every shipment also includes **full order insurance** at no additional cost, protecting your package in the event it is lost, stolen, or damaged in transit.

### Cold Shipping & Temperature Stability

We do **not** ship our products with cold packs or via cold-chain carrier services. All OROS Research compounds are provided in **lyophilized (freeze-dried)** form, which is specifically designed to remain stable without refrigeration during standard shipping conditions. 

Short-term exposure to elevated temperatures—such as what may occur during transit or final-mile delivery—does **not** compromise the integrity of the material, even if the vial feels warm upon arrival. 

Degradation risk is associated with **sustained exposure to high heat and humidity over extended periods** , not brief temperature fluctuations during normal shipping windows or short periods after delivery. 

Cold-chain shipping is typically reserved for **reconstituted or compounded products** , not lyophilized research materials intended strictly for **in-vitro use**. 

### Expedited Shipping

Expedited shipping options are available during checkout. Please note that this affects carrier delivery times only and does _not_ expedite internal order processing.

### When Will My Tracking Number Be Available?

Tracking numbers are emailed to you automatically as soon as your shipping label is created. Please allow up to **24 hours** for tracking updates to appear once your order is scanned by the carrier.

### Tracking & Delivery

Carrier delivery timelines are estimates and not guaranteed. OROS Research is not liable for delays caused by UPS or other carriers once a package is in transit.

### Shipping Issues & Insurance Claims

All orders include shipping insurance. If your order is lost, damaged, or stolen, please contact us within **7 days of delivery** , or if your tracking has not updated within **3 business days** , at [support@orosresearch.com](mailto:support@orosresearch.com).

We are unable to assist with shipping issues reported beyond the 7 day window.

Returns & Restrictions

### Returns & Refunds

**All sales are final.** Due to the nature of our products, we do not accept returns or offer refunds. If a pricing error or issue occurs, store credit may be issued at our sole discretion.

### Do You Ship Internationally or to Military Bases?

No. At this time, we only ship within the United States. We do not ship to U.S. territories, international addresses, or military (APO/FPO/DPO) bases.
