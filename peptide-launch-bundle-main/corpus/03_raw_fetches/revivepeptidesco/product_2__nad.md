**NAD+ (Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide)** is an essential coenzyme present in all living cells and fundamental to energy metabolism and cellular repair processes. As NAD+ levels naturally decline with age, it has become a major focus in research surrounding mitochondrial health, metabolic optimization, and longevity science.

This compound is widely utilized in performance, wellness, and anti-aging research protocols due to its broad impact on cellular function, energy systems, and recovery pathways.

NAD+ plays a critical role in mitochondrial function, helping drive ATP production—the primary energy source for every cell. Essential for maintaining peak cellular performance and vitality.

Widely studied for its involvement in DNA repair, sirtuin activation, and cellular resilience. NAD+ is a cornerstone compound in longevity and anti-aging research.

  * **Enhances Metabolic Function & Efficiency**  
Linked to glucose metabolism, fat oxidation, and overall metabolic balance, making it highly relevant in studies focused on body composition and metabolic health.

  * **Cognitive Performance & Recovery Support**  
Researched for its role in brain health, mental clarity, and recovery from oxidative stress and physical exertion—supporting both cognitive and physical performance optimization.
