01What makes your testing different from other peptide suppliers?+

Most suppliers test in-house — which means they grade their own work. We use independent third-party labs with no commercial relationship to our synthesis facilities. HPLC purity, mass spec identity, and LAL endotoxin are all run externally. The signed analytical records are posted to your account before dispatch. You are not being asked to take our word for it.

02Why does endotoxin testing matter for research?+

Endotoxin contamination is one of the most common sources of confounded results in peptide research. Bacterial lipopolysaccharides (LPS) trigger inflammatory responses at nanogram concentrations — masking or mimicking the effects of the compound under study. We report endotoxin on every lot via LAL assay. Most catalog suppliers do not.

03How is analytical purity documented?+

Every lot is assayed by an independent third-party laboratory. HPLC quantifies purity, mass spectrometry confirms identity, and LAL assay records endotoxin level. The signed analytical record is posted to the account before dispatch.

04What are the intended research applications?+

All compounds are supplied strictly for in-vitro research, laboratory, and analytical use. They are not intended or authorized for human or veterinary administration, diagnostic use, or any purpose involving living subjects.

05Are analytical records browsable before purchase?+

Yes. The full COA library is publicly browsable. Every active lot is documented with HPLC trace, mass spec, endotoxin record, and signed analyst note.

06What are the dispatch and handling timelines?+

Orders placed before 14:00 PST dispatch the same business day. Standard transit is 1–3 business days within the contiguous U.S. Temperature-controlled packaging is included by default.

07What is the documentation-mismatch policy?+

If a lot fails to match its analytical record by mass, identity, or purity within stated tolerance, the lot is replaced or the order is refunded in full. Unopened, sealed material may be returned within 30 days.

08Who are these materials supplied to?+

Independent research accounts, institutional buyers, university laboratories, and industry research divisions. By placing an order, the account holder certifies they are 18 or older and acquiring the material for non-clinical research use only.
