Research Overview

## The incretin  
research  
landscape.

The past decade has produced a rapid expansion in peptide-based metabolic research — driven by the structural diversity of incretin hormone analogs and the mechanistic depth available through receptor-selective study designs.

All compounds on this page are supplied for in-vitro and laboratory research use only. They are not drugs, supplements, or therapeutics.

### GLP-1 receptor agonist analogs

Glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) is a 30-amino acid incretin hormone secreted from intestinal L-cells in response to nutrient intake. Research-grade GLP-1 receptor agonist (GLP-1RA) analogs are structurally modified to resist DPP-4 degradation and extend receptor engagement, making them tractable for mechanistic in-vitro studies across pancreatic beta-cell lines, hypothalamic neuronal models, and adipocyte preparations.

Key research applications include glucose-stimulated insulin secretion (GSIS) assays, cAMP pathway activation studies, GLP-1R internalization and desensitization models, and energy expenditure mechanistic studies in differentiated adipocyte lines.

### Dual GLP-1 / GIP co-agonists

Glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide (GIP) and GLP-1 are both incretin hormones — their receptors are co-expressed in pancreatic beta cells, hypothalamic circuits, and adipose tissue. Unimolecular dual agonists permit simultaneous activation of both receptor populations in a single compound, enabling researchers to study complementary incretin signaling without the confounders introduced by combining separate GLP-1RA and GIPR ligands.

A 2023 study in the _New England Journal of Medicine_ (Jastreboff et al.) demonstrated robust effects in human subjects — the preclinical mechanistic foundation for that work was built on in-vitro receptor co-activation studies of this class.

### Triple agonist analogs (GLP-1 / GIP / Glucagon)

Triple receptor co-agonists add glucagon receptor (GCGR) engagement to the GLP-1R / GIPR substrate — providing research access to hepatic glucose output modulation, thermogenesis pathway interactions, and the mechanistic interplay between opposing metabolic receptor programs within the same experimental system.

This class is among the most structurally complex in the metabolic peptide research catalog and requires the highest purity and lowest endotoxin burden to produce interpretable results. Impurities at this complexity level are more likely to produce confounded signals than with simpler analogs.

### Quality markers: what to require from any supplier

✓

Third-party HPLC at ≥98%

In-house purity claims are unverifiable. Require a signed analytical record from an independent lab with the HPLC chromatogram. ≥98% is the floor for cell-based assays.

✓

Mass spectrometry identity confirmation

HPLC confirms purity; mass spec confirms you have the right molecule. Both are required. A supplier that offers only one should be treated with skepticism.

✓

LAL endotoxin assay, lot-specific

Generic 'endotoxin tested' claims without a lot-specific LAL result are not meaningful. Require the EU/mL value and the lot number it applies to.

✓

Sequence verification

For novel or complex analogs, sequence confirmation by MS/MS or Edman degradation guards against synthesis errors that HPLC alone will not catch.

01What are metabolic research peptides?+

Metabolic research peptides are synthetic analogs of endogenous hormones — principally GLP-1, GIP, and glucagon — studied in laboratory and in-vitro settings for their mechanisms in adipose tissue regulation, insulin secretion pathways, and cellular energy metabolism. Supplied strictly for non-clinical research.

02What purity level is required?+

Published metabolic peptide protocols typically call for ≥98% purity by HPLC. Below that threshold, process-related impurities can confound receptor binding assays and introduce variability in cell-based models. Each Celebrity Peptides lot includes HPLC trace and mass spectrometry confirmation in the downloadable COA.

03Why does endotoxin testing matter?+

Bacterial lipopolysaccharide (LPS) activates TLR4 signaling at sub-nanogram concentrations, directly altering insulin sensitivity and lipid metabolism readouts — masking or amplifying the peptide effect. Every metabolic lot is assayed by LAL method before dispatch.

04Monoagonist vs. dual/triple agonist — what's the research difference?+

Monoagonists permit pathway-isolated GLP-1R studies. Dual agonists add GIPR co-activation for complementary incretin research. Triple agonists add GCGR engagement for hepatic glucose output modulation. Each class is not interchangeable in research design.
