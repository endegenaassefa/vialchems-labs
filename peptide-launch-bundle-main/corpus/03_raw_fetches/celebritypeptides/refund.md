# Returns  
 _& Replacements._

Chain-of-custody is the product. Once a vial leaves our climate-controlled fulfillment line, we cannot reintroduce it to inventory. This document explains the narrow set of cases where a return or replacement is possible, and the procedure to claim one.

§ 01

## The principle

Each shipment leaves our fulfillment line under documented temperature, light, and handling control. The Certificate of Analysis attached to your lot reflects the product's state at the moment of release. We have no visibility into storage conditions once a parcel is out of our custody, so reintroducing returned material to general inventory would compromise the chain-of-custody promise we make to every other buyer.

Accordingly, returns are limited to the cases set out below. Where a case applies, we resolve it quickly and at our cost.

§ 02

## Eligible cases

We will replace, credit, or refund product in the following circumstances:

  * Defect of manufacture: visible particulate, broken seal, mass deviation outside the spec on the Certificate of Analysis, or any failure to meet a published specification at the time of receipt.
  * Damage in transit: vial breakage, crushed packaging, evidence that cold-chain was broken (where ordered with cold-chain shipping).
  * Wrong item shipped: lot label, item, dose, or quantity does not match the order confirmation.
  * Non-delivery: a parcel marked delivered by the carrier that did not in fact arrive, after a reasonable carrier investigation.



Outside these cases

Change of mind, after-the-fact research-plan changes, ordering errors by the buyer, and conditions developed after acceptance of the parcel are not eligible. We are happy to apply credit toward a future order in some of these cases — write us.

§ 03

## Claim window

Defect, damage, and wrong-item claims must be filed within fourteen (14) calendar days of delivery. Non-delivery claims must be filed within thirty (30) days of the carrier's last status update. Claims filed after these windows are at our discretion.

§ 04

## Claim procedure

Send an email to [[email protected]](/web/20260507195149/https://www.celebritypeptides.com/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection) with the subject "RTN — [order number]" and include:

  * Order number and the lot number(s) on the affected vial(s).
  * A clear photograph of the affected vial, its outer packaging, and any cold-chain indicator strip if applicable.
  * A short description of the issue, including time of delivery and any carrier notes.
  * Your preferred resolution: replacement, credit, or refund.



Our compliance desk acknowledges receipt within one (1) business day, opens a ticket against the affected lot, and resolves the claim within five (5) business days. If we need to inspect the vial, we will issue a prepaid return label with cold-chain materials where appropriate; do not return product before we issue the label, as unauthorized returns cannot be inspected and are not credited.

§ 05

## Refunds & credits

Approved refunds are issued to the original method of payment within five (5) business days of resolution. Bank settlement times are outside our control and typically run an additional three to five business days. Credits are applied to the buyer's account immediately upon resolution and do not expire.

§ 06

## Non-returnable items

The following are not eligible for return or credit under any circumstance:

  * Product whose seal has been broken or whose lyophilized cake has been reconstituted.
  * Custom-blended or special-order lots produced to a buyer's specification.
  * Reference materials and Certificates of Analysis supplied as documentation only.
  * Product purchased more than ninety (90) days prior to the claim date, regardless of cause.



§ 07

## Carrier disputes

For non-delivery and damage-in-transit claims involving a third-party carrier, we will open and pursue a parallel carrier claim. We do not require you to engage with the carrier; the desk handles that work for you. Settlement of the carrier claim does not delay our resolution to you — replacements ship as soon as the eligibility is confirmed.
