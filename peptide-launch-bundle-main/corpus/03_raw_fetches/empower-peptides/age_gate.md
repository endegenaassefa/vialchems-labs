# AgeChecker.Net Verification

## Age Checker.Net Verification 

To maintain compliance and protect our customers,  
Empower Peptides uses secure age and identity verification for first-time orders.

## Why Verification Matters

At Empower Peptides, protecting our customers and maintaining transactional integrity is a core priority. Verifying age and identity ensures all customers are 21+ and using authorized payment methods.  
  


This process helps us:

  * Stay compliant with age-restriction requirements
  * Prevent unauthorized card usage
  * Reduce fraud attempts and chargebacks
  * Protect our research-only environment



## How the Verification Works

### 1 — Checkout Completed

Customer selects research materials, fills out their details, and clicks 'Place Order'.

### 2 — Verification Triggered

Immediately after placing the order, AgeChecker.net verification automatically begins.

### 3 — Identity Confirmation

Customers quickly confirm their age and identity using our secure verification popup.

### 4 — Checkout Access Granted

Once verified, the order is officially confirmed and immediately sent to our team for processing.

## When Verification Happens

Verification is required **only for the first order**.

The process follows this workflow:

  * Customer adds items to cart
  * Completes checkout details and clicks 'Place Order'
  * AgeChecker verification begins immediately
  * Customer completes verification to confirm the order
  * Future orders skip verification completely
