# Shipping Policy

## Shipping Policy – Empower Peptides

**Effective Date: 05/01/2025**

Empower Peptides is committed to fast, reliable, and compliant shipping for all research supply orders. Please read the following carefully before placing your order.

* * *

## Order Processing

  * Orders are processed within **1 – 2 business day**, Monday through Friday. With regards to Friday, to ensure the integrity of your vials and avoid them sitting in a UPS warehouse over the weekend, we’ll be shipping them out on Monday, unless we receive written consent via email at info@empower-peptides.com that you would like your order shipped Friday.

  * Orders are processed within **1–2 business days**. For orders received before 2:30pm, We try our best to ship the same day.

  * Orders are not processed or shipped on weekends or federal holidays.




* * *

## Shipping Carriers

We use **USPS and UPS** , depending on your location and selected shipping method. Empower Peptides reserves the right to change carriers without notice if doing so will result in faster or more secure delivery.

* * *

## ❄️ Heat-Sensitive Product Notice (if applicable)

Some products may be temperature sensitive. While Empower Peptides may include **cold packs or insulation** when appropriate, we **do not guarantee** the temperature upon arrival. Please store all peptides immediately in a **cool, dry, and stable environment** upon delivery.

* * *

## ⚠️ Delivery Liability & Risk Transfer

Once a package is marked “**delivered** ” by the carrier, **ownership and risk transfer to the customer**. Empower Peptides is not liable for:

  * Delays caused by carriers (e.g. weather, holidays, high volume)

  * Packages lost, stolen, or misdelivered after carrier confirmation

  * Incorrect addresses entered during checkout

  * Packages unclaimed from post office or pickup centers




**Please double-check your shipping address** at checkout to avoid delays or loss.

**Signature Requirement for High-Value Orders**

For security and fraud-prevention purposes, Empower Peptides reserves the right to require **signature confirmation upon delivery for orders exceeding Three Hundred Dollars ($300 USD).**

Customers placing orders above this threshold acknowledge that the shipment may require **an adult signature at the delivery address** before the package can be released by the carrier.

In certain circumstances, Empower Peptides may also request **identity verification or government-issued identification** prior to shipment or upon delivery in order to prevent fraud, chargebacks, or unauthorized transactions.

Failure to cooperate with requested verification procedures may result in **order cancellation or delayed shipment at the sole discretion of Empower Peptides.**

* * *

## Return-to-Sender & Re-Shipment Policy

If your package is returned due to:

  * Incomplete or incorrect address

  * Failure to sign or pick up from the carrier




…you may request a reshipment at your expense. We do **not** offer refunds for returned packages due to address errors or missed pickups.

* * *

## Shipping Restrictions

  * We **do not** ship internationally

  * We **do not** ship to P.O. Boxes or freight forwarders

  * We **do not** offer local pickup or drop-off services




* * *

## Force Majeure

Empower Peptides shall not be held liable for shipping delays or non-performance resulting from circumstances outside our control, including but not limited to: natural disasters, carrier disruptions, pandemics, supply chain issues, or government restrictions.

* * *
