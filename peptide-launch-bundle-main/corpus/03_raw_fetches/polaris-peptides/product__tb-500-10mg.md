> Research Note: Nearly all forms of TB-500 found available for research are actually the full molecule of Thymosin Beta 4 (TB4), including the peptide we offer for research. We are actively seeking new sources for the actual TB-500 peptide for research purposes. This is generally noted on lab analysis results as “TB-500 (TB4)”.

TB4 is a naturally occurring peptide consisting of 43 amino acids, found in virtually all human and animal cells. It plays a pivotal role in many biological processes, including actin sequestration, tissue repair, inflammation reduction, and cell migration. TB-500, on the other hand, refers to a synthetic fragment of TB4, typically consisting of the active region of TB4 that involves healing and tissue repair. This fragment, often encompassing the sequence of amino acids that exhibit the highest degree of actin-binding and regenerative properties, is designed to mimic the effects of the naturally occurring peptide. TB-500 is favored in research and therapeutic applications due to its smaller size, which potentially enhances its stability and ability to circulate through the body more efficiently than the larger TB4 molecule.

In addition to its regenerative capabilities, TB-500 has been examined for its anti-inflammatory properties. Studies seek to elucidate how this peptide may reduce inflammation, thereby facilitating a more conducive environment for healing and tissue repair. The scope of research encompasses TB-500’s potential effects on improving flexibility and reducing fibrosis, thus contributing to its reputation as a peptide of interest for supporting tissue recovery and health. Through these scientific inquiries, TB-500 continues to be a subject of study for its biological actions and potential benefits, focusing on its ability to modulate various physiological processes without implying specific human applications.
