#  Epithalon 10 mg 

$26.95

Epithalon (also known as Epitalon or Epithalamin) is a synthetic tetrapeptide with the amino acid sequence Ala-Glu-Asp-Gly. It acts as a mimetic of epithalamin, a peptide naturally secreted by the pineal gland. In laboratory research settings, Epithalon is a primary subject of investigation regarding the mechanisms of aging, particularly its role in the regulation of telomerase activity. Telomerase is the enzyme responsible for maintaining the length of telomeres, the protective caps at the ends of chromosomes that shorten with each cell division.

Scientific literature extensively explores Epithalon’s potential to induce telomere elongation, thereby influencing cellular lifespan and proliferation capacity. Beyond telomere biology, researchers utilize this peptide to study the normalization of circadian rhythms, the regulation of melatonin secretion, and the modulation of antioxidant defense systems. It is frequently employed in experimental models examining the physiological processes of senescence, neuroprotection, and endocrine function in non-human biological systems.
