#  GHK-Cu 

$28.95 – $34.95Price range: $28.95 through $34.95

GHK-Cu is a naturally occurring copper complex of the tripeptide glycyl-L-histidyl-L-lysine. The peptide has a strong affinity for copper(II) and was first isolated from human plasma. In laboratory research settings, GHK-Cu is widely investigated for its ability to modulate collagen synthesis, enzyme activity, and cellular proliferation.

Current scientific literature explores GHK-Cu’s potential role in signaling pathways related to wound healing, antioxidant enzyme activation, and tissue remodeling. It is a subject of intense interest in studies examining skin regeneration, anti-inflammatory responses, and genomic regulation.
