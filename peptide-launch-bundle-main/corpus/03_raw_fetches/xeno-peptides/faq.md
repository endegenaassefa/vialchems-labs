# Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our research peptides

##  __General Questions

__What are research peptides?

Research peptides are short chains of amino acids used exclusively for scientific research and laboratory experimentation. They are NOT intended for human consumption or clinical use.

__Are your peptides pharmaceutical-grade?

Yes. All Xeno Peptides products are manufactured in certified facilities and undergo rigorous third-party testing to ensure pharmaceutical-grade quality and 98%+ purity.

__Do you provide Certificates of Analysis (COA)?

Absolutely. Every order includes a Certificate of Analysis from an independent third-party laboratory, verifying purity, composition, and batch information.

##  __Ordering & Shipping

__How fast do you ship?

Orders placed before 2 PM EST ship the same day via USPS Priority Mail. Most orders arrive within 2-3 business days.

__Do you ship internationally?

Currently, we only ship within the United States.

__How is my order packaged?

All orders are shipped in discreet, unmarked packaging to protect your privacy. Peptides are packaged with appropriate insulation to maintain stability during transit.

__Can I track my order?

Yes! You'll receive a tracking number via email as soon as your order ships.

__What if my peptides arrive warm?

Peptides can tolerate brief temperature fluctuations during shipping. Upon arrival, immediately store them at the recommended temperature (typically 2-8°C for unopened vials, -20°C after reconstitution).

##  __Product Information

__What is the purity of your peptides?

All Xeno Peptides products are 99%+ pure, verified by third-party laboratory testing.

__How should I store my peptides?

**Unopened (lyophilized powder):** Store at 2-8°C (refrigerator) or -20°C (freezer) for long-term storage  
**After reconstitution:** Store at 2-8°C (refrigerator) and use within 30 days, or freeze at -20°C for longer storage  
  
Always protect from light and avoid repeated freeze-thaw cycles.

__What is the shelf life?

Properly stored lyophilized peptides typically remain stable for 2-3 years. Reconstituted peptides should be used within 30 days when refrigerated.

__How do I handle and store peptides for research?

Lyophilized peptides should be stored according to standard laboratory practices for research chemicals. For reconstitution and handling protocols specific to your research application, please consult peer-reviewed scientific literature and follow your institution's standard operating procedures. We cannot provide protocol guidance as our products are strictly for research purposes.

__Do your peptides come with instructions?

Each product includes general storage and handling guidelines. For specific research protocols, consult published scientific literature and follow laboratory best practices.

##  __Legal & Compliance

Yes. Research peptides are legal for purchase and use in laboratory research settings by qualified professionals. They are NOT approved for human consumption.

Our products are intended for purchase by:

By purchasing, you certify that you are 21+ years of age and that products will be used solely for research purposes.

__Can I use these peptides for personal use?

**No.** These products are strictly for in vitro research and laboratory experimentation only. They are NOT dietary supplements, drugs, or intended for human or animal consumption.

__Are your products FDA-approved?

No. Research peptides are exempt from FDA regulation under 21 CFR Parts 100-740 as they are not intended for human use. They are classified as research chemicals for laboratory use only.
