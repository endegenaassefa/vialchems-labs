## Product Description

**REVIVA | CORE RESEARCH COMPOUND**

**G2-T** is a high-purity lyophilized research compound described in pre-clinical literature as a **dual agonist** at the GIP and GLP-1 receptors. This receptor profile makes it a useful tool in laboratory environments studying nutrient signaling, metabolic pathway interactions, and receptor-level dynamics.

In controlled non-clinical models, G2-T has been referenced in research exploring **energy balance, substrate utilization, and metabolic regulation**. Studies often examine how simultaneous activation of GIP and GLP-1 receptors influences downstream signaling cascades, including pathways related to glucose handling, fat-oxidation mechanisms, and mitochondrial activity. Its dual-pathway characteristics also make it valuable for comparative assessments versus single-agonist compounds.

The compound’s lyophilized format supports stable storage and precise reconstitution, making it suitable for **in-vitro investigations, receptor-binding analysis, and mechanistic pathway studies**. All available findings originate from controlled laboratory settings, and results should be interpreted within the scope of non-clinical research.
