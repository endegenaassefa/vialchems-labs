# Shipping & Returns

Last Updated: December 12, 2025

### USA Only

We currently ship within the United States only.

### Tracked Shipping

All orders shipped via USPS or UPS with tracking.

This Shipping & Returns Policy explains how RevivaCore processes and fulfills orders, including conditions for returns and replacements.

#### 1\. Shipping Information

### Policy:

No card transactions are allowed for international shipping.

We respect federal state and city laws, our shipping restrictions are detailed below on the footer and

they are automatically updated by WAAVE compliance.

### Regions:

We currently ship within the United States only.

Carriers:

  * All orders are shipped using tracked USPS or UPS services.
  * Saturday delivery is included where available.



### Estimated Delivery:

  * 1–3 business days (not guaranteed)
  * Weather, carrier delays, or operational disruptions may impact delivery times.



### Shipping Confirmation:

You will receive tracking information once your order has shipped.

Risk Transfer:

Ownership and risk transfer to the buyer once the package is handed to the carrier.

#### 2\. Order Processing

  * Orders are processed once payment has fully cleared.
  * Orders may be held for security verification.
  * Orders placed on weekends or holidays may ship the next business day.



#### 3\. Returns & Refunds

  * Due to the sensitive nature of our research products, **all sales are final**.

We do not accept returns or exchanges.

Refunds will only be issued under the following conditions:

    * You received the wrong item
    * The item arrived damaged

To request a refund or replacement:

    * Contact support@revivacore.com within 48 hours of delivery
    * Include clear photos of the product and packaging
