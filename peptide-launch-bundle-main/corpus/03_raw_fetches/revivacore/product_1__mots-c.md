## Product Description

**REVIVA | CORE RESEARCH COMPOUND**

**MOTS-c** is a high-purity mitochondrial-derived peptide (MDP) widely referenced in pre-clinical research for its involvement in **cellular energy regulation, metabolic-signaling networks, and adaptive stress-response pathways**. Because of its mitochondrial origin, MOTS-c is frequently used in studies examining how peptide messengers influence intracellular communication and nutrient-sensing mechanisms.

In controlled non-clinical models, MOTS-c has been utilized to study:

  * **AMPK-related signaling cascades**

  * **cellular energy-balance pathways**

  * **nutrient-responsive metabolic adjustments**

  * **stress-adaptation and mitochondrial regulatory signals**




Researchers often incorporate MOTS-c into mechanistic in-vitro experiments to better understand how mitochondrial peptides interact with broad regulatory networks governing cellular metabolism and homeostatic signaling.

Its lyophilized format allows for precise reconstitution and consistent performance across **biochemical pathway mapping, receptor-interaction studies, and metabolic-signaling assays**. All findings originate strictly from laboratory research, not from clinical or human studies.
