## Product Description

**REVIVA | CORE RESEARCH COMPOUND**

**Epitalon (Epithalamin / Epithalon)** is a high-purity synthetic tetrapeptide widely referenced in pre-clinical research for its involvement in **telomeric regulatory pathways, circadian-rhythm signaling, and cellular-aging mechanisms**. Originally derived from studies on pineal-gland biochemistry, Epitalon continues to be used in laboratory environments investigating how short peptides interact with **genomic-adjacent signaling processes** and cellular-resilience pathways.

In controlled non-clinical models, Epitalon has been utilized to study:

  * **Telomeric structure–associated signaling**

  * **Circadian-rhythm regulatory mechanisms**

  * **Pineal-peptide biochemical pathways**

  * **Oxidative-stress response and adaptive signaling cascades**

  * **Cellular-aging–related pathway modulation**




Researchers often incorporate Epitalon into mechanistic in-vitro experiments aimed at understanding how small peptides influence **genomic maintenance systems** , **peptide–protein interactions** , and **downstream regulatory responses**.

The peptide’s lyophilized format provides stability and precision for **receptor-binding studies, biochemical pathway mapping, and cellular-signaling assays**. All findings originate strictly from controlled laboratory research and do not represent clinical or human outcomes.
