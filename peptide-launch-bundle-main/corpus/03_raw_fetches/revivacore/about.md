# What We Do

RevivaCore sources, documents, and distributes research-grade compounds and peptide reference materials in accordance with research-only standards. Access to our catalogue is restricted to verified adult members, and all product information emphasizes laboratory context and analytical use.

We prioritize:

  * consistent product labeling and documentation

  * verification of access and compliance

  * controlled distribution processes

  * clear research-only positioning

#### Access & Compliance

To maintain appropriate access controls and compliance standards, access to RevivaCore’s product catalogue is limited to registered members. This member access model supports traceability and accountability in research environments.

RevivaCore does not provide dosing information, protocols, usage instructions, or claims regarding outcomes.
