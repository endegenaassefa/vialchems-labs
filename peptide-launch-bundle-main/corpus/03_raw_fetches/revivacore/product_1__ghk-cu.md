## Product Description

**REVIVA | CORE RESEARCH COMPOUND**

**GHK-Cu (Copper Peptide)** is a naturally occurring tripeptide (Gly-His-Lys) that binds copper ions and has been extensively studied in pre-clinical and molecular biology settings. Research has explored its effects on **wound healing, cellular regeneration, collagen production, and anti-inflammatory signaling** , making it one of the most widely referenced regenerative peptides in non-clinical literature.

### **Research Focus Areas**

Across published scientific work, GHK-Cu has been investigated for its potential roles in:

**• Tissue Repair & Regeneration**  
Modulating pathways related to fibroblast activity, collagen synthesis, and extracellular matrix rebuilding.

**• Anti-Inflammatory Action**  
Reducing markers of oxidative stress and inflammation in cellular and animal models.

**• Skin Renewal & Cosmetic Biology**  
Examined for impacts on dermal thickness, elasticity, angiogenesis, and antioxidant response.

**• Copper Transport & Gene Modulation**  
GHK-Cu has been noted for influencing gene expression linked to repair, immunity, and cellular stress responses.
