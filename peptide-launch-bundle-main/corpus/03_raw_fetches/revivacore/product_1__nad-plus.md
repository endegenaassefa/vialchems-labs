## Product Description

**REVIVA | CORE RESEARCH COMPOUND**

**NAD+ (Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide, oxidized form)** is a high-purity biochemical compound widely referenced in pre-clinical literature for its role in **cellular energy transfer, metabolic signaling, and redox-cycle dynamics**. As a central coenzyme in electron-transport reactions, NAD+ is frequently used in laboratory environments to study mitochondrial function, oxidative pathways, and cofactor-dependent enzymatic systems.

In controlled non-clinical models, NAD+ is used to examine processes involving **NAD⁺/NADH turnover, ATP-related metabolic pathways, and oxidative stress responses**. Its involvement in redox cycling makes it a valuable tool for researchers investigating biochemical interactions, cellular metabolism, and coenzyme-supported enzymatic reactions. Comparative studies may evaluate how changes in NAD+ availability influence downstream signaling networks or mitochondrial activity.

The compound’s lyophilized format supports accurate preparation and consistent performance across **in-vitro assays, biochemical workflows, and mechanistic metabolic studies**. All information available on NAD+ originates from controlled laboratory research, not from human or clinical use.
