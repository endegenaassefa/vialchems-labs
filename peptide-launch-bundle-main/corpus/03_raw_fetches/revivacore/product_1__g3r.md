## Product Description

**REVIVA | CORE RESEARCH COMPOUND**

**G3-R** is a high-purity, lyophilized research compound categorized in pre-clinical literature as a **triple-agonist** at the **GIP, GLP-1, and glucagon receptors**. Its unique multi-pathway activity has made it a compound of interest in laboratory settings focused on metabolic signaling, nutrient processing pathways, and receptor-level interactions.

In controlled non-clinical studies, the compound has been referenced in models exploring **energy balance, substrate utilization, and metabolic regulation**. These models often examine how simultaneous activation of GIP, GLP-1, and glucagon receptors may influence downstream signaling cascades, including pathways associated with **fat-oxidation mechanisms, glucose handling, and mitochondrial activity**. Because of its multi-receptor profile, G3-R is frequently used for comparative research alongside single-agonist or dual-agonist compounds to evaluate differential receptor engagement and pathway specificity.

Its lyophilized form allows for stable storage and precise reconstitution, making it well suited for **in-vitro investigations, receptor-binding assays, and metabolic pathway analysis**. Researchers may utilize G3-R in studies designed to characterize receptor affinity, evaluate sequence-specific biological interactions, or compare agonist profiles within broader metabolic frameworks.

G3-R is not an approved therapeutic agent, and available information originates from **pre-clinical environments, controlled laboratory assessments, and non-human models**. Long-term or translational outcomes remain under active investigation, and published findings should be interpreted within the scope of laboratory research parameters.
