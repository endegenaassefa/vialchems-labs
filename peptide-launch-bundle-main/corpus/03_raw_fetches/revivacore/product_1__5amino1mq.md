## Product Description

**REVIVA | CORE RESEARCH COMPOUND**

**5-Amino-1MQ (“5-Amino”)** is a small-molecule research compound widely referenced in pre-clinical literature for its activity as an **inhibitor of nicotinamide N-methyltransferase (NNMT)**. Because NNMT plays a regulatory role in cellular methylation cycles and NAD+-related metabolism, 5-Amino is frequently used in laboratory models exploring **energy-balance pathways, metabolic signaling networks, and enzyme-modulated biochemical cascades**.

In controlled non-clinical environments, 5-Amino has been studied in vitro to evaluate how NNMT inhibition may influence **cellular energy turnover, methyl donor availability, and downstream metabolic responses**. Researchers often employ 5-Amino in comparative assays to understand how small-molecule NNMT modulators affect pathway-specific signaling processes.

The compound’s stable format supports precise preparation and consistent performance across **biochemical assays, enzyme-interaction studies, and mechanistic pathway investigations**. All available findings originate from controlled research settings, not from clinical or human contexts.
