# **BPC-157 10MG**

**SKU:** BPC-157-10MG**| Category:** Regenerative

_Pentadecapeptide | Body Protection Compound | Lyophilized | ≥99% purity_

BPC-157 (Body Protection Compound 157) is a synthetic pentadecapeptide (GEPPPGKPADDAGLV) derived from a partial sequence of human gastric juice protein. It is resistant to enzymatic degradation in vitro due to its sequence architecture, making it a stable model compound for proteolytic stability studies.

Research applications include investigation of angiogenic signaling pathways (particularly VEGFR2 and nitric oxide synthase activation), fibroblast migration and proliferation assays, tendon and ligament cell culture models, and gastrointestinal epithelial repair studies. BPC-157 has been widely used in rodent model research examining connective tissue, musculoskeletal, and gut mucosa biology.

Each vial contains 10mg of lyophilized BPC-157, manufactured in ISO 9001 certified, GMP-compliant facilities. Purity independently confirmed at ≥99% by HPLC via ISO/IEC 17025 accredited laboratory. COA available on the testing page.

**Storage:** Store lyophilized at -20°C. Reconstituted: 4°C, use within 28 days.
