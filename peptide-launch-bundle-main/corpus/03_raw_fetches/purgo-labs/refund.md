We offer a 60-day return window for unopened items. Customers are responsible for return shipping costs.

Money-Back Guarantee

## How returns work

  * • Email support@purgolabs.com with your order number.
  * • Tell us the reason and include photos if items are damaged.
  * • Our team confirms eligibility and next steps.
  * • Returned items must be unopened to qualify.
  * • Customers pay return shipping costs.
  * • Once approved, your refund is processed back to the original payment method.



## Coverage details

  * • Wrong item, damaged package, or shipping issue: fully covered.
  * • Dissatisfaction within 60 days: eligible if returned items are unopened.
  * • Requests submitted after 60 days may not qualify.
  * • Keep order packaging and documentation for faster processing.
