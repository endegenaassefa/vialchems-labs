# Shipping Information

Fast UPS shipping with live checkout rates, transparent pricing, and end-to-end tracking.

Carrier update: As of April 5, we changed from FedEx to UPS for all new shipments.

Same-Day Fulfillment

Weekday orders before 2PM EST are fulfilled same business day.

Live Tracking

Shipment Protection

Coverage is available for loss, damage, or theft in transit.

## Shipping methods

  * • UPS Ground: $4 flat rate
  * • UPS 3 Day Select: live rate by address
  * • UPS 2nd Day Air: live rate by address
  * • UPS Next Day Air: live rate by address
  * • Free UPS 3 Day Select on orders over $250 after discount code deduction.



## Estimated delivery windows

  * • UPS Ground: typically 5 business days
  * • UPS 3 Day Select: typically 3 business days
  * • UPS 2nd Day Air: typically 2 business days
  * • UPS Next Day Air: typically next business day
  * • Delivery windows can shift during carrier delays or peak periods.
