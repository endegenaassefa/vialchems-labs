Cryptocurrency offers a decentralised, quick, and safe payment option that is gaining international acceptance. Bitcoin is the go-to option for most online consumers due to its relatively lower fees.

Merchants are responsible for paying transaction fees, as well as setup fees for many payment processors. PayPal, for instance, charges close to 4% per transaction (and sometimes more). Cryptocurrencies charge much lower fees, if any. Some bitcoin exchanges offer fees under 1%.

Pay directly using your credit or debit card (Visa, Mastercard, JCB, Discover). Simply enter your card details at checkout, confirm the transaction, and your payment will be processed instantly.
