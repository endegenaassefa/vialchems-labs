# X Ads Policy - Healthcare excerpt (verbatim and triangulated)

## Verbatim primary rule (per acceleratedigitalmedia.com 2026 guide quoting X policy)

"Unless listed below, the promotion of health and pharmaceutical products and services is prohibited"

## Permitted exceptions (per same source)

X permits advertising for:
- Telemedicine services (with state compliance and prior authorization from X)
- Brick-and-mortar pharmacies (excluding online pharmaceutical sales promotion)
- FDA-approved medical devices
- NABP-accredited online pharmacies

## Country-specific restrictions

The reproduction notes X "has a large healthcare policy section that covers restrictions unique to different countries around the world" - country-by-country detail not extractable.

## Coverage of peptides / supplements / research chemicals / unapproved drugs

Source-text reproduction does NOT contain the words "peptides," "supplements," "research chemicals," or "unapproved drugs" by name. Inference: research peptides do not fit any of the four permitted exception buckets and therefore fall under the prohibited default.
