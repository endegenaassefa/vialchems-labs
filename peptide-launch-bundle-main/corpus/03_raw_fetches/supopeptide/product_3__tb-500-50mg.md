## How it Works

TB-500 works by increasing angiogenesis (blood vessel formation) around damaged tissue. See Philp D, Kleinman HK (April 2010). [Animal studies with thymosin beta, a multifunctional tissue repair and regeneration peptide](https://web.archive.org/web/20240724085532/https://zenodo.org/record/1230768#.XZZJPfxKgUs).

It also upregulates actin, a vital cellular protein critical to muscle contraction, cell mobility, and signaling between cells.See M. Santra (May 2014). [Thymosin β4 Upregulation of MicroRNA-146a Promotes Oligodendrocyte Differentiation and Suppression of the Toll-like Proinflammatory Pathway.](https://web.archive.org/web/20240724085532/https://http//www.jbc.org/content/early/2014/05/14/jbc.M113.529966.full.pdf)

TB-500 further enhances stem cell differentiation, cell mobility, and increases cell survival.
