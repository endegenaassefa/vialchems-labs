If you receive an item that is defective, damaged prior to shipment, or incorrect, you must notify us within forty-eight (48) hours of delivery.

To be eligible for review, you must provide:

  * Your order number

Reports submitted after the 48-hour window will not be eligible for review.

Any refund, replacement, or store credit is issued solely at the discretion of Instant Peptides and may be reduced by any non-refundable payment processing fees.

## 3\. Shipping Issues, Lost, Stolen, or Damaged Packages

### Delivery Guarantee

At checkout, customers may optionally add our Delivery Guarantee, a merchant-managed protection service (not insurance) for packages that are lost, stolen, or damaged during transit.

  * If the Delivery Guarantee was purchased, claims can be submitted at [claims.instantpeptides.com](https://claims.instantpeptides.com)

  * All claims are subject to fraud risk assessment, and Instant Peptides reserves the right to approve or deny any claim based on fraud risk scores
  * For packages marked as delivered but not received, the claimant must provide supporting evidence (e.g., statements from household members, security camera footage, or a police report)
  * Instant Peptides reserves the right to fulfill any approved claim by issuing the equivalent value in Instant Rewards points in lieu of a physical replacement or monetary reimbursement



Our support team reviews all Delivery Guarantee claims directly. Claims that cannot be verified or that do not pass our review will be denied.

### Orders Without Delivery Guarantee

If the Delivery Guarantee is declined at checkout:

  * Title and risk of loss pass to you once the package is handed to the carrier
  * Instant Peptides cannot issue refunds, replacements, or credits for carrier-related issues, including loss, theft, misdelivery, or transit damage



By declining the Delivery Guarantee, you acknowledge and agree that you assume all risk for your shipment.

You are responsible for inspecting your shipment immediately upon delivery.

  * Any visible damage or shortages must be reported within forty-eight (48) hours

If the Delivery Guarantee was purchased, claims can be submitted at [claims.instantpeptides.com](https://claims.instantpeptides.com).

## 6\. Payment Processing Fees

All approved refunds, credits, or adjustments are issued net of any non-refundable payment processing fees, including but not limited to merchant processing, network, or gateway fees.

Chargebacks do not replace or supersede the claims and review process outlined in this Policy.
