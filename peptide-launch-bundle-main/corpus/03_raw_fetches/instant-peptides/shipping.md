# Shipping & Delivery Policy

**Effective Date:** September 26, 2025

## Order Processing

Orders are processed within 1 business day after payment is received. Orders placed on weekends or holidays will ship the next business day. Once your order ships, you will receive an email with tracking information.

## Shipping Methods and Delivery Times

We currently ship to addresses within the United States using FedEx 2-day shipping.

Estimated delivery times: 2 to 3 business days. Some rural areas may experience slight delays.

Shipping carriers may experience delays during peak seasons or due to weather and other external factors. Delivery estimates are not guaranteed.

## Free Shipping

We offer free standard shipping on all single orders over $200 (before tax and after discounts). Orders must meet the $200 minimum in one transaction. Orders cannot be combined or modified after checkout.

## Delivery Guarantee

At checkout, customers may choose to add our Delivery Guarantee, a merchant-managed service that provides protection for packages that are lost in transit, stolen, damaged on arrival, or contain missing items. **The Delivery Guarantee is not insurance** and does not constitute a contract of insurance in any form.

**If the Delivery Guarantee is purchased:** Instant Peptides will review claims for replacement or reimbursement for qualifying shipping issues. You may [submit a claim here](https://claims.instantpeptides.com).

All Delivery Guarantee claims are evaluated and approved or denied at the sole discretion of Instant Peptides. As part of our review process, we conduct a fraud risk assessment on all claims and reserve the right to approve or deny any claim based on fraud risk scores. We may also request additional information or documentation before processing any claim. Claims that cannot be verified or that do not pass our review will be denied.

For claims where a package is marked as delivered but was not received, the claimant must provide supporting evidence (e.g., security camera footage or a police report). Claims without adequate proof may be denied.

Instant Peptides also reserves the right to fulfill any approved Delivery Guarantee claim by issuing the equivalent value of the order in Instant Rewards points in lieu of a physical replacement or monetary reimbursement.

**If the Delivery Guarantee is not purchased:** the customer assumes full responsibility for any lost, stolen, or damaged packages. Instant Peptides is not liable under any circumstances for shipping issues once the order has been handed to the carrier, including loss in transit, misdelivery, theft after delivery, carrier error, or damaged items.

Declining the Delivery Guarantee means you agree to assume all risk for your shipment and understand that Instant Peptides cannot replace, refund, or credit orders affected by carrier-related issues.

## Order Changes and Cancellations

Orders are final once placed. We do not allow modifications, additions, cancellations, or combining multiple orders. Please double-check your cart and shipping address before completing your purchase.

## Product Handling

All products are packaged securely to ensure integrity during transit. Items are handled according to proper storage practices for research materials.

## Lost or Delayed Packages

If your package is marked as delivered but you did not receive it, or if the carrier reports the shipment as lost or delayed, please contact the carrier directly for assistance. For packages marked as delivered but not received, you should gather any available supporting evidence (e.g., security camera footage or a filed police report).

Instant Peptides is not responsible for lost, stolen, or misdelivered packages unless the Delivery Guarantee was purchased. Without the Delivery Guarantee, no replacements, refunds, or credits will be issued for any carrier-related delivery issue.

If you purchased the Delivery Guarantee, please [file a claim here](https://claims.instantpeptides.com) for the fastest resolution.

## Damaged Shipments

If your order arrives damaged, contact us within 48 hours of delivery at [support@instantpeptides.com](mailto:support@instantpeptides.com) with your order number and clear photos of the packaging and product.

**Without the Delivery Guarantee:** Instant Peptides is not able to replace or refund orders damaged during transit. Only the Delivery Guarantee provides coverage for transit-related damage.

**With the Delivery Guarantee:** please [file a claim here](https://claims.instantpeptides.com) to receive a replacement or reimbursement.

## Address Accuracy

Customers are responsible for entering accurate shipping information. We are not liable for delivery issues resulting from incorrect or incomplete addresses.
