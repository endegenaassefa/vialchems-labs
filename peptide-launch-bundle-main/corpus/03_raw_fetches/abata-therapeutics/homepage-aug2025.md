#### The Abata Approach

## A New Era: Treg Cell Therapy

Our targeted, autologous Treg cell therapies for progressive multiple sclerosis, type 1 diabetes, and other serious autoimmune diseases are designed to stop immune-mediated destruction, restore homeostasis – a state of harmony – and promote repair in affected tissues.
