## The Short Version

We went looking for a peptide vendor we could trust. What we found instead was fake COAs, recycled batch results from months ago, manufacturer-only certificates, vague batch documentation, and websites that felt like they were built for one very specific audience that didn’t include us.

So we built the vendor we wanted to buy from.

Panda Peptides is a US-based company out of California. We source research-grade peptides, send batches to an independent third-party analytical laboratory for verification, and publish the batch reports we post on the relevant product pages and in our [Lab Reports directory](/coa/). Not a summary. Not a badge. The actual Certificate of Analysis, tied to the batch it covers.

We keep the documentation visible: product pages connect back to the relevant lab reports, and the COA directory keeps batch reports in one place so the testing trail is easier to inspect before purchase.

## Meet the Founder

Panda Peptides was founded by Cory — a California-based mechanical engineer who got tired of the status quo in the peptide supply market. After ordering from multiple vendors and consistently running into the same problems — unverifiable COAs, inconsistent quality, and zero transparency about sourcing or testing — he decided to build the company he wished existed.

The premise was simple: find reliable synthesis partners, send every batch to an independent lab that the research community already trusts, and publish the results before listing anything for sale. No in-house testing. No manufacturer certificates passed off as independent verification. Real third-party analysis from Janoshik Analytical — an independent laboratory with no financial relationship to us.

Today, Cory handles supplier evaluation, quality standards, and the testing pipeline. Every batch goes through the same process: source from vetted manufacturers, ship samples to Janoshik, review the COA, and only list the product if it meets our strict quality control standards. Batches that fall short get rejected — they don’t ship, and they don’t get listed.

Panda Peptides is a research materials supplier, not a pharmaceutical company. We sell compounds labeled exclusively for research use — we don’t make health claims, we don’t provide usage guidance, and we don’t cut corners on verification. The work is straightforward: source it right, test everything, prove it publicly.

## How We Think About Quality

We vetted dozens of suppliers before we placed our first order. Initial testing, identity confirmation, purity analysis. The ones that passed became partners. The ones that didn’t — and most didn’t — were dropped.

We don’t test once and call it done. Every batch goes through independent verification. If the quality isn’t where it needs to be, that batch doesn’t ship. The bar doesn’t move.

### 01 — Source

Established synthesis facilities, vetted through initial testing and ongoing quality audits. If a supplier can’t maintain our purity standards consistently, we find a new one.

### 02 — Test

Published batch reports are sent through [Janoshik Analytical](https://janoshik.com) — an independent third-party laboratory. They verify purity, identity, and composition. Posted reports are tied to the product page and our [Lab Reports directory](/coa/).

### 03 — Document & Ship

Orders are processed in 1–2 business days, and published batch reports stay connected to the product page and Lab Reports directory so verification is easy to find.

## Why This Matters Now

Interest in peptide research is growing. Computational modeling and AI-driven screening tools are making it faster to characterize compound structures and predict binding profiles. The pace of discovery is accelerating, and so is the demand for reliable reference materials.

That makes access to high-purity reference compounds more important, not less. Researchers need materials with verified identity, confirmed purity, and documented handling — because unreliable inputs produce unreliable data.

We’re a research materials supplier, not a pharmaceutical company. We sell compounds labeled for research use only — we don’t make efficacy claims and we don’t provide usage guidance. What we do is ensure that every product leaving our facility meets the purity and identity standards that serious research demands.
