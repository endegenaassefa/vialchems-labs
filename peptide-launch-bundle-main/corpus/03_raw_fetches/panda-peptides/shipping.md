# Shipping Policy

**Language notice**

Spanish-language content on this website is provided for convenience only. In the event of any conflict or inconsistency, the English-language version controls and governs.

**Spanish summary for convenience**

If you are viewing Spanish-language content for this policy elsewhere on the site, that content is provided for convenience only. The full English Shipping Policy on this page controls.

**Last updated: March 2026**

### Processing Time

Orders are typically processed within 1-2 business days. Orders placed on weekends or holidays will be processed the next business day.

### Shipping Methods

We currently ship domestic (US) orders via USPS Priority Mail.

  * **USPS Priority Mail:** $10 flat rate
  * **Free USPS Priority Mail:** orders $50+



### Estimated Delivery Window

After dispatch, USPS Priority Mail typically arrives in 1-3 business days. Most orders arrive within 2-5 business days total, including processing time.

### Carriers

Orders normally ship via USPS Priority Mail. We may substitute an equivalent service only when necessary to complete delivery.

### Domestic Shipping Only

We currently only ship within the United States. International orders are not available at this time.

### Tracking

Once your order ships, you will receive a tracking number via email. You can track your package through our website or directly on the carrier’s website.

### Delivery Issues

If your package is lost, damaged, or significantly delayed, please contact us at [support@pandapeptides.com](mailto:support@pandapeptides.com) within 30 days of the expected delivery date (or delivery date if damaged).

  * **Lost packages:** We will file a carrier claim and, if verified as lost, reship your order at no cost. See our Return Policy for full details on lost shipment coverage.
  * **Damaged packages:** Please photograph the damage and contact us. See our Return Policy for documentation requirements and reshipment eligibility.



### High-Risk Addresses

Shipments to PO boxes, freight forwarders, package forwarding services, or APO/FPO addresses ship at the buyer’s risk. We may not cover losses to these address types due to elevated delivery risk. We recommend using a residential or business address for best results.

### Address Accuracy

Please ensure your shipping address is accurate. We are not responsible for packages delivered to incorrect addresses provided by the customer. If you need to change your shipping address after placing an order, contact us immediately at [support@pandapeptides.com](mailto:support@pandapeptides.com) — changes can only be made before the order ships.
