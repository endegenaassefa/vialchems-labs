## Product Description

Healing and Repair capsules are a synergistic peptide combination featuring three research compounds: **BPC-157** (gastric pentadecapeptide), **Thymosin Beta-4 Fragment 1-4** , and **GHK-Cu** (copper-binding tripeptide).

This capsule formulation supports in vitro studies of tissue repair, collagen synthesis, and cellular regeneration. Research applications include wound healing models, anti-aging cellular repair mechanisms, and skin rejuvenation pathways.

Each peptide offers distinct mechanisms for laboratory investigations of regenerative processes.
