# CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin Blend

Research-grade peptide blend combining 12mg Ipamorelin and 6mg CJC-1295 (No DAC) for growth hormone studies.

## Product Description

This lyophilized blend combines two research peptides targeting growth hormone pathways in laboratory studies.

**Ipamorelin** is a selective pentapeptide growth hormone secretagogue that binds to ghrelin receptors. Research shows it stimulates pituitary release without affecting cortisol or prolactin levels.

**CJC-1295 (Modified GRF 1-29)** is a synthetic GHRH analog designed for extended activity. Studies focus on its ability to amplify natural growth hormone pulses through pituitary stimulation.

The 12mg:6mg ratio reflects common research protocols examining synergistic mechanisms. Each batch undergoes third-party testing for purity verification. Manufactured in USA facilities under pharmaceutical-grade standards for in vitro research applications only.
