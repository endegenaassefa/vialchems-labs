## Ipamorelin Peptide Description

Ipamorelin peptide is a selective synthetic pentapeptide that functions as a growth hormone secretagogue (GHS) in laboratory studies. This research-grade compound binds specifically to ghrelin receptors without affecting cortisol, prolactin, or ACTH levels—a selectivity profile that distinguishes it from other growth hormone releasing peptides.

Manufactured in USA facilities under pharmaceutical-grade standards, each batch undergoes independent third-party testing to verify minimum purity. The white lyophilized powder maintains stability when stored at -20°C and reconstitutes readily in bacteriostatic water.
