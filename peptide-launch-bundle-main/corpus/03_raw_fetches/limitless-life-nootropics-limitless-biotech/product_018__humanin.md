## Product Description

Humanin is a 24 amino acids mitochondrial-derived peptide that research indicates may influence cellular responses to oxidative stress and programmed cell death.

Studies show Humanin peptide interacts with specific receptors to modulate apoptosis pathways. Research applications may examine how Humanin protects cells from oxidative stress and reactive oxygen species damage.

Researchers have investigated its cytoprotective and neuroprotective properties in models of age-related cellular degeneration. Labs study how levels of Humanin relate to mitochondrial function, neuronal survival, insulin sensitivity, and retinal pigment cell viability.
