## Product Description

P21 (also known as P021 or Peptide 021) is a synthetic tetrapeptide mimetic derived from the active region of ciliary neurotrophic factor (CNTF), specifically amino acid residues 148-151. This small-molecule peptide contains an adamantylated glycine at the C-terminus that increases lipophilicity and blood-brain barrier permeability while reducing enzymatic degradation.

Research investigations have examined P21 in studies of BDNF expression modulation, neurogenesis pathways, tau protein phosphorylation mechanisms, and GSK-3β activity in neurodegeneration models. 

The compound is used in controlled laboratory environments for investigating neuroprotective mechanisms, synaptic plasticity, and cognitive function pathways. Supplied as pharmaceutical-grade lyophilized powder with third-party verified purity testing for qualified research institutions conducting in vitro research applications only.
