## Oxytocin Product Description

Oxytocin is a cyclic nonapeptide hormone featuring a characteristic disulfide bridge between cysteine residues at positions 1 and 6. This research-grade peptide serves as a critical tool for investigating neurohypophysial hormone pathways, social behavior mechanisms, and reproductive physiology in laboratory settings.

Our pharmaceutical-grade oxytocin maintains high purity through rigorous third-party testing protocols. Manufactured in USA facilities under GMP standards, this compound supports diverse in vitro research applications including receptor binding studies, signal transduction analysis, and peptide structure-function investigations. Research use only.
