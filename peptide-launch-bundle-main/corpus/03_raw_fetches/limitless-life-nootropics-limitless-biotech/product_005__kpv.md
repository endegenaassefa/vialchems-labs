## Product Description

KPV is a tripeptide consisting of three amino acids: lysine, proline, and valine. This peptide represents the C-terminal sequence of alpha-MSH (alpha-melanocyte stimulating hormone).

Research has examined KPV in laboratory models studying anti-inflammatory pathways at the cellular level. In vitro studies have investigated its interaction with inflammatory signaling molecules and wound healing mechanisms. The peptide enters cells directly, where it has been observed modulating inflammatory responses.

USA-manufactured with pharmaceutical-grade purity standards. Third-party tested for research applications examining inflammation and tissue repair processes.
