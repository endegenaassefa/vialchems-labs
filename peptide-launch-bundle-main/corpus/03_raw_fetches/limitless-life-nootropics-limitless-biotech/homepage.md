## **Frequently Asked Questions**

### 

### Where can I buy research peptides with third-party testing?

Limitless Biotech offers research peptides for sale online with independent laboratory verification. Each batch includes certificates of analysis confirming purity specifications, molecular composition, and contaminant screening. Orders ship directly to qualified research institutions and laboratories.

### 

### Are your research peptides USA made?

Yes. All research compounds are manufactured in United States facilities under pharmaceutical-grade protocols. Our USA-made peptides follow Good Manufacturing Practices throughout synthesis, purification, and quality verification.

### 

### What quality control procedures do you use?

Our quality control procedures include HPLC purity testing, LC-MS molecular verification, sterility screening, endotoxin testing, and chemical contaminant analysis. Third-party laboratories at certified facilities perform independent verification on every batch before release.

### 

### What is endotoxin testing and why does it matter?

Endotoxin testing screens for bacterial toxins that can compromise research results. We test all research peptides for endotoxin contamination to meet research-grade safety standards. This verification protects the integrity of in vitro laboratory applications.

### 

### Do you offer peptide blends for research?

Yes. We manufacture proprietary peptide blends designed for specific research applications. Each blend undergoes the same rigorous testing as individual compounds, including third-party verification and complete analytical documentation.

### 

### What peptide synthesis methods do you use?

We use solid-phase peptide synthesis with advanced purification techniques. Our peptide synthesis process includes precision cleavage steps and multi-stage purification to maintain molecular integrity. This produces high-purity compounds suitable for scientific research applications.

### 

### How do I verify the purity of research peptides I purchase?

Each order includes a certificate of analysis showing HPLC purity results, LC-MS molecular confirmation, and contaminant screening data. These documents provide the analytical verification needed for scientific research protocols and laboratory quality standards.
