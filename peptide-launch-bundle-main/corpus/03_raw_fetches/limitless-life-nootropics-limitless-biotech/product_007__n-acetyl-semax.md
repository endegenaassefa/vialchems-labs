## Product Description

N-Acetyl Semax is a modified ACTH(4-10) analog with N-terminal acetylation for extended stability. Research applications include neurotrophic factor modulation studies, dopaminergic pathway investigations, and neuroprotection mechanism analysis in vitro. The acetyl modification provides researchers with a compound exhibiting altered pharmacokinetic properties compared to base Semax.

Laboratory studies examine its interactions with brain-derived neurotrophic factor (BDNF) pathways, monoamine systems, and cognitive signaling cascades. Research protocols investigate neuroplasticity markers, oxidative stress responses, and neuropeptide receptor interactions under controlled conditions across neuroscience research applications.
