## How Does Wolverine Work?

The Wolverine peptide blend combines BPC-157 and TB-500 to synergistically enhance the body’s natural healing and recovery processes.

  * BPC-157 (Body Protection Compound-157):



BPC-157 is a synthetic peptide derived from a naturally occurring peptide (Body Protection Compound) found in gastric juice. Studies have shown that BPC-157 acts locally at sites of injury to promote angiogenesis (new blood vessel growth), stimulate tissue regeneration (muscle, tendon, ligament, bone), reduce inflammation, and support gut health. The result is increased delivery of growth factors and cells important for healing to sites of injury, activation of cells important for healing, and reduction of inflammatory cytokines. 

  * TB-500 (Thymosin Beta-4 Acetate):



TB-500 is the active portion of Thymosin Beta-4, a naturally occurring peptide made in the thymus gland. Research has shown that TB-500 promotes cell migration to sites of injury, activation of cells important for healing, and reduction of inflammation. The result is aggregation of cells important for healing in the areas of injury, reduction of inflammation, and reduction of scarring. 

  * Wolverine Blend (BPC-157 & TB-500):



Together they work synergistically by increasing blood flow (more delivery of healing agents), aggregating cells important for healing, activating cells important for healing, and reducing inflammation and scarring. The rationale for use is stimulating the body’s natural healing and recovery mechanisms. Pharmaceuticals don’t do this. 

## The Clinical Science Behind Wolverine Peptide Blend

Emerging preclinical and early clinical studies in humans highlight the potential of BPC-157 and TB-500. Systematic reviews show BPC-157 promotes structural and functional recovery in tendon ruptures and other musculoskeletal injuries. TB-500 has been studied for its role in wound healing, with evidence supporting its regenerative effects through angiogenesis and cell migration. A wealth of anecdotal data suggests faster post-surgical recovery, faster recovery from musculoskeletal injuries, and relief from conditions like tendinopathy and plantar fasciitis.

When used under medical guidance, Wolverine peptide blend is generally well-tolerated with a strong safety profile in studies. Possible side effects include injection-site reactions, headache, nausea, and fatigue. Side effects, when they occur, are typically mild and resolve quickly.
