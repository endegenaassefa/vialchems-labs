##  About MD Total Wellness

At MD Total Wellness, we’re redefining what wellness care looks like in Orange County. Unlike online telehealth companies or cookie-cutter clinics, we’re a physician-owned medical practice offering personalized, results-driven care — in person, not over a screen. We believe in-person care is better care.

Dr. Oliak and PA Mollet have worked together since 2006 — first in Dr. Oliak’s bariatric surgery practice, now in functional wellness. They founded MDTW to address a growing gap in traditional healthcare: expert, evidence-based hormone therapy, safe and effective weight loss, and personalized wellness solutions that genuinely improve quality of life.

Dr. Oliak’s decision to transition from surgery to wellness wasn’t just professional — it was personal. His own experience with testosterone therapy in 2023 was transformational, reinforcing his belief that these treatments change lives. PA Mollet brought the same conviction about hormone therapy for women, having experienced its benefits firsthand. MDTW exists because they both believe in what they offer.

Your safety, your results, and your long-term success are always our top priority. We take the time to listen, understand, and guide you — with care that’s personal, evidence-based, and built around your goals.

####  Why should I consider testosterone therapy? 

Testosterone replacement therapy (TRT) can be life-changing for men with low testosterone. Low testosterone is common, even in younger men, and has become more prevalent over time — studies show average testosterone levels have dropped by at least 25% over the past several decades.

Symptoms of low testosterone include low energy, fatigue, low sex drive, weak or less frequent erections, ED, loss of muscle, weight gain, low metabolism, low motivation, irritability, anxiety, depression, and feeling past your prime. Men often attribute these symptoms to normal aging — but they are frequently due to hormone loss, and they are treatable.

If any of these symptoms sound familiar, schedule a free consult and in-office blood test to find out if testosterone therapy is right for you.

####  Why should I consider bioidentical hormone therapy? 

Bioidentical hormone therapy is among the most effective treatments available for perimenopause and menopause symptoms — addressing the root cause rather than masking symptoms. These include hot flashes and night sweats, vaginal dryness and atrophy, brain fog, irritability, low libido, difficulty achieving orgasm, joint pain, mood changes, heart palpitations, and migraine headaches.

Beyond symptom relief, bioidentical hormone therapy may also reduce the risk of chronic conditions including heart disease, osteoporosis, and dementia — making it one of the most impactful long-term wellness investments available. Schedule a free consult with PA Mollet to find out if hormone therapy is right for you.
