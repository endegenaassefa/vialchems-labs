## Introduction to Selank 

Selank is a synthetic peptide, modeled after the naturally occurring human protein tuftsin, used to treat anxiety and enhance cognition. Developed and studied in Russia, where it’s approved for clinical use as an alternative to SSRIs and anxiolytics, Selank is used off-label in the U.S. for similar benefits.

We offer pharmaceutical-grade (>99% purity), professional-use-only Selank that is manufactured in the USA. Administered as a subcutaneous injection, it provides a non-sedating treatment option compared to conventional anxiolytics.   * Reduced Fatigue:

Selank is not habit-forming and doesn’t cause withdrawal symptoms, unlike traditional anti-anxiety medications.

## How Does Selank Work? 

Selank works by modulating neurotransmitters and influencing genes expression in the brain. Key mechanisms of action include:

  * GABA Modulation:



Selank interacts with GABA receptors, enhancing the inhibitory, calming effects of the neurotransmitter GABA without causing sedation.

  * Neurotransmitter Regulation:



Selank influences levels of serotonin and dopamine, which helps stabilize mood, reduce stress, and improve focus.

  * Gene Expression:



Selank influences the expression of genes related to neurotransmission and immunity, potentially supporting overall brain health and cognitive function.

  * Sleep/Wake Balance:



Selank influences the expression of genes which helps regulate the balance between sleep and wakefulness, contributing to its anti-anxiety effects without causing drowsiness.

## Clinical Science About Selank

Key clinical research findings:

Research highlights Selank’s potential for anxiety and cognitive support. Gene expression studies demonstrate changes in GABA-related genes after administration. It enhances the effects of diazepam (Valium) in anxiety models and impacts neurotransmission similar to olanzapine (a drug used for bipolar disorder). Clinical observations note rapid and sustained responses in treating generalized anxiety disorder. In Russia it is used for conditions like anxiety and stress as an alternative to SSRIs and anxiolytics. 

  * Anxiolytic & Antistress Effects:



Studies show Selank acts similarly to low-dose benzodiazepines (reduce fear, anxiety, stress-induced behavior) but lacks their side effects, such as memory impairment and muscle relaxation. Studies note rapid and sustained responses in treatment generalized anxiety disorder.

  * Cognitive Enhancement:



Research suggests Selank can enhance cognitive functions, including memory and learning, particularly under conditions of stress or cognitive impairment.

  * Safety:



Clinical evidence suggests it is safe, effective, and free from the addiction or withdrawal issues associated with benzodiazepines.
