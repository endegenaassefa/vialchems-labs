####  What is peptide therapy, and how does it work? 

Peptide therapy uses short chains of amino acids—naturally occurring building blocks for proteins in the body—to mimic or enhance biological signals that promote healing, vitality, and overall wellness. At MD Total Wellness in Orange County, peptides like Sermorelin stimulate growth hormone release to support metabolism and muscle growth, while others like BPC-157 accelerate tissue repair by reducing inflammation and activating repair cells. This approach targets root causes rather than just symptoms, offering an alternative to pharmaceuticals with potentially fewer side effects. If you’re curious about peptide therapy in Orange County, contact us to get started.

####  What are the benefits of peptide therapy? 

Peptide therapy offers targeted benefits backed by scientific research, including faster recovery from injuries or workouts, improved body composition through enhanced metabolism and fat loss, sharper cognitive function, reduced anxiety, healthier skin and hair via collagen production, and boosted libido. For example, our Wolverine Blend (BPC-157 and TB500) promotes healing, while GHK-Cu promotes skin, hair, and nail health. MD Total Wellness offers personalized peptide therapy plans that include quality medication and professional guidance and support.

####  What is NAD+ therapy, and how does it benefit overall health? 

NAD+ (Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide) therapy replenishes a vital coenzyme that declines with age, powering cellular energy production and DNA repair. Administered via injections, NAD+ therapy combats fatigue, enhances mental clarity, supports anti-aging processes, and aids recovery from stress or illness. Scientifically, it activates sirtuins—proteins linked to longevity—and improves mitochondrial function. For those seeking NAD+ therapy benefits in Orange County, it’s an effective option for boosting vitality without relying on stimulants or drugs. Many clients notice improved energy within days.

Unlike traditional healthcare, which typically treats disease with synthetic pharmaceuticals that can have broad side effects, peptide therapy at MD Total Wellness targets root causes using evidence-based compounds that work with your body’s own pathways to achieve specific wellness results. NAD+ therapy is well-tolerated, with potential mild side effects like temporary flushing or nausea. These effects typically resolve quickly, and can generally be addressed with dosing adjustments.
