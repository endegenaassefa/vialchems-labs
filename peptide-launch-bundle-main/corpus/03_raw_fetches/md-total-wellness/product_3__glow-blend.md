## Introduction to GLOW Peptide Blend 

GLOW is an injectable peptide blend combining GHK-Cu, BPC-157, and TB-500, designed for regenerative health. GHK-Cu is a naturally occurring copper-binding tripeptide found in human plasma, BPC-157 is a peptide derived from a naturally occurring gastric protein, and TB-500 is a synthetic analog of thymosin beta-4, a naturally occurring peptide made in the thymus gland. This synergistic combination of peptides is used off-label for tissue optimization and offers a multi-faceted approach to healing and regeneration, unlike single-target treatments.

## The Science Behind GLOW

Research on GLOW’s peptides demonstrates their regenerative potential. Studies on GHK-Cu show increased collagen production by up to 70% and anti-inflammatory actions through NFκB suppression. Preclinical models of BPC-157 indicate improved outcomes in musculoskeletal injuries by boosting growth factors and reducing cytokines. Studies on TB-500 demonstrate accelerated wound healing via angiogenesis and collagen deposition. Combined, they provide synergistic support, with animal studies showing faster recovery and reduced fibrosis.

## Safety & Side Effects 

When used under medical guidance, injectable GLOW is well-tolerated with a strong safety profile. Possible side effects are typically mild and temporary, including: 

  * Injection site reactions like redness, swelling, or irritation.



  * Occasional headaches, nausea, or lightheadedness.



  * Metallic taste in mouth.



Rare risks include copper sensitivity or toxicity with overuse – prolonged use requires monitoring of copper levels. Contraindications include copper metabolism disorders (e.g., Wilson’s disease), pregnancy, breastfeeding, or active cancer. Medical supervision and quality peptide medications minimize risk and ensures optimal results.

Our peptides are fully vetted – they are pharmaceutical-grade (99%+ purity), manufactured in the USA, and free of contaminants and untraceable ingredients.
