### **Description**

Semax is a synthetic **heptapeptide** (Met-Glu-His-Phe-Pro-Gly-Pro) derived from a fragment of adrenocorticotropic hormone (ACTH 4–10). It is widely examined in laboratory research for its potential role in **neurological signaling, neuroprotection, and cognitive pathway studies**.

In vitro research models commonly explore Semax for its potential influence on **brain-derived neurotrophic factor (BDNF) expression** , **synaptic plasticity** , and the **central nervous system’s adaptive response** to stress or injury.

This 10mg lyophilized preparation is supplied for **controlled laboratory experimentation only** and is not intended for any clinical or personal application.
