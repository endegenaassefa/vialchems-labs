## Description

Hospira Bacteriostatic Water (30mL) is a sterile, non-pyrogenic solution formulated with 0.9% benzyl alcohol as a bacteriostatic preservative.  
It is designed to function as a solvent or diluent for compatible compounds used in controlled laboratory research applications.

The benzyl alcohol component helps inhibit bacterial growth, allowing for **multiple withdrawals** from a single vial when proper aseptic techniques are followed.
