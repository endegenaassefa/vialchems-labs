## Description

### **Ipamorelin | 10mg | Selective Growth Hormone Secretagogue**

**For In Vitro Research Use Only — Not for Human or Veterinary Use**

* * *

#### **Description:**

Ipamorelin is a selective growth hormone secretagogue (GHS) and ghrelin receptor agonist known for its high specificity and minimal side effects compared to earlier GHRPs. This pentapeptide mimics the natural action of ghrelin, stimulating growth hormone (GH) release from the pituitary gland without significantly affecting cortisol or prolactin levels in research models.

Ipamorelin is widely studied for its role in regulating GH secretion, muscle and bone metabolism, and cellular regeneration pathways. Supplied as a 5mg lyophilized peptide, this compound is intended solely for in vitro research applications.

* * *

#### **Specifications:**

  * **Unit Size:** 10mg (lyophilized powder, single vial)

  * **Molecular Formula:** C₃₈H₄₉N₉O₅

  * **Molecular Weight:** ~711.9 Da

  * **Form:** Lyophilized powder

  * **Storage:** Store lyophilized powder in cold, dry, dark environment.  
After reconstitution, refrigerate between **2°C–8°C**




* * *

#### **Application Context:**

Ipamorelin is researched in vitro for its potential roles in:

  * Stimulating pulsatile growth hormone release

  * Modeling pituitary and hypothalamic signaling

  * Supporting muscle and tissue regeneration studies

  * Evaluating selective ghrelin receptor (GHS-R1a) activation

  * Comparative studies of GHRP family peptides
