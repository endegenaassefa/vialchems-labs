## Description

### **GHK-Cu | 100mg | Copper Peptide Complex**

**For In Vitro Research Use Only — Not for Human or Veterinary Use**

* * *

#### **Description:**

GHK-Cu (Copper Tripeptide-1) is a naturally occurring copper-binding peptide composed of glycine, histidine, and lysine. It is studied for its potential to support cellular regeneration, collagen synthesis, and anti-inflammatory responses in research models.  
This 100mg lyophilized peptide complex exhibits strong copper affinity and plays a role in wound healing, tissue remodeling, and gene expression associated with repair processes. GHK-Cu is widely used in biochemical and molecular biology research for its ability to modulate cell signaling and antioxidant activity.

* * *

#### **Specifications:**

  * **Unit Size:** 100mg (lyophilized powder, single vial)

  * **Molecular Weight:** ~340.8 Da

  * **Form:** Lyophilized powder

  * **Storage:** Store lyophilized powder in a cold, dry, dark environment.  
After reconstitution, keep refrigerated between **2°C–8°C**




* * *

#### **Application Context:**

GHK-Cu is commonly studied in vitro for its influence on:

  * Cellular repair and gene expression related to regeneration

  * Collagen and elastin synthesis

  * Anti-inflammatory and antioxidant pathways

  * Copper-mediated enzymatic activity

  * Modeling tissue remodeling and wound healing mechanisms
