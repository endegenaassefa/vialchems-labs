## Description

### **CJC-1295 No DAC | 10mg | Short-Acting GHRH Analog**

**For In Vitro Research Use Only — Not for Human or Veterinary Use**

* * *

#### **Description:**

CJC-1295 No DAC (without Drug Affinity Complex) is a synthetic peptide analog of Growth Hormone-Releasing Hormone (GHRH) designed to promote pulsatile growth hormone (GH) release in research models. Unlike CJC-1295 with DAC, this version lacks the Drug Affinity Complex modification, resulting in a shorter half-life and more physiologic, pulse-like GH secretion patterns in vitro.  
CJC-1295 No DAC is commonly studied for its role in stimulating pituitary function, regulating GH/IGF-1 pathways, and exploring short-acting GHRH analog pharmacodynamics. Supplied as a lyophilized 5mg peptide, it is intended exclusively for advanced laboratory research applications.

* * *

#### **Specifications:**

  * **Unit Size:** 10mg (lyophilized powder, single vial)

  * **Molecular Weight:** ~3367.88 Da

  * **Form:** Lyophilized powder

  * **Storage:** Store lyophilized powder in a cold, dry, dark environment. After reconstitution, refrigerate between 2°C–8°C




* * *

#### **Application Context:**

CJC-1295 No DAC is studied in vitro for its potential effects on:

  * Pulsatile growth hormone release and pituitary signaling

  * GH and IGF-1 pathway modulation

  * Short-acting endocrine modeling

  * Comparative analysis with long-acting GHRH analogs (CJC-1295 w/ DAC)
