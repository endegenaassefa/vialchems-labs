### **Description**

GLP-2T is a synthetic **dual incretin receptor agonist** designed to target both **GLP-1** and **GIP** receptors involved in metabolic and energy-balance signaling.  
In vitro investigations commonly evaluate GLP-2T for its potential effects on:

  * **Insulin and glucose regulation pathways**

  * **Appetite and satiety signaling**

  * **Energy metabolism and lipid utilization**

  * **Endocrine communication in metabolic research models**




Due to its dual-receptor profile, GLP-2T is a widely studied compound in metabolic, obesity, and glycemic regulation research environments.  
This material is supplied exclusively for **controlled scientific experimentation**.
