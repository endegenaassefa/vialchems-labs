How quickly will my order ship? 

All packages are sent via **USPS Priority 2-Day Mail**. Most customers receive their order within **2–3 business days** , depending on USPS processing times.

What forms of payment do you accept? 

We currently accept all major credit cards.

Will I receive tracking information? 

Yes. As soon as your order ships, you’ll receive an email containing your **tracking number** so you can follow your package’s progress.

Can I change or cancel my order? 

If you need to make changes, please contact us as soon as possible. Because we ship **next business day** , modifications can only be made before your order enters processing.

Do you offer refunds or returns? 

Due to the nature of research materials, we cannot accept returns. However, if there is an issue with your order, please reach out and we will work with you to resolve it.

Are your products for human use? 

No. All products sold by Blue Ridge Peptides are strictly **for research use only**. They are not intended for human consumption, medical use, or veterinary use.

Where are your products tested for purity? 

All peptides undergo **two independent rounds of purity testing**. The first test is performed at a certified laboratory in **Europe** , and every batch is then re‑tested by Freedom Diagnostics in **Franklin, Tennessee** before being released for sale. This dual‑testing process ensures accuracy, consistency, and confidence in the quality of our research‑use‑only products.

Do you provide Certificates of Analysis (COAs)? 

Yes. COAs are maintained for all products and are available on our **Test Results** page. Each batch is tested twice—once in Europe and again in the United States to ensure the highest level of purity and accuracy.
