## How We Ship

At Blue Ridge Peptides, we understand that timely delivery is critical to your research. All packages are shipped via **USPS or UPS and generally arrive within 2-3 Days** at no additional cost to you. This ensures your materials arrive quickly and safely, with built-in tracking and insurance for peace of mind. 

Our packaging is carefully designed to protect your peptides during transit, with proper temperature control and cushioning. We take pride in ensuring that every order reaches you in optimal condition. 

## Order Processing & Timelines

**Processing:** Orders are typically processed and dispatched within 1 business day of receipt. 

**Delivery:** Once shipped via USPS Priority Mail, your package will arrive generally within 2-3 business days to most U.S. locations (excluding weekends and holidays). 

**Tracking:** Every shipment includes a tracking number, which will be provided via email upon dispatch. You can monitor your package's progress in real time through the USPS tracking portal. 

## Packaging & Temperature Control

We use high-quality packaging materials to ensure your peptides remain stable throughout transit. All orders include: 

  * Protective cushioning to prevent physical damage

  * Clear labeling for safe handling



## Shipping FAQ

### What shipping method do you use?

All packages are sent via **USPS or UPS and generally arrive within 2-3 Days**. This is included in your order at no additional cost and ensures reliable, trackable delivery to most U.S. locations. 

### How long does shipping take?

After your order is processed (typically within 1 business day), USPS or UPS and generally arrive within 2-3 Days to most U.S. locations. 

### Can I track my shipment?

Yes! Every order includes a tracking number sent via email upon dispatch. You can use this number on the USPS or UPS website to monitor your package in real time. 

### Is my package insured?

USPS Priority Mail includes automatic insurance coverage. For orders requiring additional coverage, contact our support team to discuss custom insurance options. 

### What if my package is damaged?

Contact us immediately with photos of the damage. We''ll work with USPS and replace your order at no cost. Our commitment is to ensure you receive your peptides in perfect condition. 

### Do you ship internationally?

Currently, we ship within the United States only. For international inquiries, please contact our sales team to discuss available options.
