## Meet Our Founders

Blue Ridge Peptides is led by Ross and Julie, a husband-and-wife team bringing complementary strengths across operations, systems, and responsible support. Ross’s background in business, combined with his service as an Army combat medic and first responder, informs a disciplined, detail-oriented approach grounded in accuracy, responsibility, and ethics. Julie brings extensive experience in information systems and enterprise environments, with a focus on compliance, process design, and scalable operations—helping ensure consistent service and research-aligned practices across the organization. 

## Where we’re based

Blue Ridge Peptides is a family-owned and operated company founded with a commitment to precision, integrity, and responsible scientific support. Located in Northern Virginia, our headquarters sits at the foothills of the Blue Ridge Mountains in close proximity to several major universities, research institutions, and biotechnology corridors—placing us at the center of an active and collaborative research community. 

This environment allows us to stay closely aligned with evolving scientific standards while maintaining efficient fulfillment and responsive service for our customers across the United States. 

## Our Mission

At Blue Ridge Peptides, we are dedicated to supporting the research community by providing high-purity, well-characterized peptides with transparent practices and dependable service. 

Our mission is not only to supply research materials, but to earn long-term trust through consistency, accountability, and a genuine commitment to those advancing scientific discovery.
