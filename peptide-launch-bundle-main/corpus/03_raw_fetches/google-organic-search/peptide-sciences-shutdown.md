# Peptide Sciences shutdown — landscape impact

Quote (homepage notice): "After careful consideration, Peptide Sciences has made the decision to voluntarily shut down operations."

Date: March 6, 2026 (~2:00 PM ET).
Last reported revenue: ~"$7.4 million in monthly online sales" (late 2025) per peptidedossier.

Driving factors:
- FDA warning letters to 4 vendors Dec 2024 (Prime Peptides, Xcel Peptides, SwissChems, Summit Research)
- FDA raid on Amino Asylum facility (June 2025)
- 50+ FDA warning letters to GLP-1 compounders in single month (September 2025)
- 19 peptides moved to FDA Category 2
- ITC General Exclusion Order on tirzepatide imports (January 2025)

SERP impact: A long-time top-3 ranker for many compound queries (e.g. BPC-157, GHK-Cu, MK-677) is now stale or partially deindexed — opens ranking opportunity for replacements. Several "alternatives to Peptide Sciences" listicles now ranking themselves (peptidedossier, NorthPeptide, PeptideLaws, Pure US Peptides, AdaptPeptides, ThePeptideCatalog), forming a sub-genre of opportunistic SEO content.

Note: Peptide Sciences was widely cited as the most-discussed brand on Reddit (~74k monthly brand searches per outliyr aggregate). Its disappearance is the single biggest landscape change in 2026.
