## GHK-CU

Category: [Peptides](https://ascendamino.net/product-category/peptides/)SKU: GHK50

GHK-Cu 50 mg is a copper-binding tripeptide supplied as a research-grade reference compound for use in in-vitro and non-clinical laboratory settings. It is commonly referenced in controlled research environments for peptide characterization, metal–peptide interaction analysis, and analytical investigation.

In laboratory contexts, researchers examine GHK-Cu to support general scientific understanding of peptide structure, binding properties, and stability under controlled experimental conditions. This material is intended for qualified professionals engaged in scientific and educational research only.
