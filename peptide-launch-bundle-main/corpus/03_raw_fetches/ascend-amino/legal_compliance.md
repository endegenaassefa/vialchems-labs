### 1\. Introduction

Ascend Amino operates this website [www.ascendamino.net](https://ascendamino.net/) to provide peptides and related products exclusively for laboratory research purposes. This Legal & Compliance page outlines our commitment to operating in accordance with applicable laws and regulations, as well as the legal responsibilities of our customers. By accessing or using our Website or purchasing our products, you agree to comply with all applicable laws and our [Terms and Conditions](https://ascendamino.net/terms-and-conditions).

Our products are not approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) or any other regulatory authority for any purpose other than scientific research. Customers are responsible for ensuring that their use of our products complies with all federal, state, local, and international laws and regulations, including but not limited to the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act and the Controlled Substances Act.

Disclaimer: Ascend Amino does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Any information provided on the Website is for informational purposes only and should not be construed as an endorsement or approval of any non-research use of our products.

### 3\. Customer Eligibility and Responsibilities

To purchase products from Ascend Amino, you must:

  * Be at least 21 years of age or the age of majority in your jurisdiction.

  * Be a qualified researcher, academic, or professional affiliated with a legitimate research institution or laboratory.

  * Provide accurate and verifiable information during the purchase process.

  * Use the products solely for lawful research purposes in compliance with all applicable regulations.




Customers are solely responsible for ensuring proper handling, storage, and use of our products in accordance with industry standards and legal requirements. Ascend Amino reserves the right to refuse or cancel orders if we suspect misuse or non-compliance with these requirements.

### 4\. Regulatory Compliance

Ascend Amino is committed to operating in compliance with all applicable laws and regulations governing the sale and distribution of research peptides. However, due to the complex and evolving nature of peptide regulations, customers are responsible for verifying that their purchase and use of our products comply with the laws of their jurisdiction. This includes, but is not limited to:

  * Compliance with FDA regulations in the United States.

  * Adherence to import/export laws for international shipments.

  * Compliance with any local or institutional regulations governing the use of research chemicals.




We strongly recommend consulting with legal or regulatory professionals to ensure compliance with all applicable laws before purchasing or using our products.
