# FAQ

Answers to common questions about our lab-certified peptides and research applications.

Shipping, Returns and Refunds

1 ____Do you accept returns?

Because of our product nature, returns are not accepted. We will, however, quickly address any order issues.

2 ____Do you issue refunds?

Refunds are processed individually, on a case-by-case basis, by our customer service team. When requesting a refund, please provide: Order number Description of the issue (with photos)

3 ____Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?

Orders can only be changed or canceled before they ship. Once shipped, orders are considered final due to the sensitive nature of our products.

4 ____What if my package is missing products or contains the wrong items?

f your order arrives with missing or incorrect items, please contact us promptly and include clear photos of the items received, the packing slip, and the outer packaging. For quality assurance purposes, all orders are photographed with their corresponding invoice and shipping label prior to shipment. Once the issue is reviewed and verified against our fulfillment records, we will reship the correct items at no additional cost.

5 ____What if my package is lost, stolen, or delivered to the wrong mailbox?

Once an order is shipped and confirmed as delivered by the carrier, we are not responsible for lost, stolen, or misdelivered packages. If this occurs, we recommend contacting the carrier directly with your tracking information to file a claim or resolve the issue. While we are unable to replace or refund packages marked as delivered, we will gladly assist you with documentation or details needed to support your claim with the shipping provider. Customer may add Route Shipping Protection at checkout. If Route is not purchased, Ascend Amino is not responsible for packages that are lost, stolen, or damaged after the carrier has taken possession. If Route is purchased, Route will handle eligible claims for covered shipping issues pursuant to their terms.

6 ____Do you offer package insurance or shipping protection?

Yes. We offer optional Route Shipping Protection at checkout. When selected, Route provides coverage for eligible lost, stolen, or damaged shipments in accordance with their terms and conditions. If Route Shipping Protection is not selected, Ascend Amino is not responsible for lost, stolen, or damaged packages once the shipment has been handed over to the carrier.

7 ____What if I enter the wrong shipping address at checkout?

Customers are responsible for ensuring that all shipping information entered at checkout is accurate and complete. Ascend Amino is not responsible for delivery issues resulting from incorrect or incomplete address details provided by the customer. If a package is returned to sender due to an address error, we may reship the order once it is received back, subject to additional shipping costs. We recommend contacting the carrier directly to request a reroute or forwarding when possible.

8 ____When will my order ship?

Order placed before 3 PM PST ship the same day (Monday-Friday), 12 PM PST Saturdays, and orders placed on Sunday will be shipped out Monday. Delivery typically takes 2-5 business days depending on your location.

9 ____How long does shipping take?

We offer multiple shipping options to accommodate different delivery needs. Priority Shipping typically delivers within 2–5 business days, while Express Shipping typically delivers within 1–5 business days, depending on the carrier and destination. All delivery timeframes are estimates provided by the carrier. Once an order has been shipped and handed off to the carrier, we are not responsible for delays, interruptions, or transit issues outside of our control.

Why are Ascend Amino labeled "Not For Human Consumption"?

Ascend Amino products are intended exclusively for **research use only (RUO)** and are sold solely to qualified personnel operating within controlled laboratory environments. These materials are **not approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA)** for human or animal use, including clinical studies, therapeutic applications, or consumption.

Ascend Amino operates in accordance with applicable regulatory requirements to ensure all products remain strictly limited to professional research and development applications.

**Notice:** Ascend Amino does **not** provide instructions, guidance, handling information, or recommendations regarding the use of any products. Purchasers assume full responsibility for compliance with their own institutional standards, laboratory practices, and applicable laws.

Are your products safe for ingestion?

No. All products sold by **Ascend Amino** are strictly intended for _in vitro laboratory research and analytical purposes only._ These products are **not** intended for human or animal consumption, injection, inhalation, topical application, diagnostic use, or any form of administration.

None of the products offered by Ascend Amino have been evaluated or approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) for safety, efficacy, or therapeutic use. They are not drugs, dietary supplements, cosmetics, medical devices, or medical products.

Any attempt to ingest, administer, diagnose with, or otherwise use these materials outside of a qualified research setting may pose significant health risks and is strictly prohibited.

By purchasing from Ascend Amino, the purchaser acknowledges, agrees, represents, and certifies that:

  * They are a qualified researcher or are purchasing on behalf of a qualified research institution.
