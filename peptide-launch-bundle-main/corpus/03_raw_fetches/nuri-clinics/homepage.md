## Frequently Asked **Questions**

###### **What makes Nuri different?**

Nuri offers structured, at-home wellness research programs designed to help people explore peptide research with more clarity and guidance.

Unlike buying “research-only” peptides online, Nuri programs include:

  * An eligibility-based intake and approval process
  * Educational guidance and clear program structure
  * Carefully sourced materials with cold-chain shipping
  * All supplies included, delivered directly to you



Nuri was built for people who want to explore peptides, without the guesswork

###### **Is this legal?**

Yes. Nuri operates as a non-profit research organization delivering structured wellness research programs within a university-backed research environment.

###### **Is this safe?**

No one can promise “risk-free” anything. What we _can_ do is make the experience more responsible: screening, clear instructions, proper handling/shipping, and access to support if questions come up. If something doesn’t feel right, you stop and reach out.

###### **Do I have to inject?**

Some Nuri programs involve self-administration by injection, while others use non-injectable formats. The specific format depends on the program you’re exploring, and this will be clearly explained before you decide to move forward.

###### **What if I’m nervous about injecting?**

That’s completely normal. Many participants are new to self-administration.

Nuri provides:

  * Clear, step-by-step educational instructions
  * All necessary supplies
  * Support resources to help you feel confident
  * Needles sized for the least amount of discomfort



You’re never required to move forward if you’re uncomfortable.

###### **How quickly will I notice results?**

There is no guaranteed timeline. Peptide research outcomes vary widely based on the individual, the program, consistency, and many other factors. 

Some people notice changes early, while others need a few weeks of steady participation. These programs are structured within specific timelines because **progress usually comes from consistency, not hacks**.

###### **What’s included in the program?**

Depending on the program, they include:

  * Program-specific peptides or products
  * All necessary supplies
  * Educational guidance and materials
  * Cold shipping to maintain quality



What’s included will be clearly outlined before purchase.

###### **Do I need lab work?**

Routine lab work is not required to participate in most Nuri programs. If lab work is relevant to a specific program or individual situation, this will be discussed during the intake process.

###### **Can I do a program while taking other medications or supplements?**

Maybe, but you need to disclose everything in your intake. Our team reviews for obvious conflicts and risk factors, and you should also check with your primary care provider, especially if you’re on prescriptions or managing a condition. Nuri does not provide medical advice, and participation does not replace care from your personal healthcare provider.

###### **What side effects should I know about?**

Side effects vary by individual and program. Common ones are usually mild, like injection-site irritation (redness, soreness, swelling). Some people may experience headache, nausea, or appetite changes. If anything feels off, stop and reach out

###### **Who is eligible?**

Eligibility is determined through a brief intake and review process. Not everyone is approved, and eligibility can vary by program. Must be 18+ years of age.

###### **Is this a replacement for medical care?**

No. Nuri programs are **not medical treatment** and do not replace care from a licensed healthcare provider. Participation is educational and research-oriented.

###### **Are your programs covered by insurance?**

No. Nuri programs are not covered by insurance. To help make participation more accessible, interest-free payment plans are offered through Affirm, Klarna and Afterpay at checkout.

###### **What’s your refund policy?**

Because these programs include temperature-sensitive materials and ship as a complete kit, once a program ships, it is non-refundable. If something arrives damaged or there’s a shipping issue, contact support immediately and we’ll help resolve it.

###### **How does shipping work?**

To help maintain product quality and integrity, approved program kits are **cold-shipped overnight**. Shipments go out **Mondays and Thursdays** , and tracking information is provided once your kit is on the way.

###### **Can I travel with the kit?**

Often yes, but storage and temperature matter depending on what’s included. If you tell us your travel dates and how long you’ll be away, we can advise on best practices. Participants are responsible for understanding airline, local, and international regulations when traveling.

###### **Will I need lifestyle changes?**

You don’t need perfection. But most people get better outcomes when basics are handled: sleep, hydration, movement, protein, and consistency. We’ll give practical guidance that fits real life.

###### **What are peptides, in plain English?**

Peptides are short chains of amino acids that naturally occur in the body and play roles in many biological signaling processes. In research settings, specific peptides are studied for how they may support or influence normal structure and function pathways.

###### **Will this work for me?**

No one can predict individual outcomes, and experiences vary. That said, **being approved through the initial intake is a sign you may be a strong fit for participation** based on the program’s eligibility criteria. 

Your call is a chance to ask questions and understand what participation looks like before deciding.

###### **Is my information private?**

Yes. Nuri takes privacy seriously and handles participant information according to applicable privacy and data protection standards.
