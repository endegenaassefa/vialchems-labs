## GHK-Cu FAQs

###### What is GHK-Cu and how does it support skin and hair health?

GHK-Cu is a naturally occurring copper peptide found in human plasma. It functions as a signaling molecule that supports the body's production of collagen, elastin, and glycosaminoglycans — the key structural components of healthy skin. It is also studied for its role in supporting hair follicle health and promoting the active growth phase of the hair cycle. GHK-Cu levels decline significantly with age, which is associated with reduced skin firmness and changes in hair growth patterns.

###### How is this different from topical copper peptide products?

Topical copper peptide serums work on the surface layers of the skin. The Hair & Skin Program delivers GHK-Cu via subcutaneous administration at a concentrated 50mg dose, which allows it to enter systemic circulation and support skin and hair health from the inside out — reaching tissues and follicles that topical products cannot access as effectively.

###### How long does it take to notice changes?

Individual experiences vary significantly. Many participants report noticing improvements in skin texture, hydration, and firmness within the first 4–6 weeks. Hair-related changes — such as reduced shedding and improved thickness — often take longer to become apparent, as the hair growth cycle operates on a longer timeline. The 12-week structure is designed to allow sufficient time for meaningful changes to develop.

###### Can I use this alongside my existing skincare routine?

Yes. The Hair & Skin Program is designed to complement — not replace — your existing skincare and haircare routine. Many participants find that their topical products work more effectively when combined with systemic GHK-Cu support, as the peptide supports the underlying structures that topical products target from the surface.

###### What are possible side effects?

GHK-Cu is generally considered well-tolerated. Some participants report mild, temporary side effects including injection site reactions (redness, mild discomfort) or mild headaches. These typically resolve quickly. Our clinical team will review your intake and discuss any individual risk factors before approval.

###### Who is eligible for the Hair & Skin Program?

Eligibility is determined on an individual basis by our licensed clinicians following a detailed medical intake. Generally, participants must be adults in generally good health without contraindicated conditions. Complete the brief questionnaire to begin the eligibility process.

###### How is the program administered?

The program uses subcutaneous administration — a small injection just under the skin, similar to how insulin is administered. Your kit includes all necessary supplies, and our written and video guides walk you through the process step by step. Our clinical team is available to answer any questions.

###### Can I combine this with the Glow or Wolverine Program?

This is a question best discussed with our clinical team during your intake. The Glow Program already contains GHK-Cu alongside BPC-157 and TB-500, so running both simultaneously may not be recommended. Our clinicians will assess your individual situation and make a recommendation based on your health history and goals.

###### Is this program covered by insurance?

No. As an experimental research wellness program, the Hair & Skin Program is not covered by insurance. The total program cost is $399, with flexible payment options available.

###### What results can I expect?

The Hair & Skin Program is designed to support the body's natural collagen production, skin health, and hair follicle function over 12 weeks. Many participants report improvements in skin firmness, texture, and hydration, as well as positive changes in hair quality and thickness. We do not make specific outcome guarantees — individual results vary, and GHK-Cu remains an area of active research.
