# **A Clearer Way to Explore** Wellness Research

Nuri Clinic is a non-profit organization that designs and delivers structured wellness research programs within a university-backed research environment.  
Our goal is simple: to make participation in wellness research more informed, more consistent, and more supported than the typical online route.  
We focus on programs that explore general wellness and structure/function outcomes. Our programs are not medical treatment, and they are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.

## How **Nuri Programs** Work

### 1\. Education First

Before anything else, we believe people deserve clear information.

Our website and materials are designed to explain:

  * What each program includes  

  * What participants are exploring  

  * How the process works  

  * What participation does and does not mean  




No pressure. No hidden steps.

### 2\. Eligibility-Based Participation

Nuri programs are not one-size-fits-all.

Participants complete a short intake to help determine whether a program is appropriate for them within our research framework. This helps create a more consistent and responsible experience for everyone involved.

### 3\. Program Access & Checkout

Participants who wish to move forward can purchase a program or product directly through our secure checkout.

We use Stripe for payments and offer interest-free payment options through:

  * Affirm  

  * Klarna  

  * Afterpay  




Purchasing a program does **not** mean you are receiving a prescription or medical treatment.

### 4\. Intake Review & Final Approval

After checkout, participants complete a more detailed intake so we can confirm eligibility.

This step exists to ensure programs are delivered responsibly and consistently. If someone is not approved, they are not shipped a program and are refunded.

### 5\. Cold-Shipped Program Delivery

Approved participants receive their program kits shipped cold to maintain quality and integrity.

All kits are sourced from carefully vetted suppliers and delivered as part of a structured research program, not a retail supplement purchase.

## What Makes **Nuri** Different

### Not Medical Treatment

Nuri does not practice telemedicine, prescribe medications, or provide medical care. Our programs and products are not FDA approved.

Participation should not be viewed as medical treatment and should not replace care from a licensed healthcare professional.

### A Research-Driven Approach

Rather than selling products with little guidance, Nuri focuses on:

  * Clear structure  

  * Defined participation steps  

  * Consistent intake standards  

  * Responsible delivery  




This creates a more thoughtful alternative to navigating wellness products on your own.

### University-Backed Research Environment

Our programs operate within a university-backed research environment designed to support ethical participation, consistent standards, and responsible handling of participant information.

## Our**Philosophy**

We believe wellness exploration should feel:

  * Informed, not confusing  

  * Structured, not improvised  

  * Supported, not isolated  




Nuri exists to create a calmer, more intentional way to participate in wellness research, without exaggerated promises or unclear boundaries.

## **Important** Disclosures

  * Programs and products offered by Nuri Clinic are not FDA approved  

  * These programs are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease  

  * Individual experiences and outcomes may vary  

  * Always consult with a licensed healthcare professional before beginning any new wellness program
