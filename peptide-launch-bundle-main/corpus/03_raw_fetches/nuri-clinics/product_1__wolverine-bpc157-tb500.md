###### What are the potential side effects of BPC-157 and TB-500?

While still experimental, BPC-157 and TB-500 are generally well-tolerated, but like any supplement or program, they can have potential side effects. Some users may experience mild headaches, dizziness, nausea, or injection site reactions such as redness or swelling.  
  
While these side effects are uncommon, it’s important to consult with a healthcare professional before starting and to monitor your body’s response closely during use.

###### Can BPC-157 and TB-500 be used safely together?

Yes, BPC-157 and TB-500 can be used together, and they are often combined to enhance your body's natural recovery process. When used in tandem, these peptides can provide synergistic effects, improving recovery from joint and muscle strain more effectively than when used alone.

###### Who is eligible for Nuri's BPC-157 and TB-500 program?

Eligibility is determined based on your health history, current medications, and goals, along with safety criteria defined in our protocol. To see if you may qualify, you’ll start with a short online pre-qualification form (about 5 minutes).

###### What can I expect to achieve?

Outcomes vary widely. Some participants report stable and consistent improvements in recovery over the 12 weeks; others notice subtler changes. Because these compounds are experimental and responses differ from person to person, we cannot guarantee any specific result.  
  
Our focus is on providing a safe, supervised setting to explore how you personally respond.

###### How long does it take to notice effects with BPC-157 and TB-500?

Results vary depending on the individual and the specific goal. Many users begin to notice improvements in joint pain, muscle recovery, and wound recovery within 2-4 weeks of consistent use. However, for chronic conditions or more severe strains, it may take longer to see significant results.  
  
The 12-week structure is meant to give enough time to observe patterns rather than chasing overnight changes.

Detailed administration guidance, safety instructions, and support from licensed clinicians are provided to participants. We also encourage you to discuss any injection-based protocol with your own healthcare provider.
