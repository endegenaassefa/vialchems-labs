Clients report steadier energy and easier portion control; experiences vary.

###### What are the potential side effects of Retatrutide?

Early studies suggest many people tolerate Retatrutide well. Most common are GI effects—nausea, indigestion/heartburn, stomach discomfort, diarrhea or constipation—especially during dose changes.   
  
Less common: headache, dizziness, fatigue, dry mouth, mild injection-site irritation, small heart-rate increases; seek urgent care for severe belly pain, repeated vomiting/diarrhea, signs of gallbladder issues or pancreatitis, allergic reaction, or low blood sugar (especially with glucose-lowering meds).  
  
The daily, natural supplements provided are specifically formulated to support the reduction and ease of these side effects.
