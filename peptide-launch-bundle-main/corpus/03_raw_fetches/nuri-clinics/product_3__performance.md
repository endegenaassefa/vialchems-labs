###### How often will follow-up appointments be needed during the Performance program?

Follow-up appointments are essential to monitor progress, make adjustments if necessary, and address any concerns or questions. An intake form must be filled out before shipment, and we request final feedback on your results.  
  
Schedule a call at anytime during your program to discuss special needs. Be sure to consult with your own primary care provider before starting any new regimen.

###### What are the potential side effects of CJC-1295 & Ipamorelin?

As with any experimental compound, there are potential risks and side effects. These may include injection-site reactions, changes in appetite, fluid retention, or shifts in blood sugar and blood pressure, among others. During your intake and informed consent process, a clinician will review **known and possible risks** in more detail and help you decide whether participation is appropriate. We also strongly encourage you to discuss the program with your primary care provider.

###### Who is eligible for the Performance Program with Nuri?

Eligibility is determined based on your **health history, current medications, and goals** , along with safety criteria defined in our approved protocol. To see if you may qualify, you’ll start with a short online pre-qualification form (about 5 minutes). If you pass that screen, you can schedule a call with a licensed clinician to decide whether participation is appropriate.

###### What can I expect to achieve with Nuri's 12-week Performance program?

Outcomes vary widely. Some participants report **improvements in recovery, sleep quality, training capacity, or body composition markers** over time; others notice subtler changes. Because these compounds are experimental and responses differ from person to person, we **cannot guarantee any specific result**. Some individuals report noticing changes within the **first few weeks** , while others take longer or experience more gradual shifts. The 12-week structure is meant to give enough time to observe patterns rather than chasing overnight changes.

Within the Performance Program, CJC-1295 and Ipamorelin are administered via **subcutaneous injection** as part of the investigational protocol. Detailed administration guidance, safety instructions, and support from licensed clinicians are provided to participants.
