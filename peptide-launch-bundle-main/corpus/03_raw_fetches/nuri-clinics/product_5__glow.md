## Three Peptides. One advanced approach.

### BPC-157 | The Signal

Your body already has systems in place to respond to physical stress. BPC-157 is a peptide derived from a protein naturally found in the digestive tract, and it’s widely studied for its role in supporting tissue function, blood flow, and a balanced inflammatory response.  
  
Think of it as supporting how your body communicates where attention is needed most.

### TB-500 | The Reach

Support doesn’t just happen in one place. TB-500 is a peptide being studied for how it supports movement, flexibility, and how the body as a whole adapts to physical demand.  
  
It’s often included to support a more whole body approach, helping you stay consistent and active over time.

### GHK-Cu | The Amplifier

GHK-Cu is a copper peptide your body produces naturally, with levels that change over time. It’s being studied for its role in supporting collagen signaling, skin appearance, and overall tissue quality.  
  
When paired with BPC-157 and TB-500, it adds another layer of support, including benefits for skin and overall appearance.

These three peptides are brought together to support multiple systems at once, helping your body stay adaptable, consistent, and resilient over time.

## Glow FAQs

###### What is GHK-Cu and why is it in the Glow Program?

GHK-Cu is a naturally occurring copper peptide found in human plasma, saliva, and urine. It acts as a powerful signaling molecule that supports gene expression related to tissue repair and collagen synthesis — the structural protein essential for tendons, ligaments, and connective tissue. It is included in the Glow Program to amplify the recovery and repair effects of BPC-157 and TB-500, with skin health as an additional benefit.

###### Can BPC-157, TB-500, and GHK-Cu be used safely together?

Based on current research and participant data, these three peptides are generally well-tolerated when used together as part of a structured, clinician-guided protocol. All Nuri programs are reviewed by an IRB and overseen by licensed clinicians. That said, these compounds are experimental and not FDA-approved. Your eligibility will be assessed individually by our clinical team.

###### How long does it take to notice changes?

Many participants report noticing improvements in recovery and how their joints and soft tissue feel within the first 4–6 weeks. More significant changes in resilience and overall recovery capacity are typically observed in months 2 and 3. Individual results vary based on health status, consistency, and other factors.  


###### What are possible side effects?

Commonly reported side effects include mild injection site reactions (redness, swelling, or discomfort). Some participants report temporary fatigue or mild nausea, particularly early in the protocol. GHK-Cu is generally considered well-tolerated. Our clinical team will review your intake and discuss any individual risk factors before approval.

###### Who is eligible for the Glow Program?

Eligibility is determined on an individual basis by our licensed clinicians following a detailed medical intake. Generally, participants must be adults in generally good health without contraindicated conditions. Complete the brief questionnaire to begin the eligibility process.

###### How is the program administered?

The Glow Program uses subcutaneous administration — a small injection just under the skin, similar to how insulin is administered. Your kit includes all necessary supplies, and our written and video guides walk you through the process step by step. Our clinical team is available to answer any questions.

###### Is this program covered by insurance?

No. As an experimental research wellness program, the Glow Program is not covered by insurance. The total program cost is $1,599, with flexible payment plans available through Afterpay, Affirm, and Klarna.

###### What can I expect to achieve?

The Glow Program is designed to support your body's natural tissue repair processes, promote a healthy inflammatory response, and enhance recovery resilience over 12 weeks. Many participants also report improvements in skin quality as a secondary benefit. We do not make specific outcome guarantees — individual results vary, and these compounds are experimental.

Please review all program information carefully and complete the medical intake honestly to ensure the program is appropriate for you before purchasing.
