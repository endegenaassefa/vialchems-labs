## Glow FAQs

###### What is NAD+ and why would I want to support my levels?

NAD+ is a coenzyme found in every cell of the body, essential for converting nutrients into cellular energy (ATP) and for activating key repair enzymes like sirtuins and PARPs. Research has documented a significant decline in NAD+ levels with age, which is associated with reduced energy production efficiency and cellular repair capacity. The Cellular Energy Program is designed to support the body's NAD+ levels through a direct, clinician-guided protocol.

###### Why is this program only 4 weeks?

The 4-week format is designed as a focused, accessible introduction to NAD+ support. It allows participants to experience the protocol, track their response, and gather meaningful data — while keeping the commitment and cost accessible. Many participants choose to continue with additional cycles or transition to one of our longer recovery programs afterward.

###### How does injectable NAD+ differ from oral supplements?

Oral NAD+ precursors (NR, NMN) must be converted by the body through a multi-step metabolic process before becoming available as NAD+. Subcutaneous administration delivers NAD+ more directly into systemic circulation, bypassing this conversion pathway. Many participants and clinicians report a more noticeable response from injectable forms, though individual results vary.

###### How long does it take to notice changes?

Individual experiences vary significantly. Some participants report noticing improvements in energy, sleep quality, or mental clarity within the first 1–2 weeks. Others notice more gradual changes over the full 4-week protocol. We encourage all participants to track their baseline metrics before starting so they have a clear reference point.

###### What are possible side effects?

NAD+ is generally considered well-tolerated. Some participants report mild, temporary side effects including injection site reactions (redness, mild discomfort), flushing, or mild nausea — particularly early in the protocol. These typically resolve quickly. Our clinical team will review your intake and discuss any individual risk factors before approval.

###### Who is eligible for the Cellular Energy Program?

Eligibility is determined on an individual basis by our licensed clinicians following a detailed medical intake. Generally, participants must be adults in generally good health without contraindicated conditions. Complete the brief questionnaire to begin the eligibility process.

###### How is the program administered?

The program uses subcutaneous administration — a small injection just under the skin, similar to how insulin is administered. Your kit includes all necessary supplies, and our written and video guides walk you through the process step by step. Our clinical team is available to answer any questions.

###### Can I do this program alongside the Wolverine or Glow Program?

This is a question best discussed with our clinical team during your intake. Some participants choose to run NAD+ support alongside or between other Nuri protocols. Our clinicians will assess your individual situation and make a recommendation based on your health history and goals.

###### Is this program covered by insurance?

No. As an experimental research wellness program, the Cellular Energy Program is not covered by insurance. The total program cost is $399, with flexible payment options available.

###### What can I expect to achieve?

The Cellular Energy Program is designed to support the body's natural cellular energy production pathways, mitochondrial function, and cellular repair mechanisms over 4 weeks. Many participants report improvements in energy, mental clarity, sleep quality, and physical recovery. We do not make specific outcome guarantees — individual results vary, and NAD+ supplementation remains an area of active research.
