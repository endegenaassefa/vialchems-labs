#### The Peptide Labs

Advancing Research Through Purity.

Welcome to **The Peptide Labs**. We are a proudly Canadian company dedicated to supplying the highest quality research peptides to the scientific community, academic institutions, and independent researchers across Canada.

#### Uncompromising Quality & Testing

We do not cut corners. Every batch of peptides we stock undergoes rigorous third-party analytical testing, including High-Performance Liquid Chromatography (HPLC) and Mass Spectrometry (MS), to ensure purity levels of ≥99%. We believe in total transparency and are committed to providing the highest standard of research materials on the market.

###### Our Mission

To accelerate Canadian research by providing guaranteed purity and unmatched reliability.

We believe that precise research requires precise materials. Whether you are conducting cellular studies, biochemical analysis, or development testing, the integrity of your data depends on the integrity of your reagents. We exist to ensure you never have to question the quality of your compounds.

###### Proudly Canadian

We are based in Canada and ship exclusively from within Canada.
