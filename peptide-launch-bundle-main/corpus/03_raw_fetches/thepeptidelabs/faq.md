###### Shipping Information

Do you offer free shipping?____

Yes!**The Peptide Labs** offers **free expedited shipping on orders over $150 within Canada**.

Do you ship outside Canada?____

No. We only ship to**Canadian addresses** , unless otherwise stated.

When will my order ship?____

Orders paid**Monday to Friday before 1:00 PM PST** are eligible for **same-day shipping**.

Orders paid **after 1:00 PM PST** , on weekends, or on holidays will ship on the **next business day**.

Do you offer express shipping?____

We typically ship via Canada Post Xpresspost. Most orders are processed within 24 business hours. Once shipped, delivery estimates are:

  * **British Columbia, Alberta, and Manitoba:** 1-3 business days
  * **Eastern Provinces (Ontario, Quebec, Atlantic, etc.):** 2-5 business days



If this is an emergency, please [contact us](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#8ee7e0e8e1cefae6ebfeebfefae7eaebe2efecfda0edef) and we'll do our best to accommodate.

_***Note: Remote areas may take slightly longer._

Will I receive tracking information?____

Absolutely. Once your order ships, you’ll receive an**email with tracking details** so you can follow your package every step of the way.

Can shipping rates or options change?____

Yes. Shipping rates, delivery options, and promotional offers (including free shipping)**may change at any time without prior notice**.

Is the packaging discreet? ____

Yes. We understand the importance of privacy. All orders are shipped in plain, unbranded packaging with no indication of the contents on the outside label.

###### Storage & Handling

How should I store the peptides? ____

**Lyophilized (Powder Form):** Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. For long-term storage (months/years), they should be kept in a freezer at -20°C. **Reconstituted (Mixed):** Once mixed with a solvent (such as bacteriostatic water), the solution must be kept refrigerated (2°C to 8°C) and used within the timeframe dictated by your research protocol (typically 4-6 weeks).

What is the shelf life of your products? ____

In their lyophilized (powder) state, our peptides remain stable for up to 24 months if stored properly in a freezer.

###### Return & Exchange

Can I cancel my order?____

Yes. If your order hasn’t shipped yet, you can cancel it by contacting us at**[[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#97e4f6fbf2e4d7e3fff2e7f2e7e3fef3f2fbf6f5e4b9f4f6)**. Once confirmed, we’ll process your refund.

Can I return a product once received? ____

Due to the sensitive nature of research peptides and the risk of degradation once they leave our controlled environment, all sales are final. We cannot accept returns or exchanges. However, if there is an error with your order or a product arrives damaged, please contact our support team immediately so we can make it right.

How long does it take to receive a refund?____

If we have not shipped your order out and have approved a refund, your refund typically appear in your account within**2–3 business days** after being processed.

What about bulk or special orders?____

For bulk or special orders, please contact us directly. Our friendly customer service team will be happy to assist you with any specific requests or questions.
