# Shipping, Delivery, & Return Policies

_Effective Date: February 10, 2026_

This policy outlines our shipping methods, delivery timeframes, and return procedures for all orders placed with PurePeptides.Bio.

## 1\. Shipping & Delivery

All orders are shipped via USPS Priority Mail, which typically arrives in 3–4 business days. USPS Priority Express is available for 1–2 business day delivery. Orders are processed and shipped within 1–2 business days of payment confirmation. Delivery may take up to 15 business days due to factors beyond our control, including postal delays, weather conditions, and high seasonal volume. All orders are shipped with current Certificate of Analysis and Endotoxin Certificate for that lot.

## 2\. Tracking Information

Tracking numbers are typically provided within 24-48 hours after an order is placed. Orders placed after 3:00 PM MST on Friday will receive a tracking number the following Tuesday. Tracking information is sent to the email address provided at checkout when your package ships. Please ensure your email is entered correctly.

## 3\. Missing or Delayed Packages

If your order is marked as delivered but has not been received, please first check with your local USPS office and neighbors to verify its location. In some cases, USPS may mark a package as delivered up to 48 hours before actual delivery. PurePeptides.Bio is not responsible for packages lost, stolen, or delayed due to incorrect shipping addresses provided by the customer, USPS delivery errors, or circumstances beyond our control.

If USPS confirms in writing that the package is lost and the shipping address provided at checkout was accurate, we will reship the order one time at no additional cost.

## 4\. Issues With Your Order

PurePeptides.Bio is committed to the highest standards of quality and customer satisfaction. We understand that issues may occasionally arise, including missing items, damaged products, or other concerns. The following policies ensure a swift resolution.

**How to Report an Issue:**

For any issues including wrong, missing, or damaged items, please fill out our Contact US form with a detailed description along with any relevant photos and documentation.

**Reporting Timeframe:**

Customers must report discrepancies within 3 business days of the date of receipt as indicated by the tracking number. Claims submitted after this period will not be honored.

**Resolution Timeline:**

We will respond within 24–48 hours after investigating and always strive to resolve issues to our customers’ satisfaction.

## 5\. Returns & Refunds

Due to the nature of research compounds and for reasons related to quality control, contamination risk, and legal compliance, all sales are final. Returns, exchanges, or refunds are not accepted. If you receive a damaged or incorrect item, you must contact us within 3 business days of delivery to request a replacement, following the process outlined in Section 4 above.
