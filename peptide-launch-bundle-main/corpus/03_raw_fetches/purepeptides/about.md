## **Our Process: Synthesis to Shipment**

#### **Step 1: Solid-Phase Peptide Synthesis (SPPS)**

Peptides are assembled amino acid by amino acid using solid-phase synthesis — the industry-standard method for producing high-fidelity peptide chains with precise sequence accuracy.

#### **Step 2: HPLC Purification**

After synthesis, Each lot undergoes high-performance liquid chromatography (HPLC) purification. This process isolates the target peptide from synthesis byproducts, achieving purity levels of ≥99%.

#### **Step 3:** **Analytical Verification**

Molecular identity is confirmed through mass spectrometry analysis, ensuring the final compound matches the intended sequence and molecular weight. No Lot moves forward without verified structural confirmation.

#### **Step 4: Sterile Filtration**

Purified peptides are passed through 0.22 µm membrane filters to remove particulate matter and microbial contaminants prior to vialing.

#### **Step 5: Lyophilization & Vialing**

Compounds undergo a controlled freeze-dry cycle to produce a stable lyophilized powder. vials are sealed under sterile conditions.

#### **Step 6: Independent US Laboratory Testing**

Above all, we use an independent US laboratory to rigorously test each lot before release. We ship every order with a current Certificate of Analysis (COA) and Endotoxin Report.

If we can’t verify it, we don’t sell it.
