## Product Description

GHK-Cu (Glycyl-L-Histidyl-L-Lysine Copper Complex) is a naturally occurring copper-binding tripeptide found in human plasma, saliva, and urine. Discovered in 1973, it has been extensively studied for its role in collagen synthesis, stem cell proliferation, and gene expression modulation affecting tissue remodeling.

**Molecular Profile:**

  * Molecular Formula: C₁₄H₂₄CuN₆O₄
  * Molecular Weight: 403.93 g/mol
  * Sequence: GHK:Cu²⁺ (1:1 complex)
  * CAS Number: 49557-75-7



**Research Applications:**

  * Collagen I, III, and elastin synthesis stimulation
  * Wound healing and skin remodeling studies
  * Anti-aging and dermal regeneration research
  * Stem cell and mesenchymal cell differentiation
  * Antioxidant and anti-inflammatory effects
  * Gene expression modulation (up to 4,000 genes identified)
  * Hair follicle enlargement and growth cycle regulation



**Quality Standards:**

  * Purity: ≥99% verified by HPLC
  * Third-party tested with COA included
  * Lyophilized powder with copper chelation verified
