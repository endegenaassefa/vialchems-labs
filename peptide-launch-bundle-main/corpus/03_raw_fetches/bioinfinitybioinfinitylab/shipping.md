# Shipping Policy

Last updated: February 2025

### ⚡ Same-Day Shipping Available!

Orders placed **before 2:00 PM EST** Monday through Friday are shipped the same business day!

## Business Hours

Our fulfillment center operates **Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM EST**.

Orders placed after 2:00 PM EST or on weekends/holidays will be processed the next business day.

## Shipping Methods & Rates

Method| Delivery Time| Cost  
---|---|---  
Standard Shipping (UPS Ground)| 3-4 Business Days| $7.99  
2-Day Shipping (UPS 2-Day)| 2 Business Days| $15.99  
Overnight Shipping (UPS Next Day Saver)| 1 Business Day| $49.99  
FREE 2-Day Shipping| 2 Business Days| FREE (orders $200+)  
  
🎉 FREE 2-Day Shipping on orders over $200!

All orders over $200 qualify for FREE UPS 2-Day shipping within the continental United States.

## Shipping Locations

We currently ship to all 50 US states. We do not offer international shipping at this time.

All packages ship from our facility in the **United States**.

## Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you will receive an email with tracking information. You can track your package using the provided tracking number on the carrier's website.

## Packaging

All orders are shipped in discreet packaging with no indication of the contents. Products are carefully packed to ensure they arrive in perfect condition. Temperature-sensitive items include appropriate packaging materials.

## Lost or Damaged Packages

If your package is lost or arrives damaged, please contact us within 7 days of the expected delivery date at [support@bioinfinitylab.com](mailto:support@bioinfinitylab.com). We will work with the carrier to resolve the issue and ensure you receive your order.

## Contact Us

For shipping inquiries, please email us at [support@bioinfinitylab.com](mailto:support@bioinfinitylab.com) during our business hours: **Monday-Friday, 9AM-5PM EST**.
