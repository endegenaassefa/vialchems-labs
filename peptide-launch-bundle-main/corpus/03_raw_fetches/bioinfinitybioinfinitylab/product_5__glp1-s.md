## Product Description

GLP1-S is a GLP-1 receptor agonist peptide researched for its effects on glycemic regulation, appetite modulation, and metabolic pathways. It mimics the incretin hormone GLP-1, binding to GLP-1 receptors to stimulate insulin secretion in a glucose-dependent manner.

**Molecular Profile:**

  * Classification: GLP-1 Receptor Agonist
  * Target: GLP-1R (Glucagon-Like Peptide-1 Receptor)
  * Mechanism: Incretin mimetic with extended half-life



**Research Applications:**

  * Glucose-dependent insulin secretion studies
  * Beta-cell proliferation and survival research
  * Appetite regulation and satiety signaling
  * Gastric emptying rate modulation
  * Cardiovascular protective effect investigation
  * Neuroprotective pathway studies
  * Body composition and metabolic rate analysis



**Quality Standards:**

  * Purity: ≥99% verified by HPLC
  * Third-party tested with COA included
  * Lyophilized powder form



**Storage & Handling:**

  * Store at -20°C (lyophilized) or 2-8°C (short-term)
  * Reconstitute with bacteriostatic water
  * Protect from light and repeated freeze-thaw cycles
