## Product Description

GLP3-R is a triple-agonist peptide targeting GLP-1, GIP, and glucagon receptors simultaneously. This tri-agonist approach is being researched as the next frontier in metabolic peptide science, leveraging three complementary pathways for maximal metabolic modulation.

**Molecular Profile:**

  * Classification: Triple GLP-1/GIP/Glucagon Receptor Agonist
  * Targets: GLP-1R + GIPR + GCGR
  * Mechanism: Tri-receptor incretin and glucagon activation



**Research Applications:**

  * Triple receptor synergy versus dual/mono agonists
  * Enhanced energy expenditure via glucagon receptor activation
  * Superior body composition changes in preclinical models
  * Hepatic lipid metabolism and NASH/NAFLD research
  * Thermogenesis and brown adipose tissue activation
  * Long-term metabolic homeostasis studies



**Quality Standards:**

  * Purity: ≥99% verified by HPLC
  * Third-party tested with COA included
  * Available in 4 sizes for dose-response studies
