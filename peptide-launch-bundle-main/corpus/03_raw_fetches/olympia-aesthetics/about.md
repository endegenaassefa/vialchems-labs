# ABOUT

## Olympia Aesthetics

## Meet Olympia 

OLYMPIA THALASSITES-MORRIS, MPAS, PA-C

Olympia Morris is a board-certified Physician Assistant and the owner of Olympia Aesthetics. Originally from Miami, she grew up in Palm Harbor, Florida.

Olympia attended USF, majoring in Biomedical Sciences with a minor in Biomedical Physics. While at USF, she was a freelance make-up artist working in the modeling and bridal industry, sparking her passion for aesthetics.

Driven by her love for medicine and desire to make a difference, Olympia pursued further education at Butler University in Indiana, earning a second Bachelor’s degree in Health Sciences. She then completed her Master’s Degree in Physician Assistant Studies in 2013.

Throughout her education and career, Olympia’s fascination with advanced aesthetic medicine grew stronger. She specializes in injectables including Botox, dermal fillers, and collagen-stimulating treatments, as well as advanced laser resurfacing, RF microneedling, and sclerotherapy for vein treatments. She combines medical precision with artistic skill to deliver safe, natural-looking, and transformative results.

What sets Olympia apart is her empathetic, personalized approach. She understands that every patient has a unique aesthetic journey, and she takes the time to listen, design individualized treatment plans, and provide care that addresses both skin, facial and body structure. Her goal is to help patients feel confident, radiant, and empowered.

Today, as a medspa owner, Olympia provides state-of-the-art aesthetic treatments and wellness services in a welcoming, professional environment. Her mission is to merge science, medicine, and artistry to help her patients look and feel their best.

## Meet Oliver 

OLIVER MORRIS, DO – Medical Site Director

Dr. Oliver Morris is a Board-Certified Physician specializing in wellness and preventative medicine. Dr. Morris is dedicated to helping patients achieve their full health potential through personalized, evidence-based wellness programs.

He specializes in peptide therapy, GLP-1 weight management, hormone replacement therapy, and ExoMind brain health optimization, using advanced medical knowledge to design individualized treatment plans that address both physical and cognitive well-being. Dr. Morris’ approach combines science, innovation, and patient-centered care, ensuring that every patient receives treatments that are safe, effective, and tailored to their goals.

Dr. Morris believes that true wellness is about addressing metabolism, cognitive function, and overall vitality. He takes the time to listen, evaluate, and create strategies that empower patients to take control of their health, improve quality of life, and achieve sustainable results.

At Olympia Aesthetics & Wellness, Dr. Morris personally guides each patient’s wellness journey, providing a supportive and professional environment where patients can optimize their health and performance. His mission is to merge cutting-edge medical therapies with compassionate care, helping patients feel stronger, sharper, and more vibrant in every aspect of life.

ANDREA, MEDICAL ESTHETICIAN

Andrea is your go-to Medical Esthetician with a passion for enhancing natural beauty and empowering you to feel your absolute best!

As a Master HydraFacial Certified professional, Andrea brings a wealth of expertise in delivering personalized skincare solutions. From luxurious custom facials to precision dermaplaning and microneedling, she is dedicated to tailoring treatments that suit your unique needs.

Andrea’s commitment to excellence extends to the art and science of skincare, specializing in HydraFacials that rejuvenate and revitalize your skin, leaving you with that coveted radiant glow. 

Armed with advanced techniques such as SkinPen microneedling and VI chemical peels, Andrea strives to elevate your skincare experience. These transformative treatments are designed to promote collagen production, reduce fine lines, improve dull skin to reveal a more youthful complexion while addressing any specific concerns such as acne, acne scarring and pigment irregularities. 

With an in-depth understanding of our skincare products, Andrea is here to guide you on the journey to healthier skin with a personalized regimen that addresses your unique concerns. 

At Olympia Aesthetics we are dedicated to unlocking the beauty that lies within, revealing your heavenly beauty, radiant you.
