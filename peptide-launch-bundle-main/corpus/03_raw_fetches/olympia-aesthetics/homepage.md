## ABOUT OLYMPIA

[Olympia Morris](https://olympiaaesthetics.com/about/) is a board-certified Physician Assistant as well as the owner and proprietor of Olympia Aesthetics & Wellness. Originally from Miami, she grew up in Palm Harbor, Florida.   


[Olympia](https://olympiaaesthetics.com/about/) attended USF, majoring in Biomedical Sciences with a minor in Biomedical Physics. Driven by her love for medicine and desire to make a difference, [Olympia](https://olympiaaesthetics.com/about/) pursued further education at [Butler University in Indiana](https://www.butler.edu/), earning her second Bachelor’s degree in Health Sciences. She then completed her Master’s Degree in Physician Assistant studies in 2013.

Throughout her educational journey, [Olympia](https://olympiaaesthetics.com/about/)’s passion for skincare, science, and helping others grew stronger. Her admiration for the science behind laser technology and its transformative abilities in skincare further fueled her commitment to helping individuals achieve their best possible skin. Today, as a med spa owner, [Olympia](https://olympiaaesthetics.com/about/) provides state-of-the-art medical aesthetic treatments and procedures for her patients. Her dedication to the art and science of aesthetic medicine empowers individuals to look and feel their absolute best.
