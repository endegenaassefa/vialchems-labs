# Join / Login to the Affiliate Program

#### **SwissChems Affiliate Program**

Welcome to SwissChems Affiliate program – **We offer 20% commission and 10% coupon code that you can share with your audience.** We have no contract nor grace period when you can start generating sales.

The goal of the Affiliate Program is to promote our products and not the discounts that the customers will get. The discount code is an additional benefit, but the highlight should be on our products and services, and how you will promote us to increase the traffic you may potentially send to our website.

You can promote based on your target market and the platform that you are using.  
You may discontinue being our affiliate whenever you wish to. 

**Benefits and Earnings of being an Affiliate.**

  * ✔ 20& commission from referral link 💸
  * ✔ 10% commission from discount code.
  * ✔ Free sample products.
  * ✔ Personal discount codes for your personal orders.
  * ✔ Wholesale discounts.



**Please note** that Discount code websites are NOT allowed. Once we found out that your source is a coupon website, regardless of which and when you started promoting us there, your commission will not be paid and will be forfeited.

Targeting keywords on PPC or SEO that include our brand “Swiss Chems” is not allowed. If you break this rule, your commissions will not be paid.

**NOTE:** Please remember that orders that are placed using your registered email with your code applied to it and or using your affiliate link, will not be counted as your sale.

We do not allow manual application of coupon codes, if your client forgot to apply your code, then it’s no longer valid and they may apply it to their next purchase.

  * • Share success stories and testimonials from you or satisfied customers.
  * • Leveraging Social Media: Keep your social media active, we provide images and banners that you can use for posting.
  * • Use platforms like Instagram, TikTok, X, Facebook, and YouTube.
  * • Post engaging content, workout tips, product demos, and user reviews.
