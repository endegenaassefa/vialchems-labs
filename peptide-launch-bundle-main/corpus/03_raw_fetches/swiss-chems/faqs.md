## Orders and Shipping

### __HOW LONG DOES IT TAKE FOR ME TO RECEIVE MY ORDER?

All orders are processed once the payment is received, not when the order was placed. Orders take 1 to 3 business days to process, excluding weekends and holidays. In case of delays due to product unavailability, we will email you with the expected shipping date. US orders typically arrive in 2-5 business days, while international shipping times vary. Refer to the shipping page for details. Please understand that once your order leaves our facility, we can’t ensure precise arrival times due to potential delays beyond our control. Tracking information may take a few days to update.

### __HOW DO I CHANGE MY SHIPPING ADDRESS?

If you need to change or correct your address, email us at [support@swisschems.is](mailto:support@swisschems.is) immediately. Shipping address changes are possible only if the order is still processing. Once an order is endorsed for shipping, we cannot modify it. Check your order status in the “Orders” tab anytime.

Please also take note that we cannot refund or reship orders with incorrect customer-provided addresses. We only follow what is indicated on the order form details. In case this unfortunate incident occurs, you’ll be eligible for a single 20% discount on future transactions.

### __HOW DO I TRACK THE STATUS OF MY ORDER?

Once your order is dispatched, an email containing your tracking details will be sent to the email address provided in your order form. Please ensure to check your inbox, as well as spam or junk folders. To track your order, click the link in the email. For any further inquiries about these shipping terms, feel free to reach us at [support@swisschems.is](mailto:support@swisschems.is).

### __WILL YOU KEEP MY CREDIT CARD INFORMATION ON FILE?

When you complete a payment on our site, we handle your payment details with the utmost care. Once the transaction is done, we don’t retain any payment information, ensuring the safety of your sensitive data against potential breaches.

### __DO YOU SHIP TO MY COUNTRY?

We provide global shipping, with country availability shown during checkout. If your country is not listed, our 24/7 support team can assist in placing your order.

Be mindful of possible shipping restrictions and fees due to local import rules. Stay informed about import restrictions, processes, and charges as per local laws.

We strongly recommend confirming your country’s import regulations before completing your order. Additionally, review our Shipping Policy and Terms and Conditions before making a purchase.

### __WILL MY ITEMS COME IN ONE PACKAGE?

If your order includes a mix of SARMs, PCT, and Peptides, note that some peptides can ship with SARMs or PCT, while others are usually sent separately. This means you might get two tracking numbers. You can also log in to your Swiss Account to track your order.

If you receive an incomplete order, contact us right away with a photo of the received items. We’ll verify if your order is arriving in separate parts.

### __I FORGOT TO ENTER MY DISCOUNT. CAN YOU APPLY IT AND ADJUST MY ORDER?

Unfortunately, once an order is placed, we’re unable to apply the discount code retroactively. Our system doesn’t allow modifications to processed transactions without incurring extra charges. Feel free to use the discount code for your next order.

### __CAN I PRE-ORDER AN ITEM THAT IS SOLD OUT?

If you’re interested in purchasing an item that is currently out of stock, feel free to contact us through any of our communication channels. We can assist you in placing an advance order and provide information on the expected restocking timeline.

Additionally, you have the option to enter your email address on the out-of-stock product page. This way, you’ll receive a notification as soon as the item is back in stock.

### __HOW DO I FIND STOCK AVAILABILITY FOR AN ITEM?

Some products show the available stock count directly, while others might not display the count on the product page. However, for products without a stock count, you’ll notice a clickable and highlighted “Add to Cart” button, indicating availability. On the other hand, unavailable products will have an “Out of Stock” label instead of a button.

### __HOW OFTEN DO YOU RESTOCK?

New stocks typically arrive within 2 weeks, unless specified otherwise. Subscribe to our newsletter to stay updated on restocks and new product launches. Simply scroll down to find the subscription box at the bottom-left corner of our homepage.

### __ARE THERE DISCOUNTS OR VOUCHERS AVAILABLE?

Certainly! Subscribe to our newsletter and follow us on Facebook, Instagram, and Twitter to stay in the loop about our exclusive deals and promotions.

### __PO BOX AND MILITARY ADDRESS

Only USPS can ship to these addresses.

## Peptides

### __IN WHAT FORM ARE YOUR PEPTIDES SUPPLIED?

The peptides you’ll receive are in lyophilized powder form, as any alternative type would lead to rapid degradation during transit. Consequently, the bacteriostatic water for reconstitution needs to be bought separately

###  __STORAGE AND HANDLING

Lyophilized peptides remain stable when stored at room temperature for several days to weeks in their original packaging. Ideally, you may store peptides at 4°C or below or frozen at -20°C for longer-term preservation. It’s essential to shield peptides from light exposure.

Lyophilized peptides remain stable when stored at room temperature for several days to weeks in their original packaging. Ideally, you may store peptides at 4°C or below or frozen at -20°C for longer-term preservation. It’s essential to shield peptides from light exposure.

When removing peptide preparations from the freezer, allow the vials to reach room temperature before opening them. This prevents atmospheric water from condensing onto the peptide, which is crucial because peptides are often sensitive to moisture and can easily absorb it. To counteract this, avoid moisture-related deterioration or crystal formation that can complicate handling due to their powdered state. We recommend aliquoting the peptides based on your experimental needs, minimizing freeze-thaw cycles and infrequent vial openings to maintain peptide integrity.

### __PEPTIDE VIAL OR KIT, WHAT DOES IT MEAN?

We have peptide products available for individual purchase per vial or in kits of 10 vials. Peptides sold per vial arrive without labels, matching the website’s images. Each vial’s identification relies on its unique top color, as indicated on the product page.

### __IDENTIFYING PEPTIDES

Our Peptides are exactly as shown on our website and can be recognized by the distinctive top colors displayed on each Peptide product page.

In rare instances when the designated top colors are unavailable, an alternative color will be used to ensure no delay in your order.

If you require help identifying the Peptide vial you’ve received, feel free to reach us via email at [support@swisschems.is](mailto:support@swisschems.is) or through our chat feature.

### __PEPTIDE KIT MISSING FROM AN ORDER

Peptide per vial and Peptide kits are shipped as separate items. Please look into your inbox, spam, or junk folders for the second tracking number. If you can’t find it there, you may ask for the tracking numbers by emailing us at [support@swisschems.is](mailto:support@swisschems.is).

### __PEPTIDE KIT CAME IN A DIFFERENT BOX

We send Peptides in kits using a dedicated box designed for customs clearance. Although we can’t ensure absolute delivery safety, the management has observed a reduced seizure rate due to this specialized box.

This box is exclusively meant for your local customs process and does not pertain to the vials within. Please disregard any text on the box. If you need help identifying the vials you’ve received, please reach us via email at [support@swisschems.is](mailto:support@swisschems.is) or through our chat.
