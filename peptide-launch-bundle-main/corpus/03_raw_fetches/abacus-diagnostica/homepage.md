# Abacus Diagnostica is now part of Uniogen

Three life science companies united their forces in 2022 to form Uniogen – a global diagnostics pioneer and a one-stop-shop for clinical diagnostics, point-of-care testing, and instrumentation for life sciences.

Based in Turku, Southwest Finland, Uniogen is a team of 100 highly motivated life science professionals. Uniogen’s products are being used to make clinical diagnosis faster, easier, and more cost-effective in more than 100 countries worldwide.
