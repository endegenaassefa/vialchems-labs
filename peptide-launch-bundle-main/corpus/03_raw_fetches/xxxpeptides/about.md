##  **About Us**

XXXPeptides started this journey to bring reliable, consistent Pure safe tested affordable peptides to the research community for consistent results in research.

Peptide research demands rigorous multitiered multi batch, testing, providing consistent compounds for the scientific industry.

XXXPeptides is a company built to supply the needs of the industry, from Popper testing to proper handling, expedient fast safe shipping, US base support.

XXXPeptides rigorously tests compound’s with the use a RP-HPLC system with a C8 column analysis, panel purity, and quantity (sterility USP 71–14 day TAT) (bioburden USP 61–5 day TAT) Endotoxin and heavy metal analysis designed specifically for peptide analysis, ensuring the compound that is ordered is the compound you receive.
