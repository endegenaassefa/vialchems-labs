# Retatrutide – Synthetic Triple Receptor Agonist Peptide | Research Use Only

**Retatrutide** is a synthetic, multi-functional peptide engineered to act as a triple hormone receptor agonist in experimental models. In research literature, it is described as a **GLP-1, GIP, and Glucagon (GCG) receptor agonist**.

This peptide is structurally modified with a lipidated fatty diacid moiety to extend stability and half-life in laboratory settings.

**Research Use Only:** This material is supplied strictly for laboratory developmental research use. It is not intended for human or veterinary consumption.

  * **Classification:** Triple hormone receptor agonist (research context)

  * **Structural Feature:** Lipidated peptide with fatty diacid modification
