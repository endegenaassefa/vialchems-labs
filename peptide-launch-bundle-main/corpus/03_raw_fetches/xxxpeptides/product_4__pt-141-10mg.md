# PT-141 (Bremelanotide) – Synthetic Cyclic Heptapeptide | Research Use Only

**PT-141** , also known as **Bremelanotide** (or Bremelanotide acetate in salt form), is a synthetic cyclic heptapeptide derived from alpha-melanocyte-stimulating hormone (α-MSH). It is classified in scientific literature as a melanocortin receptor agonist and is structurally engineered with a lactam-bridged cyclic core.

PT-141 contains an N-terminal acetylation and a cyclic peptide structure designed for enhanced receptor interaction in experimental models.

**Research Use Only:** This material is supplied strictly for laboratory developmental research use. It is not intended for human or veterinary consumption.

* * *

## Scientific Classification

  * **Common Name:** PT-141
  * **Scientific Name:** Bremelanotide
  * **Classification:** Synthetic cyclic heptapeptide
  * **Functional Category (Research Context):** Melanocortin receptor agonist
  * **Parent Molecule:** α-MSH analog



* * *

## Chemical & Structural Information

  * **Amino Acid Sequence:** Ac-Nle-cyclo[-Asp-His-D-Phe-Arg-Trp-Lys]-OH
  * **Structure:** Cyclic lactam-bridged heptapeptide with N-terminal acetylation
  * **Molecular Weight (Free Base):** ~1025.16 Da
  * **Molecular Formula (Free Base):** C50H68N14O10
  * **Appearance:** White to off-white lyophilized (freeze-dried) powder



* * *

## Research Context

Within controlled laboratory environments, PT-141 has been examined in studies involving:

  * Melanocortin receptor (MC receptor) binding models
  * Structure–activity relationship (SAR) analysis of cyclic peptides
  * GPCR-mediated signaling pathway investigations
  * Neuroendocrine pathway research
  * Peptide cyclization and receptor selectivity studies



PT-141 is frequently referenced in experimental peptide research evaluating melanocortin receptor modulation in vitro. All descriptions above are limited strictly to laboratory and investigational research contexts.
