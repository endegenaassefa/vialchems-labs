# TB-500 (Ac-LKKTETQ) – Thymosin Beta-4 Fragment (17–23) | Research Use Only

**TB-500** is a synthetic peptide representing the active amino acid fragment (17–23) of the naturally occurring protein **Thymosin Beta-4 (Tβ4)**. It is composed of seven amino acids and is studied in laboratory settings for peptide–protein interaction and signaling pathway research.

This short-chain fragment is structurally derived from the parent thymosin beta-4 sequence and is used in controlled experimental models.

**Research Use Only:** This material is supplied strictly for laboratory developmental research use. It is not intended for human or veterinary consumption.

* * *

## Scientific Classification

  * **Common Name:** TB-500
  * **Fragment Designation:** Thymosin Beta-4 (aa 17–23)
  * **Sequence Length:** 7 amino acids
  * **Classification:** Synthetic heptapeptide (research peptide)
  * **Parent Protein:** Thymosin Beta-4 (Tβ4)



* * *

## Chemical & Structural Information

  * **Sequence:** Ac-Lys-Lys-Thr-Glu-Thr-Gln (Ac-LKKTETQ)
  * **Amino Acids:** Leu/Lys-Lys-Thr-Glu-Thr-Gln (see specific fragment reference)
  * **Molecular Formula:** C38H68N10O14
  * **Molecular Weight:** ~889.018 g/mol
  * **Appearance:** White to off-white solid or lyophilized powder



* * *

## Research Context

Within controlled laboratory environments, TB-500 has been examined in studies involving:

  * Actin-binding protein interaction models
  * Cell migration and cytoskeletal signaling pathway research
  * Peptide–protein binding investigations
  * Thymosin Beta-4 fragment structure–activity relationship (SAR) studies
  * Extracellular matrix and tissue signaling research (in vitro)



All references above are limited strictly to laboratory and investigational research contexts.
