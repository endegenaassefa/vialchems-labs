## Return & Refund Policy

## ​

### 1\. All Sales Are Final

### ​​

### Due to the highly sensitive nature of laboratory research compounds and the strict temperature and light-controlled storage requirements, all sales are final. * Once a shipment has left our facility, we cannot accept returns or exchanges.

  * This policy is in place to ensure the integrity and purity of our catalog for all researchers; we cannot "re-sell" a returned compound as its stability cannot be verified once it has left our possession.




​​

### 2\. Damaged or Missing Items​

We take great care in packaging our research vials. However, if your order arrives damaged or is missing items, we will rectify the situation immediately.

  * Notification Period: You must report any damage or shortages within 24 hours of the delivery timestamp provided by the carrier.

  * Required Evidence: Please email PepGenics Research LLC with your order number and clear photographic evidence of the damaged vials and the original shipping packaging.

  * Resolution: Upon verification, we will issue a replacement for the damaged items at no additional cost to the researcher.




​​

### 3\. Order Cancellations​

  * Orders can only be canceled if they have not yet reached the "Fulfilled" or "Shipped" status.

  * Once a tracking number has been generated, the order is in the hands of the carrier and can no longer be canceled or intercepted.




​​

### 4\. Shipment Issues (Carrier & Customs)​

  * Domestic: If a package is lost in transit by the carrier, we will work with you to file a claim and potentially reship the research materials.
