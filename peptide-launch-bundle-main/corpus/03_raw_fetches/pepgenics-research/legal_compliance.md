#### Legal Overview & Compliance

### Commitment to Research Integrity

 

At PepGenics Research LLC, our mission is to facilitate scientific advancement by providing the global research community with authenticated, high-purity analytical reagents. We recognize that the foundation of any credible study is the integrity of the materials used; therefore, we operate with a commitment to total transparency and rigorous compliance.

​

This Compliance & Legal Overview serves as a roadmap for our professional standards—outlining our strict quality control protocols, our regulatory standing, and the legal responsibilities of the researchers we serve. By maintaining these high-barriers to entry, we ensure that the PepGenics name remains synonymous with precision and professional excellence in the field of laboratory research.

## Compliance & Legal Overview

## Last Updated: 2/24/2026

## ​

### 1\. Regulatory Standing & Non-FDA Status

  * Research Use Only: All compounds provided by PepGenics Research LLC are strictly intended for in vitro laboratory research and analytical purposes.

  * Non-Pharmaceutical Classification: We are not a 501(c)(3) or a pharmaceutical manufacturer.

  * FDA Disclaimer: These products are not FDA-approved and are not classified as drugs, supplements, food additives, or medical devices.

  * No Medical Application: Our assets are not for the diagnosis, cure, mitigation, or treatment of any disease or medical condition.




​​

### 2\. Laboratory Standards & Verification

  * Identity & Purity: Every batch of research material undergoes rigorous HPLC (High-Performance Liquid Chromatography) and MS (Mass Spectrometry) analysis to verify molecular identity and purity.

  * Transparent Documentation: We maintain a public COA Library where researchers can verify the specific test results for their assigned Batch Number.

  * Stability Assurance: Our assets are shipped in lyophilized (freeze-dried) form to ensure maximum structural integrity during transit.




​​

### 3\. Qualified Researcher Requirements

  * Age Restriction: Access to PepGenics assets is strictly limited to individuals 18 years of age or older.

  * Professional Certification: By acquiring these materials, the buyer warrants they are a qualified researcher or scientist with the necessary equipment and training for safe chemical handling.

  * Strict Consumption Ban: Any use of these products for human or animal consumption is strictly prohibited and constitutes a violation of our Terms of Service.




​​

### 4\. Monitoring & Misuse Policy

  * Zero Guidance: We do not provide dosing protocols, reconstitution instructions, or any guidance for in vivo application.

  * Right of Refusal: PepGenics reserves the right to cancel orders or ban accounts if we suspect misuse or non-compliance with research-only standards.

  * Legal Cooperation: We maintain the right to cooperate with regulatory authorities to ensure the safe and legal distribution of research chemicals.




​​

### 5\. Logistics & Integrity

  * Chain of Custody: We utilize secure, tracked shipping via USPS and FedEx to maintain a verifiable chain of custody from our facility to your laboratory.

  * International Compliance: Researchers are responsible for ensuring all acquisitions comply with their local and regional import regulations.
