# Security

# Data Sovereignty & Secure Procurement Protocol

# ​

At PepGenics, we believe that the security of your research data is as critical as the purity of our chemical assets. In an era of increasing third-party processor vulnerabilities, credit card fraud, and data leaks, we have established the Zero-Persistence Billing Model.

​

### The Failure of Traditional Retail Processing

Most research chemical suppliers utilize high-risk, third-party credit card processors that often store sensitive financial data in vulnerable cloud databases. PepGenics has intentionally decoupled from these automated gateways to ensure total data sovereignty for our clients.

​

### Our Secure Fulfillment Pathways

### ​

#### 1\. Direct ACH Transfer (Preferred)

We utilize bank-to-bank ACH (Automated Clearing House) settlement—the gold standard for secure, professional transactions.

  * Encrypted Link: Your payment is handled directly between financial institutions, just like a standard corporate bill payment.

  * No Stored Data: PepGenics never sees, touches, or stores your banking credentials.

  * Fraud Mitigation: By bypassing the credit card "grid," you eliminate the risk of card-skimming and unauthorized rebilling.




####  

#### 2\. Manual Verification & Invoicing

We have replaced the "faceless retail cart" with a Human-to-Human Verification system.

  * Precision Review: Once your cart is submitted, our Scientific Liaison team manually reviews the order for accuracy.

  * Verified Documentation: You receive a professional, itemized invoice. This ensures a clear audit trail for your records and prevents the "automated errors" common in retail carts.

  * Secure Payment Link: Each invoice contains a one-time secure link or instructions for direct settlement, ensuring no persistent data is left behind.




####  

#### 3\. Digital Asset Settlement (Cryptocurrency)

For researchers requiring the highest level of decentralized privacy, we offer settlement via major cryptocurrencies. This provides:

  * Peer-to-Peer Security: No central point of failure.

  * Immediate Confirmation: Real-time settlement without traditional banking delays.




### Zero-Persistence: Our Privacy Guarantee

"Zero-Persistence" means that your sensitive financial footprint exists only for the duration of the transaction. Once your order is settled:

  * No "On-File" Data: We do not keep credit cards or bank details "on file" where they can be accessed by hackers or unauthorized staff.

  * Metadata Protection: By using direct invoicing, we prevent third-party merchant aggregators from tracking your research procurement habits.

  * Human Oversight: Your transaction is handled by a dedicated Scientific Liaison, not an automated bot. This ensures any irregularities are caught instantly, providing a level of security a standard retail cart cannot replicate.




###  

### Procurement Workflow: What to Expect

  1. Cart Submission: Assemble your required assets from the Gold Elite Series and submit your request.

  2. Invoice Generation: You will receive a professional, secure invoice via email for review.

  3. Secure Settlement: Choose your preferred method (ACH, Crypto, or Direct Transfer) and complete the settlement using the instructions provided.

  4. Fulfillment: Once verified, your assets are dispatched via our secure logistics chain, and your tracking data is sent directly to you.
