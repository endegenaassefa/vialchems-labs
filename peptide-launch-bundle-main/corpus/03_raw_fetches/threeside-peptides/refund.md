# Refund & Returns Policy 

[Home](https://threesidepeptides.com) / Refunds & Returns

## Effective Date: December 30, 2025 

At **ThreeSide Peptides,** we take quality control and customer satisfaction seriously. Because our products are intended strictly for **research and laboratory use only** , we maintain a clear and firm refund and returns policy to ensure safety, compliance, and product integrity.

By placing an order with ThreeSide Peptides, you agree to the terms outlined below.

## All Sales Are Final 

Due to the nature of our products, **all sales are final** once an order has been processed and shipped.

We **do not accept returns** of any products for reasons including, but not limited to:

  * Change of mind
  * Incorrect ordering
  * Incompatibility with intended research
  * Improper storage or handling after delivery



This policy exists to protect product integrity and ensure compliance with applicable safety standards.

## Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Orders 

If you receive an order that is:

  * Damaged during shipping
  * Defective upon arrival
  * Incorrect (wrong product or quantity)



You must notify us within **48 hours of delivery.**

To request a resolution, contact us at **[support@threesidepeptides.com](mailto:support@threesidepeptides.com)** and include:

  * Your order number
  * A brief description of the issue
  * Clear photos of the product(s), packaging, and shipping label



If your claim is approved, **ThreeSide Peptides** may, at its sole discretion:

  * Issue a replacement product, or
  * Issue a refund to the original payment method



No replacement or refund will be issued without proper documentation.

## Lost or Stolen Packages 

Once an order has been marked as **delivered** by the shipping carrier, ThreeSide Peptides is **not responsible** for lost or stolen packages.

If a package is lost in transit (never marked as delivered), please contact us and we will work with the carrier to investigate. Resolution is subject to the carrier’s findings.

## Order Cancellations 

Orders may be canceled only if they have not yet been processed or shipped.

To request a cancellation, contact **[support@threesidepeptides.com](mailto:support@threesidepeptides.com)** immediately after placing your order. Once an order enters processing or ships, it **cannot be canceled.**

## Shipping Errors 

If an incorrect shipping address is provided at checkout, ThreeSide Peptides is not responsible for:

  * Delayed delivery
  * Lost shipments
  * Additional shipping fees



Please verify your shipping details carefully before submitting your order.

## Non-Refundable Fees 

Shipping costs, handling fees, and any applicable processing fees are non-refundable, except in cases where ThreeSide Peptides is at fault.

## Chargebacks and Disputes 

We encourage customers to contact us directly before filing a chargeback or payment dispute.

Unjustified chargebacks may result in:

  * Order refusal or account restriction
  * Permanent suspension from future purchases



## Policy Updates 

ThreeSide Peptides reserves the right to modify or update this Refund & Returns Policy at any time without prior notice. Changes will be effective immediately upon posting to this page.
