# Shipping Policy 

[Home](https://threesidepeptides.com) / Payment & Shipping

## Effective Date: December 30, 2025 

At **ThreeSide Peptides** , we aim to process and ship orders as quickly and accurately as possible while maintaining product integrity and compliance. This Shipping Policy outlines how orders are processed, shipped, and delivered.

By placing an order with ThreeSide Peptides, you agree to the terms below.

## Order Processing 

  * Orders are typically processed within**1–2 business days** after payment is successfully received.
  * Orders placed on weekends or holidays will be processed on the next business day.
  * Processing times may be extended during high-volume periods, promotions, or unforeseen circumstances.



Once an order has been processed, it cannot be modified or canceled.

## Shipping Methods and Rates 

Shipping methods, carriers, and rates are displayed at checkout and vary based on:

  * Destination
  * Package weight and size
  * Selected shipping speed



We reserve the right to select the shipping carrier that best meets delivery and compliance requirements.

## Shipping Destinations 

ThreeSide Peptides currently ships to:

  * **Addresses within the United States**
  * We do not ship internationally at this time.
  * We do not ship to P.O. boxes or freight forwarders.



## Delivery Timeframes 

Estimated delivery times are provided at checkout but are **not guaranteed.**

Delivery timelines may be affected by:

  * Carrier delays
  * Weather conditions
  * Incorrect or incomplete shipping addresses
  * Events beyond our reasonable control



ThreeSide Peptides is not responsible for carrier-related delays once the shipment has been tendered to the carrier.

## Address Accuracy 

Customers are responsible for ensuring that shipping information is accurate at checkout.

ThreeSide Peptides is not responsible for:

  * Delays or losses due to incorrect addresses
  * Packages returned to sender due to address errors



Additional shipping fees may apply to reship returned packages.

## Order Tracking 

Once your order ships, you will receive a shipping confirmation email containing tracking information.

Tracking updates are provided by the carrier and may not reflect real-time status immediately.

## Lost, Stolen, or Damaged Packages 

  * If a package is lost in transit and never marked as delivered, contact us and we will work with the carrier to investigate.
  * If a package is marked as**delivered** but cannot be located, ThreeSide Peptides is not responsible for theft or loss after delivery.
  * For packages damaged during transit, you must notify us within**48 hours of delivery** and provide photos of the damaged product and packaging.



Approved claims may result in a replacement or refund at our discretion.

## Shipping Restrictions 

Due to regulatory and safety considerations:

  * Products are shipped **for research use only**
  * Customers are responsible for ensuring lawful receipt and use of products in their jurisdiction



We reserve the right to cancel or refuse shipment to any address or individual at our discretion.

## Non-Refundable Shipping Costs 

Shipping and handling charges are **non-refundable** , except in cases where ThreeSide Peptides is at fault.

## Policy Updates 

ThreeSide Peptides reserves the right to modify this Shipping Policy at any time. Changes take effect immediately upon posting to this page.
