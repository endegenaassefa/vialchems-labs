# Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our peptides, quality standards, ordering process, and shipping. Get the answers you need to research with confidence.

How pure are your peptides?▶

Every NeuroTide peptide meets a minimum purity level of 98%, verified through HPLC and Mass Spectrometry analysis on each individual batch before it leaves our facility.

Is it suitable for human consumption?▶

No. All NeuroTide peptides are manufactured exclusively for laboratory and scientific research purposes. They are not intended for human consumption, veterinary use, or any application outside of a controlled research setting.

Where do you ship your products?▶

We ship to addresses across the United States, including Armed Forces APO/FPO locations, as well as select international destinations. **Please note:** international shipments are sent Delivered Duty Unpaid (DDU) — the carrier will contact you before delivery to collect any applicable duties or taxes.

What quality control measures do you follow?▶

Each batch goes through a full quality control process that includes purity testing, identity verification, and stability assessment — all completed before any product is dispatched.

What are peptides used for in research?▶

Peptides are widely used in biological and medical research to study cell signaling, tissue regeneration, metabolic processes, and drug development applications.

Peptide stability during shipping?▶

Lyophilized (freeze-dried) peptides remain stable throughout transit and will not degrade during shipping. Once received, store them in a cool dry place for short-term use, or in a freezer for long-term preservation.

Do you provide Certificates of Analysis (COAs)?▶

Yes. COAs are available in two places on our site. Each product page includes a COA image for the current batch, plus a dedicated COA tab with direct links to lab reports for the current and all previous batches.

What payment methods do you accept?▶

We accept all major credit cards along with a selection of verified secure online payment options. Contact our support team for the most current list of accepted methods.

When will my order ship and when will I receive it?▶

We aim to dispatch orders placed before 6PM Eastern Time on the same business day. Domestic delivery typically takes 1–7 business days depending on your chosen shipping method. International orders may take longer. Orders paid via offline methods won’t begin processing until payment is confirmed by our bank.

What is your return policy?▶

Due to the nature of our products, all sales are final and we do not accept returns or offer refunds. If your order arrives damaged, incorrect, or incomplete, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos and a description — we’ll review your case and may arrange a replacement or store credit at our discretion.

Can you help with reconstitution, dosing, or usage?▶

No. Our products are **not for human or animal use** and are sold **strictly for qualified laboratory research**. We cannot advise on reconstitution, dosing, administration, or usage of any kind. Please refer to published scientific literature or consult a qualified research professional.
