### Overview

GLP3-R (Retatrutide) is a synthetic triple-receptor agonist peptide supplied in lyophilized form for controlled laboratory research. It is studied for its interactions with GLP-1, GIP, and glucagon receptor pathways, making it a subject of interest in metabolic and obesity-related research. Provided as a freeze-dried powder to ensure maximum stability during storage and transport.
