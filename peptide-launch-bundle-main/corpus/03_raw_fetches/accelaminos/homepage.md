What are Peptides? 

Peptides are short chains of amino acids that are widely studied in laboratory and scientific research. They are commonly used in analytical, biochemical, and experimental research settings.

No. All products sold by Accel Aminos are intended **strictly for research use only** and **not for human consumption**.

What does “research-grade” mean? 

Research-grade indicates that the peptide is manufactured for laboratory and scientific research purposes, with a focus on purity and consistency.

How are your peptides manufactured? 

Our peptides are produced using standardized manufacturing processes designed to support consistency and quality. Each product is formulated for controlled laboratory research environments.
