**FAQ - Why are peps labeled not for human or animal use?**

Why products are labeled “Not for human or animal consumption”

There are a number of reasons that research peptides are labeled “not for human or animal consumption.” It is a multifaceted issue involving multiple factors related to the F.D.A. and Big Pharma that most of the general public is unaware of. The primary elements to the best of our ability are:

F.D.A. approval costs-The biggest factor? The fact that the F.D.A. charges millions of dollars to consider medications for its approval process. Not just a prohibitive factor for most peptide companies, but an absolute impossibility.

Scale of required trials - To meet the size and scope of the mandatory preclinical (animal) and clinical (human) research trials requires hundreds of thousands to millions of dollars on top of the approval funding, easy for Big Pharm, impossible for smaller firms.

Patent limitations - The composition of many peptides disqualifies them for patent protection, since they are usually comprised of proteins naturally present in the human body, thus making them less appealing to a profit-driven industry.

“Off-label” competition - Doctors may prescribe drugs “off-label” for purposes beyond initial F.D.A. approval, which further broadens competition with major players even when peptide counterparts exist.

This is not to say that these companies or their partners are distributing unevaluated drugs to the public. There are many studies which prove the safety and efficacy of these drugs, just not on the scale required by the F.D.A.

Reputable peptide companies are a safe, effective alternative to the unreasonably overpriced counterparts from Big Pharma. We hold the safety of our customers in the highest regard, and send all batches to a third party lab to verify both their sterility and content prior to distribution.

**FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTION-why is powder not loose?**

Why is the powder not loose in the vial?

Our vials contain lyophylized powder. These are called “pucks”.

The peptide is frozen solid, then the ice is removed by a vacuum, turning it directly from solid → vapor.  
What’s left is a dry, stable powder.

Why we do it-  
Lyophilization makes the product more stable (long shelf life), no refrigeration until reconstituted), it’s protected from heat & moisture, and its preserved without degrading!
