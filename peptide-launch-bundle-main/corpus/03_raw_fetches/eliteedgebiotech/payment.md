# **💳 Payment Options**

At Elite Edge Biotech, customer safety and convenience are our top priorities.  
All payment methods offered on our website comply with U.S. financial regulations and industry-level security standards.

Below is a detailed overview of the available payment options.

* * *

# **1\. Credit & Debit Cards**

### **Fast, Secure, and Widely Accepted**

We accept all major credit and debit cards:

  * Visa
  * Mastercard



### **How It Works**

  1. Select **Credit/Debit Card** at checkout
  2. Enter your details through our **secure, encrypted payment gateway**
  3. Your bank may require additional verification (2FA or OTP)
  4. Once approved, your order is confirmed instantly



✔ Your card information is fully encrypted  
✔ We **never store** your card details on our servers

* * *

# **2\. CashApp**

### **Simple & Secure Mobile Payment**

CashApp allows fast, hassle-free mobile payments.

### **How It Works**

  1. Choose **CashApp** at checkout
  2. You will receive payment instructions and our CashApp handle
  3. Complete payment in the CashApp mobile app
  4. Provide a screenshot or confirmation code if requested
  5. Once verified, your order is processed



**Important:** CashApp payments must be completed **within 30 minutes** of placing your order.

* * *

## **3\. Bitcoin (NOWPayments)**

**Secure & Decentralized Cryptocurrency Payment**

We accept Bitcoin payments via **NOWPayments** , a trusted crypto payment gateway that enables fast, secure, and privacy-focused transactions.

#### **How It Works**

  1. Choose **Bitcoin (NOWPayments)** at checkout
  2. You will be redirected to the secure NOWPayments payment page
  3. A unique Bitcoin wallet address and exact payment amount will be generated
  4. Send the exact amount of BTC from your wallet
  5. Payment is automatically detected and confirmed on the blockchain
  6. Once confirmed, your order is processed



**Important:**

  * Bitcoin payments must be completed within the time window shown on the payment page
  * Send the **exact amount** displayed to avoid delays
  * Blockchain confirmation times may vary depending on network traffic
  * Orders are processed only after payment is fully confirmed



### **4\. Link Money ACH Payment**

**Secure & Reliable Bank Transfer**

Link Money allows customers to pay directly from their U.S. bank account using a secure ACH bank transfer system.

#### **How It Works**

  1. Choose **Link Money ACH** at checkout
  2. Securely connect your bank account via Link Money
  3. Authorize the ACH bank transfer
  4. Payment is submitted for verification
  5. Once confirmed, your order is processed



**Important:**

**Save 10% automatically** when paying with Link Money ACH

ACH payments may take **1–3 business days** to verify

Orders are processed **after payment confirmation**

ACH payments are available for **U.S. bank accounts only**

## 5\. **Zelle**

**Fast & Secure Bank-to-Bank Payment**

Zelle allows quick, direct bank transfers without additional fees using your trusted banking app.

#### **How It Works**

  1. Choose **Zelle** at checkout
  2. You will receive payment instructions and our **Zelle email or phone number**
  3. Send the payment using Zelle through your bank’s mobile app or online banking
  4. Provide a screenshot or confirmation if requested
  5. Once verified, your order is processed



**Important:**  
Zelle payments must be completed within **30 minutes** of placing your order. Unpaid orders may be automatically canceled.
