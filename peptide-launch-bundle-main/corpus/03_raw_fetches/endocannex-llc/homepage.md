#####  What are the benefits of NeuroLeaf teas?

NeuroLeaf teas incorporate sustainably harvested botanicals known for their cognitive, relaxation, and immune-supporting properties. Each blend is designed to provide a natural, plant-based wellness experience.

#####  What’s the best way to store my products?

Keep your tinctures, flower, and concentrates in a cool, dark place away from direct sunlight to preserve potency and freshness.

#####  Do your tinctures contain artificial additives or fillers?

No, our tinctures are formulated with organic MCT oil and carefully extracted cannabinoids to ensure a clean, natural experience with no unnecessary additives.

We source from GMP labs across the country for our cannabinoids that utilize remediation policies that remove harmful additives. No Heavy metals. For our herbal components, we partner with local herbalists to hand pick herbs for our blended components we purchase from USDA certified organic farms.

Yes, all of our hemp-derived products are Farm Bill compliant and contain less than 0.3% Delta-9 THC in accordance with federal regulations. However, state laws vary, so check local regulations before purchasing.
