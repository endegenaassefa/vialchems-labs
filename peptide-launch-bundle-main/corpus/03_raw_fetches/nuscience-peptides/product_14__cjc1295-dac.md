### Description – CJC-1295 W/DAC

CJC-1295 W/DAC is a synthetic peptide developed to be an analogy of growth hormone releasing hormone (GHRH)[1](https://web.archive.org/web/20240423114222/https://www.extremepeptides.com/product/cjc-1295-with-dac/#fn:1). The 29 amino acid sequence is derived from GRF(1-29), a naturally occurring growth hormone releasing factor. CJC-1295 W/DAC is reported to be a potent agent for the stimulation of long-term growth hormone (GH) and IGF-1 release[2](https://web.archive.org/web/20240423114222/https://www.extremepeptides.com/product/cjc-1295-with-dac/#fn:2). These biochemical actions have been shown to result in the promotion of lean body mass, enhanced strength, increased appetite, and an improved quality of sleep in animal test subjects[3](https://web.archive.org/web/20240423114222/https://www.extremepeptides.com/product/cjc-1295-with-dac/#fn:3).

### Product Comparison

Though CJC-1295 W/DAC is an analogy of GRF(1-29) and acts to release GH through a similar pathway, CJC-1295 W/DAC benefits from a much improved half-life relative to its native counterpart[4](https://web.archive.org/web/20240423114222/https://www.extremepeptides.com/product/cjc-1295-with-dac/#fn:4). By existing in the bloodstream for long periods of time before degradation, CJC-1295 W/DAC is able to promote sustained GH release.

In spite of having a similar site of action, CJC-1295 W/DAC (and [CJC-1295 no DAC](https://web.archive.org/web/20240423114222/https://nusciencepeptides.com/)) does not stimulate GH production using the same biochemical pathway as the popular GH secretagogue GHRP-6[5](https://web.archive.org/web/20240423114222/https://www.extremepeptides.com/product/cjc-1295-with-dac/#fn:5). In light of this observation, it has been possible for researchers to administrate CJC-1295 species and GHRP-6 concurrently in order to achieve an additive positive effect on GH release.

### Drug Affinity Complex

The drug affinity complex (DAC) is a chemical group fixed to CJC-1295 that helps the peptide escape degradation by natural enzymes. By adding a component that gives CJC-1295 an affinity for albumin[6](https://web.archive.org/web/20240423114222/https://www.extremepeptides.com/product/cjc-1295-with-dac/#fn:6), a naturally occurring protein in blood plasma, the peptide can achieve a much longer systemic half-life.

Preference for a long or short peptide half-life depends entirely on the research application of these agents.
