## FAQs

____ What are peptides in science?

Peptides are short amino acid chains, building blocks of proteins. They serve vital roles in biology, acting as messengers, hormones, and enzymes, influencing various physiological processes.

____ Are peptides legal?

Peptide legality varies. Prescribed medical use is generally legal. Unauthorized use, especially for performance enhancement, may face legal restrictions.

____ What are the uses of peptides?

Peptides find roles in medicine (drug development), cosmetics (anti-aging), research, and biotechnology (protein production).

____ Do peptides have side effects?

Peptides may cause mild effects like redness or swelling. Proper use under professional guidance is crucial to avoid potential adverse reactions.
