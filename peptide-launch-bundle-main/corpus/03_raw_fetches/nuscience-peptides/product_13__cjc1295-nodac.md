### Description – CJC-1295 NO-DAC

CJC-1295 NO-DAC is a synthetic peptide developed to be an analogy of growth hormone-releasing hormone (GHRH)[1](https://web.archive.org/web/20240613203352/https://www.extremepeptides.com/product/cjc-1295-no-dac-2mg-mod-grf-1-29/#fn:1). The 29 amino acid sequence is derived from GRF(1-29), a naturally occurring growth hormone-releasing factor. As its name implies, CJC-1295 no DAC lacks the ‘drug affinity complex’ described below[2](https://web.archive.org/web/20240613203352/https://www.extremepeptides.com/product/cjc-1295-no-dac-2mg-mod-grf-1-29/#fn:2). This structural difference infers a shorter peptide half-life than its DAC-inclusive counterpart, which elicits a GH release profile that more closely reflects what occurs in nature. CJC-1295 no DAC is reported to be a potent agent for the stimulation of pulsatile growth hormone (GH) and IGF-1 release[3](https://web.archive.org/web/20240613203352/https://www.extremepeptides.com/product/cjc-1295-no-dac-2mg-mod-grf-1-29/#fn:3). These biochemical actions have been shown to result in the promotion of lean body mass, enhanced strength, increased appetite, and improved quality of sleep in animal test subjects[4](https://web.archive.org/web/20240613203352/https://www.extremepeptides.com/product/cjc-1295-no-dac-2mg-mod-grf-1-29/#fn:4).

### Product Comparison – CJC-1295 NO-DAC

In spite of having a similar site of action, CJC-1295 no DAC (and [CJC-1295 with DAC](https://web.archive.org/web/20240613203352/https://nusciencepeptides.com/product/cjc-1295-w-dac/)) does not stimulate GH production using the same biochemical pathway as the popular GH secretagogue GHRP-6[5](https://web.archive.org/web/20240613203352/https://www.extremepeptides.com/product/cjc-1295-no-dac-2mg-mod-grf-1-29/#fn:5). In light of this observation, it has been possible for researchers to administrate CJC-1295 species and GHRP-6 concurrently in order to achieve an additive positive effect on GH release.

### Drug Affinity Complex – CJC-1295 NO-DAC

The drug affinity complex (DAC) is a chemical group fixed to CJC-1295 DAC that helps the peptide escape degradation by natural enzymes. By adding a component that gives CJC-1295 an affinity for albumin[2](https://web.archive.org/web/20240613203352/https://www.extremepeptides.com/product/cjc-1295-no-dac-2mg-mod-grf-1-29/#fn:2), a naturally occurring protein in blood plasma, the peptide can achieve a much longer systemic half-life.

Preference for a long or short peptide half-life depends entirely on the research application of these agents.
