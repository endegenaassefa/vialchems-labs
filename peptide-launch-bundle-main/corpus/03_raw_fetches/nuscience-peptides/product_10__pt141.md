### Description – PT-141

PT-141 is a synthetic variant of the naturally occurring peptide hormone alpha-melanocyte stimulating hormone (α-MSH)[1](https://web.archive.org/web/20240613203118/https://www.extremepeptides.com/product/pt-141-bremelanotide-10mg/1) and has been proven as an effective agent for the improvement of sexual dysfunction in both male and female animal test subjects[2](https://web.archive.org/web/20240613203118/https://www.extremepeptides.com/product/pt-141-bremelanotide-10mg/2).Naturally occurring α-MSH acts as an agonist of melanocortin receptors MC1, MC3, and MC4; receptor MC1 has been shown to be responsible for skin pigmentation effects, while MC3 and MC4 are responsible for effects on appetite, metabolism, and sexual activity[3](https://web.archive.org/web/20240613203118/https://www.extremepeptides.com/product/pt-141-bremelanotide-10mg/3). Using this understanding of the native peptide, PT-141 was developed as an agent meant only to bind melanocortin receptors MC3 and MC4, thereby acting as a selective stimulator of cellular metabolic and erectile effects[4](https://web.archive.org/web/20240613203118/https://www.extremepeptides.com/product/pt-141-bremelanotide-10mg/4).PT-141 has been shown to be a lipolytic (fat cell-destroying) agent[5](https://web.archive.org/web/20240613203118/https://www.extremepeptides.com/product/pt-141-bremelanotide-10mg/5), a suppressant of appetite[6](https://web.archive.org/web/20240613203118/https://www.extremepeptides.com/product/pt-141-bremelanotide-10mg/6), and a stimulant of libido in animal test subjects[4](https://web.archive.org/web/20240613203118/https://www.extremepeptides.com/product/pt-141-bremelanotide-10mg/4). PT-141 has demonstrated great efficacy in reducing caloric intake, while eliminating cholesterol and fat stores in obese mice through its targeted metabolic action[7](https://web.archive.org/web/20240613203118/https://www.extremepeptides.com/product/pt-141-bremelanotide-10mg/7).

### Product Comparison

PT-141 is structurally similar to the biosynthetic peptide hormone Melanotan II, but has shown to be a potent binding agent of MC3 and MC4 only, demonstrating no affinity for MC1. In animal tests, PT-141 has proven quite effective at correcting sexual dysfunction[2](https://web.archive.org/web/20240613203118/https://www.extremepeptides.com/product/pt-141-bremelanotide-10mg/2), while Melanotan II induces an additional effect on skin tone.

Unlike other agents that have an effect on erectile response (e.g. Viagra), PT-141 performs no action on the vascular system, but rather induces arousal and heightens sexual desire via the nervous system – binding important receptors in the brain[8](https://web.archive.org/web/20240613203118/https://www.extremepeptides.com/product/pt-141-bremelanotide-10mg/8).

Melatonan I is also structurally similar to PT-141, both being synthetic analogues of α-MSH, but Melanotan I is only active in binding the MC1 receptor[3](https://web.archive.org/web/20240613203118/https://www.extremepeptides.com/product/pt-141-bremelanotide-10mg/3), thereby producing skin tanning effects and neglecting any stimulation of sexual response. Though these agents look alike, they could not be more separate in function.
