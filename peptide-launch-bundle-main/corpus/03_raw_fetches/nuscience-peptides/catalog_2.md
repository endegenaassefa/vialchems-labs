## Frequently Asked Questions

What does a peptide serum do?

* * *

Peptide serums promote collagen production, enhancing skin elasticity and reducing wrinkles. They support skin repair and contribute to a more youthful, radiant complexion.

Do dermatologists recommend peptides?

* * *

Yes, dermatologists often recommend peptides for their skin-renewing benefits. Peptides can address various skincare concerns and are a popular choice in dermatological routines.

Is peptide serum better than retinol?

* * *

Peptide serums and retinol serve different purposes. Peptides focus on collagen synthesis and skin repair, while retinol targets fine lines, wrinkles, and overall skin texture. Combining both can offer comprehensive skincare benefits.

Is peptide serum better than hyaluronic acid?

* * *

Peptide serums and hyaluronic acid serve complementary roles. Peptides support collagen, promoting skin firmness, while hyaluronic acid hydrates and plumps. A combination of both can address hydration and anti-aging needs effectively.
