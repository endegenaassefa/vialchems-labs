#  Community Guidelines 

**Pure Peptide Labs – Community Guidelines**

**Effective Date:** April 2025

At Pure Peptide Labs, we are committed to maintaining a professional, legally compliant, and respectful environment for discussion, feedback, and research-focused engagement. These Community Guidelines apply to all user-generated content posted on or linked to our website, social media accounts, product review platforms, third-party forums, and other online channels where our brand is mentioned.

* * *

### 1\. Research-Only Environment

Pure Peptide Labs sells products for **laboratory research use only**. Discussions or suggestions implying or promoting **human or animal use** , including references to injection, dosing, self-experimentation, or medical benefits, are strictly prohibited.

**Examples of Prohibited Content:**

  * “This worked great for my weight loss”

  * “I injected this and saw results in a week”

  * “How much should I use per kg of body weight?”




Posts like these violate our Terms of Service and may be removed or reported.

* * *

### 2\. No Medical or Dosage Advice

Users may **not post** or solicit medical advice, dosing protocols, titration schedules, reconstitution tips, or compound stacking information. Pure Peptide Labs does not provide guidance on any use-related matter, and any appearance of endorsement or support is inaccurate.

If a user asks such questions, we encourage others to respond with:

> “These products are for research only. No usage guidance is allowed here.”

* * *

### 3\. No Affiliation with Content Creators or Influencers

Pure Peptide Labs does **not sponsor, affiliate, or compensate** influencers, bloggers, or YouTubers. Any third-party content that suggests otherwise is unauthorized and not reflective of our practices or policies.

If you see a video or post implying Pure Peptide Labs encourages or enables human use—please report it to us.

* * *

### 4\. Respectful and Professional Conduct

We welcome scientific discussion, constructive criticism, and user reviews—so long as they remain respectful and research-focused. The following behaviors are not permitted:

  * Harassment, personal attacks, or slurs

  * False claims or misleading product use statements

  * Spam, promotional links, or commercial solicitation




Violators may be banned or reported to platform moderators.

* * *

### 5\. Our Right to Moderate

We reserve the right to:

  * Remove, hide, or report any comment or review

  * Block or restrict users who violate these guidelines

  * Cooperate with platform hosts, regulatory agencies, or law enforcement if unlawful content is posted




* * *

### 6\. Reporting Violations

To report community guideline violations, please contact us through our [Contact Page] or email support@purepeptidelabs.com with a link and brief description.

* * *

### Reminder: Legal Purpose of Products

All products sold by Pure Peptide Labs are strictly intended for **non-clinical research and laboratory use only**. Any statements or posts suggesting otherwise are misleading, and may result in account restrictions or other actions.

Let’s keep this space professional, compliant, and research-driven.

* * *

**Thank you for supporting safe and responsible research.**
