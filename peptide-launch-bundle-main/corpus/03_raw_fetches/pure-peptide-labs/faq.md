#  FAQ 

**Pure Peptide Labs – Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)**

**Effective Date:** April 2025

At Pure Peptide Labs, we understand that clear, accurate information is key to a smooth and compliant customer experience. Below are answers to the most common questions we receive—always framed within the boundaries of research use only.

* * *

### 🧪 Product & Research Use Questions

**Are your products for human or animal use?**  
**No.** All of our products are sold strictly for **laboratory research and analytical use only**. They are not approved for human or veterinary consumption, injection, or topical use.

**Are these products FDA approved?**  
No. Our products are classified as **Research Use Only (RUO)** materials and are **not approved by the FDA** for diagnostic, therapeutic, or clinical use.

**Do you provide reconstitution or dosing instructions?**  
**Absolutely not.** We do not provide any instructions or guidance related to dosage, reconstitution, injection, or use. Doing so would violate our Terms of Service and compliance standards.

**What does "Research Use Only" mean?**  
"Research Use Only" (RUO) refers to products intended **solely for use in laboratory settings** , such as in vitro testing or analytical research. These products are **not intended for use in or on humans or animals**.

**How are your products tested?**  
All compounds are tested via **High-Performance Liquid Chromatography (HPLC)** and **Mass Spectrometry (MS)**. Certificates of Analysis (COAs) are provided for transparency and verification.

* * *

### 📦 Shipping & Order Questions

**When will my order ship?**  
Orders placed before **1:00 PM CST on business days** are typically shipped the **same day**. Orders after this cutoff are shipped the next business day.

**Which carriers do you use?**  
We ship with **UPS and USPS**. Please note: **UPS does not deliver to PO Boxes**. Select USPS at checkout if using a PO Box.

**Do you offer expedited shipping?**  
Yes. We offer **UPS Next Day Air, Second Day Air, and USPS Express options**. These can be selected at checkout.

**How do I track my shipment?**  
Once your order ships, you’ll receive a confirmation email with tracking information. You can track shipments directly via the carrier’s website.

* * *

### 💳 Payment & Return Questions

**How do I pay for my order?**  
We use **Square Invoicing** as our secure payment method. After placing an order, you will receive an invoice via email directly from Square. Payment is due by **end of day**. If unpaid, the invoice will **automatically cancel** , and the order will not be fulfilled.

**What payment methods do you accept?**  
Square Invoicing accepts all major credit and debit cards.

**Can I return a product?**  
Yes. We offer a **30-day money-back guarantee** on unopened, untampered products. Please refer to our Return Policy for full details.

**How do I request a refund?**  
Email us at **support@purepeptidelabs.com** with your order number and reason for return. We will guide you through the return process.

* * *

### 💝 Philanthropy

Yes! A portion of our **monthly sales revenue** is proudly donated to **organizations dedicated to childhood cancer research and support**. We believe in **giving back** , and contributing to **life-saving efforts** for children and families facing serious illness is a cause **close to our hearts**.

Yes. Every order you place contributes to our monthly donation to St. Jude. Thank you for helping us support pediatric cancer research and compassionate care.

### 📜 Legal & Compliance

**Do you allow reviews or social media discussions about dosage or usage?**  
No. Per our Community Guidelines, any content suggesting human use, dosing, or injection will be removed or reported. We are not responsible for third-party content and do not endorse it.

**Can I use your products in clinical trials?**  
No. Our products are **not intended for diagnostic, therapeutic, or clinical use**. They are for **research use only** and not to be administered to living beings.

**Where can I find your legal and compliance policies?**  
Visit our Policies Page for a full breakdown of our Terms of Service, Shipping Policy, Compliance Overview, and more.
