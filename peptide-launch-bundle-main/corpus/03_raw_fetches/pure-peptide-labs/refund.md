# Refund policy

**Return Policy**

**Effective Date:** April 2025

At Pure Peptide Labs LLC, customer satisfaction and product quality are our top priorities. We stand behind the integrity of our research compounds and offer a fair return process to protect both your confidence and our compliance standards.

* * *

### 1\. 30-Day Satisfaction Guarantee

We offer a **30-day money-back guarantee** on all eligible orders. If you are not satisfied with your purchase, you may return the item(s) **within 30 days** of the original purchase date for a full refund.

* * *

### 2\. Return Eligibility Requirements

To qualify for a refund:

  * Items must be **returned unopened, unused, and in original condition**

  * Items must be received within **30 days of the purchase date**

  * Returned products must be in their **original packaging with security seals intact**




Due to the sensitive nature of research chemicals, **opened, tampered, or used products are not eligible for return or refund**.

* * *

### 3\. How to Initiate a Return

To start a return, please contact us at: 📧 **support@purepeptidelabs.com**

Provide your order number and reason for return. Our team will respond with return instructions.

Return shipments should be addressed to:

**Pure Peptide Labs LLC**  
720 S Bell Blvd  
Building 10, Suite C  
Cedar Park, TX 78613

* * *

### 4\. Refund Processing

Once your return is received and inspected:

  * You will be notified by email of your refund status

  * Approved refunds are issued to the **original form of payment** within **5–7 business days**




Shipping charges for expedited services are **non-refundable** , unless the return is due to an error on our part.

* * *

### 5\. Damaged or Incorrect Items

If you receive a defective, damaged, or incorrect product, please email us **within 48 hours** of delivery with clear photos. We will work quickly to resolve the issue and, if necessary, ship a replacement at no cost to you.

* * *

### 6\. Final Notes

Pure Peptide Labs reserves the right to:

  * Refuse returns that do not meet the eligibility criteria

  * Deny returns suspected of policy abuse or fraudulent activity




We appreciate your understanding and cooperation as we maintain strict standards around product quality and compliance.
