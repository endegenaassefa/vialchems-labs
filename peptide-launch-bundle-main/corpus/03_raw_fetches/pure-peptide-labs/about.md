#  💡 Why We Started 

  


Hey there 👋 We’re **Mark and Lisa** — a husband and wife team from Texas with a shared love for science, small business, and giving back ❤️

We started **Pure Peptide Labs** in a quiet study room at our hometown library, driven by one big idea: to make it easier for researchers to access **honest, high-purity peptides** without overpriced markups or shady sourcing.

From day one, we’ve done everything ourselves — from testing and packaging to customer support and shipping. Every vial we send out is packed with care, because this isn’t just a business to us — it’s personal.

And just as important as quality is **purpose**. A portion of every sale goes to support the fight against **childhood cancer** , a cause that’s close to our hearts 💛

  
After seeing so many overpriced, under-tested, or sketchy peptide vendors flooding the market, we decided to start something better — something honest.

Pure Peptide Labs was created to bring **real researchers** like you:

  * **Top-quality peptides** tested via HPLC & Mass Spectrometry
  * **> 99% purity** with **Certificates of Analysis** included
  * And no ridiculous markups or vague sourcing



We’re proud to be a **U.S.-based small business** , and we treat every order like it’s going to someone we know — because sometimes, it is.
