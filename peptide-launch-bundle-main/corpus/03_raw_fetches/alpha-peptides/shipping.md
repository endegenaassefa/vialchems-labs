# Shipping & Refund Policy

This Shipping & Refund Policy outlines how Alpha Peptides handles order processing, delivery, cancellations, and refund eligibility. All purchases made through alpha-peptides.com are subject to this policy. 

**Effective Date:** July 1, 2025  |  **Last Updated:** April 1, 2026 

**Research Use Only:** All products sold by Alpha Peptides are intended strictly for laboratory and scientific research use. They are not for human consumption, veterinary use, or medical application. 

## 1) Overview

Alpha Peptides (operated by ALPHAPEP LLC) is committed to operational transparency and consistent fulfillment. This policy governs all purchases made through alpha-peptides.com and applies to shipping, delivery issues, cancellations, returns, and refund eligibility. 

By placing an order on our website, you acknowledge that you have read and agree to this Shipping & Refund Policy in its entirety. 

## 2) Shipping Policy

### 2.1 Shipping Coverage

We currently ship within the United States only. Orders requesting international delivery must receive prior written approval from our team before checkout. 

### 2.2 Processing Time

  * Your order will be shipped between 24 and 48 hours after payment confirmation.
  * Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding federal holidays.
  * High-volume periods, order verification reviews, or unforeseen circumstances may extend processing time. You will be notified if your order is delayed beyond 3 business days.



### 2.3 Shipping Methods & Transit Time

Transit times vary depending on the carrier and shipping method selected at checkout. Standard shipping typically takes 3–7 business days after dispatch. Once fulfilled, tracking information will be provided via email when available. 

### 2.4 Shipping Costs

Shipping rates are calculated at checkout based on order weight, dimensions, and destination. Any applicable shipping charges are displayed before payment is finalized. 

### 2.5 Address Accuracy

Customers are responsible for ensuring the accuracy of their shipping address at the time of checkout. Alpha Peptides is not responsible for delays, misdeliveries, or returned shipments caused by incorrect or incomplete address information. If a package is returned to us due to an address error, the customer is responsible for any additional shipping costs to reship the order. 

### 2.6 Lost, Delayed, or Stolen Packages

  * If tracking shows a delay, please allow reasonable additional carrier time (up to 5 business days beyond the estimated delivery date).
  * If marked as delivered but not received, check with neighbors and contact the carrier directly.
  * If confirmed lost in transit by the carrier, contact us within 14 days of the expected delivery date to discuss next steps. We will work with the carrier to file a claim and, if approved, issue a replacement or refund at our discretion.
  * Alpha Peptides is not responsible for packages stolen after confirmed delivery.



### 2.7 Damaged Packages

Claims for damaged shipments must be submitted within 7 days of delivery by emailing [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection). Please include clear photographs of the outer packaging, the shipping label, and the damaged product. We will review all claims within 3 business days and respond with a resolution. 

## 3) Return Policy

### 3.1 No Returns Accepted — All Sales Are Final

Due to the nature of research-grade laboratory products and strict quality-control requirements, **Alpha Peptides does not accept returns and all sales are final**. Once an order has been shipped, it cannot be returned for a refund or exchange. 

This policy exists because research products must maintain documented chain-of-custody and storage conditions. Once a product leaves our facility, we cannot verify that proper handling and storage protocols were maintained, and therefore cannot resell or restock the item. 

### 3.2 No Exchanges

We do not offer product exchanges. If you wish to order a different product, please place a new order through our website. 

## 4) Refund Eligibility & Process

### 4.1 Limited Refund Exceptions

While all sales are final, Alpha Peptides will review refund requests in the following limited circumstances only: 

  * **Wrong item shipped:** If we sent a product different from what you ordered due to a fulfillment error on our part.
  * **Confirmed shipping damage:** If the product arrived damaged during transit and you have submitted photographic evidence within 7 days of delivery.
  * **Pre-fulfillment cancellation:** If you cancel your order before it has been processed and shipped.



### 4.2 How to Request a Refund

To request a refund for an eligible situation:

  * **Step 1:** Email [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection) with the subject line “Refund Request — Order #[your order number]”.
  * **Step 2:** Include your full name, order number, a description of the issue, and any supporting photographs (required for damage claims).
  * **Step 3:** Our team will review your request and respond within 3 business days with a decision.



### 4.3 Refund Processing

If your refund request is approved, the refund will be issued to your original payment method. Please allow 5–10 business days for the refund to appear on your statement, depending on your financial institution. You will receive an email confirmation when the refund has been processed. 

### 4.4 Return Shipping for Eligible Claims

In cases where a return shipment is required (e.g., wrong item sent), Alpha Peptides will provide a prepaid return shipping label at no cost to the customer. Do not ship any product back to us without prior authorization and a return shipping label from our team. 

### 4.5 Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will not be issued for:

  * Change of mind or buyer’s remorse after purchase
  * Products that have been opened, used, or tampered with (except for damage claims)
  * Orders shipped to an incorrect address provided by the customer
  * Packages confirmed as delivered by the carrier
  * Requests submitted more than 14 days after delivery



## 5) Order Cancellations

Orders may be canceled free of charge only before processing or fulfillment has begun. To cancel an order, email [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection) with your order number as soon as possible. 

Once an order has been processed and tracking information has been issued, cancellation is no longer possible and the order is subject to our All Sales Final policy above.
