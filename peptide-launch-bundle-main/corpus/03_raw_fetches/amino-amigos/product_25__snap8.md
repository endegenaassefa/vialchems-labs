Description

[COA SNAP8](https://aminoamigos.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/COA_SNAP-8_2025-06-21_20250714-2.pdf)

SNAP-8 (Acetyl Octapeptide-3) is a synthetic octapeptide designed as an extension of the SNAP-25 substrate domain, a component involved in the SNARE complex, which regulates vesicle docking and neurotransmitter release.

In vitro studies suggest that SNAP-8 may influence the formation of the SNARE complex, thereby modulating the release of neurotransmitters associated with muscle contraction signaling. Due to this mechanism, it is frequently explored in research related to neuromuscular signaling pathways and cosmetic peptide applications.
