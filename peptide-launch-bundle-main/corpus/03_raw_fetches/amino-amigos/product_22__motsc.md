  1. **Metabolic Homeostasis** : MOTS-c has been investigated for its role in promoting metabolic homeostasis. It is believed to play a regulatory role in energy metabolism, mitochondrial function, and insulin sensitivity.
  2. **Longevity** : Some studies suggest that MOTS-c may have implications for longevity. By influencing cellular processes and metabolic pathways, it is theorized to contribute to overall health and longevity.
  3. **Exercise Capacity** : Research has indicated that MOTS-c may improve exercise capacity. This could be attributed to its potential impact on mitochondrial function, which is essential for energy production during physical activity.
  4. **Obesity and Insulin Resistance** : MOTS-c has been explored for its potential to reduce obesity and improve insulin sensitivity. It may have an impact on adipose tissue and metabolic pathways related to insulin action.
  5. **Osteoporosis** : There is some research suggesting that MOTS-c might have beneficial effects on bone health, potentially addressing issues related to osteoporosis. The exact mechanisms and implications in this context may require further investigation.
