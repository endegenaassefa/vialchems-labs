#### Description

AOD9604 is a modified version of the growth hormone (GH) fragment 176-191, derived from human growth hormone (hGH). It incorporates a di-sulfide bridge and a tyrosine residue. Originally developed for its lipolytic (fat-burning) properties, AOD9604 has shown promise in various areas based on research up to January 2022.

Key Points:

  1. **Lipolysis** : AOD9604 stimulates the breakdown of fat, demonstrating potential as a weight loss or fat reduction agent.
  2. **Cardiovascular Benefits** : Studies suggest that AOD9604 may have protective effects on the heart, indicating potential benefits for heart disease.
  3. **Musculoskeletal Health** : AOD9604 has been explored for its potential in promoting cartilage repair and addressing issues related to osteoarthritis, suggesting a role in musculoskeletal health.
  4. **Metabolic Syndrome** : Research indicates that AOD9604 may modulate lipid metabolism and improve metabolic parameters, making it a subject of interest in addressing metabolic syndrome.
