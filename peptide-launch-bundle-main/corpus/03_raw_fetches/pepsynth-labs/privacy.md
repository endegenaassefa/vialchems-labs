## Privacy Policy

At **Pepsynth Labs** , we value your privacy and are dedicated to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you visit or make a purchase from our website.

* * *

#### **1\. Information We Collect**

When you interact with our site, we may collect the following types of information:

  * **Personal Information:** Name, billing/shipping address, email address, phone number, and payment details.

  * **Account Information:** Login credentials if you create an account on our website.

  * **Order Information:** Details about purchases, returns, and customer service inquiries.

  * **Technical Information:** IP address, browser type, device information, and usage data (through cookies or analytics tools).




* * *

#### **2\. How We Use Your Information**

We use your information to:

  * Process and fulfill orders.

  * Communicate with you regarding your orders, products, or services.

  * Improve our website, performance, and customer experience.

  * Maintain compliance with legal and regulatory obligations.

  * Send marketing or promotional materials (only if you opt in).




* * *

#### **3\. Sharing of Information**

We never sell or rent your personal information. However, we may share it with:

  * **Service Providers:** Payment processors, shipping companies, IT support, and website hosting partners.

  * **Legal & Compliance:** To comply with applicable laws, regulations, or lawful requests.

  * **Business Transfers:** In case of a merger, acquisition, or other business restructuring.




* * *

#### **4\. Cookies & Tracking Technologies**

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to:

  * Enable core website functionality (shopping cart, login, checkout).

  * Analyze performance and improve user experience.

  * Personalize your browsing experience and product recommendations.




You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, some features may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

* * *

#### **5\. Data Security**

We use industry-standard security practices — including encryption, secure servers, and firewalls — to protect your data. While we strive to ensure the safety of your information, no online platform can guarantee absolute security.

* * *

#### **6\. Your Rights**

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  * Access the personal data we hold about you.

  * Request corrections or updates.

  * Request deletion of your information.

  * Unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time.




To exercise your rights, please contact us at **support@pepsynthlabs.com**.

* * *

#### **7\. Data Retention**

We retain your data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by law.

* * *

#### **8\. Third-Party Links**

Our website may include links to external websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices and encourage you to review their individual privacy policies.

* * *

#### **9\. Changes to This Policy**

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The latest version will always be available on this page with an updated “Effective Date.”

* * *

#### **10\. Contact Us**

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us:

**support@pepsynthlabs.com**
