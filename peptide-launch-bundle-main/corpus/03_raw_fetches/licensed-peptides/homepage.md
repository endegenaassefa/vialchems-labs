## The 6-Point Quality Standard That Others Don't Use

Independent verification at every stage. Transparent results. No exceptions. Every lot is tested by third-party accredited laboratories before it leaves our facility.

[1]

### HPLC Purity Analysis

High-Performance Liquid Chromatography confirms the compound makes up 99% or more of total content. No dilution, no shortcuts - the full separation profile is documented and published.

[2]

### Mass Spectrometry Identity

Confirms the exact molecular identity and sequence accuracy of each peptide. Protects against mislabeling and ensures you receive precisely what was ordered.

[3]

### Endotoxin Screening

Limulus Amebocyte Lysate (LAL) testing detects bacterial endotoxins that cause immune reactions - even when the compound looks completely clean. Most suppliers skip this step.

[4]

### Sterility Verification

Performed by an ISO/IEC 17025:2017 accredited laboratory. Confirms no viable microorganisms are present - going beyond sterile filtration alone.

[5]

### Content Quantification

Verifies the stated weight and concentration is accurate. Every milligram you receive is what the vial claims - no underdosing, no overpromising.

[6]

### Appearance & Physical Check

Visual inspection and lyophilization quality. Verifies proper freeze-drying, packaging integrity, and that no physical contamination occurred during production.
