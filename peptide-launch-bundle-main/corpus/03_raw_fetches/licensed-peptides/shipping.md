  * If incorrect information is provided, we are not responsible for lost shipments
  * Address changes must be requested within 3 hours of placing the order



#### 9\. Damaged or Missing Orders

If your order arrives damaged, incorrect, or is missing:

  * You must contact us immediately upon delivery
  * Proof (photos, order details) may be required
  * Claims are handled in accordance with our Return & Refund Policy
