#  Frequently Asked Questions 

Below are answers to common questions regarding ordering, handling, and research material information. 

What are research peptides?

Research peptides are laboratory materials used for analytical and scientific study. They are supplied for controlled experimental environments only.

Are these products for human consumption?

No. All materials offered by PeakBiologic are strictly intended for laboratory and analytical research applications.

How should materials be stored?

Lyophilized materials should be stored in a cool, dry environment and protected from light. Long-term storage is typically performed at low temperatures. Avoid repeated freeze-thaw cycles after reconstitution.

When will my order ship?

Orders are processed and shipped five days per week. Tracking information is provided once the shipment is prepared and dispatched.

Can I return a product?

Returns are accepted within 15 days of delivery if the item remains unopened and in its original packaging. Return authorization must be obtained before sending items back.

What if my package is delayed?

Carrier transit times may vary. Once shipped, delivery speed is controlled by the shipping provider. Tracking will update as the package moves through the carrier network.

Do you guarantee purity?

All materials are produced to research-grade standards and undergo quality control procedures prior to release.

What payment methods are accepted?

Available payment methods are displayed during checkout and may vary based on order location and processing availability.

How can I contact support?

You can reach our support team via email or phone during listed service hours. Please include your order number when contacting support.
