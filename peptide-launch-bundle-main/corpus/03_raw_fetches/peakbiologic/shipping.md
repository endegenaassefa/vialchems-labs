#  Shipping & Returns Policy 

PeakBiologic is committed to fast processing, careful packaging, and dependable delivery. Please review the information below regarding order handling and return eligibility. 

## Shipping Information

Orders are processed and shipped **five days per week**. Once an order is prepared and dispatched, a shipping confirmation will be issued when tracking becomes available. 

  * Orders ship on business days only
  * Processing occurs in the order received
  * Tracking details provided after dispatch
  * Delivery times depend on carrier transit speed



Please ensure shipping information is accurate at checkout. PeakBiologic is not responsible for delivery delays caused by incorrect addresses or carrier disruptions. 

## Returns & Eligibility

We stand behind the quality and handling of our materials. Return requests must meet the following conditions: 

  * Request submitted within **15 days** of delivery
  * Product must remain unopened and unused
  * Original packaging must be intact
  * Return authorization required prior to shipping back



Items returned without authorization or outside the eligibility window may not be accepted. 

## Return Process

  1. Contact support to request return approval
  2. Receive return instructions
  3. Ship the unopened product back
  4. Inspection and processing completed



Approved returns are processed after inspection confirms eligibility requirements have been met.
