# Custom Peptide Synthesis

### Custom Peptide Synthesis Services Tailored to Your Research Needs

 

At Phoenix Pharmaceuticals, we specialize in high-quality custom peptide synthesis to support critical research in academia, healthcare, and biotechnology. With over 30 years of peptide manufacturing expertise, we deliver precise, reliable, and scalable solutions tailored to your unique research requirements.

 

### About our Custom Peptide Synthesis Capabilities

 

Our custom peptide synthesis service is trusted by leading universities, hospitals, and scientific research institutions worldwide. We offer:

  * **Peptide Purity Levels Up to 98%****  
** Whether you require crude peptides for screening or high-purity peptides for in vivo studies, we deliver exactly what your protocol demands.
  * **Wide Range of Modifications****  
** From phosphorylation and cyclization to fluorescent labeling, PEGylation Reagents and PEG Spacers, we offer extensive chemical modifications to meet your experimental goals.
  * **Ability to Synthesize Difficult Peptide Sequences****  
** Our in-house team synthesizes diverse peptides, including insoluble trimers to peptides with multiple disulfide bridges and linked peptide chains.
  * **Scalable Synthesis from mg to kg****  
** Whether you’re in early-stage research or preclinical development, we scale our synthesis to match your project needs without compromising quality. Minimum quantity for a custom peptide is 1mg.
  * **Rigorous Quality Control****  
** Every peptide is accompanied by a certificate of analysis, including HPLC and mass spectrometry data to ensure structural integrity and batch consistency.
  * **Fast Turnaround Times****  
** We understand the time-sensitive nature of scientific research. Our streamlined peptide synthesis process ensures efficient delivery—without sacrificing precision. Delivery time for custom peptide synthesis orders depend on the sequence and difficulty. We typically deliver in 2-3 weeks.



### Applications We Support

 

Our custom peptide synthesis services are ideal for a wide variety of research applications, including:

  * Receptor binding and signaling studies
  * Vaccine development
  * Enzyme substrate design
  * Immunological assays (e.g., epitope mapping, antibody production)
  * Proteomics and biomarker discovery
