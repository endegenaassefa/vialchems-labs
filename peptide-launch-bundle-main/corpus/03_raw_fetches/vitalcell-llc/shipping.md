## Shipping Policy

**Effective Date: 9/14/2025**

We aim to ship all orders promptly while ensuring product quality and integrity.

### **1\. Processing Times**

  * Orders are typically processed within **1–2 business days**.

  * Orders placed on weekends or holidays will be processed on the next business day.




### **2\. Shipping Methods & Costs**

  * We offer **standard and expedited shipping** options.

  * Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on destination and delivery speed.




### **3\. Order Tracking**

Once your order ships, you will receive a **tracking number** via email for real-time updates.

### **4\. International Shipping**

  * We ship to most countries; however, customers are responsible for any **customs duties, taxes, or import restrictions** in their country.




### **5\. Delayed or Lost Packages**

While rare, we are not liable for shipping delays caused by carriers, weather conditions, or customs inspections.
