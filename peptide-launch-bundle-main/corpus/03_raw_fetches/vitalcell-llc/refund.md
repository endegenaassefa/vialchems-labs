## Refund Policy

**Effective Date: 9/14/2025**

At VitalCell Peptides, customer satisfaction and product integrity are our top priorities.

### **1\. Eligibility for Refunds**

  * Refund requests must be made within **7 days** of receiving your order.

  * Products must be **unused, unopened, and in original packaging**.




### **2\. Non-Refundable Items**

  * Any product that has been opened or used

  * Products purchased under promotional discounts or clearance sales

  * Digital products or downloadable materials




### **3\. Refund Process**

  1. Contact our support team on the [contact us](https://vitalcellpeptides.com/contact/) page by filling out a form and including your order number.

  2. Our team will review your request and provide return instructions if eligible.

  3. Refunds will be processed within **5–7 business days** after receiving returned items.




### **4\. Damaged or Incorrect Items**

If you receive a damaged or incorrect product, contact us immediately with photos. We will arrange a replacement or full refund at no additional cost.
