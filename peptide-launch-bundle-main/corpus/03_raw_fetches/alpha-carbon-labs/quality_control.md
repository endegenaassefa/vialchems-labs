# Quality Control & Testing

Every peptide we produce undergoes rigorous dual purity verification to ensure the highest standards for scientific research.

## Dual Purity Verification

All our peptides undergo two independent purity verification tests using state-of-the-art analytical methods, ensuring consistency and reliability for your research applications.

### HPLC Analysis

High-Performance Liquid Chromatography is performed twice by independent technicians to verify peptide purity and detect any impurities or degradation products.

### Mass Spectrometry

Advanced MS/MS techniques confirm molecular weight and structural identity, ensuring you receive exactly what you ordered with verified composition.

### Amino Acid Analysis

Comprehensive amino acid sequencing validates the correct peptide structure and confirms the absence of substitutions or deletions.

### Endotoxin Testing

All peptides undergo LAL (Limulus Amebocyte Lysate) testing to ensure endotoxin levels are below acceptable limits for research use.

### Sterility Testing

Rigorous sterility protocols and testing ensure that all peptides are free from microbial contamination and safe for research applications.

### Stability Testing

Long-term and accelerated stability studies confirm proper storage conditions and expected shelf life for optimal research outcomes.

## Certificate of Analysis (COA)

Each batch comes with a comprehensive Certificate of Analysis that includes:

  * Dual HPLC purity verification results (both independent tests)
  * Mass spectrometry data confirming molecular weight
  * Amino acid analysis results
  * Endotoxin testing results
  * Batch number and manufacturing date
  * Storage recommendations and expiration date



## Our Standards

We maintain strict adherence to Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) guidelines and operate in ISO-certified facilities. Our quality control team has decades of combined experience in peptide synthesis and analysis.

Every peptide is guaranteed to meet or exceed ≥99% purity as verified by our dual testing protocol. If a product doesn't meet our stringent standards, it never reaches our customers.
