# Return Policy

Last updated: May 7, 2026

## Return and Exchange Window

We accept returns and exchanges within **15 days** of the date you receive your order. To be eligible for a return or exchange, please contact our customer service team within this timeframe.

## How to Initiate a Return or Exchange

To initiate a return or exchange, please follow these steps:

  1. Contact our customer service team via our Contact Us page or email within 15 days of receiving your order.
  2. Provide your order number and reason for the return or exchange.
  3. Wait for return authorization and shipping instructions from our team.
  4. Ship the item(s) back using the provided instructions.



## Condition of Returned Items

Returned items should be in their original packaging and unused condition when possible. However, please note that due to the nature of our research products and our commitment to quality and safety, all returned or exchanged items will be disposed of in accordance with our policy on returned goods. Returned items will not be added back to our inventory.

## Policy on Returned Goods

To maintain the highest standards of product integrity and ensure the safety of our research community, Alpha Carbon Labs has a strict policy regarding returned merchandise. All returned or exchanged products are permanently discarded and will not be restocked or resold. This policy ensures that every product shipped from our facility meets our rigorous quality standards.

## Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your return, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund. If approved, your refund will be processed to your original payment method within 5-10 business days. Please note that depending on your financial institution, it may take additional time for the refund to appear in your account.

## Exchanges

If you would like to exchange an item for a different product, please indicate this when contacting our customer service team. Exchanges are subject to product availability. If the requested exchange item is unavailable, we will issue a refund for the returned product.

## Damaged or Defective Items

If you receive a damaged or defective item, please contact us immediately. We will provide instructions for returning the item and will ship a replacement or issue a full refund at no additional cost to you.

## Non-Returnable Items

Certain items may not be eligible for return, including custom synthesis orders or items that have been opened and used in a manner inconsistent with research purposes. Please contact us if you have questions about the eligibility of a specific item.
