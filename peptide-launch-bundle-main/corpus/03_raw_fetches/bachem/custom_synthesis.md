## Custom Peptide synthesis services

Get more from your research with custom Peptides that are manufactured for consistent and high-quality results. Whether your focus is life science, drug discovery, or in-vitro diagnostics, your needs lead the way. Backed by in-depth scientific expertise, state-of-the-art technologies and a rigorous quality management system, we deliver customized solutions fit for your demand. 

[ Contact us  ](https://www.bachem.com/contact/) [ Discover our locations  ](https://www.bachem.com/about-bachem/our-locations/)

##  Services tailored to your needs 

###  Non-GMP synthesis to the required scale and purity levels 

Bachem offers non-GMP synthesis from 5 mg to 100 g, delivering materials at the scale you need with purity levels ranging from 80% to 97%.

###  Chemical modifications according to customer specifications 

Customized chemical modifications, including biotinylation, clickable and dye‑labeled Peptides, diverse cyclization strategies, PEGylation, branching, and stabilizing modifications such as TFA removal to HCl, acetate, sodium, or ammonium.

###  Analytical services 

Analytical services include standard QC release testing using HPLC and MALDI‑MS, supported by an extended portfolio of advanced analyses such as net Peptide content (AAA, CHN), water and acid content, counter ion content, ESI‑MS/MS and endotoxin analysis.

###  Documentation 

Our documentation package includes a standard Analytical Data Sheet (ADS) with QC results, as well as additional documentation such as TSE/BSE certificates and a Medical Device File which can be provided upon request.

###  Aliquoting 

Precise aliquoting starting at 0.5 mg per vial with a +/- 2% accuracy to ensure consistent sample handling.

##  Chemical modifications for your Peptides 

##  Backbone & sequence-level modifications 

Changes to the peptide backbone or chirality that affect conformation, proteolysis and stability.

        * **Retro-inverso peptides**
        * **Incorporation of D-enantiomers and unusual amino acids**
        * **N‑methylated amino acids**
        * **Reduced peptide bonds**

##  Terminal (N-/C-terminus) functionalization 

Specific modifications at the peptide ends.

        * **Acetylation, formylation, acylation** _(typically N-terminus; also side chains possible)_
        * **Amidation** _(C-terminus)_
        * **Biotinylation**
        * **Introduction of C-terminal alcohol and aldehyde moieties**
        * **Introduction of C-terminal ester and thioester groups**

##  Side-chain post-translational-type modifications 

Natural PTM mimics and small-group chemical additions on side chains.

        * **Phosphorylation and sulfation**
        * **Methylation, alkylation**
        * **Lipidation**
        * **Biotinylation**
        * **Maleimido peptides** _(maleimide functional handle for thiol conjugation)_
        * **Acylation** _(when applied to side chains)_

##  Cyclization & crosslinking 

Conformational constraints for stability or structure.

        * **Cyclizations, head to tail and side chain (lactam bridge, thioether)**
        * **Hydrocarbon-stapled peptides**
        * **Multiple disulfide bond formation**

##  Conjugation & ligation strategies 

Chemistries to build or join Peptides and attach payloads.

        * **Ligations** _(e.g., native chemical ligation, oxime, click-assisted ligations)_
        * **Conjugation to small molecules (including imaging agents)**
        * **Conjugation to polymers**
        * **Clickable Peptides** _(azide/alkyne handles, etc.)_
        * **Maleimido Peptides** (e.g. for thiol conjugation)

##  Labels, dyes & reporter moieties 

Fluorescent, chromophoric, and isotopic labels for detection/assays.

        * **Dye-labelled Peptides**
        * **Biotinylation**
        * **Incorporation of fluorophores or chromophores**
        * **Chromogenic and fluorogenic carboxypeptidase substrates**
        * **Labeling with Tide Fluor™ and Tide Quencher™**
        * **Labeling with stable isotopes**

##  Chelators & metal-handling groups 

For radiometal labeling, MRI, and metal coordination.

        * **Incorporation of chelating moieties such as DOTA, DPTA** _(often DTPA)_

##  Format & architecture 

Higher-order or non-linear peptide formats.

        * **Branched Peptides**

##  Stabilization strategies 

Approaches to increase half-life, protease resistance, and pharmacokinetics.

        * **PEGylation**
        * **N-methylated amino acids**
        * **Reduced Peptide bonds**
        * **Lactam/thioether cyclization**
        * **Hydrocarbon-stapled Peptides**
        * **Multiple disulfide bond formation**
