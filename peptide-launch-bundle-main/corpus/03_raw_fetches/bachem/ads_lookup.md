#  Analytical data sheet (ADS)

To download the appropriate Analytical Data Sheet as a pdf file, please enter the product number (without suffix) and the lot number of your product:

  


### Where can product and lot numbers be found?

        * on product labels
        * on invoices
        * on packing list

Click the images below to enlarge the examples.

[](https://www.bachem.com/knowledge-center/analytical-data-sheet-ads/attachment/invoice/)

[](https://www.bachem.com/knowledge-center/analytical-data-sheet-ads/attachment/delivery/)

[](https://www.bachem.com/knowledge-center/analytical-data-sheet-ads/attachment/ads/)

### What is an analytical data sheet (ADS)

The Analytical Data Sheet gives you a detailed summary of the quality of your product including the structure.

To find the Analytical Data Sheet for your product please use the product (without suffix) and origin batch number. The Lot number is the identification number assigned to the product.

Both numbers can be found on the product label.
