# Catalog Peptide Special Offers & Discounts

## Bachem offers discounts when ordering multiple vials or large quantities (bulk) of the same product:

#### **Bulk discount**

Large quantities are available at discounted prices. The minimum amount required for bulk discount is five fold the largest pack (i.e. if the largest pack is 5mg, quantities of at least 25mg qualify for bulk discount). Please [contact us](https://shop.bachem.com/product-inquiry/) for more details.

 

#### **Pack multi-vial discount**

By ordering two or more of the same product and pack size, you will receive the following discount:

  * **2 vials – 5% discount**
  * **3 to 5 vials – 10% discount**
  * **6 to 9 vials – 15% discount**
  * **10 or more, please inquire**
