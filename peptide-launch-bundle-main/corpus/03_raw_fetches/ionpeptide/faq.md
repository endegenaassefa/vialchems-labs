Product FAQ

## Product

Answers about research-use-only requirements, testing, product appearance, peptide form, storage, and temperature exposure. 

Research Use Only / Not for Human Consumption

Ion Peptide products are for laboratory research use only. They are not intended for human or animal consumption and are not designed to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. These products are not to be used as food, drugs, cosmetics, or household items. 

By accessing this website or purchasing from Ion Peptide, you agree that compliance with all applicable laws and regulations is your sole responsibility. No information provided should be considered medical advice. 

Please review our [Terms of Service](/terms-of-service/). 

Product Testing

Ion Peptide verifies every batch of every product through accredited, independent third-party laboratories. Because each compound requires specialized analytical methods, we work closely with qualified laboratories and PhD-level experts in analytical and organic chemistry to develop appropriate testing protocols. 

Analytical methods may include NMR, FTIR, HPLC, LC-MS, GC-MS, TLC, ICP-MS, gravimetry, UV-Vis spectroscopy, titration, melting point analysis, and organoleptic evaluation. Testing primarily focuses on purity, content, sterility, and endotoxin/LPS levels. 

Testing is conducted by Freedom Diagnostics in Franklin, Tennessee. Lab results are available at [Lab Results](https://listsitefast.com/lab-results/). 

Product Appearance Variations

Natural variations in color, texture, odor, or biochemical properties may occur and do not indicate reduced quality or purity. 

Peptide Form

Peptides are supplied in lyophilized powder form to enhance stability and extend shelf life during storage and transit. 

Peptide Top Colors

Product images may show white vial tops for consistency. Actual vial top colors may vary. 

Peptide Powder Appearance

Lyophilized peptides typically form a solid cake. Movement during transit may cause the cake to crack or appear uneven. This is an aesthetic variation and does not affect quality, potency, or purity. 

Certain peptides and proteins may appear larger due to buffering requirements. This is normal and does not indicate a variance in content. 

Storage Instructions

**General storage:** keep products in a cool, dry place away from heat, moisture, and direct sunlight. 

**Best practice:** refrigeration or freezing is recommended to maintain long-term stability. 

Extreme Temperature Exposure

All molecules degrade over time, but proper storage greatly slows this process. 

Low temperatures are generally safe and may cause temporary precipitation without increasing degradation. Prolonged high temperatures can accelerate degradation. 

Brief temperature excursions, such as short-term heat exposure or less than 24 hours at room temperature for frozen products, do not typically cause measurable degradation when the product is returned to proper storage, unless combined with exposure to oxygen, moisture, or sunlight.

## Shipping

Answers about shipping hours, processing times, tracking, shipping regions, order changes, delays, damaged items, and carrier issues. 

Shipping Hours

Monday–Friday: 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM EST. 

Saturdays: 1:00 PM – 3:00 PM EST. 

Processing and Shipping Times

Orders placed before 6:00 PM EST ship the same day. 

Orders ship via UPS Ground or FedEx 2-Day Express, with estimated delivery in 2–3 business days. 

All timeframes are estimates. Actual delivery times may vary due to weather, holidays, order volume, or courier delays beyond our control. 

Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you will receive a confirmation email with your tracking number. You may also log into your account to view tracking details at [My Account Orders](https://listsitefast.com/my-account/orders/). 

Shipping Regions

We currently ship to the United States and all US territories. 

Order Modifications and Cancellations

Due to our rapid shipping process, there is a very limited window to modify or cancel an order once it has been placed. While we will make reasonable efforts to accommodate changes or cancellations, they cannot be guaranteed. 

Please carefully review your shipping address before submitting your order. Incorrect address entries are considered user error and are not the responsibility of Ion Peptide. 

Late or Undelivered Orders

**Delivery Delays:** shipping delays caused by carriers are beyond our control. Shipping fees are non-refundable in cases of late delivery, as delays are the responsibility of the courier. 

**Package Marked as Delivered:** if tracking shows your package as delivered but you have not received it, please check with neighboring suites, building management, or reception areas. We also recommend contacting the courier’s local office to request GPS delivery confirmation and recipient details. 

If the courier confirms correct delivery, please allow up to one week for the package to surface before contacting us at [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#b8ebcdc8c8d7caccf8d1d7d6c8ddc8ccd1dcdd96dbd7d5). 

  * USPS: 1 (800) 275-8777
  * FedEx: 1 (800) 463-3339
  * UPS: 1 (800) 742-5877
  * DHL: 1 (800) 225-5345



Missing, Damaged, or Wrong Items

All orders are documented and photographed prior to shipment. Report any issues within 7 days of delivery. 

  * **Missing Items:** contact us immediately for verification and resolution.
  * **Damaged Item on Arrival:** contact us within 2 days of delivery with photos of both the package and damaged item.
  * **Wrong Items:** contact us immediately for verification and resolution.



Stolen, Lost, or Incorrect Address

  * **Stolen Packages:** Ion Peptide is not responsible for stolen packages.
  * **Lost Packages / Stuck in Transit:** Ion Peptide is not responsible for packages lost by carriers. Contact the carrier directly to locate your package.
  * **Incorrect Address:** Ion Peptide is not responsible for orders shipped to incorrectly entered addresses. If your order has not yet shipped, contact us immediately.

## Account and Orders

Answers about order statuses, order history, confirmation emails, lost passwords, returns, refund timing, zero-tolerance rules, and blacklisting. 

Order Status

**Payment Pending:** we are awaiting your payment. Your order did not process with BarterPay or Grid ACH. 

**On Hold:** we are awaiting or verifying your payment. If your order remains in this status, please submit a photo of your payment confirmation. This status is related to BarterPay and ACH. 

**Processing:** your payment has been confirmed, and your order is being prepared for shipment. Orders are typically dispatched within 24 hours, though processing times may vary. 

**Completed:** your order has been dispatched. You will receive an email with your tracking number shortly. 

**Cancelled:** we could not verify your payment, resulting in order cancellation. If you have made a payment and see this status, please send a photo of your payment confirmation. 

Order History

Order history is only available for registered users. Guest orders become visible once you create an account using the same email used at checkout. 

To view your order history:

  * Log in at [My Account](https://listsitefast.com/my-account/).
  * Go to the Orders tab at [My Orders](https://listsitefast.com/my-account/orders/).



For assistance, contact us with your billing details, full name, and email address. 

Confirmation Email

Order confirmation and shipping label emails may occasionally be missed. Please check all inboxes, including your spam folder. 

Lost Password

To reset your password, visit [Lost Password](https://listsitefast.com/my-account/lost-password). 

If the system does not recognize your email, you may have:

  * Used a different email address
  * Entered the email incorrectly
  * Checked out as a guest without creating an account



Return / Refund Policy

Returns are accepted within 30 days for unopened, unused items in original sealed packaging. Opened or used items are not eligible. 

Contact [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#33404643435c4147735a5c5d435643475a57561d505c5e) to start a return. Customers pay return shipping unless the error is ours. Expedited shipping fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error. 

Refunds are issued within 5–7 business days after inspection. 

Please review our [Refund / Return Policy](/refund-policy/). 

Zero-Tolerance Policy

Ion Peptide enforces a strict zero-tolerance policy for misuse or abuse of research materials. Any such activity will result in immediate blacklisting and permanent denial of services. 

Support is limited to inquiries about shipping, payments, and related matters. We do not assist with research-related tasks, as Ion Peptide is solely a supplier of research chemical materials. 

Blacklist

Ion Peptide supplies products exclusively for laboratory research purposes. To ensure regulatory compliance, we actively screen all orders. 

Individuals or entities suspected of intending to misuse our products will be permanently placed on a no-sell blacklist with no exceptions. We implement robust measures to prevent fraudulent orders.

## Payments

Answers about credit/debit card checkout, ACH bank payments, and checkout support. 

Credit/Debit Cards

We currently accept credit and debit cards at checkout. If you experience any issues during checkout, please contact us and we will provide a secure checkout link. 

Same-Day Bank Payment

We offer a 5% discount when paying via ACH bank transfer. Same-day transfers typically process within 24 hours but may take up to 72 hours, excluding weekends. 

Transfers initiated late on Friday may process the same day but usually do not clear until the following Monday when banks reopen. 

Orders will not ship until funds are received in our account, at which point order processing will begin. 

If you experience any issues with ACH payments, we can provide a secure bank payment link upon request. 

Issues with Check Out

Contact us via Live Chat if you experience any issues during checkout.
