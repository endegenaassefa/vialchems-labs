##### **Product Description**

**Selank (N-Acetyl)** is an **acetylated analog of Selank** , a synthetic heptapeptide derived from the natural immunomodulatory sequence **tuftsin**. The N-acetyl modification increases **peptide stability, resistance to enzymatic degradation** , and **blood-brain barrier permeability** , making it a preferred compound for **neurobehavioral and cognitive research**.

Researchers study N-Acetyl Selank for its effects on **GABAergic modulation, neurotransmitter regulation, BDNF expression, and stress resilience** , providing a valuable model for **anxiolytic, nootropic, and neuroprotective investigations**.

## Research

##### **Research Applications**

**Anxiety & Stress Modulation**

N-Acetyl Selank demonstrates **anxiolytic properties** without sedative effects in animal models, believed to act through modulation of **GABA receptor expression** and reduction of stress-induced gene activation [Filatova 2017].

**Cognitive Enhancement & Memory**

Studies indicate that Selank analogs enhance **BDNF expression** in hippocampal neurons, supporting memory formation and learning processes. The acetylated form provides longer activity and improved CNS penetration [Inozemtseva 2008].

**Immune & Neuro-Immune Research**

As a derivative of tuftsin, N-Acetyl Selank influences cytokine signaling and **balances pro- and anti-inflammatory markers** in stress-related immune dysregulation models [Volkova 2016].

**Neuroprotection & Stress Resilience**

Preclinical data show protection of neural tissue against oxidative damage and normalization of neurotransmitter levels (serotonin, dopamine) during chronic stress [Ashmarin 2005].

## Mechanism of Action

##### **Mechanism of Action**

  * **GABAergic Regulation:** Upregulates GABA-A receptor subunit expression, enhancing inhibitory neurotransmission [Filatova 2017].

  * **BDNF Upregulation:** Increases brain-derived neurotrophic factor, promoting synaptic plasticity [Inozemtseva 2008].

  * **Serotonin & Dopamine Balance:** Modulates monoaminergic signaling, improving mood and focus [Ashmarin 2005].

  * **Immunomodulatory Effects:** Regulates cytokine production, reducing IL-6 and TNF-α while increasing IL-10 [Volkova 2016].

  * **Enhanced Stability & CNS Absorption:** N-acetyl modification improves peptide half-life and CNS penetration for extended research efficacy.
