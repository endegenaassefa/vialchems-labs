## Overview

The products available on Ion Peptide are intended strictly for **research and laboratory use only**. By purchasing or using any products from this website, you acknowledge and agree that all materials are supplied solely for **scientific research, laboratory experimentation, or analytical purposes**. 

These products are **not intended for human or veterinary use** , and they are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. 

Research chemicals and peptides must be handled responsibly and only by **qualified professionals in appropriate laboratory environments**. 

**Important:** Ion Peptide products are supplied only for legitimate research use and must not be used outside lawful laboratory settings. 

02

## Research Use Only Designation

Products labeled **“Research Use Only” (RUO)** are chemical or biological compounds that are manufactured and supplied exclusively for laboratory research and analytical purposes. They are not approved as drugs, supplements, cosmetics, or medical treatments. 

RUO materials are intended for:

  * Laboratory research and experimentation
  * In-vitro studies
  * Analytical testing and validation
  * Scientific investigation and educational research



RUO products must **never be used for human or animal consumption** or for clinical or diagnostic purposes. 

03

## Regulatory Compliance

Ion Peptide operates in accordance with applicable research chemical regulations and industry standards. 

Research Use Only products:

  * Are **not approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA)** for medical use
  * Are **not intended for therapeutic, diagnostic, or clinical applications**
  * Must not be marketed or represented as treatments for disease
  * Must only be handled in appropriate research environments



Under U.S. regulations, any substance intended for diagnosis, treatment, or prevention of disease must undergo FDA approval before being marketed for those purposes. 

04

## Customer Responsibilities

By purchasing products from this website, the customer represents and warrants that: 

  1. They are **at least 21 years of age**.
  2. They are a **qualified researcher, laboratory professional, or institution** capable of handling research chemicals safely.
  3. The products will be used **solely for lawful laboratory research purposes**.
  4. They understand that the materials are **not for human or animal use**.
  5. They will comply with all applicable **local, state, and federal laws and regulations** governing research chemicals.



Customers agree that they assume full responsibility for the **safe handling, storage, and disposal** of all materials purchased from Ion Peptide.

## No Medical or Health Claims

Ion Peptide **does not provide medical advice** and does not make claims regarding the therapeutic benefits of any product. 

Information on this website is provided for **educational and research reference purposes only** and should not be interpreted as: 

  * Medical advice
  * Treatment recommendations
  * Dosage guidance for humans or animals



Any attempt to use research products outside of legitimate laboratory research is strictly prohibited.

  * Use of laboratory protective equipment
