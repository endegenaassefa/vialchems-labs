##### **Product Description**

**Semax/Selank** is a **balanced combination** of two synthetic heptapeptides — **Semax** , derived from **ACTH(4-10)** , and **Selank** , derived from **tuftsin**. This dual formulation provides a unique research tool for studying **cognitive enhancement, anxiolytic regulation, and neurotrophic activity** in preclinical models.

By combining Semax’s **neurotrophic and cognitive-enhancing effects** with Selank’s **GABAergic and immunomodulatory mechanisms** , this compound is used in studies on **stress resilience, learning and memory, neurotransmitter modulation, and neuroinflammation**.

## Research

##### **Research Applications**

**Cognitive Function & Neuroplasticity**

Semax enhances **BDNF (brain-derived neurotrophic factor)** and **NGF (nerve growth factor)** expression, supporting synaptic plasticity and memory formation. Combined with Selank, it provides synergistic effects on **learning, focus, and cognitive resilience** under stress [Inozemtseva 2008].

**Anxiolytic & GABAergic Modulation**

Selank acts on **GABAergic neurotransmission** and reduces stress-related overactivation of the hypothalamic–pituitary–adrenal (HPA) axis, producing anxiolytic effects without sedation [Filatova 2017].

**Neuroprotection & Stress Adaptation**

Both peptides contribute to neuroprotection by reducing **oxidative stress, neuroinflammation, and ischemic injury**. Studies show enhanced antioxidant enzyme activity and normalization of serotonin and dopamine turnover [Myasoedov 2009].

**Immunomodulation & Anti-Inflammatory Effects**

Selank’s tuftsin-derived structure influences cytokine expression, while Semax regulates immune–neuro interface pathways, providing dual modulation of **IL-6, TNF-α** , and **IL-10** [Volkova 2016].

## Mechanism of Action

**Mechanism of Action**

  * **BDNF & NGF Upregulation:** Semax increases neurotrophin gene expression, enhancing neuron growth and repair [Inozemtseva 2008].

  * **GABAergic Activity:** Selank modulates GABA-A receptor expression, promoting inhibitory tone and reducing anxiety [Filatova 2017].

  * **Neurotransmitter Regulation:** Both peptides normalize dopamine, serotonin, and norepinephrine levels in cortical and limbic regions [Ashmarin 2005].

  * **Anti-Inflammatory Modulation:** Suppresses pro-inflammatory cytokines and supports immune homeostasis [Volkova 2016].

  * **Neuroprotective Signaling:** Enhances antioxidant enzyme expression and reduces ischemic neuronal damage [Myasoedov 2009].
