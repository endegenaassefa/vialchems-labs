## How your shipment moves

This page is designed to help customers understand when orders ship, how tracking works, and where carrier limitations may affect delivery. 

01

### Order Placed

Your order is submitted through checkout with your selected shipping method.

02

### Cutoff Review

Orders placed before 5:00 PM EST on business days are typically handled the same day.

03

### Carrier Handoff

The order is prepared and handed to the selected or applicable shipping carrier.

04

### Tracking Sent

Tracking information is emailed once the order has shipped.

05

### Delivery

Delivery timing depends on the selected service, destination, carrier, and external conditions.

01

## Complimentary Shipping

We offer **free 2 Day FedEx / UPS** on all domestic U.S. orders over **$250+**. 

**Note:** Complimentary shipping applies to qualifying domestic U.S. orders and may depend on order value, available carrier service, and checkout conditions. 

02

## Same-Day Shipping Cutoff

Orders placed **before 5:00 PM EST** on business days are typically shipped the **same day**. Orders received after 5:00 PM EST are shipped on the next business day. 

We strive to process and fulfill orders as quickly as possible, but same-day shipping is **not guaranteed** during high-volume periods, holidays, or due to unforeseen logistics issues. 

**Important:** Same-day shipping depends on order timing, business-day processing, carrier availability, and operational volume. 

03

## Expedited Shipping Options

For faster delivery, customers may select upgraded shipping methods at checkout: 

  * **UPS / FedEx Express 2 Day**
  * **UPS / FedEx Next Day Delivery**



Shipping speed is determined by your chosen service and destination. All delivery timelines are estimates provided by the carrier. 

04

## Carrier Selection & PO Boxes

We ship using **UPS, FedEx, and United States Postal Service (USPS)**. 

**Important:**

  * **UPS cannot deliver to PO Box addresses.**
  * **FedEx cannot deliver to PO Box addresses.**



Please use a physical delivery address when selecting UPS or FedEx to avoid delays, returned shipments, or carrier delivery issues. 

05

## Tracking & Delivery Confirmation

Once your order is shipped, you will receive a tracking number via email. This number can be used to monitor your shipment’s progress directly through the carrier’s website. 

If tracking shows a package as delivered but you did not receive it, please contact the carrier directly before reaching out to our support team. 

06

## Shipping Liability Disclaimer

Ion Peptide is **not responsible** for: 

  * Carrier delays, errors, or lost/stolen packages
  * Delivery to incorrect addresses due to customer error
  * Weather-related delays or force majeure events
  * Customs delays or international transit issues, if applicable



While we will always assist in tracking or escalating issues with carriers, we cannot offer refunds for shipping problems beyond our control. 

**Important:** Carrier timelines, delivery scans, weather delays, and delivery exceptions may fall outside Ion Peptide’s direct control. 

07

## Address Accuracy & Order Review

Please ensure that all shipping information is accurate at checkout. Incorrect addresses may lead to delivery issues, return-to-sender fees, or restocking delays. 

If you realize there is an error, contact us **immediately** after placing your order. 

08

## Questions?

For questions related to your shipment, expedited services, carrier limitations, delivery status, or tracking issues, please contact Ion Peptide support. 

For the fastest support, include your order number, tracking number if available, shipping address, and a short description of the issue.
