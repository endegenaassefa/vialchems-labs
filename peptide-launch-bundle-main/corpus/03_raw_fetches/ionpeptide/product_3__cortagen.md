## Description

**Research-Grade Peptide Bioregulator (Cortical Tissue)**  
**Tagline:** _Cortical Gene Regulation Research_

* * *

##### **Product Description**

**Cortagen** is a **short synthetic peptide bioregulator** designed to model regulatory peptides associated with **cerebral cortical tissue**. As part of the **tissue-specific peptide bioregulator (cytomedins)** class, Cortagen is used to investigate how short peptides may **modulate gene expression, protein synthesis, and cellular homeostasis** within cortical neurons and supporting cells.

In laboratory research, Cortagen is used as a **reference peptide** for studies of **cortical gene regulation, neuronal metabolic signaling, aging-related molecular changes, and epigenetic interactions**. Cortagen’s effects are studied at the **gene-regulatory level** rather than through classical receptor pharmacology.

## Research

##### **Research Applications**

**Cortical Gene Expression Regulation**

Short peptide bioregulators are studied for their ability to influence **gene expression patterns** in specific tissues. Cortagen is used in models focused on **transcriptional control in cortical neurons and glia**.

**Neuronal Homeostasis & Aging Research**

Peptide bioregulators contribute to investigations of **age-associated molecular changes** in the cortex, including **protein synthesis balance and stress response pathways**.

**Epigenetic & Nuclear Signaling Research**

Due to their small size and nuclear accessibility, short peptides like Cortagen are used in research on **epigenetic mechanisms** and **gene modulation** in neural tissues.
