01

## 30-Day Satisfaction Guarantee

Eligible orders may be returned within **30 days of the original purchase date** for a full refund. This guarantee applies **only to products that remain unopened, unused, and in their original condition**. 

**Important:** The 30-day window does not override product condition requirements. Opened, used, or tampered items are not eligible for return. 

02

## Return Eligibility Requirements

To qualify for a refund:

  * Items must be **unopened, unused, and in original condition**.
  * Returns must be **received within 30 days** of the original purchase date.
  * Products must be in their **original packaging with intact security seals**.



**Important:** Due to the sensitive and research-specific nature of our products, **opened, tampered, or used items are ineligible for return or refund**. 

03

## How to Initiate a Return

To start a return:

  1. Fill out the return form at:   
[**https://ionpeptide.com/return/**](/return/)
  2. Ship the return to the instructed return address. 
  3. We will process the return once we receive the item. 



**Return Address:**

Return shipments must be addressed to the location provided in the instructions. Customers are responsible for shipping costs unless the return is due to our error. 

04

## Refund Processing

After receiving and inspecting your returned item(s):

  * You will be notified by email regarding the status of your refund.
  * Approved refunds will be issued to the **original form of payment** within **5–7 business days**.
  * **Shipping charges for expedited services are non-refundable** , except when the return is due to an error on our part.



Refund approval is based on inspection and confirmation that the returned item meets the stated eligibility requirements. 

05

## Damaged or Incorrect Items

If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect product: 

  * Email us **within 48 hours of delivery** with clear photographs of the item(s).
  * We will promptly review the claim and, if approved, **ship a replacement at no cost**.



Claims submitted after the 48-hour review window may be limited or denied depending on the circumstances. 

06

## Final Notes

ION Peptide reserves the right to:

  * Refuse returns that **do not meet eligibility criteria**.
  * Deny returns **suspected of policy abuse or fraudulent activity**.



We maintain strict standards to protect product quality, legal compliance, and research integrity. By purchasing from ION Peptide, you acknowledge and accept these terms.
