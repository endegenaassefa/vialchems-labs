## Description

##### **Bacteriostatic Water (H Brand)**

**Research-Grade Multi-Use Diluent**  
**Tagline:** _Trusted Brand for Laboratory Reconstitution_

##### **Product Description**

**Bacteriostatic Water (H Brand)** is a sterile, non-pyrogenic solution of **water for injection containing 0.9% benzyl alcohol** as a bacteriostatic preservative. Hospira’s pharmaceutical-grade manufacturing provides a high level of purity and reliability, making this a preferred choice for research laboratories that require **consistent quality and sterility** for compound reconstitution.

Researchers use Hospira Bacteriostatic Water to reconstitute **peptides, proteins, lyophilized drugs, and other research materials** , ensuring multi-use capability with minimal contamination risk.

> **For Laboratory and Scientific Research Use Only. Not for Human Consumption.**

* * *

##### **Why Researchers Choose Bacteriostatic Water (Hospira)**

  * **Pharmaceutical-Grade Quality:** Produced by Hospira (Pfizer), a trusted global manufacturer.

  * **Sterile, Non-Pyrogenic:** Meets USP standards for water for injection.

  * **Bacteriostatic Agent:** Contains 0.9% benzyl alcohol to inhibit bacterial growth.

  * **Multi-Use Vial:** Allows multiple withdrawals for up to 28 days under aseptic conditions.

  * **Batch Verified:** Sterility, pH, and benzyl alcohol concentration confirmed for every lot.

## Research

##### **Research Applications**

**Peptide & Protein Reconstitution**

Hospira Bacteriostatic Water is commonly used to reconstitute lyophilized peptides and proteins for in vitro and in vivo research experiments [1].

**Repeated Access Applications**

Its 0.9% benzyl alcohol content allows multiple aseptic withdrawals from the same vial over a 28-day period, minimizing waste and preserving sterility [2].

**Pharmaceutical Development & Formulation Studies**

Widely used as a vehicle in drug formulation and stability research requiring sterile aqueous diluents [3].
