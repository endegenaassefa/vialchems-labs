**Research-Grade Neuropeptide**

* * *

##### **Product Description**

**KSPTN**(also known as **Metastin** or **KISS1-derived peptide**) is a neuropeptide encoded by the **KISS1 gene** , and is the endogenous ligand for the **GPR54 (KISS1R) receptor**. It plays a crucial role in **puberty onset, hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal (HPG) axis regulation, and reproductive function**.

Researchers use KSPTN in preclinical and clinical models to study **GnRH secretion, LH/FSH release, fertility regulation, and neuroendocrine control of reproduction**. It is also studied for its role in **cancer metastasis suppression** and **energy homeostasis**.

> **For Laboratory and Scientific Research Use Only. Not for Human Consumption.**

* * *

##### **Why Researchers Choose KSPTN**

  * **Key Regulator of Reproduction:** Direct stimulator of GnRH neurons and pituitary gonadotropin release.

  * **Puberty Research Applications:** Studied for its role in initiating reproductive maturity.

  * **Cancer Metastasis Studies:** Inhibits tumor metastasis in several experimental models.

  * **Energy Balance & Neuroendocrine Links:** Explored in research on leptin and nutritional regulation of fertility.

  * **Batch Verified:** High-purity peptide (≥98%) for reproducible results.

## Research

##### **Research Applications**

**GnRH & Gonadotropin Release Studies**

KSPTN potently stimulates GnRH release from hypothalamic neurons, resulting in LH and FSH secretion from the anterior pituitary [1].

**Puberty Onset & Fertility Models**

Knockout studies show KSPTN signaling is essential for normal puberty and reproductive function [2].

**Cancer Research**

KSPTN was initially identified as a metastasis suppressor, making it relevant in oncology research [3].

**Metabolic-Endocrine Link**

Investigated as a mediator of leptin’s effects on fertility, linking energy status to reproductive capacity [4].

## Mechanism of Action

##### **Mechanism of Action (How KSPTN Works)**

  * **GPR54 (KISS1R) Activation:** KSPTN binds to its receptor on GnRH neurons, triggering GnRH release [Seminara 2003].

  * **GnRH Cascade Initiation:** Activates the hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal axis, leading to LH/FSH release and downstream sex steroid production [d’Anglemont de Tassigny 2007].

  * **Puberty Trigger:** Essential for the timing of puberty and reproductive maturation [Seminara 2003].

  * **Metastasis Suppression:** Inhibits tumor metastasis via modulation of cell adhesion and migration genes [Lee 1996].

  * **Metabolic Integration:** Communicates nutritional status to reproductive centers, coordinating fertility with energy availability [Castellano 2009].
