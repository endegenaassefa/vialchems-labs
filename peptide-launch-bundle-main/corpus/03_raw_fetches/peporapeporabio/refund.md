REFUNDS AND RETURNS POLICY  
Last updated: January 29, 2026

  1. REFUNDS & RETURNS POLICY

**ALL SALES FINAL – NO RETURNS – NO REFUNDS**

Due to the specialized, non-consumer nature of our products and strict quality control procedures,

all sales are final. Pepora does not accept returns or offer refunds once an order has been placed.

**DAMAGED, INCORRECT, OR INCOMPLETE ORDERS**

If an order arrives damaged, incomplete, or contains incorrect items, you must contact us within 48

hours of confirmed delivery with supporting evidence. After internal verification, Pepora may, at its

sole and absolute discretion, issue a replacement shipment. Refunds will not be issued under any

circumstances.

**ORDER CANCELLATIONS**

Orders may only be canceled before shipment. Once an order has shipped, it cannot be canceled,

refunded, or reversed.

Pepora does not offer refunds. Any exceptional accommodation, if granted, is voluntary,

case-specific, and does not establish precedent or waive this policy for any future transaction.

Customers are solely responsible for providing accurate billing, contact, and shipping information at

checkout. Pepora is not liable for delivery failures, losses, or delays resulting from incorrect or

incomplete information supplied by the customer.

Pepora reserves the right to contest any chargeback or payment dispute, refuse replacements,

refunds, or future service, and permanently ban customers who initiate chargebacks in bad faith or

attempt to circumvent this policy. Chargeback abuse constitutes a violation of our Terms of Service.

**ACKNOWLEDGMENT OF FINAL SALE**

By completing a purchase, the customer acknowledges that all sales are final, understands that

refunds and returns are not available, and accepts full responsibility for the order once submitted.
