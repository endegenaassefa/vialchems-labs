# Shipping Policy

SHIPPING POLICY  
Last updated: January 29, 2026

This Shipping Policy explains how orders placed with Pepora through peporabio.com are processed and delivered.

  1. SHIPPING AVAILABILITY  
We currently ship within the United States only.

  2. SHIPPING METHODS AND COSTS  
Available shipping options may include standard and expedited services provided by reputable carriers such as USPS and FedEx. Shipping methods and costs are displayed at checkout.

  3. FREE SHIPPING  
Free shipping is available on orders with a merchandise total of $150 USD or more. This threshold may change at any time without prior notice.

  4. ORDER PROCESSING  
Orders are processed after payment is successfully received and approved.




Orders placed and paid before 12:00 PM (local business time) on business days are typically processed the same day. Orders placed after this cutoff time, on weekends, or on U.S. federal holidays are processed on the next business day.

  5. DELIVERY ESTIMATES  
Delivery times are estimates provided by carriers and are not guaranteed. Pepora is not responsible for delays caused by carriers, weather conditions, or other factors beyond our control.

  6. TRACKING  
Once an order has shipped, you will receive a confirmation email with tracking information.

  7. ADDRESS ACCURACY  
Customers are responsible for providing accurate and complete shipping information at checkout. Orders shipped to incorrectly provided addresses may result in delays, additional fees, or non-delivery. Pepora is not liable for orders lost due to incorrect or incomplete address information provided by the customer.

  8. RETURNS AND REPLACEMENTS  
Due to the nature of our products, returns are not accepted.




Replacements may be issued only if:

  1. an incorrect item was shipped, or

  2. the order arrives damaged or incomplete.




Any issues must be reported within a reasonable time after delivery. Approved replacements are issued at Pepora’s discretion.

  9. PAYMENTS, PRICING, AND PROMOTIONS  
We accept payment methods displayed at checkout. Prices are listed in U.S. dollars unless otherwise stated. Promotions, discounts, and free-shipping thresholds may change without prior notice.

  10. ORDER VISIBILITY  
All purchases require a registered account. Customers may log in at any time to view order history, check order status, and update account information.

  11. PRIVACY AND SECURITY  
All transactions are processed using industry-standard security measures. Personal and order information is used solely for order fulfillment and customer support, in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

  12. CONTACT INFORMATION  
If you have questions about shipping, order status, or delivery issues, contact us at:  
Email: support@peporabio.com  
Website: peporabio.com
