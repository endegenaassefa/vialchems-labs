# Google Pharmaceutical Manufacturers Policy

URL: https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15597836 (accessed 2026-05-06)

## Permitted Advertisers — verbatim quote

"Bulk drug manufacturers, medical professional suppliers, and suppliers of antibodies, peptides, and compounds for commercial labs may advertise in the following locations only:" — restricted to **Canada and United States**.

## Required Certification

The policy requires pharmaceutical manufacturers to be certified by Google before serving ads. Specific certification types must be obtained based on business model (e.g., Online Pharmacy, Telemedicine).

## Application Process

Applicants must complete Google's online application form at the child account level (not MCC level), provide their Google Ads customer ID, certified domain, and all requested documentation.

## Important Restriction

The policy explicitly prohibits promotion of prescription opioid painkillers by all pharmaceutical manufacturers, regardless of location.

## KEY INTERPRETATION

This is the only Google Ads policy slot that explicitly mentions "peptides." The pathway exists — but it is gated to entities classified as "suppliers of antibodies, peptides, and compounds for commercial labs" (i.e. sell-to-lab B2B). It is NOT a path for "research peptide" e-commerce vendors selling RUO single vials to individual purchasers. Cross-reference: third-party accreditation (LegitScript / NABP / G2) is typically additionally required.
