## Critical disambiguation

Although this case is frequently surfaced in queries about "peptide paid advertising," **the platform is Meta (Facebook/Instagram), NOT Google Ads.** Verbatim: "Ad Platform: Meta (Facebook / Instagram)."

## Pre-engagement state (verbatim)

"The ad account had been fully suspended twice in six months."

Pre-engagement spend ~$26K/month at 3.5x ROAS.

## Post-engagement state

- Phase 3 peak: 8.58x ROAS at $150,000+/month stable
- Daily ceiling raised from $900 to $5,000+
- Zero suspensions in the post-engagement window

## Suspension-avoidance tactics (verbatim where quoted)

- Migration to "enterprise-level agency ad accounts"
- Removal of "23 specific trigger phrases from creative and landing pages"
- Elimination of customer testimonials referencing health outcomes
- 90%+ first-pass approval rate target before scaling
- "15-20% budget increases every 72 hours with monitoring windows"
- Compliance review of all creative before submission
- No mention of cloaking or pivot landing pages
