## Notable: Limitless Life Nootropics top keywords

Top keywords with cost data shown by Similarweb:
- "limitless life nootropics" ($1.00)
- "limitless biotech" ($4.26)
- "semax" ($0.83)
- "limitless life" ($2.47)
- "limitless peptides" ($4.37)

Almost all top keywords are branded (vendor's own name). The "$1–$4 cost" estimates are Similarweb's modeled CPC estimates, not actual paid spend confirmation.

## Interpretation

The "Paid 20.39%" figure for limitlesslifenootropics.com appears to be a Similarweb estimate that may be inflated by branded-defensive bidding (anchor vendor possibly bidding on its own brand to control SERP), affiliate paid traffic flowing to the domain, or competitor bidding on Limitless terms — not necessarily evidence of broad category-keyword Google Ads activity. It is an indicator worth follow-up but not by itself proof of an active "buy peptides"-style paid campaign.

For the other 7 anchor vendors with surfaced data: paid-search share is at or near 0%, consistent with the consensus narrative that direct Google paid search is unviable for the research-peptide category.
