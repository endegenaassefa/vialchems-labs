# Google Healthcare and Medicines Advertising Policy

Per Google's Healthcare and medicines advertising policy page (URL: https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/176031, accessed 2026-05-06), the following key restrictions apply:

## Prescription Drugs
"Google restricts the promotion of services related to the online prescribing, dispensing, and sale of prescription drugs."

## Unapproved Substances
The policy prohibits promotion of:
- "Products that contain ephedra"
- "Products containing human chorionic gonadotropin (hCG) in relation to weight loss"
- "Herbal and dietary supplements with active pharmaceutical or dangerous ingredients"
- All items on the "non-exhaustive list of unapproved pharmaceuticals and supplements"

## Opioids
"Google doesn't allow ads promoting or selling prescription opioid painkillers" (limited treatment-program exceptions).

## Experimental Treatments
"Promotion of speculative and/or experimental medical treatments" is not permitted, except for FDA-approved cell/gene therapies in the US.
