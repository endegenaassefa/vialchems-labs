# Policy Canary: April 2026 FDA Warning Letters

URL: https://policycanary.io/blog/fda-research-peptide-warning-letters-april-2026 (accessed 2026-05-06)

## Seven vendors named in FDA warning letters (April 7 2026)

1. Pink Pony Peptides (Lovega LLC, Wellington FL)
2. Mile High Compounds LLC (Clifton CO)
3. Prime Sciences (Scottsdale AZ)
4. Gram Peptides (Rancho Santa Fe CA)
5. PekCura Labs (Pensacola FL)
6. FormPour (Canton MI)
7. Guangzhou Huli Technology dba Fantasy Face (Chicago IL)

## FDA verbatim quote (Gram Peptides letter)

"Despite statements on your product labeling marketing your products for 'Research Use Only,' and 'not intended for human consumption, medical use, or veterinary use,' evidence obtained from your website establishes that your products are intended to be drugs for human use."

## Marketing channels documented

- Their own websites (product pages)
- eBay stores (FormPour, Fantasy Face)
- Skool community forum (Mile High Compounds)
- **No paid Google Ads observed in this enforcement action** — FDA evidence was websites + marketplaces + community forum, not Google paid search.
