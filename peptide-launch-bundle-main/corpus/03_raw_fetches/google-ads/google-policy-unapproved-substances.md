# Google Ads Unapproved Substances Policy

URL: https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15595718 (accessed 2026-05-06)

## Explicitly listed prohibited items (verbatim, paraphrased where bullet-fragmented)
- "All items on this non-exhaustive list of unapproved pharmaceuticals and supplements"
- Products containing ephedra
- Products with human chorionic gonadotropin (hCG) related to weight loss
- "Herbal and dietary supplements with active pharmaceutical ingredients"
- Products implying equivalence to prescription drugs
- Non-government approved products marketed as safe/effective for disease treatment
- Products subject to government warnings
- Products with names similar to unapproved pharmaceuticals
- DHEA products (except US-targeted campaigns)

## Substances NOT explicitly named in the help-article body

The compounds central to research-peptide vendor catalogs — BPC-157, TB-500, IGF variants, GHK, CJC, ipamorelin, sermorelin, melanotan, thymosin, generic "peptide" — **do not appear** in this article body.

The policy refers users to "external resources like LegitScript for comprehensive prohibited substance lists" but does not reproduce those catalogs in the page itself. Practical effect: the catch-all clauses ("herbal and dietary supplements with active pharmaceutical ingredients" + "non-exhaustive list of unapproved pharmaceuticals and supplements") give Google enforcement latitude to disapprove research-peptide ads even though specific compound names are not enumerated.
