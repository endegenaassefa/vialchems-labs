## Companion guide quotes (yourpeptidebrand.com/beginners-guide-to-google-ads-for-peptide-businesses/)

"Compliance is the cornerstone of any RUO peptide marketing effort."

"Google's advertising policies echo these restrictions, requiring advertisers to avoid language that suggests medical efficacy, serving size instructions, or research subjects outcomes."

Approvable descriptors: "high-purity peptide," "certified GMP manufacturing," and "custom labeling available"

Non-approvable language: "therapeutic claims," "medical efficacy," "serving size instructions," or implied outcomes like "anti-aging" or "performance boost"

Mandatory disclaimer: "Research Use Only – No therapeutic claims"

Framing: "laboratory research only; not for human consumption" / "peptide samples," "research grade," and "laboratory use."
