# Google Ads Circumventing Systems Policy (cloaking)

URL: https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15938075 (accessed 2026-05-06)

## Verbatim prohibitions

**Cloaking — different content to Google vs. users:**
"Showing Google a landing page or an ad's destination that complies with Google Ads' policies while showing people different content."

**Misleading ad-to-destination mismatch:**
"Tricking people into clicking on your ad by promoting a topic they're interested in and then leading them to a website for an entirely different topic"

**Bypassing review:**
"Bypassing enforcement mechanisms and detection by creating variations of ads, domains or content that have been disapproved"

**Intrusive redirects:**
"A pop-up ... that blocks access to the majority of your website's content, meaning Google cannot access your landing page"

**Click-tracker abuse:**
"Using click trackers ... to redirect people to websites with content prohibited by Google Ads' policies"

## Enforcement note

Cloaking is the standard tactic that would be required to push a "research peptides for sale" landing page past Google's healthcare ad review (e.g. show Google a generic "peptide chemistry education" page; redirect humans to a vendor checkout). Google explicitly characterizes this as a violation of the Circumventing Systems policy, and this category is associated with account-level termination, not just individual ad disapproval.
