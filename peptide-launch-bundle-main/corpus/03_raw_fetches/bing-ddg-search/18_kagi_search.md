# Kagi search engine — peptide vertical

## Key facts (from search-engine consensus across multiple results)

- **Kagi is paid/subscription. No ads. No sponsored results. No advertising business at all.**
- "Kagi has no ads or sponsored search results, being based on an account-based, subscription service instead. There are no ads, no sponsored shopping results, and no trackers."
- "One page of Kagi results will show 30-50 organic results compared to just 10 results typically shown on other search engines."
- "Kagi downranks web pages that contain ads and trackers, as this has empirically proven to improve search result quality for members."

## Implication for peptide vendors
- **Zero paid acquisition opportunity** on Kagi.
- Kagi user base is small (~30k subscribers as of late 2025 per Wikipedia, growing) and skews privacy-conscious technical users — not necessarily aligned with peptide buyer demographic.
- Organic ranking on Kagi can be influenced by users' personal "Lenses" and ranking lists, plus Kagi's own quality signals. A peptide vendor cannot game Kagi via ads.

## Bottom line
Kagi is a non-channel for peptide acquisition.
