# DuckDuckGo — "How DuckDuckGo Makes Money"

## Key extracted quotes

> "private ads on our search engine"
> ads are "based on the search results page you're viewing instead of being based on who other companies and their tracking algorithms assume you are as a person."

> Microsoft's ad network handles "most ad clicks."
> "Microsoft Advertising does not associate your ad-click behavior with a user profile."

> DuckDuckGo also has "a similar arrangement with TripAdvisor."

> Ads appear as "sponsored links that appear adjacent to search results."

## Implication
DDG's ads are essentially Microsoft Advertising inventory. Therefore Microsoft Advertising policy = the operative policy on what can be advertised on DDG. There is NO separate DDG ad policy.
