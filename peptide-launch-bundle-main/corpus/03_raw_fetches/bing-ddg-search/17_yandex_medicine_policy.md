# Yandex Direct — Medicine and Pharmaceuticals policy

## Allowed categories (verbatim, Russian)
- "Аптеки и лекарства" (Pharmacies and medicines)
- "Ветеринарные аптеки и препараты" (Veterinary pharmacies and drugs)
- "Медицинские изделия" (Medical devices)
- "Медицинские услуги" (Medical services)
- Educational content on medicine

## Prohibited categories (verbatim)
- "Наркотические и психотропные вещества" (Narcotics and psychotropic substances)
- "Рецептурные препараты и лекарства с наркотическими или психотропными веществами" (Prescription drugs containing narcotics or psychotropic substances)
- Abortion-related services

## Sensitive topics restriction
- "деликатные темы" — treatment of certain serious illnesses, diets, pregnancy
- Such ads cannot use behavioral targeting/retargeting; search display only.

## Notes
- **Peptides, hormones, research chemicals are not explicitly addressed.**
- Yandex's market is overwhelmingly Russian-speaking. Targeting US peptide buyers via Yandex would be infeasible — practically zero relevant US traffic.
- Even within Russia, the pharmaceutical advertising space is heavily regulated and centered on licensed pharmacies.
