# Microsoft Q&A: "It is possible to run ads on DuckDuckGo only?"

## Verbatim answer (from Shaynne, Microsoft Advertising Support Specialist)

> "Currently, there's no option to limit your ad delivery in DuckDuckGo only. With Microsoft Advertising, search ads can be shown to potential customers across a variety of search engines and partner sites, including Microsoft Bing, AOL, Yahoo, DuckDuckGo, Ecosia, and others."

## Workaround mentioned
- "Use website exclusions to exclude specific underperforming sites from your campaigns" — but cannot isolate DDG completely.
- "Use the Website URL (publisher) report to identify which sites are performing poorly, then exclude them individually at the campaign or ad group level."

## Implication
There is no DDG-only ad spend. A peptide vendor that wanted to test "private/anonymous-search" buyer intent specifically would have to run on the bundled Microsoft Network and use the publisher report to retroactively analyze DDG conversion.
