# Shipping Policy

At **FitAminos.com** , we are committed to providing fast and reliable shipping to ensure you receive your order in a timely manner. Please review our shipping policy below for details on processing times, shipping rates, and delivery expectations.

#### **1\. Processing Time**

  * All orders are processed within **1-2 business days** (Monday to Friday, excluding holidays).
  * Orders placed after **2:00 PM (PST)** will be processed on the next business day.
  * Once your order is processed, you will receive a confirmation email with tracking details.



#### **2\. Shipping Rates & Delivery Time**

We currently offer the following shipping options within the United States:

Shipping Method| Delivery Time| Cost  
---|---|---  
Standard Shipping| 3-5 Business Days| $7 per order  
Expedited Shipping| 2-3 Business Days| $15 per order  
Overnight Shipping| 1 Business Day| $30 per order  
  
  * **Free Shipping:** We may offer free shipping on certain promotions or orders over a specified amount.
  * Delivery times are estimated and may vary due to external factors such as weather conditions, carrier delays, or high order volumes.



#### **3\. Shipping Destinations**

Currently, we ship to addresses within the **United States only**. We do not offer international shipping at this time.

We **do not** ship to:

  * P.O. Boxes
  * APO/FPO/DPO addresses



#### **4\. Order Tracking**

Once your order is shipped, you will receive an email with a tracking number and a link to track your shipment. You can also track your order by logging into your **[FitAminos](https://fitaminos.com/product/bpc-157/ "BPC-157 \(10mg-20mg\)").com** account.

If you do not receive tracking information within **3 business days** after placing your order, please contact us at **support@fitaminos.com**.

#### **5\. Lost, Stolen, or Damaged Packages**

We take every precaution to ensure your package arrives safely. However, in rare cases, issues may arise:

#### **5.1 Lost or Stolen Packages**

  * If your tracking information shows **delivered** , but you have not received your package, please check with neighbors or your local post office.
  * If you still cannot locate your package, please contact us within **5 days** of the delivery date. We will assist you in filing a claim with the carrier.



#### **5.2 Damaged Items**

  * If your package arrives damaged, please take **clear photos** of the packaging and damaged items and email us at **support@fitaminos.com** within **48 hours** of delivery.
  * We will review your case and offer a replacement or store credit as appropriate.



#### **6\. Address Changes & Delivery Issues**

  * **Incorrect Address:** If you entered the wrong shipping address, please contact us **immediately** at **support@fitaminos.com** or **949-892-9866**.
  * **Orders cannot be modified once they have been shipped.** We are not responsible for incorrect addresses entered by customers.
  * **Failed Delivery Attempts:** If the carrier is unable to deliver your package after multiple attempts, it may be returned to us. You may be responsible for additional shipping fees for re-delivery.



#### **7\. Shipping Delays**

We strive to meet our estimated delivery times, but shipping delays can occasionally occur due to:

  * Severe weather conditions
  * Holiday shipping surges
  * Carrier delays
  * Unexpected supply chain disruptions



If your order is delayed beyond **7 business days** , please contact us for assistance.

#### **8\. Returns & Refunds**

For information on returns and refunds, please refer to our **Refund Policy**.

#### **9\. Contact Us**

If you have any questions or concerns about your order, feel free to reach out to our support team:

**Address:** 2048 S 87th St, Mesa, AZ 85209  
**Email:** support@fitaminos.com  
**Phone:** 949-892-9866
