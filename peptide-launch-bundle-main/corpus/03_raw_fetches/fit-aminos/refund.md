# REFUND Policy

At **FitAminos.com** , customer satisfaction is our top priority. If you are not completely satisfied with your purchase, we offer a refund or exchange under the conditions outlined below. Please read our refund policy carefully before making a request.

### **1\. Eligibility for Refunds & Returns**

To be eligible for a return, refund, or exchange:

  * The item must be **unused** , **sealed** , and in its **original packaging**.
  * The request must be made within **14 days** of the delivery date.
  * Opened, used, or tampered products **cannot** be returned for safety reasons.
  * Digital products and gift cards are **non-refundable**.



If your order qualifies, please follow the return instructions below.

### **2\. Non-Returnable & Non-Refundable Items**

We **do not** accept returns or refunds for:

  * Opened or used research chemicals and supplements
  * Digital products (eBooks, downloadable guides, etc.)
  * Clearance or final sale items
  * Gift cards
  * Items damaged due to improper handling by the customer



### **3\. How to Request a Refund or Return**

To initiate a return or refund, please follow these steps:

#### **Step 1: Contact Us**

Email us at **support@fitaminos.com** with:

  * Your **order number**
  * The **reason** for the return
  * **Photos** of the item (if applicable)



#### **Step 2: Return Authorization**

Once we review your request, we will send you a **Return Authorization (RA) number** and the return shipping address.

#### **Step 3: Ship the Item**

  * You must return the item within **7 days** of receiving the RA number.
  * Items must be in their **original condition** and **packaging**.
  * You are responsible for return shipping costs unless the return is due to our error.



#### **Step 4: Refund Processing**

  * Once we receive and inspect the item, we will process your refund within **5-7 business days**.
  * Refunds will be issued to the **original payment method**.
  * Shipping fees are **non-refundable**.



#### **4\. Late or Missing Refunds**

If you haven’t received your refund after **7 business days** , please:

  1. Check your **bank account** again.
  2. Contact your **credit card company** (it may take some time for the refund to be officially posted).
  3. If the issue persists, contact us at **support@fitaminos.com**.



### **5\. Exchanges**

We only replace items if they are **defective or damaged upon arrival**. If you need an exchange, email us at **support@fitaminos.com** with photos of the damage.

### **6\. Order Cancellations**

  * Orders can be canceled **within 1 hour** of purchase if they have not been processed.
  * Once an order is processed, it cannot be canceled.



### **7\. Contact Us**

If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us:

**Address:** 9221 E Baseline Rd, Ste 109-174, Mesa, AZ 85209  
**Email:** support@fitaminos.com  
**Phone:** 949-892-9866
