## Description

# **GLP-2T (10mg / 30mg)**

GLP-2T is a synthetic peptide analog of glucagon-like peptide-2 (GLP-2), engineered for enhanced stability and extended activity in controlled laboratory environments. GLP-2 is an intestinal trophic hormone naturally produced in the gastrointestinal tract and studied for its involvement in gut-associated signaling pathways and mucosal regulation.

GLP-2T is commonly explored in research settings for its interaction with GLP-2 receptors and its role in epithelial growth signaling, intestinal barrier modeling, and nutrient absorption–related mechanisms.

This product is available in both 10mg and 30mg vial options to support varying research protocols and study scales.

* * *

## **Mechanism of Action (Research Overview)**

### **GLP-2 Receptor Activation**

GLP-2T binds to GLP-2 receptors located primarily in the gastrointestinal tract. Activation of these receptors initiates intracellular signaling pathways involved in epithelial cell proliferation and mucosal maintenance research models.

### **Intestinal Epithelial Signaling**

In laboratory studies, GLP-2 analogs have been evaluated for their influence on intestinal crypt cell activity, villus height measurements, and barrier integrity parameters within controlled environments.

### **Barrier Function & Absorptive Surface Modeling**

GLP-2 pathway activation has been studied for its role in maintaining epithelial integrity and supporting structural adaptations in gastrointestinal tissue models.

### **Modified Stability Profile**

GLP-2T is designed to resist rapid enzymatic degradation compared to native GLP-2, allowing for more sustained receptor interaction in experimental protocols.

* * *

## **Research Focus Areas**

GLP-2T has been explored in controlled research environments involving:

  * Intestinal epithelial growth modeling

  * Mucosal integrity and barrier function studies

  * Nutrient absorption pathway analysis

  * Gastrointestinal hormone signaling evaluation

  * Gut-associated immune response models

  * Enterocyte proliferation and tissue adaptation research




These focus areas reflect measurable endpoints evaluated in published scientific literature and are provided for informational context only.

  * Synthetic GLP-2 analog

  * Studied for GLP-2 receptor activation

  * Commonly researched in gastrointestinal signaling models

  * Modified structure for enhanced stability

  * Lyophilized (freeze-dried) for storage and handling
