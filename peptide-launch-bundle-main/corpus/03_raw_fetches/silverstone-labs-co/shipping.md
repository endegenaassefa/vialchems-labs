# Shipping Policy

**Order Processing**  
Orders are typically processed within **1 to 2 business days** after payment is received. Orders placed on weekends or holidays will begin processing the next business day. Processing time does not include carrier transit time.

**Shipping Methods and Rates**  
We offer **flat-rate shipping of $15 per order** within the United States. Orders are shipped using UPS or USPS (United States Postal Service) based on destination, package size, and service availability. Carrier selection is determined at our discretion to ensure timely and reliable delivery. Shipping rate is a flat fee of $15 are calculated at checkout and vary based on destination and order size. An optional Shipping Protection may be added at checkout for **$4.00 per order** on orders valued up to **$399**.

**Shipping Protection (Optional)**  
Shipping Protection may be added at checkout for **$4.00 per order** on orders valued up to **$399**. Shipping Protection is optional. This optional coverage is designed to provide additional support in the event a package is lost in transit after carrier acceptance and within the defined investigation timeframe. Shipping Protection does not cover delays, packages marked as delivered, theft after delivery, incorrect or incomplete addresses, or carrier reroutes. Claims are subject to carrier verification and review.

**Tracking**  
Tracking is provided for all orders shipped via UPS or USPS. Once your order is processed and handed off to the carrier, you will receive an email with your tracking number to the email address entered at checkout. Tracking updates are generated by the carrier and may take up to **24–48 hours** to appear after carrier acceptance. If you do not receive tracking information, please check your spam or promotions folder, then contact us at [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection), so we can confirm the email address on file and resend your tracking details.

**When an Order Is Considered Shipped**  
An order is considered shipped once it has been handed off to the carrier and marked as accepted by the carrier’s tracking system. Once carrier acceptance is confirmed, responsibility for transit is transferred to the carrier.

**Lost, Delayed, or Misdelivered Packages**  
If a package shows carrier acceptance but has not been delivered, customers are encouraged to contact the carrier first using the provided tracking number. A package will be considered **lost after 7 business days (not including weekends or holidays) without movement** following carrier acceptance for domestic shipments. Orders with Shipping Protection may qualify for review and assistance based on the carrier’s investigation outcome.

**Delivered or Stolen Packages**  
Silverstone Labs Co. is **not responsible for packages marked as delivered by the carrier** , including those reported as stolen after delivery. Customers are responsible for providing a secure delivery location.

**Incorrect or Invalid Addresses**  
Customers are responsible for ensuring shipping addresses are accurate at checkout. Orders returned due to incorrect or invalid addresses may be resent at the customer’s expense.

**International Shipping**  
At this time, we do not offer international shipping.
