# Refunds & Returns Policy

We aim to keep our refunds and returns process clear, consistent, and fair. Each request is reviewed individually so we can resolve issues accurately while maintaining product integrity and compliance.

**Damaged Items**  
If your order arrives damaged, please contact us within **48 hours of delivery**. To help us assess the issue quickly, include the following in your message:

  * Your order number
  * Photos of the outer shipping packaging
  * Photos of the inner packaging
  * Photos of the damaged item(s)



Once received, we will review the information and determine the appropriate resolution, which may include a replacement or other reasonable solution.

**Incorrect Items**  
If you received an incorrect item, please contact us within **48 hours of delivery** with your order number and clear photos of the item received. Once verified, we will correct the issue and provide further instructions as needed.

**Returns and Refunds**  
Due to the nature of our products, **returns are not accepted**. Refund eligibility, if applicable, is on a case-by-case basis based on the circumstances of the order and compliance with this policy. Shipping fees are **non-refundable** , except in cases where an error was made on our part.  
In many cases, our preferred resolution is to offer a one time **replacement or alternative solution** rather than a refund, as this allows us to address issues quickly and fairly while ensuring product integrity.  
  
**Cancellations**  
If you need to cancel an order, send an email us as soon as possible to: [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#d3a0a6a3a3bca1a793a0babfa5b6a1a0a7bcbdb6bfb2b1a0b0bcfdb0bcbe).   
Orders may be canceled only if fulfillment has not begun. Once an order has entered processing or has shipped, cancellation may no longer be available. However, we will do our best to find a resolution that supports both us as a company and you as a consumer.

**Chargebacks and Disputes**  
We encourage our customers to contact us directly with any concerns before initiating a chargeback or payment dispute. Most issues can be resolved quickly through our support team when given the opportunity to review and assist. Our support team is operational Monday- Friday and can be reached at [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#572422272738252317243e3b21322524233839323b36352434387934383a).
