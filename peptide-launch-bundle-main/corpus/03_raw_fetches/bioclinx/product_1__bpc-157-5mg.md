# **Product Overview**

BPC-157 (Body Protection Compound-157) is a high-purity synthetic peptide derived from a naturally occurring protein in gastric juice. Comprising 15 amino acids, this stable pentadecapeptide is widely studied for its regenerative and protective properties in various research models. BPC-157 is a top choice for scientists investigating tissue repair, inflammation modulation, and gastrointestinal health.

## **Key Features**

  * **Tissue Repair and Healing** : BPC-157 promotes angiogenesis, tissue regeneration, and anti-inflammatory effects in preclinical models.

  * **High Purity** : Each batch is rigorously tested for purity and sterility, ensuring reliable research outcomes.

  * **Lyophilized Powder** : Provided in a sealed, sterile vial for easy reconstitution and storage.

  * **Research-Grade** : Formulated specifically for in-vitro and pre-clinical studies, This product is sold strictly for **scientific research purposes only**




## **Research Applications**

BPC-157 is a novel peptide under investigation for its potential in:

  * **Wound Healing Studies** : Enhances tendon, ligament, and muscle repair in non-human models.

  * **Gastrointestinal Research** : Explores protective effects on gut mucosa and ulcer healing in preclinical studies.

  * **Anti-Inflammatory Effects** : Investigates reduction of inflammation in various tissue injury models.

  * **Neuroprotective Potential** : Examines benefits in brain and nerve repair in experimental settings.




## **Product Specifications**

  * **Form** : Lyophilized powder in a sterile vial

  * **Molecular Weight** : Approximately 1,419 Da

  * **CAS Number** : 137525-51-0

  * **Available Sizes** : 5mg

  * **Storage** : Store at -20°C, protected from light and moisture
