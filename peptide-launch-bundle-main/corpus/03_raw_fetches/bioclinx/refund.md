## **1\. Refund and Return Policy**

**1.1 Eligibility for Refunds or Replacements**

  * Unopened and sealed products may be returned for a full refund or replacement within 30 days of delivery.

  * Opened products may be eligible for a refund or replacement only if:

    * Clear documentation (e.g., photos) is provided showing potential defects such as damaged packaging or quality issues, and

    * The issue is reported within 5 Business days of opening the product.




**1.2 How to Request a Refund or Replacement**

To initiate a return or refund request:

  * Contact our support team at bioclinx@gmail.com with your order number and a detailed explanation of the issue.

  * If the product is confirmed to be defective, return shipping costs will be covered by BioClinx LLC.

  * All eligible returns please send an email to [bioclinx@gmail.com](mailto:bioclinx@gmail.com) to get the shipping adress. 



**1.3 Processing Refunds**

  * Approved refunds will be processed within 5–7 business days after the returned product is received or the issue is validated.

  * Refunds will be issued to the original payment method used at the time of purchase.




**1.4 Exceptions and Conditions**

  * Refunds or replacements will not be provided for products damaged due to improper handling, mixing, storage, attempted use, or any deviation from proper laboratory procedures after delivery.

  * The Company reserves the right to deny any return or refund request if there is evidence of tampering, attempted use, or product degradation.

  * All returns must be received in their original packaging with intact labeling and seals
