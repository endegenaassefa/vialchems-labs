## Frequently Asked Questions

1\. What are the products from Coffee&Peppers intended for? 

All items sold by Coffee&Peppers are strictly for laboratory research use only. They are not for human or animal consumption, not for therapeutic use, and not cleared for incorporation into food, cosmetics, medical devices, or drugs. By purchasing, you confirm you are a qualified professional or organization capable of safely and lawfully handling these materials.

2\. Do you provide Certificates of Analysis (COAs)? 

Yes. Certificates of Analysis are available for most products upon request. These third-party lab reports verify the purity and composition of the materials. Please note that COAs are provided for informational purposes only and do not represent medical approval or guarantees.

3\. How long does it take to receive my order? 

Orders are typically processed within 3-5 business days. Delivery time varies depending on the carrier and shipping destination. Once a package has left our facility, Coffee&Peppers cannot control courier delays, customs processing times, or issues caused by inaccurate shipping information.

4\. What forms of payment do you accept? 

We accept major credit cards and secure online payments processed through trusted third-party providers. For customer safety, Coffee&Peppers does not store or directly process credit card information.
