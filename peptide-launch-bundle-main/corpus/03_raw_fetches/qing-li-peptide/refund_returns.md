# **Refund & Returns Policy**

At Qing Li Peptide, we strive to ensure that every customer is satisfied with their purchase. If you are not fully satisfied with your order, we are here to help.

#### **1\. Return Policy**

  * **Eligibility for Return** :

We accept returns within **30 days** of receiving your order. To be eligible for a return, the product must be in its **original, unopened condition** and **unused**.

  * **Non-Returnable Items** :

Unfortunately, we cannot accept returns on **opened products** , **customized orders** , or **clearance items**. If you have any questions about whether your item is eligible for return, please contact our support team.




#### **2\. Refund Policy**

  * **Refund Process** : Once we receive your returned item, we will inspect it and notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund. If your return is approved, we will initiate a refund to your original method of payment (PayPal).

  * **Refund Amount** : Your refund will cover the **full purchase price** of the product, excluding shipping costs. Return shipping fees will be **the responsibility of the customer** , unless the return is due to a product defect or error on our part.




#### **3\. How to Return Your Product**

  1. Contact our customer service team at [353813357@qq.com](mailto:353813357@qq.com) to request a return authorization.

  2. We will provide you with instructions on how to ship your return item back to us.

  3. Make sure to securely package your item and include a copy of your order receipt or invoice in the return package.

  4. Once we receive the returned item and inspect its condition, we will issue your refund.




#### **4\. PayPal Refunds**

If you have made your payment via **PayPal** , the refund will be processed directly to your **PayPal account**. Please note that PayPal may take a few days to reflect the refund in your account, depending on their processing time.
