What are your shipping costs?

What is shipping insurance?

## We offer shipping insurance at checkout to protect your order against theft or loss by the carrier. If insured, we will reship your order if it doesn’t arrive as expected.   
  
Orders valued at $400 or higher (pre-discount) are required to have shipping insurance due to an increase in lost or stolen packages.   
  
We are not responsible for lost or stolen packages without purchased insurance.

What happens if my package is delayed, lost, stolen, or damaged in transit?

## If your package appears to be delayed or has been in transit for 3 days or more without an update, please contact us immediately so we can start an investigation with the shipping carrier. Once an investigation is initiated, it can take up to 8 business days to reach a resolution.   
  
If the carrier determines that the package was indeed lost, stolen, or damanged in during transit, we will ship a replacement order—provided that shipping insurance was purchased with your original order.   
  
IMPORTANT: If shipping insurance was not purchased with your order, we are unable to provide replacements for lost, stolen, or damaged packages, and we cannot assume liability for such incidents.   
  
Customers must notify us of any issues within 7 days of the delivery date as marked by the carrier. We are not liable for any lost, stolen, or damaged packages if we are informed after this timeframe.

Do you ship International?

## At this time we do not ship outside of the United States, nor do we ship to U.S. Territories including Puerto Rico and the U.S. Virgin Islands. In addition, we cannot ship to U.S. Military bases. All orders placed with these delivery locations will be canceled and refunded.
