## 3rd Party Testing

**3rd Party Testing for Purity & Quality**

At Accelerate Labs, we take the integrity of our research peptides seriously. That’s why every batch undergoes rigorous 3rd party testing in the USA to ensure the highest standards of purity and quality before they are made available for purchase. We’ve partnered with [**MZ Biolabs**](https://www.mzbiolabs.com/), a trusted, DEA schedule III licensed facility, to handle our Certificates of Analysis (COAs) and verify that each product meets the specifications required for research.

MZ Biolabs uses advanced analytical methods, including **HPLC with UV detection** and **mass spectrometry** , to accurately identify and confirm the purity of our research peptides. This process guarantees that our research compounds—whether in raw powder form or formulated solutions—adhere to stringent quality standards.

**Transparency You Can Trust**

We believe in full transparency and encourage our customers to reach out to [**MZ Biolabs**](https://www.mzbiolabs.com/) directly to confirm the authenticity and accuracy of our COAs. For those seeking additional peace of mind, we also invite you to have our research peptides tested by a 3rd party lab of your choosing.

When it comes to research, quality is everything. With 3rd party testing at the core of our process, you can trust that the peptides you purchase from Accelerate Labs meet the highest purity standards, giving you confidence in your research outcomes.
