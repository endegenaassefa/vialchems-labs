All orders ship **free of charge** through our U.S.-based fulfillment partners.

### Returns & Order Resolution

Due to the nature of our research materials, **all sales are final** and returns are not accepted.  
If your order arrives **damaged or incorrect** , please contact our support team within **48 hours of delivery**.  
Email **support@imaginefirstlabs.com** with your order number, a brief description of the issue, and **clear photos of the item and packaging**.  
Each request is reviewed individually to ensure product integrity and appropriate resolution.  
  
  


### Product Care & Handling

Research materials should be handled and stored in accordance with the information provided on the product label.  
Store in a **cool, dry environment** , protected from heat, light, and moisture. Keep containers **sealed when not in use** to preserve material integrity.  
Proper handling and storage are essential to maintaining consistency, stability, and quality throughout the lifecycle of the research material.
