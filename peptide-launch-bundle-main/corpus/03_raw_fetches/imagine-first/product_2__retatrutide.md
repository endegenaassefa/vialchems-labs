##  Understanding the Science 

###  One Molecule. Multiple Signaling Pathways. 

GLP-3R is studied in research settings for its interaction with three distinct hormone receptor pathways: GIP, GLP-1, and glucagon. Each receptor is associated with a different metabolic signaling process that researchers examine independently.

By engaging multiple receptor pathways at the same time, GLP-3R is used in scientific research to better understand how complex metabolic signaling systems function together.

[ Nature Cell Discovery - Published 2024 ](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41421-024-00700-0)
