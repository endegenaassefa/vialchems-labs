In accordance with California law and due to the nature of the products offered by **Imagine First** , **all sales are final**. We do **not accept returns, exchanges, or issue refunds** once an order has been placed.

Our products are sold strictly as **research-use-only laboratory supplies** and are not eligible for return after shipment or delivery.

* * *

## **No Returns or Exchanges**

We do not accept returns or exchanges for any products under any circumstances, including but not limited to:

  * Customer error or change of mind

  * Improper storage, handling, or use after delivery

  * Misinterpretation of product descriptions or disclaimers

  * Carrier delays or delivery issues outside our control




* * *

## **No Refunds**

Refunds are not issued for any orders. By completing a purchase, you acknowledge and agree that **refunds will not be provided** except where required by applicable California law.

* * *

If you receive an item that is **damaged during transit** or **incorrect due to a fulfillment error** , please notify us within **48 hours of confirmed delivery** by contacting **support@imaginefirstlabs.com**

To assist with verification, please include:

Upon verification, **our sole obligation** , at our discretion, is to provide a **replacement product or store credit**. Refunds are not issued.
