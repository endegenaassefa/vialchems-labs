  * Additional time may be required for custom compounds



### Shipping Options

  * **Expedited Shipping:** $19.95 (2-3 business days)

### Special Handling

  * **Temperature-controlled** packaging for sensitive compounds
  * **Signature required** for all deliveries
  * **Discreet packaging** with no external branding
  * **Tracking provided** for all shipments



## 🔄 RETURNS POLICY

⏰ 7-DAY RETURN WINDOW

Returns must be initiated within 7 days of delivery

### ✅ Returnable Items

  * **Unopened products** in original packaging
  * Items with **intact seals and labels**
  * Products stored at **proper temperature**
  * Standard catalog compounds only



### ❌ Non-Returnable Items

  * **Opened or used products** (safety policy)
  * Custom synthesized compounds
  * Products past expiration date
  * Items compromised by improper storage



### Return Process

  1. **Contact us** at [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#d1b8bfb7be91a1b4a1a5b8b5b4a2bab8bfb6b5bebcffb2bebc)
  2. **Receive RMA number** and return instructions
  3. **Ship back** using our prepaid label
  4. **Refund processed** within 3-5 business days



## 📍 SHIPPING RESTRICTIONS

### ✅ We Ship To:

  * All 50 United States
  * Universities and research institutions
  * Licensed laboratories
  * Qualified research facilities



### ❌ We Cannot Ship To:

  * International addresses
  * P.O. Boxes or mail forwarding services
  * Residential addresses without research verification
  * Addresses flagged by carriers
