## Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our purity standards, testing, shipping, and support—presented with total clarity. 

__ How pure are your peptides, and how is purity verified? ____

Every lot is independently verified by third-party laboratories to confirm identity and **99%+ purity**. We use validated methods such as HPLC and LC-MS, and we only release batches that meet our GMP-aligned specifications. 

__ Do you provide COAs, and can I match them to my vial? ____

Yes. A **Certificate of Analysis** is provided for every order. COAs contain lot numbers and expiry details that match the label on your vial for end-to-end traceability. 

__ How should peptides be stored to maintain stability? ____

Store lyophilized peptides in a cool, dry environment away from light. For longer-term storage, refrigeration is recommended. Once reconstituted, follow the storage guidance shown on the COA and product page. 

__ Are your products for human use or research only? ____

All products are sold for **laboratory research use only**. They are not intended for human consumption, medical, veterinary, or household use. 

__ How fast do you ship, and is the packaging discreet? ____

Orders typically ship within **1-3 business day** from our U.S. facility in discreet, unmarked packaging. Tracking is provided with every shipment. 

__ Do you ship internationally? ____

We currently ship to select regions. Availability and transit times vary by destination and local regulations. Please check the checkout page for the latest coverage. 

__ Are your facilities GMP aligned? ____

Yes, we follow **GMP-aligned** procedures focused on documentation, batch control, and contamination prevention. Each lot must meet release specifications before shipping. 

__ What if I have a question about a specific order or lot? ____

Our support team can help with **lot-specific documents** , storage guidance, and COA details. Reach out with your order number and the lot shown on your vial label.
