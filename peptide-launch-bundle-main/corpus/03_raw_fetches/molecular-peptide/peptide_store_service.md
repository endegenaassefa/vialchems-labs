## Most Peptide Brands Build Their Store Wrong From Day One

You're launching a peptide brand.

You have inventory, branding, and a marketing plan.

The last thing you want to do is waste time and money on technical decisions. But here's the truth: the platform you choose in week one will determine your entire trajectory. Choose wrong, and you'll spend the next 18 months fighting limitations, paying unexpected fees, or worse—rebuilding everything from scratch.

Choose right, and you scale confidently with a store built for your growth.

__ Wrong Platform Choice  You start on Shopify or a template builder, then realize you're locked into their policies and limits. Rebuilding wastes 6 months and $20K+.

__ No Compliance Foundation  Building compliance later is expensive and risky. FDA labeling requirements, research-use-only disclaimers, and proper product structuring need to be baked in from day one

__ Poor Conversion Design  Generic templates convert at 1-2%. A custom-built peptide store converts at 3-5%. That's the difference between $50K and $250K in first-year revenue.

## What a Custom Peptide Website Design Actually Gets You

A 1% difference in conversion rate doesn't sound like much until you do the math.

If your peptide store gets 10,000 visitors in month one, a 1% conversion rate = 100 orders. A 3% conversion rate = 300 orders. At an average order value of $150, that's the difference between $15,000 and $45,000 in revenue from the same traffic.

Every feature is designed with this reality in mind: peptide buyers have specific needs, concerns, and behaviors. A generic store ignores them. A custom-built store optimizes for them.

COA upload system, lab result display panels, research-use-only disclaimers, and proper product labeling — structured to reduce payment processor risk and build buyer trust from the first page view.

We install and configure 2–3 payment processors with automatic failover logic. If one processor flags your account or goes down, your store keeps taking orders without interruption.

Flat rate, free threshold, zone-based, or weight-based — we build shipping rules that match how you actually fulfill, not what the default WooCommerce settings assume.

Automated emails that follow up with buyers who left without completing their order. A properly timed 3-step sequence typically recovers 5–15% of abandoned carts — pure recovered revenue on autopilot.

We connect and configure your email platform (Klaviyo, Mailchimp, or similar) with your store — list segmentation, welcome flow, and transactional emails included. Your list starts working the day you launch.
