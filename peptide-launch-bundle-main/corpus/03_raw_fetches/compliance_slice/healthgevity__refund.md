# Verbatim Refund / Return Excerpts

"Unopened items purchased directly from Healthgevity can be returned or exchanged within 30 days of the purchase date."

Returns after 30 days: 20% restocking fee + prior return authorization required.

Items purchased from resellers must be returned to the reseller.

Order discrepancies must be reported within three days of receipt; Healthgevity issues return label for their errors.

Returning six or more identical items: 20% restocking fee.

# Verbatim Shipping Excerpts

Domestic US & territories: free standard shipping on orders over $150 (2-6 business days). Standard shipping to Hawaii, Puerto Rico, and Alaska arrives in 1-2 weeks. Guam and US Virgin Islands take 3-5 weeks.

Orders process and ship by 3:00pm EST weekdays; later orders ship next business day.

# International

"All sales are final once package leaves the US."
Duties, taxes, and brokerage fees are buyer's responsibility.
Seized/destroyed items not refundable.

# Notable Absences

No age verification. No US-state exclusions. No country exclusions named.
