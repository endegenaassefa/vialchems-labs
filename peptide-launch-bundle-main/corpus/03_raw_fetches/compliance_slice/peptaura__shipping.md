# Status: NOT APPLICABLE / PARTIAL

Peptaura does not stock or ship product itself. From homepage:

"Peptaura is a platform that connects you to compound merchants. We do not hold stock nor test any of the [compounds]"

Each individual supplier sets its own shipping policy. Peptaura functions as marketplace, escrow, and dispute mediator only.
