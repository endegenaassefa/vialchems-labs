# Shipping Policy (Verbatim where quoted)

"Orders are typically packed and handed off to the selected carrier within 36 hours"

Methods:
- "UPS 2nd Day Air" — "typically arrive within two business days after shipment"; free over $200
- "FedEx 2Day" — "typically arriving within two business days after shipment"; free over $200
- "USPS Priority Mail" — used only for customers outside UPS service areas or with PO Boxes

Route package protection available at checkout.

# Return / Refund (Verbatim and summary)

"Orders may be canceled within 1 hour of placement" via email only.

Damaged/Defective: contact within 72 hours with order number and photos. Replacements, partial refunds, or store credit at company discretion.

Refund eligibility: products must be "unused, unopened, untampered, and in its original packaging" and requests must arrive within 30 calendar days of delivery.

Non-refundable: opened, used, or improperly stored products; ordering errors; claims after 30 days.
