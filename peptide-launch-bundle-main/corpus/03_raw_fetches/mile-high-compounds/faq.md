## Frequently Asked Questions

###### This is where you’ll find answers to the most common questions about Mile High Compounds.

### __What are research compounds, and how are they used?

Research compounds are chemical substances intended strictly for scientific and laboratory research. They are not for human consumption and are used by professionals for experimental, analytical, or educational purposes.

### __Are your compounds third-party tested?

Yes. All of our products undergo rigorous independent third-party testing for purity, identity, net quantity, heavy metals, endotoxins, and sterility. There are QR codes to scan on the labels for batch traceability.

### __Why does my tracking number come from California and not Colorado?

Our company is based out of Colorado, but we ship directly from our fulfillment center in California. This way, we are able to provide you with the fast and reliable service you deserve.

### __Do you accept returns or exchanges?

Yes, we accept unused, unopened product returns less than 60 days after the original purchase date. You will be issued a full refund, minus the cost of shipping and the fulfillment fee once we receive the item.

### __Can I track my order?

Absolutely. Once your order ships, you’ll receive a tracking number via email so you can monitor delivery in real time.

### __Do you ship internationally?

Yes we do! Worldwide Shipping is available for all orders. We highly recommend using Route shipping insurance when ordering Internationally, in case of lost or stolen packages.

### __What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all Major Credit Cards and Zelle for your convenience.
