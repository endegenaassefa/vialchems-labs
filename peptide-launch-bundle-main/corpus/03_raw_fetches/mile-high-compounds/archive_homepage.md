## Frequently Asked Questions

### __Are your compounds third-party tested?

Yes. All of our products undergo independent third-party testing to ensure purity, consistency, and quality. We provide Certificates of Analysis (COAs) for full transparency.

### __What happens if my package is lost, stolen, or damaged?

We’ve partnered with Route to provide protection for “in-transit” related issues. Please file a claim with the Route team [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20251225194943/https://claims.route.com/) for quick assistance with your order issue!

### __Do you ship worldwide?

Yes! We do ship worldwide to select countries via FedEx International Connect Plus. Delivery time on average is 5-12 business days. Please view the country drop-down menu at checkout to see if we support shipping to your country.

### __Do you accept product returns or exchanges?

Yes, we accept unused, unopened product returns less than 60 days after the original purchase date. You will be issued a full refund, minus the cost of shipping once we receive the item.
