# About

[Home](/web/20251225195348/https://milehighcompounds.is/home) / About

## About Mile High Compounds

Mile High Compounds is a trusted supplier of premium research-use compounds, dedicated to unmatched quality, safety, and transparency. We proudly serve laboratories, academic institutions, and professional researchers across the world with fast, reliable service.

Every batch is produced in cGMP-certified facilities and verified through rigorous third-party testing for identity, purity, endotoxins, heavy metals, and sterility. Certificates of Analysis (COAs) are provided with every compound, ensuring researchers have the confidence and clarity they need for consistent results.

With same-day shipping and a commitment to quality, transparency, and a customer-first approach, Mile High Compounds is your dependable partner for research excellence.

## our mission

At Mile High Compounds, our mission is to empower researchers by supplying premium, 5x tested compounds designed exclusively for research use. We are committed to upholding the highest standards of purity, accuracy, and transparency, ensuring that every product we offer contributes to credible, reliable scientific outcomes.

We understand the importance of speed and trust in research environments. That’s why we provide fast, dependable U.S. & worldwide shipping and responsive customer support, giving laboratories and academic institutions the confidence they need to move their work forward without compromise.

## What Makes Us Different

At Mile High Compounds, we don’t just supply research compounds — we set a higher standard for how they’re sourced, verified, and delivered. Every product in our catalog is manufactured in state-of-the-art, cGMP-certified, FDA-audited facilities and undergoes rigorous third-party testing for identity, purity, endotoxins, sterility, and heavy metals.

What sets us apart is our unwavering commitment to transparency and trust. Every compound is independently verified, and Certificates of Analysis (COAs) are available for complete visibility — no hidden data, no guesswork.

From our Colorado-based operation, we provide fast, same-day shipping to keep your lab stocked and your timelines on track. Whether you’re running a professional laboratory, an academic study, or an independent research project, Mile High Compounds delivers the confidence to move forward — backed by science, precision, and integrity.
