So with everything going on, I got a little bit behind on this series of basic exercise techniques.  And since I still don't have my act together, this is a little bit of a diversion.

Specifically I want to pose a thought experiment:

It's taken as a matter of faith that you MUST do compound exercises (and usually the big barbell movements) to get big.

But why?

Rather, the question I want to pose is this: in a fundamental physiological sense, why couldn't you use nothing but isolation movements to get as big as your genetics will allow.

The crux of that question rests on two different points, both of which I'll address.

Because whenever I pose this question, I never get an answer in a physiological sense.  Yes, there are other practical considerations.  That's not my point.

Physiologically why wouldn't it work?

I'll leave it to everybody to argue in the comments.  Just remember that I don't read 'em.
