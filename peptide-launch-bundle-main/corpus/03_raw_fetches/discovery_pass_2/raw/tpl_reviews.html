BioLongevity Labs

Tested

BioLongevity Labs runs a full transparency testing program across their entire supply chain. When raw materials arrive at their USA labs, they are independently third-party tested to verify they match the manufacturer COA. After manufacturing at one of their 4 US partner labs, products undergo another round of testing and are sent out for third-party verification. Once BioLongevity Labs receives finished goods, they are third-party tested twice more before shipping to customers. This multi-stage QC process makes them one of the most rigorously tested vendors in the research peptide space.
