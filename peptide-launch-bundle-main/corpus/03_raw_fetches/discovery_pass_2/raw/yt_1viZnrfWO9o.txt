===DESCRIPTION===
This peptide shit has gotten completely out of hand at this point. Every time I log in there's another influencer in my feed raving about some compound they probably just learned about on a podcast 3 weeks ago, and now they're out here just shamelessly shoving their affiliate link in everyone's face as if they're even remotely qualified to be recommending what people inject into their body. And look, I'm not here to tell you peptides are "bad". It's a broad category with a lot of different compounds being used for different things, and some do seem to have legitimate use for certain people. But what I am against is these reckless "fitness coaches" promoting this stuff to their audience like it's a fucking protein bar. These are injectable compounds, a lot of them have little to no human research, unclear long-term risks, often sourced from random labs with zero oversight so you don't even know what you're actually taking. And who's buying this stuff? Half the time it's beginners who don't even have the basics nailed yet and now some clown with a ring light has them thinking the answer is to start pinning. That's where the fitness industry is at now and it's honestly just fucking sad to watch.
