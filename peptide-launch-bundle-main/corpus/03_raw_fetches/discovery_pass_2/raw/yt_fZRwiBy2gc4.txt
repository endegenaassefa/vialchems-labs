In this episode, we sit down with Ryan Andersen, Special Operations/Army Veteran, entrepreneur, and advocate for helping veterans find purpose beyond the uniform. This conversation was about identity, discipline, health, and rebuilding a life after service.

We kick things off with a rapid-fire round to get to know Ryan, then dive deep into the moments that shaped him. From his childhood bond with his grandparents to the calling he felt watching his grandfather serve. Ryan walks us through joining the military, navigating life in college, and the shock of transitioning back into civilian life.

Ryan opens up about:
-His time in the Army and journey into Special Operations
-Where his passion for military service began
-How life changed after college and after leaving the military
-The search for identity and purpose after service
-Struggling with motivation, getting lazy, and rebuilding routines
-How focusing on his health, including peptides prescribed by medical professionals, helped him feel better and get back on track
-Starting his own real estate company
-Why is he determined to help fellow veterans improve their mindset, health, and post-service path

Today, Ryan is all about purpose. He uses his story to elevate other veterans and create better outcomes for those transitioning into their second life.
