Today's paper in question is:
Camargo JBB et al.  Large increases in resistance training volume do not impair skeletal muscle hypertrophy or anabolic–catabolic molecular signalling in trained individuals.  bioRvix preprint

Note: this IS a preprint meaning it has not passed peer review.

In short it compared both the growth and molecular level responses to recreationally trained individuals who had their habitual quad volumes increased by either 20% or 120%.

Their primary goal was to see if the higher volumes were negative more than anything else. But we all know that's not how the Internet fitness industry is going to describe it.

What did they find?  THE ANSWERS MAY SHOCK YOU!

Full paper can be found here
