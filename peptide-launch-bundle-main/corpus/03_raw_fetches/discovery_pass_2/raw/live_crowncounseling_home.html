We accept out of network insurance

At Crown Counseling, we believe access to high-quality mental health care should never be limited by insurance networks. That’s why we proudly work with clients who have out-of-network (OON) benefits.

If your insurance plan includes OON coverage, you may be eligible for partial reimbursement for your therapy sessions. We are happy to provide you with a superbill (detailed invoice) to submit to your insurance company for reimbursement.

How It Works:

  • You pay for your sessions upfront.

  • We provide a monthly superbill with all necessary coding.

  • You submit the superbill to your insurance provider.

  • Your insurer may reimburse a portion of the session fee based on your specific plan.

Important Notes:

  • Reimbursement rates vary significantly between plans.

  • Most PPO and some POS plans offer OON benefits.

  • We do not bill your insurance directly for OON claims.

  • It is your responsibility to confirm coverage with your insurer.

Need Help?
We’re happy to guide you through checking your OON benefits. Contact our intake team for support or to receive a benefits verification checklist.
