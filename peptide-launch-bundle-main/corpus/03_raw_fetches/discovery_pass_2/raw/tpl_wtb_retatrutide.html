How to Choose a RETATRUTIDE Vendor

Check for COA

Always request a Certificate of Analysis showing purity testing from a third-party lab. Look for 98%+ purity and recent test dates.

Read Reviews

Check customer reviews on our platform and elsewhere. Look for consistent positive feedback about product quality and customer service.

Payment Options

Reputable vendors offer multiple payment methods. Be cautious of vendors accepting only cryptocurrency or wire transfers.

Shipping & Returns

Check shipping times, cold-pack options for temperature-sensitive peptides, and return/reship policies.

Quality & Legitimacy Checklist

  • Certificate of Analysis (COA) available for each batch
  • Third-party testing from independent labs (Janoshik, etc.)
  • Purity level 98% or higher
  • Clear product labeling with batch numbers
  • Proper storage and shipping conditions
  • Responsive customer support
  • Clear return/refund policy
  • Transparent about peptide source/manufacturing

Red Flags to Avoid

  • No COA or refuses to provide testing documentation
  • Prices significantly below market average
  • Claims peptides are for "human consumption"
  • No clear contact information or physical address
  • Only accepts cryptocurrency with no alternatives

What is the average shipping time for RETATRUTIDE?
