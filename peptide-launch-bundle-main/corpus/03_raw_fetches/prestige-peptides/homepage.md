## Frequently Asked Questions

Are your peptides already mixed? __ __

Peptides come in a freeze-dried or lyophilized powder form for stability

How do we ship orders? __ __

All orders are shipped via FedEx Express 2-Day service to ensure fast and reliable delivery anywhere in the U.S.

Do we ship on weekends? __ __

FedEx does not deliver on weekends (Saturday or Sunday) for Express 2-Day service. Deliveries occur Monday through Friday only.

When will my order ship? __ __

Orders placed Monday through Thursday before 1 PM (CST) will typically ship the same day. Orders placed after the Thursday cut-off time will not ship until Monday, as FedEx does not pick up or deliver on weekends.
