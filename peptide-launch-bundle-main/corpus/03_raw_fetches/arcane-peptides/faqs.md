____ Product Testing

We verify every batch of every product through accredited, independent third-party laboratories. Since each product requires unique analytical methods and equipment, we collaborate with specialized laboratories and PhDs in analytical and organic chemistry to design our testing protocols.

Analytical techniques we utilize include: **NMR, FTIR, HPLC, LC-MS, GC-MS, TLC, ICP-MS, Gravimetry, UV-Vis, Titration, Melting Point, and Organoleptics.**

____ Product Appearance Variations

Variations in **color, texture, odor or even biochemical properties** may occur due to **polymorphy** (differences in crystal structure).These variations do not compromise product quality.

____ Peptide Form

Our peptides are provided in **lyophilized powder form** to ensure maximum stability. In any other format, peptide integrity would rapidly degrade before reaching the customer.

  * **Optimal storage:** Below –20 °C.
  * **Short-term storage:** Refrigeration at 4 °C for up to 2 years.
  * **Room temperature tolerance:** 4–6 weeks with minimal degradation if protected from direct sunlight (suitable for international shipping).



____ Peptide Top Colors

Images on our site display white tops for illustration purposes only. In practice, vial tops may vary in color to help our staff distinguish products during processing.

____ Peptide Powder Appearance

Lyophilized peptides typically form a solid cake. However, during transit, the cake may break or appear uneven. This is an **aesthetic issue only** and does not impact **quality, potency, or purity.**

Some peptides and proteins may appear larger due to buffering requirements. This is normal.

____ Storage Instructions

Storage instructions are provided on each product listing and product label.

  * **General storage:** Store in a cool, dry place away from heat, moisture, and sunlight.
  * **Best practice:** Refrigeration or freezing is recommended for long-term stability.



____ Solution Flakes, Precipitation, and Separation

Solution-based products may occasionally:

  * **Crystallize** (form flakes)
  * **Precipitate** (fall out of suspension)
  * **Separate** (form layers)
  * **Lose homogeneity**



This typically occurs during cold transit or accidental freezing. To restore uniformity:

  1. Warm the vial in **50–70 °C water** for 5–10 minutes.
  2. Shake vigorously to re-suspend.



If the product does not return to homogeneity, please contact us.

____ Extreme Temperature Exposure

All molecules degrade over time, but proper storage significantly slows this process.

  * **Low temperature exposure:** Generally safe; may cause precipitation in solutions but does not accelerate degradation.
  * **High temperature exposure:** Prolonged exposure may accelerate degradation.
    * Example: A product requiring room temperature storage exposed to 115 °F for a few days will not show measurable degradation once properly stored again.
  * **Cold-storage products (–20 °C):** Short-term exposure (<24h) to ambient temperatures does not significantly degrade the product unless oxygen, moisture, or sunlight are also present.



____ Accidental Exposure or Ingestion

These products are intended for laboratory research purposes only, and are not for human or animal consumption. The products are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. The products should not be used as a food, drug, cosmetic, or other household use.

In the event of accidental exposure or ingestion:

  * Contact your local **toxicologist**.  
**United States:** Poison Help Line — **1-800-222-1222**
  * Seek immediate **medical attention**.
  * Always handle products using proper **PPE**.
