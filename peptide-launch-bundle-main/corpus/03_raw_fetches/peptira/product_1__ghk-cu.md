## Description

##### **Product Specifications:**

  
**CAS Number:** 89030-95-5  
**Molecular Formula:** C14H24N6O4Cu  
**Molecular Weight:** ~403.9 g/mol  
**Quantity:** 70mg per vial (lyophilized powder)  
**Purity:** ≥99% (HPLC verified)  
**Form:** Sterile, freeze-dried powder  
**Storage:** Store lyophilized peptide at −20 °C (or 2–8 °C short-term). Reconstituted solution should be refrigerated at 2–8 °C, protected from light.
