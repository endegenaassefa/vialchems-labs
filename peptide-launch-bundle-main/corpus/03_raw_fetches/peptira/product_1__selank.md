##### **Product Specifications:**

**Molecular Formula:** C₃₃H₅₇N₁₁O₉  
**Molecular Weight:** ~751.88 g/mol  
**Quantity:** 10mg per vial (lyophilized powder)  
**Purity:** ≥99% (HPLC verified)

**Key Research Applications:**

  * Anxiolytic and stress response studies

  * Neurotransmitter modulation (serotonin/dopamine)
