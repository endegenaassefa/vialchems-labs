## Description

##### **Product Specifications:**

  
**CAS Number:** 69558-55-0  
**Molecular Formula:** C19H31N5O4  
**Molecular Weight:** ~393.48 g/mol  
**Quantity:** 10mg per vial (lyophilized powder)
