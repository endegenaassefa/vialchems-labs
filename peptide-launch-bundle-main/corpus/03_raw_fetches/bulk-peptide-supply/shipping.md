## Shipping Policy

Thank you for choosing to shop with us! We strive to ensure that your order reaches you as quickly as possible.

**Delivery Timeframe:**

– Orders typically arrive within ** 10 days** on average.

– However, please allow up to **21 days** for delivery, as shipping times can vary based on your location, Warehouse location, and other factors.

**Processing Time:**

– Once your order is placed, it will be processed and shipped within 1-3 business days. The tracking number may take up to 14 days to activate worst case scenario.

**Tracking Your Order:**

– You will receive a tracking number via email once your order has shipped, allowing you to monitor its progress..

**Delays:**

– While we aim to meet our delivery estimates, unexpected delays can occur due to external factors like weather, holidays, or carrier issues. We appreciate your understanding and patience.
