TB-500, also known as Thymosin Beta-4, is a peptide composed of 43 amino acids. Studies in animal models suggest that Thymosin Beta-4 promotes the formation of new blood vessels, supports wound healing, reduces inflammation, and lessens oxidative stress in the heart and central nervous system. It plays a vital role in protecting tissues, aiding repair and regeneration, and facilitating the remodeling of damaged areas. Additionally, it is being actively explored for its potential in anti-aging research.
