**GHK-CU**

 

GHK-Cu is a naturally occurring peptide found in human blood plasma, urine, and saliva. Animal research indicates that GHK-Cu enhances wound healing, strengthens immune function, and supports skin health by stimulating collagen production, activating fibroblasts, and encouraging blood vessel formation. Evidence also suggests that it serves as a feedback signal released following tissue injury. Additionally, GHK-Cu helps protect against free-radical damage, making it a powerful antioxidant.
