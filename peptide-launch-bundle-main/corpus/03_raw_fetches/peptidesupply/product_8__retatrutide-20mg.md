Retatrutide is a novel triagonist peptide that simultaneously activates the GLP-1, GIP, and glucagon receptors. By mimicking these endogenous incretin hormones, Retatrutide enhances insulin secretion, suppresses glucagon release, and increases energy expenditure through hepatic and adipose metabolic modulation. Early human and animal studies demonstrate profound effects on glycemic control and body weight reduction, far exceeding the efficacy of traditional mono-agonists. Ongoing trials suggest potential therapeutic roles in obesity, type 2 diabetes, and cardiometabolic disorders due to its ability to optimize metabolic homeostasis and fat oxidation.
