Tirzepatide is a dual incretin receptor agonist that acts on both the glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide (GIP) and glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) receptors. This dual mechanism enhances insulin secretion, improves β-cell function, and reduces appetite and food intake. Clinical studies have demonstrated significant reductions in hemoglobin A1c and body weight in subjects with type 2 diabetes. Tirzepatide’s multimodal action improves metabolic efficiency, insulin sensitivity, and lipid profiles, positioning it as one of the most effective agents in the management of metabolic syndrome and obesity.
