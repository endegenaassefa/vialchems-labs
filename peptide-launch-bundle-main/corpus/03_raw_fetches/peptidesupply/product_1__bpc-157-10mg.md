**BPC-157**

BPC-157 (Body Protection Compound-157) is a 15-amino acid peptide derived from a naturally occurring protective protein found in gastric juice. Extensive preclinical research has shown that BPC-157 accelerates the healing of various tissues, including muscles, tendons, ligaments, and the gastrointestinal tract. Its regenerative effects are mediated through the upregulation of growth factors such as VEGF and FGF, enhancement of angiogenesis, and modulation of nitric oxide pathways. BPC-157 also exhibits strong anti-inflammatory and cytoprotective effects, reducing oxidative stress and protecting organs from toxin-induced damage.
