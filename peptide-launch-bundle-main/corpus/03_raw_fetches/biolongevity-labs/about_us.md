# About Us

## About BioLongevity Labs

BioLongevity Labs is a U.S.-based manufacturer of research-grade peptides and bioregulators. We supply research laboratories, academic institutions, and biotech organizations with analytically verified compounds produced under strict Good Manufacturing Practices.

Every compound we produce is tested by three independent certified laboratories. Researchers can access full documentation with every order, including Certificates of Analysis, HPLC purity reports, and LC-MS molecular confirmation.

All products are intended exclusively for in vitro laboratory research.

### Our Mission

We exist to support serious research. Our mission is to give laboratories and qualified researchers access to the highest-purity, independently verified peptides and bioregulators available — with the analytical documentation to prove it.

Scientific progress depends on compound reliability. We hold every batch to the same standard: 99%+ purity, independently confirmed, no exceptions.

## Quality and Manufacturing

BioLongevity Labs manufactures all compounds in U.S.-based GMP-certified facilities. Our production process follows strict chain of custody protocols from synthesis through packaging.

Every batch goes through a full analytical battery before it ships:

  * HPLC for purity verification
  * LC-MS for molecular identity confirmation
  * Chemical contaminant screening



Certificates of Analysis from all three independent labs are available for review before purchase at [biolongevitylabs.com/all-coas/](https://biolongevitylabs.com/all-coas/)

### Triple Third-Party Testing

BioLongevity Labs is the only research peptide supplier that independently verifies every batch through three separate certified laboratories.

This is not a marketing claim — it is a process. Three labs. Three independent results. One standard: 99%+ purity.

Researchers can access the full analytical dossier for any compound at any time on our website or by contacting help@biolongevitylabs.com.

## American Peptide Association Member

BioLongevity Labs is a member of the American Peptide Association (APA), the industry organization dedicated to compliance, education, and the responsible advancement of peptide research.

APA membership reflects our commitment to operating at the highest standards within the research peptide industry. The APA brings together manufacturers, researchers, and compliance professionals to promote best practices, regulatory awareness, and scientific accountability.

[Learn more about the American Peptide Association](https://americanpeptide.co/).

## Dr. Ky H. Le, MD — Scientific Reviewer

Dr. Ky Le serves as our scientific reviewer, ensuring all content meets rigorous standards of scientific accuracy and research validity.

Dr. Le earned his medical doctorate from St. George’s University School of Medicine in 2002 and completed his family medicine residency at Memorial Hermann Southwest Hospital. He is board-certified in family medicine and currently practices in Hawaii, with affiliations at Pali Momi Medical Center and Hawaii Pacific Health Medical Group.

With over 20 years of clinical experience, Dr. Le brings specialized knowledge to his review of research citations, study interpretations, and scientific statements across our content library.

**Dr. Le ’s review responsibilities include:**

  * Reviewing all research citations for accuracy and relevance
  * Validating interpretations of published studies
  * Fact-checking scientific statements across published content
  * Confirming that laboratory application framing is scientifically sound
