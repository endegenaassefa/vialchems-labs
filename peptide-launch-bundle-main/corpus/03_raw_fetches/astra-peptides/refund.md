# Returns & Refunds

##### **Returns Policy******

Due to our rigorous quality control standards and the fragile nature of our products, we cannot accept returns. However, if your order arrives with missing, incorrect, or damaged items, we are here to help. Please email **info@astrapeptides.com**  for assistance.

##### **Cancellations******

Cancellations must be requested by email before the order has shipped. Once the order has shipped, cancellations are no longer possible. If you need to cancel your order, please contact us at **info@astrapeptides.com**  as soon as possible.

##### **Customer Support******

Our customer support team is dedicated to ensuring your satisfaction with our product quality, delivery, and service. If you have any questions or concerns about your order, please don’t hesitate to reach out to us at **info@astrapeptides.com**.

We will do our best to assist you quickly and satisfactorily. Thank you for choosing Astra Peptides.
