## Overview

This blend contains two synthetic peptides: CJC-1295 without DAC (also called Modified GRF 1-29) and Ipamorelin. CJC-1295 without DAC (5mg) is a 29-amino-acid peptide analog of growth hormone-releasing hormone (GHRH). It was developed by modifying four amino acids in the native GHRH(1-29) sequence to increase resistance to enzymatic breakdown. Its molecular formula is C₁₅₂H₂₅₂N₄₄O₄₂ with a molecular weight of approximately 3367 g/mol. Ipamorelin (5mg) is a pentapeptide — five amino acids — with the sequence Aib-His-D-2-Nal-D-Phe-Lys-NH₂ and a molecular weight of approximately 711.86 g/mol. It was first described in 1998.

The blend totals 10mg in a 1:1 ratio, supplied as a white lyophilized powder that dissolves readily in water. Store refrigerated at 2–8 °C. This product is strictly for research purposes only.
