## Overview

This blend contains two synthetic peptides: Tesamorelin and Ipamorelin. Tesamorelin is a 44-amino-acid analog of growth hormone-releasing hormone (GHRH), formed by attaching a trans-3-hexenoic acid group to the tyrosine at position 1 of the native GHRH(1-44) sequence. It has the molecular formula C₂₂₁H₃₆₆N₇₂O₆₇S, a molecular weight of 5135.9 g/mol, and CAS number 901758-09-6. Ipamorelin is a pentapeptide with the sequence Aib-His-D-2-Nal-D-Phe-Lys-NH₂, a molecular weight of 711.86 g/mol, and CAS number 170851-70-4.

Available in two concentrations: an 8mg blend (Tesamorelin 6mg + Ipamorelin 2mg) and a 15mg blend (Tesamorelin 10mg + Ipamorelin 5mg). Supplied as a white to off-white lyophilized powder, water-soluble, stored refrigerated at 2–8 °C. This product is strictly for research purposes only.

**PubChem Reference:** [View compound profile on NIH PubChem](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/16137828)
