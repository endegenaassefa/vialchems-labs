## Handling, Storage & Reconstitution

What is reconstitution?

Reconstitution is the process of adding liquid to lyophilized peptide powder to create a solution for research use. We do not provide detailed reconstitution protocols or individualized instructions.

Do you provide dosing or reconstitution guides?

We may provide general research tools such as a calculator for reference purposes, but we do not provide medical use instructions, dosing advice, or individualized reconstitution guidance.

How should I store peptides?

Lyophilized products should be stored under cool, dark, long-term research conditions, with freezer storage commonly used for extended preservation. After reconstitution, products typically require proper refrigerated laboratory handling.

How are Royal Peptides products stored and shipped?

Products are packaged in research-ready vial format and shipped in protective packaging designed to maintain condition during normal transit. Storage after delivery remains the responsibility of the purchaser.

How are products packaged?

Orders are shipped in discreet packaging. Lyophilized products are generally stable enough for standard transit conditions and do not typically require cold-pack shipping under normal circumstances.
