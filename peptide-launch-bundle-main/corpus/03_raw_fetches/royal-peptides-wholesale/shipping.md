  * We reserve the right to cancel or decline any order at our discretion



SHIPPING POLICY

We aim to ship orders promptly using available shipping carriers. Delivery timelines are estimates only and are not guaranteed.

  * Transit times may vary based on destination, customs, weather, and carrier operations

  * Customers are solely responsible for understanding import rules in their jurisdiction

NO REFUND POLICY

Due to the nature of research materials, all sales are final unless otherwise stated in writing.

  * Any exception is made solely at our discretion



DAMAGED / INCORRECT ORDERS

If you believe your order arrived damaged or incorrect, contact us promptly with clear supporting information.
