### SCAM - AUSTRALIAN COMPANY

I work at a doctor's office in Tennessee. We received a flyer from FAX # 650-541-0475 advertising semaglutide and tirzepatide injections for the low cost of $150 or $250 for our patients. I was interested in the injections for myself so I asked my doctor to send a prescription for me. She faxed it at 1:00PM and by 1:21PM I got a TOLL FREE CALL from 1-833-851-9040 from "Daniella." She asked me how many vials I wanted and said she would email me an invoice so I could pay. 24 Hours later I got an "invoice" from [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection) and a link to pay through Paypal. I don't know of any medical company that accepts payments through Paypal so at that time I decided to research this company. While their website seems totally legit, now I realize a few things. They list an address on their website making it look like their company is in Wyoming, United States, but it is NOT. The address they have listed is just for a US Post Office location. They are really located in Australia and when you look at the Privacy Policy on their website, it confirms this, saying they follow the Australian Privacy Principles. I'm not sure whether I would have received any real product from them or not if I had paid. Some reviews here say they got nothing and one review said he got hot, unusable product. Even if they were to send some product, it couldn't remain refrigerated coming all the way from another country. I would be extremely hesitant to use any product from this company due to how misleading they are. I'm not sure if they are accredited or regulated or anything like that. For all we know, some random person in a basement in Australia could be getting our information and payment, and then just send out a random vial of saline or something like that. It's unfortunate how legit this looked. If they had asked for my payment by phone rather than sending me that paypal link I think I would have fell for this scam. I've tried to upload the flyer we were sent, the invoice they emailed me, a copy of the paypal link, etc but this website says it will not accept the files. Just be advised Total Compounding Pharmaceuticals is NOT in the US and probably not regulated in any way.
