###  What payment methods do you accept?

###  How long does shipping take? 

Shipping times vary based on location. Domestic orders typically arrive within 3-5 business days, while international orders may take 7-14 business days.

###  Do you provide tracking information? 

Yes, once your order is shipped, you will receive a tracking number via email.

###  How should I store peptides? 

Peptides should be stored in a **cool, dark place**. For long-term storage, keep them in a freezer at **-20°C**. Once reconstituted, store in the refrigerator.

###  How long do peptides last? 

Lyophilized (dry) peptides can last for **years** if stored properly. Once mixed with a solvent, their stability is reduced, so use them within a few weeks.
