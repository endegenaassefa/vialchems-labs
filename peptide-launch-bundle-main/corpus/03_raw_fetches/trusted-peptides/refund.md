# Refund policy

#### Refund and Returns

#### Customer Satisfaction

At **Trusted Peptides** , we take pride in the quality of our products and are confident that you will be satisfied with your purchase.

If you are not completely satisfied, simply email us at [**info@trustedpeptides.store**](mailto:info@trustedpeptides.store) , and we will address your issue at priority.

#### Returns & Exchanges

  * Due to the **sensitive nature** of our products for research and lab use, all purchases are **final** and **non-refundable**.
  * **No returns or refunds** are permitted unless the product received differs from what was ordered.
  * If an item is received **damaged or incorrect** , contact **customer service at[info@trustedpeptides.store](mailto:info@trustedpeptides.store)** **immediately** for assistance.
  * By placing an order, you **agree** to our returns, refunds, and cancellation policy. Non-compliance may result in order delays or cancellations.



Please note that **opened, altered, or specially handled items** (including those requiring specific temperature conditions) **cannot** be accepted for return due to the potential for degradation and contamination.

#### 

#### Return Eligibility:

  * **Incorrect Products:** If you received an incorrect product, we will cover the cost of return shipping.
  * **Missing Products:** If any items are missing from your order, we will cover the cost of return shipping.



#### Return Process:

  1. Contact us at [**info@trustedpeptides.store**](mailto:info@trustedpeptides.store) within **30 days** of receiving your order.
  2. Provide the reason for the return and include pictures of the product, if applicable.
  3. Our team will review your request and determine whether a refund is applicable.
  4. If the return is approved, we will provide instructions on how to return the item.



#### Refund Process:

  * Once the returned items are verified, we will process your exchange or refund accordingly.
  * Refunds will be issued to the original payment method.



**Note:** Opened items will not be accepted for return.

We appreciate your understanding and strive to ensure a smooth and satisfactory experience for all our customers. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us at [**info@trustedpeptides.store**](mailto:info@trustedpeptides.store).

Thank you for choosing **Trusted Peptides**!
