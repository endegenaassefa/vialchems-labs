At Zyntra Research Labs, we are committed to ensuring customer satisfaction while maintaining the highest standards for research-grade products. This Return & Refund Policy outlines the procedures, conditions, and responsibilities associated with returns and refunds. By placing an order with us, you agree to the practices described below.

**Eligibility for Returns**  
All products supplied by Zyntra Research Labs are intended strictly for research purposes and are handled under controlled conditions to ensure purity and quality. Due to the nature of our products, returns are generally accepted only in cases of shipping errors, defective items, or damaged products received during transit. Products must be returned in their original packaging and in the condition they were received.

**Non-Returnable Items**  
Products that have been opened, used, or improperly stored are not eligible for return. Additionally, orders that have been customized, specifically labeled, or produced in limited batches may not qualify for a refund unless the issue is caused by our error. Research-use-only materials cannot be returned for reasons unrelated to product quality or shipment.

**Return Process**  
To initiate a return, customers must contact Zyntra Research Labs as soon as possible after receiving the order. Our team will provide instructions regarding the return process, including packaging requirements, documentation, and shipping instructions. Products returned without prior authorization or not following the outlined procedure may not be accepted.

**Refunds**  
Once a returned product is received and inspected, Zyntra Research Labs will determine eligibility for a refund. Approved refunds will be processed using the original payment method, excluding any shipping or handling fees. Refund processing times may vary depending on the payment provider, but we aim to complete all refunds within a reasonable timeframe.

**Damaged or Defective Products**  
If a product arrives damaged or is found to be defective, please report the issue immediately upon receipt. We may request photographs or other documentation to verify the condition of the product. Zyntra Research Labs will provide a replacement, repair, or refund for eligible items at our discretion.

**Shipping Costs for Returns**  
Unless the return is due to an error or defective product from Zyntra Research Labs, customers are responsible for return shipping costs. We recommend using a trackable shipping method to ensure safe and timely return of the product.

**Policy Updates**  
Zyntra Research Labs reserves the right to update or modify this Return & Refund Policy at any time. Any changes will be effective immediately upon posting on our website. Continued use of our website or purchase of products constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

**Contact Us**  
If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding returns, refunds, or product issues, please refer to the **Contact Us page** for further communication.
