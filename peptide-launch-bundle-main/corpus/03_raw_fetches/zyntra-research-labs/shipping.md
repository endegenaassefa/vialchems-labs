At Zyntra Research Labs, we are committed to providing reliable and efficient shipping services for all our research-grade products. This Shipping Policy outlines the terms, procedures, and responsibilities associated with the shipment of orders to ensure transparency and a smooth delivery experience for our customers. By placing an order with us, you agree to the practices described below.

**Order Processing**  
All orders are processed after payment confirmation and verification of order details. We make every effort to process and ship orders promptly, typically within 1–3 business days. Certain products may require additional handling or verification due to regulatory requirements or batch-specific checks, which may extend processing times. Customers will be informed in case of any significant delays.

**Shipping Methods and Delivery Times**  
We use trusted shipping carriers to deliver our products safely and efficiently. Delivery times may vary depending on the shipping method selected, destination, and carrier schedules. Estimated delivery times are provided at checkout but are not guaranteed, as delays may occur due to customs clearance, local holidays, or unforeseen circumstances beyond our control.

**Shipping Costs**  
Shipping fees are calculated based on order weight, destination, and selected delivery method. Any applicable taxes, duties, or customs charges imposed by the destination country are the responsibility of the recipient. Customers are encouraged to review local import regulations before placing an order to ensure smooth delivery.

**Order Tracking**  
Once an order is shipped, a tracking number or shipment confirmation will be provided when available. This allows customers to monitor the delivery status of their packages and plan accordingly. Please note that some carriers may take additional time to update tracking information.

**Risk of Loss**  
Risk of loss or damage to products passes to the customer once the shipment is handed over to the carrier. Zyntra Research Labs ensures proper packaging and handling to minimize the risk of damage during transit. Customers are advised to inspect packages upon receipt and report any visible damage immediately.

**International Shipping**  
For international orders, customs regulations and import laws vary by country. Customers are responsible for compliance with all local regulations, including any duties, taxes, or restrictions applicable to the shipment. Zyntra Research Labs is not responsible for delays or additional charges caused by customs clearance processes.

**Incorrect or Incomplete Information**  
Please ensure that all shipping information provided is accurate and complete. Incorrect or incomplete addresses may result in delayed or failed delivery, and any additional shipping costs incurred due to errors will be the responsibility of the customer.

**Lost or Delayed Shipments**  
While we strive to deliver all orders on time, Zyntra Research Labs cannot guarantee delivery dates. In the event of a lost or significantly delayed shipment, customers are encouraged to contact the shipping carrier directly. We will provide reasonable support to assist in locating or resolving shipment issues.

**Contact Us**  
If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding shipping or delivery, please refer to the **Contact Us page** for further communication.
