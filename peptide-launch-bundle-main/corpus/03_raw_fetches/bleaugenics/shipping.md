# Shipping policy

At BleauGenics, we are committed to prompt and secure shipping of all research-grade products. This Shipping Policy outlines our practices for order processing, delivery, and tracking. By placing an order, you agree to the terms outlined below.

* * *

**1\. Order Processing**

  * Orders are typically processed within **1–2 business days** of payment confirmation.

  * Orders placed on weekends or holidays will be processed the following business day.

  * You will receive a confirmation email once your order has been processed and shipped.




* * *

**2\. Shipping Methods & Carriers**

  * We offer standard and expedited shipping options via trusted carriers including USPS, UPS, and FedEx.

  * Shipping options and rates are calculated at checkout based on your location and order weight.




* * *

**3\. Domestic Shipping (U.S. Only)**

  * Shipping is available to all 50 U.S. states.

  * Estimated delivery time is 4**–7 business days** for standard shipping and 3**–4 business days** for expedited options.  





* * *

**4\. International Shipping**

  * At this time, **BleauGenics does not offer international shipping**.

  * If this changes, we will update our policies accordingly.




* * *

**5\. Shipping Restrictions**

  * We cannot deliver to regions where the products we offer are restricted or prohibited by law.

  * It is the customer’s responsibility to be aware of and comply with local regulations regarding the receipt of research chemicals.




* * *

**6\. Lost, Stolen, or Damaged Packages**

  * Once a package is in transit with the carrier, BleauGenics is **not responsible** for lost or stolen parcels.

  * If your package is marked as delivered but not received, please contact the carrier directly to file a claim.

  * For products damaged in transit, please email contact us within **48 hours** of delivery with clear photos of the damage for assistance.




* * *

**7\. Address Accuracy**

  * Please ensure your shipping address is entered correctly at checkout. We are not responsible for orders shipped to incorrect or incomplete addresses.

  * If your order has not yet shipped, contact us immediately to request a correction.




* * *

**8\. Tracking Information**

  * Once your order ships, you will receive an email with tracking details.

  * You can track your shipment via the carrier’s website using the provided tracking number.
