## **Return & Refund Policy**

  


**Effective Date:** 5/10/2025

  


At **BleauGenics** , we take pride in supplying high-quality, research-grade compounds. Due to the sensitive nature of our products, we maintain a strict return and refund policy to preserve product integrity, regulatory compliance, and customer safety.

* * *

### **1\. All Sales Are Final**

  


All purchases from BleauGenics are **non-returnable and non-refundable**. Once an order has been placed and shipped, it cannot be canceled, returned, or exchanged under any circumstance.

  


This policy exists to:

  * Protect the sterility and purity of research compounds

  * Preserve the chain of custody for compliance and safety

  * Prevent risks related to improper handling or storage




* * *

### **2\. Exceptions for Damaged or Incorrect Shipments**

  


In the rare event you receive a damaged package or incorrect item:

  * Notify us at **support@bleaugenics.com** within **48 hours** of delivery

  * Include your **order number** and **clear photographic evidence**




  


Upon review, we may offer a **replacement or partial refund at our discretion**. Claims submitted beyond 48 hours or involving **cosmetic packaging flaws** that do not affect product quality will not be eligible for resolution.

* * *

### **3\. Lost or Stolen Packages**

  


BleauGenics is **not responsible** for packages that are lost, stolen, or misdelivered after they have been transferred to the shipping carrier. Customers are encouraged to:

  * Monitor tracking updates

  * Contact the carrier directly in case of delivery issues




* * *

### **4\. Chargebacks and Payment Disputes**

  


Filing a chargeback without contacting us first may result in a **permanent ban from future purchases**. We strongly encourage customers to reach out directly so we can investigate and resolve any issues fairly.

* * *

### **5\. Contact for Claims**

  


All return-related claims or concerns must be submitted via email:

  


**support@bleaugenics.com******

(Official inbox for all order-related inquiries)

  


We typically respond within **1–2 business days**.
