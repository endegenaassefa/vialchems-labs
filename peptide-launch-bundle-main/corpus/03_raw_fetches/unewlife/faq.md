##### Is TFA removed during your processing?

Yes, all our peptides are TFA-free.

The lab eliminates TFA to prevent any adverse effects. TFA (trifluoroacetic acid) is widely used in peptide chemistry, especially during synthesis and purification. However, it’s typically removed or neutralized before using peptides in biological assays or experiments. Here’s why:

  1. **Toxicity** : TFA is toxic and can harm cells or organisms in biological tests.

  2. **Acidic Nature** : It’s highly acidic and disrupts biological reactions.

  3. **Interference** : TFA disrupts mass spectrometry and NMR spectroscopy, complicating data analysis.




Methods to remove TFA include solvent evaporation, solid-phase extraction, ion-exchange chromatography, dialysis, and buffer exchange. Removing TFA ensures safe and precise peptide research.
