### **WHO WE ARE**

### 

Be Bold. Be You.

At Mew Mews, we're inspired by the beauty and individuality of women everywhere. Our brand was founded by a group of women who saw a need in the fashion industry for clothing that not only looked great, but made women feel great too. 

### 

Our Mission

Our mission is to inspire and uplift women everywhere, one piece of clothing at a time.

### 

Our Vision

To empower women worldwide through fashion and build a community of confident, beautiful, and unstoppable women who celebrate their individuality and support each other.
