# DISPATCH TIME

Our products are **made to order,** which means we create your item **after** your order has been placed. To ensure the highest quality, our products are all handmade with love and attention to details. At this time, please expect your order to ship out in **3-5 business days**. For items labeled as **handcrafted** , they are carefully stitched and require **7-15 business days** for production before dispatch. For **pre-orders** , please expect a production time of **15-30 working days** as we require this time to prepare the materials.

Good things take time, and we assure you that our products are all worth waiting for. You will receive an email with tracking number once your order is shipped out! 

# HOW TO CANCEL MY ORDER?

It may be possible to cancel an order within **2 hours** of it being placed. Requests received within this timeframe will be processed free of charge. Please email us at **info@mewmews.com** to request a cancellation. 

Cancellation requests submitted after 2 hours will incur a **5% cancellation fee**. Kindly note that we charge 5% of the total order value for the order cancellation processing fee. Once the order begins processing in our system, we may be unable to amend it. Please take extra care when placing an order, as we do not accept any changes on orders that have already been processed. By placing an order, you expressly agree to follow and abide by these terms.

## **Notice for Shipments to French Overseas Territories**

Due to a new tax policy introduced by the French authorities effective March 1, 2026, shipments sent to certain French overseas territories may be subject to a small parcel tax during customs clearance. This regulation applies to deliveries to the following regions:   
• Guadeloupe (GP)   
• Monaco (MC)   
• Martinique (MQ)  
• Réunion (RE)   
A handling tax of €2 per parcel may be charged by local customs or postal authorities. This fee is determined and collected by the local government or delivery carrier and must be paid by the recipient before the parcel can be released for delivery. Please note that this fee is not charged by our store and is outside of our control.  
If you have any questions regarding this policy, please feel free to contact our customer support team.
