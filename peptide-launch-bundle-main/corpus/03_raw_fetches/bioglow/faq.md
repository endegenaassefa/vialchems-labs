# FAQ

What is BioGlow, and who is it for? 

BioGlow is an educational platform providing exclusive access to research materials and informational tools. Our space is designed for individuals and organizations engaged in research. We believe that education should be at the forefront of any research, and we are here for you!

How does BioGlow operate differently than traditional stores? 

BioGlow is intentionally designed as an education-first platform. Because we provide access to biochemical products for research, we prioritize transparency, quality, and safety unlike other transaction-based stores. Our approval-based access model ensures that our compounds are used in alignment with their intended research-only purpose. This approach helps us create a more responsible and trustworthy experience for you!

Does BioGlow offer medical advice?​ 

No. BioGlow does not provide medical advice, nor guide treatment or cure/prevent disease or illness. ALWAYS consult a qualified healthcare provider in regards to health-related regimens or questions.

Does BioGlow test research materials? 

Yes – each batch is manufactured in certified facilities with strict purity and handling protocols. Each batch is third party tested, and COAs (Certificate of Analysis) are readily available. Rest assured, BioGlow strives to only provide quality you can trust. View the COA library here: [COA Library](https://bioglow.info/coa-library/)

How does research access work? 

The research catalog is accessible on BioGlow’s home page. Access is provided through an approval-based process to ensure appropriate use and understanding by consumers.

What should I expect after research access approval? 

Once research access is approved and confirmed, materials are prepared and fulfilled according to our internal processing and handling timelines. Estimated fulfillment details are shared directly following approval.

What is your refund policy? 

BioGlow is committed to making sure our customers are 100% satisfied. Research requests are reviewed individually in accordance with our Refund Policy.

Are any research materials for human use? 

**No.** All BioGlow products are strictly for laboratory and research purposes only. They are **not** intended for human consumption. BioGlow does **not** offer medical advice or make any medical claims to prevent, cure, or treat diseases.

How should research materials be stored? 

To maintain integrity, store research materials in a cool, dry place, preferably at 2°C – 8°C (35.6°F – 46.4°F), and away from light. For long-term storage, store products in freezer at -20°C (-4°F), but avoid freezing and thawing cycles to keep stability.

What does "research-use only" mean? 

BioGlow’s catalog of research materials are not approved for human or animal use and are intended solely for research and educational purposes.

Do you ship internationally? 

Currently, BioGlow only ships within the United States. Digital downloads and education can be accessed worldwide.
