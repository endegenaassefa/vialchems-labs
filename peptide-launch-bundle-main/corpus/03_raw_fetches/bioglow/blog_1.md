## What Are Peptides Anyway?! (And Why I Brought Them to the Midwest)

If you can answer the question, “What are peptides?” – you’re still going to want to hear this one!

Peptides are **t****iny messengers** telling your body when to do certain things – whether that involves conserving energy, releasing more hormones, or really, any natural process the body already does on a regular basis.

It’s a misconception that they’re some new phenomenon. Peptides have been around for a **long** time & studied extensively in laboratories across the world. But, why are so many people talking about them now?! Well, I have a few theories here ..

## Pepfluencers

Life, as we know it, revolves around social media, right? From Idaho 4 conspiracies to Scandavol, information spreads like wildfire through these different platforms. The peptide industry is no different. While social media brings more awareness to the space, it also makes it more important than ever to slow down, ask questions, and differentiate between the actual research and misinformation.

## Preventative Focus

There’s also been a noticeable shift in how people think about their health. Instead of waiting for something to go wrong, more people are starting to ask, _“what can I do now to feel better, longer?”_ That mindset alone has opened the door for a lot of curiosity around peptides.

## Accessibility

Another big reason peptides are suddenly everywhere? Information is more accessible than ever. What used to live in research papers, niche forums, or clinical settings is now being discussed openly across platforms, making it easier for people to learn, explore, and form their own opinions.

## Why the Midwest?

Big cities, like Miami, Los Angeles, Scottsdale, have been utilizing the power of peptides for 15+ years. Here in the Midwest? Most people still think they’re steroids.

The peptide rabbit hole with a desire to help/educate others is the exact equation that led me to building BioGlow. I’m from the Midwest, and I knew this science needed to be talked about. Why? Because it just wasn’t!

Regardless of whether you actually jump in headfirst or not, my goal is to **educate** & make the **correct information accessible** to ones that just don’t know. As they become more talked about, the need for clear information is _crucial_.

The more you know, the more you glow. Glow responsibly.
