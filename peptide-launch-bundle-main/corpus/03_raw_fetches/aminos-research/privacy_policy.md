## Privacy Policy

**What data do we gather?**

We obtain details from you when you register on our website, make a purchase, or join our mailing list.

If you decide to order or sign up on our platform, you might be prompted to provide details such as your name, email address, residential address, contact number, and payment details. However, you can also explore our website without providing any personal information.

**Why do we collect your data?**

The primary purpose of gathering your data is to facilitate order processing and ensure top-notch customer service. We ensure that your data remains confidential and isn’t shared with any external entities, unless it’s crucial for delivering the products or services you’ve acquired. For instance, to ensure smooth delivery, your address will be mentioned on the package for the courier service.

**How do we safeguard your details?**

We have several protective mechanisms in place to keep your personal data secure when you make a purchase or access your profile:

  * We employ 256-bit SSL encryption technology to deter unauthorized access to your details.
  * Our servers do not retain any of your payment details.
  * We prioritize secure communication methods.



**Do we utilize cookies?**

Indeed, we employ browser cookies to retain items in your cart until you finalize your order.

**Do we share your data with external entities?**

We never sell, exchange, or disclose your identifiable details to third parties. However, this excludes reliable third-party services that help us in managing our website and serving our users, under the condition that these third parties treat your information as confidential.

**Compliance with the Children ’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)**

In adherence to COPPA regulations, we do not gather details from individuals below the age of 13. Our services and offerings cater to those aged 18 and above.

**Terms of Service**

For more details on usage, disclaimers, and liability restrictions related to our website, please refer to our Terms and Conditions page at [https://aminosresearch.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20231010035221/https://aminosresearch.com/) for comprehensive guidelines.

**Acknowledgment**

Using our platform indicates your acceptance of our data protection policies.

**Modifications in Privacy Guidelines**

Should we make any amendments to our data protection policies, the updates will be published on this page.
