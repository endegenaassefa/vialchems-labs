# Return & Replacement Policy

**Last Updated:** December 2025

At BioGenix Peptides™, we prioritize quality and precision. Due to the sensitive nature of research chemicals, all sales are final.

* * *

## No Returns

We cannot accept returns of:

  * Opened products
  * Unopened products
  * Temperature-sensitive materials
  * Any research-grade chemicals



* * *

## Eligibility for Replacement or Store Credit

We may issue a replacement or credit if:

  * Wrong item received
  * Product arrived damaged
  * Package lost in transit (verified with the carrier)



You must notify us within 72 hours of delivery with supporting evidence including photos, order number, and a description.

* * *

## Lost Packages

If the carrier confirms loss after investigation, we will reship or issue credit.

We are not responsible for:

  * Incorrect addresses
  * Packages marked “Delivered” by the carrier



* * *

## Temperature-Sensitive Research Materials

Products requiring cold storage must be promptly refrigerated upon delivery. Failure to do so voids replacement eligibility.

* * *

## Chargebacks

Fraudulent or unjustified chargebacks may result in:

  * Account suspension
  * Reporting to payment processors
  * Legal action if abuse is detected
