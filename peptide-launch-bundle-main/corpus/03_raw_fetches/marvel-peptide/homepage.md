## Frequently Asked Questions

__ __ What types of peptides do you manufacture?

We specialize in a variety of high-purity peptides, including custom peptides, research-grade peptides, cosmetic peptides, and peptide lyophilized powders and raw materials for pharmaceutical applications.

__ __ Why do your products say: “Research Use Only”?

In the United States, only licensed pharmacies can compound peptides and resell them for human use.

Peptides in powder form can still be sold, however, they must be marked “Research Use Only,” for legal and liability reasons.

PLEASE be aware that self administering “research” peptides could be dangerous! There’s a reason certain compounds are available ONLY under the careful supervision of a doctor. We do not want to see anyone harmed by misusing our products.

Furthermore, we cannot provide medical advice of any kind. Please be safe.

__ __ What should I do if my package is lost or detained by customs?

In some countries, we have the capability to ship directly without going through customs. Furthermore, if a package is lost or detained by customs, we will resend it once free of charge.

__ __ How is the product packaged to ensure stability?

Our peptides are lyophilized (freeze-dried) and sealed in sterile vials. Products are shipped with appropriate insulation and temperature control based on destination and climate.

__ __ Where are you shipping from?

We have two warehouses, one in Hong Kong and one in the United States. We match orders according to their destinations and then ship them to various parts of the world for delivery.
