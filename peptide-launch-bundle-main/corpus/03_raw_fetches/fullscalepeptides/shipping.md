## Shipping & Returns

## At **Full Scale Peptides** , fast and reliable shipping is a priority. We are committed to providing high-quality research-use-only peptides and ensuring each order is handled with care.

* * *

### **30-Day Money-Back Guarantee (Defective Vials Only)**

If you receive a **defective vial** , you may be eligible for a **replacement or full refund** within **30 days** of purchase.

**Defects may include:**

Broken or cracked vials

Leakage during transit

Improper sealing

Compromised sterility

Proof of the defect (photos and/or video) may be required prior to approval.

* * *

### **How to Request a Refund or Replacement**

To initiate a request, please contact us with your order details:

**Email:** support@fullscalepeptides.com

Once your request is reviewed, we will provide return or replacement instructions if applicable.

* * *

### **Return Shipping**

###  If a product is confirmed to be defective or damaged, **Full Scale Peptides will cover return shipping costs**.

### Returned items must be shipped using a **trackable shipping method** to ensure proper delivery.

* * *

### **Refund Processing**

###  Approved refunds are issued to the **original payment method** after inspection of the returned item (if required).

### Please allow **several business days** for the refund to appear, depending on your payment provider.

* * *

### **Shipping Issues**

If you experience a shipping-related issue such as a delay, misdelivery, or lost package:

A reshipment may be processed **7 business days after the original estimated delivery date** , once carrier confirmation is received.

Reshipments are issued only after verification of the shipment status with the carrier.

* * *

### **Order Cancellations or Modifications**

###  Orders may be **modified or canceled within 1 hour of purchase**.

### After 1 hour, or once an order has shipped, **orders cannot be changed or canceled**.

* * *

### **Shipping Policy**

###  We currently ship to **U.S. addresses only**.

### Orders are shipped via USPS or FedEx, depending on location and service availability.

* * *

### **Carrier Delays**

Full Scale Peptides is not responsible for shipping delays caused by:

Weather conditions

Carrier delays or service disruptions

Other factors outside our control

* * *

### **Address Accuracy**

Please ensure your shipping address is entered correctly at checkout.

We are not responsible for delivery issues resulting from **incorrect or incomplete addresses** provided by the customer.

* * *

### **Delivery Confirmation**

If tracking information shows your package as **delivered** but you have not received it:

Please contact the carrier (USPS or FedEx) directly.

We are happy to assist with filing a claim; however, **Full Scale Peptides is not responsible for lost or stolen packages once delivery is confirmed**.

* * *

### **Important Notice**

All products sold by Full Scale Peptides are intended **for laboratory research use only**.  
Not for human or veterinary consumption.
