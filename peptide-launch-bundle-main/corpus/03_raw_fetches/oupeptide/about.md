## About Us

**Oupeptide Pharmaceutical** is a high-tech enterprise specializing in the research, development, and production of **peptides, proteins, and small-molecule active substances**. We are committed to providing high-purity, high-stability products for the global pharmaceutical, biotechnology, research, and health industries, supporting innovative drug development and life science research.

With advanced technologies such as **Solid Phase Peptide Synthesis (SPPS), Liquid Phase Peptide Synthesis (LPPS), and High-Performance Liquid Chromatography (HPLC)** , we ensure that every product achieves a purity level of **99% or higher** , meeting international pharmaceutical standards. Our strict adherence to **GMP and ISO quality management systems** guarantees stable and safe products, offering precise and reliable solutions for global clients.

Guided by the philosophy of **“Innovation-Driven, Quality First”** , we continue to expand the application of our products across industries, including **biopharmaceuticals, medical aesthetics, scientific research, and sports nutrition**. Additionally, we provide **custom synthesis services** , catering to clients’ specific needs in drug development and functional ingredient production.

We look forward to collaborating with partners worldwide to drive advancements in biopharmaceuticals and life sciences, creating a healthier future through technological innovation.
