# Shipping policy

  


Vantyx Research processes orders as quickly as possible. Orders are typically processed within **1–3 business days** after payment is confirmed.

Shipping times may vary depending on your location and the shipping method selected at checkout. Once your order has shipped, tracking information will be provided when available.

We are not responsible for delays caused by carriers, customs, or factors outside of our control. Please ensure that your shipping information is accurate, as we are not responsible for orders delivered to incorrect addresses provided by the customer.

VANTYX is not responsible for lost or stolen packages once marked delivered by USPS.
