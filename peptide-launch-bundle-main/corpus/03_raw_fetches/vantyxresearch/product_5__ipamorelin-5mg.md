#### **IPAMORELIN – 5MG**

**Ipamorelin** is a synthetic research peptide classified as a selective growth hormone secretagogue that interacts with ghrelin receptors. In laboratory environments, Ipamorelin is commonly studied for its role in **growth hormone signaling pathways and endocrine response mechanisms**.

Researchers may utilize Ipamorelin in **in vitro laboratory protocols** to examine peptide receptor interactions, metabolic signaling pathways, and hormonal regulation models during controlled experimental studies.

This product is supplied strictly for **laboratory research purposes only** and is **not intended for human or veterinary use**.
