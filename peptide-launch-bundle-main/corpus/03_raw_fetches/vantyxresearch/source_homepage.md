## Frequently Asked Questions

__What is Vantyx Research?

Vantyx Research is a science-driven research brand focused on precision, consistency, and uncompromising quality standards. We prioritize clear labeling, transparency, and reliable customer experience.

__Are your products intended for human or animal use?

No. All products sold by Vantyx Research are intended for research and laboratory use only. They are not intended for human or veterinary use, and are not sold as dietary supplements.

__How do you ensure product quality?

We follow strict handling and quality-control processes focused on consistency and accuracy. Where available, we provide batch-related documentation and clear labeling to support transparency.

__Do you provide Certificates of Analysis (COAs)?

When available, COAs or supporting documentation may be provided on the product page or upon request. Availability can vary by product and batch.

__How do I place an order?

Add your selected product(s) to cart, proceed to checkout, and complete payment. You’ll receive an order confirmation email immediately after purchase.

__How long does shipping take?

Orders are typically processed within 1–2 business days. Shipping times depend on your location and the carrier selected at checkout. Tracking details are sent once your order ships.

__Do you offer discreet packaging?

Yes. We use discreet, professional packaging and do not include unnecessary branding on the exterior.

__Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?

If your order has not shipped yet, we may be able to help. Contact us as soon as possible with your order number at vantyxresearch@gmail.com

__What is your return policy?

Due to the nature of research products and quality assurance, all sales are final once shipped. If your order arrives damaged or incorrect, contact us within 48 hours of delivery and we’ll make it right.
