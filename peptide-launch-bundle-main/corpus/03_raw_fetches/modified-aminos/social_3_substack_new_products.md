**A note on the testing**

Flawless was the first vendor I saw use Ethos Analytics, but it looks like more are starting to adopt them. What makes this lab notable for nasal sprays is that they can test for net content in the bottle — not just identity and purity, but actual measured dose.

The COA for the KPV spray shows 10.31mg in the bottle against a 10mg spec. That’s confirmed via HPLC with identity verified by retention time concordance against a certified reference standard (USP <621>). Bacterial endotoxins came back at less than 1.25 EU/mL against a spec of less than 5 EU/mL.

This is the level of testing I think every vendor selling pre-made nasal sprays should be doing. Knowing the stated dose and the actual measured dose are two very different things — it’s good to see vendors closing that gap.
