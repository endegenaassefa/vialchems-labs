About Modified Aminos
Modified Aminos is a U.S.-based supplier of research-use-only (RUO) laboratory materials, serving qualified researchers and institutions worldwide. The company focuses on providing high-quality peptides, bioregulators, and related laboratory solutions manufactured under GMP-aligned procedures.

All products are intended strictly for laboratory research purposes and are not for human or animal use.
