##### [RU-58841](https://www.vantanex.com/hair-loss)  
Developed in 1980's as a topical solution for male alopecia, RU58841 is a non-steroidal antiandrogen. Other names include PSK3841 or HMR 3841. Initial trials showed amazing potential to treat androgenic alopecia even at small doses. So how does it work? RU58841 binds to DHT receptors thereby preventing the binding of DHT, which is known to cause male pattern baldness.
