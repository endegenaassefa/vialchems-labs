## **Compliance & Safety Statement**

AussieBio Labs supplies research-use-only laboratory materials intended solely for non-clinical scientific work. Our products are not medicines, supplements, or therapeutic goods, and must not be used as such.

## **Research Use Only**

All products sold by AussieBio Labs are intended strictly for **laboratory research use**.  
They are:

  * **Not for human consumption**

  * **Not for animal use**

  * **Not for medical or therapeutic use**

  * **Not for diagnostic or clinical application**

  * **For in-vitro, assay development, analytical, and educational research only**




Customers must agree to our Research Use Only conditions at checkout before completing an order.

## **Purchaser Eligibility**

Purchases are restricted to:

  * Accredited laboratories

  * Qualified research professionals

  * Institutional research personnel




All customers must provide their Research Institution / Laboratory Name and Research Accreditation Number / ID during checkout.

Orders that do not meet research eligibility requirements will not be fulfilled.

## **Safety, Handling & Storage**

Customers are responsible for ensuring:

  * Products are handled only by trained laboratory personnel

  * Appropriate PPE and laboratory safety standards are followed

  * Materials are stored and disposed of in accordance with relevant regulations




AussieBio Labs is not responsible for misuse or unauthorised applications.

## **Legal Compliance**

It is the purchaser’s responsibility to ensure that:

  * Ordering, possession, and use of research materials complies with their local regulations

  * No products are used for human testing, medical treatment, or therapeutic purposes

  * All product handling is lawful and appropriate under relevant laboratory standards




AussieBio Labs reserves the right to cancel any order that appears non-compliant.

## **Contact**

If you have any questions about product handling, research eligibility, or compliance, please contact our team at info@aussiebiolabs.com.au
