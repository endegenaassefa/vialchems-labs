## Shipping & Order Policy

Please review the policy below for details on how we handle shipping and orders. 

## Order Processing Time

● Orders are processed **within 1–2 business days** (Monday–Friday, excluding holidays). ● Orders placed **after business hours, on weekends, or on holidays** will be processed on the**next business day.** ● Once your order ships, you’ll receive a **shipping confirmation email with tracking information.** Processing times are separate from the shipping transit times listed below.

We currently ship **within the United States only.** **Flat-Rate Shipping Options (USD):** **● Standard Shipping:** $10.00 

○ Estimated delivery: 2–5 business days after shipment
**● Second Day Air:** $17.95 

○ Estimated delivery: 2 business days after shipment
**● Next Day Shipping:** $45.00 

○ Estimated delivery: **1 business day** after shipment
Delivery estimates are provided by the carrier and are not guaranteed. Weather, carrier delays, holidays, peak seasons, or other factors outside our control may affect actual delivery times. We do not use weight-based or destination-based pricing—just simple, flat rates. 

## Order Tracking

● Once your order has shipped, we will send you an email containing your **tracking number** and a link to track your package. ● Tracking updates are provided by the carrier and may take several hours to appear after the shipment has been scanned.


## Discreet Packaging

Your privacy is important to us.

● All orders are shipped in **plain, unmarked packaging**.● There are **no product names, logos, or branding** on the outside of the package that would indicate the contents.


## Shipping Availability & Restrictions

● We **only ship within the United States** at this time. **● International shipping is not available.** ● We reserve the right to**limit or refuse shipping** to any address that we determine may be associated with fraud, misuse, or regulatory risk. ● If a package is returned to us due to an incorrect or incomplete address provided at checkout, the customer is responsible for any additional reshipment fees. 

## Delays, Lost, or Damaged Shipments

● Once an order is handed to the carrier, **risk of loss transfers to the customer.**   
● If your package is marked**“delivered”** by the carrier but you did not receive it, please check with neighbors, building staff, or your local post office/UPS location.   
● For packages that are **lost in transit or damaged,** contact the carrier first to open a claim, then reach out to us with your order number and claim details so we can assist where possible.

## Order Changes & Cancellations

● Because we work to process orders quickly,**changes or cancellations may not be possible** once an order is placed.   
● If you need to update or cancel your order, contact us **as soon as possible**. We’ll make every reasonable effort to accommodate your request if the order has not yet entered the shipping process.
