Batch Identification Policy

Sample Authentication Guidance

Our 3mL vials have a 1.25" x 0.75" white, BOPP label. At the top of the label is the full logo with the 'P Vial' symbol on the left, followed Peptide Partners, and underneath it says "CONNECTING THE GLOBAL RESEARCH COMMUNITY." Underneath the logo is a line identifying the molecule and weight. Underneath that is the word 'Batch' followed by an identification code. At the bottom of the label it reads "Research Use Only"
