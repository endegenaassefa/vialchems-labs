## Who Is Peptide Partners?

Peptide Partners is a US-based research peptide supplier operated by Pirsek Technologies, LLC, headquartered in Florida. They position themselves as the #1 rated peptide vendor in overall quality by independent analysis - a claim backed by Finnrick Analytics data showing 59 tested samples across 7 products with A-level ratings on four of them.

What sets Peptide Partners apart from most grey-market suppliers is the depth of their testing program. Rather than relying on a single laboratory or internal-only COAs, they publish four distinct categories of third-party certifications: purity via HPLC, bacterial endotoxin screening per USP <85>, heavy metals analysis per USP standards, and sterility testing. These are conducted by multiple independent labs including TrustPointe, BioRegen, Chromate, and Kovera, with downloadable certificates linked to batch IDs on their public certifications page.

The company runs a curated catalog of 32 products organized by mechanism category: Metabolics, Cellular Repair, Mitochondrial, Vasoactive, Anti-oxidant, Nootropics, Lipotropic, and Reconstitution supplies. They also offer unique pre-built combination vials including Wolverine Stack (BPC-157/TB-500 + CJC-1295/Ipamorelin) and Glow Blend (BPC-157/TB-500/GHK-Cu), which reduce complexity in multi-compound protocols.

Their pricing model emphasizes wholesale cost-per-milligram value with larger vial sizes than many competitors. For example, retatrutide is offered in 24mg vials rather than the 5-10mg formats common elsewhere. Combined with bulk-quantity options, this positions them as cost-effective for researchers running longer protocols. Peptide Partners is not a compounding pharmacy, and all products are sold for research use only.

Peptide Partners is a Finnrick Premium vendor with 59 independently tested samples, one of the largest public sample sets among suppliers commonly compared in PepPal research workflows. Four of seven tested products are rated A (Great), with Ipamorelin showing a 9.9 average across 7 tests.

## Beyond Finnrick: Peptide Partners' Own Testing Program

Many suppliers publish one purity document. Peptide Partners publishes four categories of third-party verification across catalog segments: purity, endotoxin, heavy metals, and sterility. This is one of the broadest self-published verification footprints currently visible in PepPal supplier research.

### Four pillars of verification

  * **Purity / Assay Testing:** HPLC identity and purity analysis showing high-consistency results (often 99%+), with labs including TrustPointe, BioRegen, Chromate, and Kovera.
  * **Endotoxin Testing (USP <85>):**Charles River Endosafe PTS-based screening; reported conforming outcomes and below-quantification thresholds where stated.
  * **Heavy Metal Testing (USP):** ICP-MS residual heavy metals screening with conformance to stated USP limits.
  * **Sterility Testing:** Applied to reconstitution solutions and liquid products using USP-aligned sterility workflows.



### COA verification workflow

Certificates are published on the [Independent Certifications page](/go/peptide-partners), including batch IDs, lab identifiers, and result details. Batch IDs can be matched to vial labels for practical traceability.

Peptide Partners also posts warnings about certificate fraud and problematic lab behavior, which is uncommon in supplier marketing and functions as an additional transparency signal.

For a practical method to validate third-party documentation, use our [COA verification guide](/blog/how-to-read-peptide-coa).
