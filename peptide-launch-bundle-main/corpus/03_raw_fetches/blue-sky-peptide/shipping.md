# Shipping & Returns Policy

This page outlines the official shipping and returns policy for **Blue Sky Peptide** , a trusted supplier of premium research peptides serving the laboratory research community since 2013.

## Order Processing & Shipping Times

Orders placed before **12:00 PM EST** , Monday through Friday (excluding holidays), typically ship the same business day. Orders placed after 12:00 PM EST ship the next business day.

Once your order is processed, tracking information will be emailed when your shipping label is generated. Please allow up to **24 hours** for tracking updates to appear.

You may also track your shipment anytime by visiting [Track My Order](https://www.blueskypeptide.com/sales/guest/form/).

Orders over **$500** require a signature upon delivery. Larger orders may require additional verification prior to shipment.

## Shipping Rates

  * **USPS Ground Advantage:** $6.99 (3–5 business days)
  * **USPS Priority:** $12.99 (2–4 business days)
  * **USPS Express:** $28.99 (1–2 business days)
  * **UPS Next Day Air:** Calculated at checkout (1 business day)



We offer **free USPS shipping on orders over $75**.

Transit times are estimated from the date your order ships and exclude holidays. Blue Sky Peptide does not guarantee delivery timelines once packages are in the possession of third-party carriers such as USPS or UPS.

## Out of Stock Items

If a research peptide is temporarily out of stock and delays your shipment, our team will contact you promptly with an update.

## Returns & Refund Policy

Because our products are sold strictly for laboratory research use, we cannot guarantee research outcomes. Additionally, due to the potential for degradation if products are improperly handled or stored, we are unable to resell items that have been in a purchaser’s possession.

If you experience an issue with a product, you may request a return within **30 days of purchase**. Approved returns are eligible for:

  * Refund minus original shipping charges
  * Exchange for product of equal or lesser value



To initiate a return, please [contact our support team](https://www.blueskypeptide.com/contact/) before sending any items back.

All outgoing orders are documented and photographed prior to shipment to prevent fraudulent claims.

## Order Cancellation

Orders that have not yet shipped may be canceled by contacting [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#bbc8cecbcbd4c9cffbd9d7cedec8d0c2cbdecbcfd2dfde95d8d4d6).

Fraudulent chargebacks may result in account restrictions and further action.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**When do orders ship?**  
Orders placed before 12:00 PM EST on business days typically ship the same day.

**Do you ship on weekends?**  
Orders ship Monday through Friday, excluding holidays.

**What if my tracking does not update?**  
Please allow up to 24 hours for tracking updates after label creation.

**Can I return research peptides?**  
Returns may be approved within 30 days, subject to review and policy guidelines.
