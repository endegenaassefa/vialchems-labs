## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are your products third-party tested?

Yes. We use independent third-party laboratories to test our products, and we provide a Certificate of Analysis for each item. Every product includes a QR code that links directly to its COA so you can verify the results quickly.

### Do you offer any kind of guarantee with your products?

Yes. We offer a 30-day, no-questions-asked guarantee on all unopened products. If you receive an order and there is an issue, we will provide a full refund or store credit once the product is returned.

If a product seal has been broken, we review the situation on a case-by-case basis. Either way, we will work with you to make it right and address your concerns and provide the best method of resolution.

### How fast do you ship?

We ship most orders the same day they are placed, if they are placed before 3pm Eastern time (noon on Saturday). We do not ship on Sundays or holidays. 2-day Air shipping is available for only $10, so we can get your order to you as fast as possible!

### What if my package gets lost / stolen / delayed?

We understand that shipping issues can happen from time to time. If your order is lost or stolen, we will reship it at no cost. Please contact our customer support team via website chat or email support@ezpeps.com to start a claim.

Once we have the required information, we will ship a replacement within 1 business day using the same shipping method selected on the original order. For severely delayed shipments, we can reship if there has been no tracking movement and it has been at least 5 business days since the shipment date.

### Do you offer any coupons or discounts?

The best way to get the best offers is by subscribing to our newsletter. You can do so in the form below. We will also post sales and some coupon offers to our promotions page at https://ezpeps.com/coupons.
