## Description

# GLP-3-RT

 _For Research Use Only | Not for Human or Veterinary Use_

* * *

**Description:**  
GLP-3-RT is an investigational triple receptor agonist consisting of 39 amino acids that targets GLP-1, GIP, and glucagon receptors simultaneously. This compound is investigated in research for enhanced metabolic regulation and weight management through activation of three complementary metabolic pathways. The triple-agonist approach combines the glucose-dependent insulin secretion of GLP-1R, the incretin effects of GIPR, and the hepatic lipid metabolism modulation of GCGR, providing comprehensive metabolic support in experimental models exceeding the efficacy of dual or single-receptor agonists.

* * *

**Scientific Specifications:**

  * **Compound Name:** GLP-3-RT (Triple GLP-1/GIP/Glucagon Receptor Agonist)
  * **Type:** Triple GLP-1/GIP/Glucagon Receptor Agonist
  * **Amino Acid Count:** 39 amino acids
  * **Molecular Weight:** ~4731.33 g/mol
  * **CAS Number:** 2381089-83-2
  * **Form:** Lyophilized powder



* * *

**Research Context:**  
GLP-3-RT is commonly studied for its potential effects on:

  * Triple receptor agonism targeting GLP-1R, GIPR, and GCGR simultaneously
  * Enhanced weight management via combined and synergistic metabolic pathway activation
  * Glycemic control and glucose-dependent insulin modulation mechanisms
  * Hepatic lipid metabolism enhancement and energy expenditure modulation
  * Appetite suppression through complementary and redundant satiety mechanisms
  * Comparative efficacy assessment versus dual-receptor and single-receptor agonist approaches
