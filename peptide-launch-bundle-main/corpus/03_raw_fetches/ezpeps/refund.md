# Return and Refund Policy

** _Effective Date: September 21, 2025_**

At **ezPeps** , we stand behind the quality of our products. If you are not completely satisfied, we offer a **30-day money-back guarantee**  on **unopened**  items, subject to the terms below.

#### 1. **Eligibility**

To be eligible for a return:

  * The item must be **unopened** , unused, and in its original condition.


  * Opened products may only be returned with a prior authorization after submitting a proper support request to [support@ezpeps.com](mailto:support@ezpeps.com).  
 
  * The return request must be submitted within **30 days**  of the delivery date.  
 



#### 2. **Return Process**

To initiate a return, please:

  1. Contact us at **[support@ezpeps.com](mailto:support@ezpeps.com)**  with your order number and reason for return or use the contact form on our webpage.  
 
  2. Wait for confirmation and return instructions.  
 
  3. Ship the product to the address provided in the return instructions. _You are responsible for return shipping costs.  _It is recommended to use a trackable shipment method to ensure safe return. ezPeps is not responsible for any returned items damaged or lost in shipping.  
 



#### 3. **Refunds**

Once we receive and inspect the returned item:

  * If approved, we will issue a **full refund**  (excluding original shipping costs) to your original payment method within 5–7 business days.  
 
  * If the item is not in acceptable condition (e.g., opened, damaged), a refund will not be issued.  
 



#### 4. **Non-Returnable Items**

We cannot accept returns for:

  * Opened or used products  
 
  * Items returned after 30 days  
 
  * Products damaged after delivery due to misuse or improper storage  
 



#### **5\. Damaged or Tampered Packages**

If your order arrives damaged or appears tampered with during shipping, please contact us within 3 days of delivery. To process a damage claim, we require **at least three clear photographs** :

  1. A photo of the **outside of the package** , clearly showing the **shipping label and tracking number**.  
 
  2. A photo showing the **damage or tampering to the exterior**  of the package.  
 
  3. A photo of the **interior packaging and/or contents** , highlighting any damage.  
 



These images help us verify the damage and expedite a resolution. Claims submitted without the required photographs may be delayed or denied. These are the **minimum**  requirements. Providing more images with more detail may aid to expedite a resolution. Once the minimum claim requirements are submitted, we will reship any missing or damaged product within 1 business day. 

#### 6. **Incorrect / Incomplete Orders**

If your order arrives incorrect or incomplete, please contact us within **3 days**  of delivery letting us know what is incorrect or missing. We will work quickly to resolve it. 

For all return inquiries, please email: **[support@ezpeps.com](mailto:support@ezpeps.com) or use the contact form on our website**

#### 7\. Reshipment of lost packages

**We will reship any lost packages. A package is considered lost when one of the following applies:**

  1. The carrier officially declares the package lost under their policy.
     * If the carrier declares the package lost, the customer may request either a reship or a refund. This is the only circumstance in which a refund may be requested instead of a reship.  

  2. The shipment is extraordinarily late.
     * UPS Ground: AFTER 10 business days or more from the ship date.
     * UPS 2-Day Air and UPS Overnight Saver Air: AFTER 5 business days or more from the ship date.



**Eligibility and return requirements**

• The customer must request a reship to be eligible.  
• If the reship is requested because of delays rather than a carrier declaration, the customer must return the original package if it should arrive later using the prepaid return label we provide within 5 business days of receipt.  
• If the customer fails to return the original package within 5 business days of receipt, the customer will forfeit any store credit equal to the order amount and will be permanently disqualified from reship eligibility on future orders.

**Definitions and notes**

• Carrier declaration means the carrier issues an official lost status or claim number.  
• Business days do not include weekends or federal holidays.  
• Replacements are for the original items and ship to the original shipping address only.
