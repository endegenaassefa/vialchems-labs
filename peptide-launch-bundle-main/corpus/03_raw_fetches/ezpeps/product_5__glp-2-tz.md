## Description

# GLP-2-TZ

 _For Research Use Only | Not for Human or Veterinary Use_

* * *

**Description:**  
GLP-2-TZ is a dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonist consisting of 39 amino acids, investigated in research for its synergistic effects on glycemic control, appetite regulation, and metabolic parameter modulation. This compound simultaneously activates both glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide (GIP) and glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) receptors, producing complementary effects on insulin secretion, weight management, and metabolic homeostasis. The dual-agonist approach provides enhanced efficacy compared to single-receptor agonists in experimental metabolic and weight management models.

* * *

**Scientific Specifications:**

  * **Compound Name:** GLP-2-TZ (Dual GIP/GLP-1 Receptor Agonist)
  * **Type:** Dual GIP/GLP-1 Receptor Agonist
  * **Amino Acid Count:** 39 amino acids
  * **Molecular Formula:** C₂₂₅H₃₄₈N₄₈O₆₈
  * **Molecular Weight:** ~4813.45 g/mol
  * **CAS Number:** 2023788-19-2
  * **PubChem CID:** 156588324
  * **Form:** Lyophilized powder



* * *

**Research Context:**  
GLP-2-TZ is commonly studied for its potential effects on:

  * Dual GIP and GLP-1 receptor activation and downstream signaling integration
  * Glycemic control via glucose-dependent insulin secretion potentiation
  * Appetite regulation and body weight reduction through complementary satiety mechanisms
  * Beta-cell function preservation and pancreatic islet signaling dynamics
  * Comparative efficacy assessment versus single-receptor agonist approaches
  * Metabolic syndrome-associated parameter modulation and cardiovascular risk improvement
