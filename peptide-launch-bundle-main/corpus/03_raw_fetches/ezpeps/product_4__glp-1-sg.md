## Description

# GLP-1-SG

 _For Research Use Only | Not for Human or Veterinary Use_

* * *

**Description:**  
GLP-1-SG is a long-acting GLP-1 receptor agonist analogue consisting of 31 amino acids, investigated in research for its effects on glycemic control, appetite suppression, and metabolic regulation. This compound activates GLP-1 receptors on pancreatic beta cells and throughout the central and peripheral nervous system, leading to glucose-dependent insulin secretion enhancement and satiety signaling. The extended half-life enables once-weekly dosing in experimental models, making it suitable for chronic metabolic and weight management research applications.

* * *

**Scientific Specifications:**

  * **Compound Name:** GLP-1-SG (GLP-1 Receptor Agonist Analogue)
  * **Type:** Long-Acting GLP-1 Receptor Agonist
  * **Amino Acid Count:** 31 amino acids
  * **Molecular Formula:** C₁₈₇H₂₉₁N₄₅O₅₉
  * **Molecular Weight:** ~4113.58 g/mol
  * **CAS Number:** 910463-68-2
  * **PubChem CID:** 56843341
  * **Form:** Lyophilized powder



* * *

**Research Context:**  
GLP-1-SG is commonly studied for its potential effects on:

  * GLP-1 receptor activation and downstream cAMP signaling cascade
  * Glycemic control and insulin secretion potentiation in glucose-dependent manner
  * Appetite suppression and body weight regulation via central and peripheral mechanisms
  * Gastric emptying modulation and nutrient absorption dynamics
  * Beta-cell preservation and pancreatic function maintenance in metabolic models
  * Cardiovascular risk marker modulation and metabolic syndrome-associated parameter improvement
