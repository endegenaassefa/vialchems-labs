#  Refund and Returns Policy 

Last Updated: December 2025

## 1\. Shipping Methods

At **PrimePeptide Laboratories Inc.** , we offer **free shipping** on all orders within the United States.

Destination | Standard Method | Possible Upgrade  
---|---|---  
Continental U.S. (excluding Florida) | FedEx 3-Day | FedEx 2-Day Air  
Florida | UPS Ground | UPS Next Business Day  
  
**Free Upgrades:** Each day, we randomly select a number of customers to receive a complimentary shipping upgrade at no additional cost.

## 2\. Processing & Cutoff Times

We offer **same business-day shipping** for orders that meet the following cutoff times:

  * **Standard Orders:** Placed before **1:00 PM EST** (Miami time), Monday through Friday
  * **Priority Processing Orders:** Placed before **2:00 PM EST** (Miami time), Monday through Friday



Orders placed after the cutoff time or during weekends and holidays will be processed and shipped the next business day.

**⚠️ Note:** We do not ship on weekends or federal holidays. All orders are processed Monday through Friday only.

## 3\. Order Tracking

Once your order has been shipped, you will receive an automated confirmation email containing your tracking number. You can use this number to track your package directly on the carrier's website (FedEx or UPS).

If you do not receive a shipping confirmation within 24 hours of placing your order (excluding weekends and holidays), please contact us at [support@primelabpeptides.com](mailto:support@primelabpeptides.com).

## 4\. Cancellations

If your order has **not yet been shipped** , you may request a cancellation by contacting us at [support@primelabpeptides.com](mailto:support@primelabpeptides.com).

We will do our best to accommodate cancellation requests, but due to our fast processing times, we cannot guarantee that your order can be cancelled once placed.

## 5\. Refund Policy

Due to the nature of our products (research-grade materials), we have a strict refund policy:

  * **Orders not yet shipped:** Full refund available upon request
  * **Orders already shipped or delivered:** No refunds available



**⚠️ Important:** Once an order has been shipped, we are unable to process refunds. This policy exists due to the sensitive nature of research materials, which cannot be restocked or resold once they leave our facility.

Refund requests for eligible orders are typically processed within **2 to 3 business days** once approved.

## 6\. Disputes & Chargebacks

We encourage customers to contact us directly at [support@primelabpeptides.com](mailto:support@primelabpeptides.com) before initiating any dispute or chargeback with their bank or credit card company.

In the event of a payment dispute, we will provide all relevant documentation to the financial institution, including:

  * Proof of delivery and tracking information
  * Order confirmation and transaction records
  * Communication history
  * Terms of Service and Refund Policy acceptance at checkout



## 7\. Bulk & Custom Orders

For bulk orders or custom research material requests, please [**contact us**](https://primelabpeptides.com/contact). Our team will be happy to assist with special requests and tailored solutions for your research needs.

## 8\. Policy Changes

Shipping policies, methods, and rates (including free shipping) may change at any time without prior notice. Please review this page periodically for updates.
