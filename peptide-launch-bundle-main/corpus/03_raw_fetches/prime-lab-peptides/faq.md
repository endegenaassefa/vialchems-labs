By placing an order, all customers confirm they are fully informed about applicable government regulations, understand potential health and safety hazards, and accept full responsibility for applying appropriate safety measures.

**Important:** Prime Lab Peptides reserves the right to cancel any order if there is evidence or suspicion that products may be used for purposes other than in-vitro research.

02 Where Is Prime Lab Peptides Located?  +

**All orders are packed and shipped from within the United States.**

We proudly operate and fulfill every order directly from our U.S.-based facilities in Miami, Florida.

03 What Is a Research Supply Company?  +

**Prime Lab Peptides is a research supply company.**

Our products are sold strictly for **laboratory research purposes only** and are **not intended for human or animal use or testing**.

Any implication or indication of such use may result in the **immediate cancellation of your order**.

## Products & Storage

04 What Size Vials Do Your Peptides Come In?  +

**All peptides are packaged in 3ml glass vials.**

An exception applies to **NAD+** , which is supplied in a **10ml glass vial**.

ℹ️

Our products are research reagents and have not been tested for safety or efficacy in any application other than in-vitro laboratory research.

05 How Do I Store Lyophilized Peptides?  +

For maximum shelf life, **lyophilized peptides should be stored in the refrigerator or freezer prior to reconstitution**.

When stored properly, they can remain stable for **several years**.

06 How Do I Store Reconstituted Peptides?  +

Once reconstituted, peptides should be kept **in the refrigerator** and used within a research-appropriate timeframe based on your laboratory protocols.

07 How Do I Store Bacteriostatic Water?  +

**Store bacteriostatic water in a cool, dry place, away from direct sunlight.**

It should **never be refrigerated or frozen**.

08 What Is the Best Temperature for My Products When Conducting Research?  +

Both **peptides** and **bacteriostatic water** should be brought to **room temperature** before use in any experimental setup.

09 What Are Research Peptides Used For?  +

Peptides are commonly used in **biochemical and molecular biology research**.

They play a vital role in applications such as **protein purification** , **protein modification** , and **binding interaction studies**.

⚠️

**Notice:** Prime Lab Peptides is a research-only supplier. Our products are intended for trained professionals only. We do not provide usage instructions for any application.

## Quality & Testing

10 Is There a Certificate of Analysis (COA) for My Peptide?  +

Yes. Each peptide includes a **Certificate of Analysis (COA)** , accessible from the product page on our website.

You can **match the batch number printed on your vial** with the COA to view product-specific quality data.

11 Are Your Products Laboratory Tested?  +

Yes. **Each batch is laboratory tested for purity** , often by multiple independent labs.

**Test results (COAs)** are published directly on each product page.

If you wish to independently verify your product, you may do so through any licensed **HPLC testing facility**.

🛡️

**Purity Guarantee:** If a product fails independent testing, we will refund your order in full, including shipping costs.

12 Do You Print Batch Numbers on Your Vials?  +

Yes. **Each peptide vial includes a batch number printed on the label** , which directly corresponds to the **COA available on the product page**.

This allows you to verify the specific analysis and purity data for your product.

## Shipping & Delivery

13 What Shipping Carriers Do You Work With?  +

Prime Lab Peptides ships with **FedEx** for most U.S. orders and **UPS** for Florida deliveries.

14 What Are Your Shipping Options and Timeframes?  +

We offer **free shipping** on all U.S. orders:

  * **Continental U.S. (excluding Florida):** FedEx 3-Day Air (free)
  * **Florida:** UPS Ground (free)



Each day, we randomly select customers to receive **complimentary shipping upgrades** at no additional cost.

Once your order is processed, you'll receive a tracking number and updates every step of the way.

15 What Days Do You Ship?  +

Orders are packed and shipped **Monday through Friday** , excluding federal holidays.

  * **Standard Orders:** Placed before **1:00 PM EST** ship the same day
  * **Priority Processing Orders:** Placed before **2:00 PM EST** ship the same day



Orders placed after the cutoff time will ship the **following business day**.

ℹ️

During sales or high-volume periods, processing time may extend by 1–3 business days after payment is confirmed.

16 Is Your Shipping Discreet?  +

Yes. We place a high value on your privacy.

Orders are shipped in **plain FedEx or UPS packaging** , with **no indication of contents**.

The return address will appear as **"SHIPPING MANAGER"** , without any reference to peptides or research materials.

17 My Package Says Delivered but I Haven't Received It  +

If your tracking shows "Delivered" but you haven't received your package:

  * **Step 1:** Double-check around your property and ask neighbors or household members
  * **Step 2:** Contact your local carrier facility (FedEx: 1-800-463-3339)
  * **Step 3:** File a missing package claim directly with the carrier
  * **Step 4:** Send us the claim number so our team can assist with a reshipment



We're here to help and will do our best to ensure your order reaches you.

18 I Entered the Wrong Shipping Address — What Should I Do?  +

If you contact us **before the shipment has gone out** , we will do our best to update your shipping address.

Please email [support@primelabpeptides.com](mailto:support@primelabpeptides.com) with your full name, order number, and corrected address.

⚠️

**Important:** If your order has already shipped, we will not be able to make any changes.

## Returns & Refunds

22 I Received the Wrong Item  +

Although rare, errors can happen.

If you receive the wrong item, please email us **as soon as possible** with your **order number** and a **photo of the incorrect item(s)**.

We will ship the correct item(s) the same or next business day.

📸

A photo is required to process a reshipment.

23 What Is Your Refund Policy?  +

Due to the sensitive nature of research materials:

  * **Orders not yet shipped:** Full refund available upon request
  * **Orders already shipped or delivered:** No refunds available



We offer **free replacements** for items that are damaged during shipping. If your item arrived damaged, please send us a **photo** so we can ship a replacement right away.

⚠️

**Important:** Once an order has been shipped, we are unable to process refunds. This policy exists due to the nature of research materials, which cannot be restocked or resold once they leave our facility.

24 Do You Accept Returns or Exchanges?  +

Due to the sensitive nature of our research materials, **all sales are final once shipped**.

  * **Orders not yet shipped:** Can be cancelled for a full refund
  * **Orders already shipped or delivered:** No returns, exchanges, or refunds



This policy exists because research materials cannot be restocked or resold once they leave our facility.

✅

**Exception:** If you received a damaged or incorrect item, contact us with a photo and we will ship a replacement at no cost.
