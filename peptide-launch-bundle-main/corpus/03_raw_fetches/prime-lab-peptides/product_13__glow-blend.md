### The Science Behind Synergistic Regeneration: Why GLOW Delivers More

**GLOW** represents a new approach to regenerative research — combining three proven peptides into a single, **synergistic formulation**. Rather than studying each compound in isolation, researchers can now investigate how GHK-Cu, TB-500, and BPC-157 interact and amplify each other's regenerative effects.

The science is compelling. **GHK-Cu** modulates over 4,000 genes related to tissue growth and inflammation, stimulating collagen synthesis and antioxidant defenses. **TB-500** binds to actin to promote cell migration, recruiting stem and progenitor cells to damaged areas while reducing fibrotic scarring. **BPC-157** activates the FAK-paxillin pathway and upregulates VEGF receptors, accelerating microvascular repair.

Together, these mechanisms create an optimal environment for tissue repair: **faster wound closure** , **stronger regenerated tissue** , and **reduced fibrosis** compared to normal healing processes. The combined anti-inflammatory action across all three peptides further supports clean, organized healing.
