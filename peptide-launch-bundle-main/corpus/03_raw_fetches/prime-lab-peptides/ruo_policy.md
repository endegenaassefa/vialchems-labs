Last Updated: December 2025

## 1\. Purpose & Scope

PrimePeptide Laboratories Inc., operating as Prime Lab Peptides, exclusively provides research-grade peptides, compounds, and related materials strictly for in-vitro research, laboratory experimentation, and educational purposes.

All products sold on this website are designated as **"Research Use Only" (RUO)** and are not intended for human or animal use, diagnostic purposes, therapeutic applications, or any form of consumption.

## 2\. What Does "Research Use Only" Mean?

"Research Use Only" (RUO) is a regulatory classification used by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) and other regulatory bodies to identify products that are intended solely for research and development purposes.

**RUO products are laboratory reagents** intended for use in research settings by qualified professionals. They have not been evaluated or approved for safety and efficacy in humans or animals, and they are not manufactured under the same regulatory standards as pharmaceutical drugs.

According to FDA guidelines, RUO products must be labeled with the statement "For Research Use Only. Not for use in diagnostic procedures." This classification exempts these products from certain regulatory requirements applicable to medical devices and drugs, provided they are used in accordance with their intended purpose.

## 3\. Research Reagents vs. Drugs

It is critical to understand the distinction between research reagents and pharmaceutical drugs:

Characteristic | Research Reagents (RUO) | Pharmaceutical Drugs  
---|---|---  
Intended Use | Laboratory research only | Human/animal treatment  
FDA Approval Required | No | Yes  
Clinical Trials Required | No | Yes  
GMP Manufacturing | Not Required | Required  
Dosage Guidelines | Not Provided | Provided  
Safety/Efficacy Testing | Not Conducted | Extensive Testing  
Legal for Consumption | No | Yes (as prescribed)
