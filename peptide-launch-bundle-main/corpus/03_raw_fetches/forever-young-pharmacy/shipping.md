# Payments and Shipping

**Shipping & Delivery**

We offer a variety of shipping options, including FREE shipping on orders over $150 (Grand Total). For orders less than $150 (Grand Total), there is a $9.99 flat rate free. All orders use USPS Priority Mail with an included tracking number that will be provided to you to track your package. Delivery for orders placed on Fridays before noon PST. Orders placed, paid, and processed before 12 noon PST typically ship out the same business day. Orders placed, paid, and processed after 12 noon PST, as well as on weekends and holidays, will ship out the following business day.

Privacy & Security 

Your online order is protected by 256 Bit SSL (Secure Socket Layer) encryption technology on our secure server. We take your privacy seriously and comply with all federal and state privacy laws to ensure your information is completely secure and confidential. We will not release your personal or order information to anyone and will only use your information to fulfill your order(s).

Returns & Replacements

Due to regulations regarding the sale of our products, returns are generally not permitted. However, if your order is shipped incorrectly, you receive items that were not ordered, or this is an issue with the order that you would like to discuss, please contact us via email at [support@foreveryoungpharmacy.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20250119101806/mailto:support@foreveryoungpharmacy.com). We will review all requests on a case-by-case basis and seek to remedy the order as appropriate.

Ordering

You can place orders with Forever Young Pharmacy 24/7 through our secure website. Once your order is submitted, approved, and payment is received, it will be filled, properly packaged, and shipped by a reputable delivery service such as USPS. We will notify you with details and provide a tracking number for your package once your order has shipped.

Payment, Pricing & Promotions

We accept all major credit cards. We offer both individual pricing and wholesale bulk pricing. Please refer to our product pages for quantity discounts. For large bulk discounts, please contact us via email.

Viewing Orders

During checkout, you have the option to register or check out as a guest. Registration is optional but recommended, as it allows you to check order status, update your account information, and access past orders by logging into our website. It also speeds up the checkout process for future orders. If you choose not to create an account and have questions about your order status, please email us. You may also check out as a Guest.

Satisfaction Guaranteed 

We are dedicated to providing outstanding quality and service. If you have any questions or issues regarding our service, please contact us at [support@foreveryoungpharmacy.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20250119101806/mailto:support@foreveryoungpharmacy.com) and let us know. We respond to all inquiries promptly.
