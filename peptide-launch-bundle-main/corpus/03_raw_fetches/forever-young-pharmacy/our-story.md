# Our Story

###### Who we are

## We are an independent functional medicine pharmacy based out of Carlsbad, California. 

###### About Us

#### The Science of Staying Young

Welcome to Forever Young Pharmacy, your trusted partner in health and wellness. We are a unique pharmacy that focuses on functional medicine, a holistic and patient-centered approach that empowers individuals to take charge of their own health.Our mission is to put health back into people’s own hands. We believe that everyone has the innate potential to heal and achieve optimal health. Our role is to provide the tools, guidance, and resources needed to unlock this potential.At Forever Young Pharmacy, we specialize in offering peptides, a groundbreaking development in the field of medicine. Peptides have the potential to target specific conditions at a cellular level, offering highly effective and personalized treatment options. These peptides although promising for human individuals are designed for research purposes only.But we don’t just provide peptides. We ensure their quality through rigorous peptide purity testing. We understand that the effectiveness of peptide therapy depends on the quality of the peptides used. That’s why we are committed to providing only the highest quality peptides to our customers.We also offer personal health testing. These tests can provide valuable insights into your health and help identify any potential health issues early. The results of these tests can guide the development of a personalized health plan tailored to your unique needs.We also offer a wide range of supplements. These supplements are carefully selected for their quality and effectiveness and can provide valuable support for various aspects of health.At Forever Young Pharmacy, we are more than just a pharmacy. We are a community of individuals committed to supporting each other in our journeys towards optimal health. We invite you to join us and experience the difference that functional medicine can make in your life

###### Dane Fredericksen

Dane Fredericksen graduated from the University of Southern California (USC) with a Doctorate of Pharmacy in 2012. His experience spans various pharmacy settings, including retail, managed care, and hospitals. After over 15 years as a pharmacist, Dane recognized the glaring disparities in healthcare. He observed that patients often had to be critically ill before receiving adequate treatment and care from their physicians. The escalating costs of insurance premiums and the increasing restrictions on drugs due to prior authorizations, driven by the high prices set by pharmaceutical companies, further exacerbated these disparities.

To address these issues, Dane delved into the study of functional medicine. This led him to a novel approach to medicine: treating the root causes of diseases rather than merely addressing the symptoms. As a result, Forever Young Pharmacy was established. Our mission is to provide affordable, safe, and effective medications aimed at preventing and treating common chronic diseases, thereby potentially improving an individual’s health and longevity. Furthermore, we strive to eliminate unnecessary costs associated with third-party insurance and pharmacy benefits managers. Our focus is on treating patients before they become critically ill.
