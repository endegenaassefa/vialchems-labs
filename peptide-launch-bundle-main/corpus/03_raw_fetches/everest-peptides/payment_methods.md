##  Common Questions 

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover) and Apple Pay on supported devices. Every transaction is processed through encrypted, PCI-compliant gateways.

Is it safe to enter my card details?

Yes. Card details are entered directly on our processor's PCI-compliant page using 256-bit SSL encryption. Your card number, CVV, and expiration date never touch our servers — we only ever see a token that confirms the payment was successful.

How does Apple Pay work at checkout?

On a supported Apple device with a card set up in Wallet, Apple Pay automatically appears as an option at checkout. Tap it, authenticate with Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode, and you're done — no card numbers to type and no shipping info to fill out. Apple never shares your real card number with us.

When does my card get charged?

Your card is charged immediately when you place your order. Once payment clears, we package and ship your order — typically the same business day or the next morning.

Why was my card declined?

Card declines are usually caused by your bank flagging the transaction as unusual, an incorrect billing address (AVS check), or insufficient funds. The fastest fix is to contact your bank to authorize the charge, then try again. If you keep having trouble, email us at contact@everestpeptides.com and we'll help.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If you need to return an unopened product within 30 days, we'll process a full refund to your original payment method. Contact our support team to get started.
