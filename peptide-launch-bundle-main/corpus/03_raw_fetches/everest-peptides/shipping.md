## Shipping Methods & Timeframes

Shipping Method | Delivery Time | Cost  
---|---|---  
USPS Ground Advantage | 3–6 Business Days | Flat Rate (Free over $150)  
UPS 2-Day Air | 2 Business Days | Calculated (Free over $250)  
  
## Order Processing

  * Orders are processed Monday through Friday, excluding holidays
  * Orders placed before **3:00 PM EST** ship the same business day
  * Orders placed after 3:00 PM EST or on weekends ship the next business day
  * Once your order is packed, you'll receive an email with tracking information



## Shipping Restrictions

Currently, Everest Peptides ships to addresses within the **United States only**. We are unable to ship to: 

  * P.O. Boxes (for expedited and overnight shipping)
  * APO/FPO addresses
  * International addresses



## Tracking Your Order

Once your order is packed, you'll receive an email with your tracking number and a link to track your package. You can also log into your account to view order status and tracking information. 

## Delivery Issues

If you experience any issues with your delivery, please contact us immediately:

  * **Package shows delivered but not received:** Please check with neighbors, building management, or other household members. If still not found, contact us within 48 hours.
  * **Damaged package:** Take photos of the damage and contact us immediately. Do not discard the packaging.
  * **Wrong items received:** Contact us and we will arrange for correct items to be shipped.
