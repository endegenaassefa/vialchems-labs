## Convergence verdict

**Pass 8 net-new = 13** (down from Pass 7's 32). Net-new ratio = 13/(13+1541) ≈ 0.84%.

The 13 finds came predominantly from **state-prefix adjacent discovery via WebSearch**
(not direct domain probing), specifically the Texas / Colorado / Ohio clusters where
real vendors use *state-anchored* brand names but **not** the literal `<state>peptides.com`
domain (which is held by speculative domainers).

The literal stem×suffix surface produced **0 net-new** despite 208 probes, confirming
saturation of that pattern.

The literal multi-word stem surface produced **0 net-new** despite 10 probes, confirming
saturation of that pattern.
