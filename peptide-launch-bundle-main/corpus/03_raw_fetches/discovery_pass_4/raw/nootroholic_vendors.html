Peptide Vendor With Third Party Testing

COA-verified peptide vendors have batch-specific Certificates of Analysis. Not questionable PDFs or outdated results from 2019. A COA matching YOUR vial’s batch number, tested within 12 months by verified labs like Janoshik, MZ Biolabs, or Colmaric.

  • How to read peptide COA: Keep an eye out for HPLC chromatogram graphs and not just numbers—Mass Spectrometry confirmation of molecular weight, and endotoxin/sterility results.
  • Peptide purity explained: HPLC reveals the percentage purity, Mass Spec confirms its identity. And both matters.
