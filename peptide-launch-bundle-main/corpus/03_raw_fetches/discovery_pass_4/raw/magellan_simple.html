Company Background & Establishment

Simple Peptide was established in late 2023 as a family-run business, founded by Alex and Melissa Ehrenthal. The company initially operated as an importer of research peptides before transitioning to work directly with a US-based manufacturer, a shift intended to improve quality control and supply chain reliability.

The business is based in Delray Beach, Florida, and positions itself around key operational values such as transparency, fast fulfillment, and customer service. These themes are consistently emphasized across the company’s website and marketing materials.

As a relatively early-stage company, Simple Peptide has experienced rapid growth and increasing visibility within the research peptide market. However, due to its recent establishment, the brand currently lacks a long-term operational track record, which may be a consideration for researchers prioritizing vendor longevity and historical consistency.

Product Range & Availability

Number of Products

Simple Peptide offers a relatively broad catalog for a vendor of its size, with an estimated 30 to 50 individual SKUs, depending on product type, dosage, blends, and delivery format. However, full visibility of the product catalog requires user registration and login, which limits pre-purchase transparency for first-time visitors.

Main Product Categories

The product lineup spans several research-focused categories, including:

  • GLP-1 and metabolic research peptides, such as Semaglutide (Sema) and Retatrutide (Reta), which appear to be among the more prominent offerings.
  • Multi-compound blends, including combinations like GHK-CU / BPC-157 / TB-500, marketed for advanced research applications.
  • Growth factors, such as MGF, targeting specialized research use cases.
  • Novel delivery formats, including dissolvable research strips (e.g., NAD+, Sleep, and Energy strips), which differentiate the brand from more traditional peptide vendors.

Ancillary Products

In addition to peptides, Simple Peptide sells several ancillary research supplies separately, including:

  • Syringes
  • Bacteriostatic water
  • Alcohol wipes

Unlike some competitors, these items are not bundled with peptide purchases, which may increase the overall cost of an order depending on a researcher’s needs.

Pricing & Discounts

Simple Peptide’s pricing structure places the brand in the mid-to-high price segment of the research peptide market. Individual product prices generally start at approximately $30 for entry-level peptides, while blends and specialty products frequently exceed $100 per vial.

  • 10% discount on orders of 10 or more vials

While these discounts can improve value for larger orders, smaller purchases remain relatively expensive when compared to budget-focused peptide vendors. Shipping & Delivery Times

Simple Peptide prioritizes fast order fulfillment, offering same-day shipping for orders placed before 3:00 PM ET. This policy allows most orders to be dispatched on the day of purchase, which is a notable advantage for time-sensitive research needs.

The company primarily uses FedEx 2Day Air as its standard shipping method. As a result, typical delivery times within the United States are approximately two business days, depending on the destination.

Overall, Simple Peptide’s shipping performance is positioned as a key strength, combining rapid processing with reliable, expedited domestic delivery.

Delivery Fees

Simple Peptide offers free shipping on orders exceeding $200, which aligns with common thresholds used by research peptide vendors utilizing expedited delivery services.

For orders below this amount, shipping costs are estimated to range between $15 and $20, reflecting the use of FedEx 2Day Air as the standard shipping method. While this ensures fast and reliable delivery, it can make smaller orders comparatively more expensive.

Quality Control & Third-Party Testing

Simple Peptide places a strong emphasis on independent, third-party testing as a core component of its quality assurance process. The company states that each batch of research peptides undergoes external analysis prior to release.

Third-Party Laboratory

Simple Peptide states that its products undergo independent third-party testing as part of its quality control process. The availability of batch-level COAs serves as an important trust indicator, supporting Simple Peptide’s quality-focused positioning despite its relatively short operational history.

Potential Downsides, Limitations & Red Flags

While Simple Peptide demonstrates several operational strengths, there are also a number of limitations and potential concerns that prospective buyers should consider before placing an order.

1. Mandatory Registration Before Viewing Products

Simple Peptide requires users to create an account and provide personal information before gaining full access to its product catalog and pricing. This registration barrier introduces unnecessary friction and reduces transparency for first-time visitors who may prefer to evaluate product offerings before sharing personal data.

2. Additional Supplies Sold Separately

Unlike some competitors (such as LeoLabRX), Simple Peptide does not include basic research supplies with peptide purchases. Essential items such as:

  • syringes
  • bacteriostatic water
  • alcohol wipes

are sold separately. This practice can significantly increase the true total cost per order, particularly for new or smaller purchases.

3. Higher Effective Cost Due to Shipping Method

Simple Peptide uses FedEx 2Day Air as its standard shipping method, ensuring fast and reliable delivery. However, this approach also results in higher shipping fees for orders below the $200 free-shipping threshold, making smaller orders comparatively expensive when compared to vendors offering slower but cheaper shipping options.

4. Affiliate-Driven Visibility

The brand maintains a strong presence on social media platforms and relies heavily on affiliate marketing and promotional codes. While this strategy enhances visibility, it may also inflate perceived popularity and introduce bias in online reviews, making it more difficult to distinguish organic customer feedback from incentivized promotion.

5. Limited Long-Term Track Record

Founded in late 2023, Simple Peptide remains a relatively young vendor. As a result, it lacks the multi-year operational history of more established competitors, which makes long-term consistency, stability, and performance harder to fully assess at this stage.

Final Verdict

Simple Peptide demonstrates strong logistics and generally solid quality positioning, supported by fast order fulfillment and publicly stated third-party testing practices. The brand places a clear emphasis on speed, modern presentation, and customer-facing operations.

At the same time, the main drawbacks are related to the overall cost structure and user experience. Mandatory account registration limits upfront transparency, while shipping fees and the absence of bundled research supplies can increase the effective cost of smaller orders. These factors make the value proposition less streamlined compared to competitors that offer more inclusive packaging.

Overall, Simple Peptide is best suited for buyers prioritizing fast delivery and a broad product selection, particularly for larger orders. Researchers who value ease of access, bundled supplies, and lower friction at checkout may find greater convenience with more established alternatives.
