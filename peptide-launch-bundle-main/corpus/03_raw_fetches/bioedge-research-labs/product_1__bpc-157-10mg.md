### BPC 157 Compared to TB 500

BPC 157 and TB 500 (Thymosin Beta 4 fragment) have both been studied in preclinical tissue models, but the published literature describes different mechanisms of action. BPC 157 research has primarily examined GI tissue integrity, connective tissue models, and nitric oxide pathway modulation. TB 500 research has primarily examined cell migration, blood vessel formation, and inflammatory regulation through actin binding.

**What is BPC 157?**  
BPC 157 is a synthetic pentadecapeptide consisting of 15 amino acids, originally identified as a fragment of a protein found in human gastric juice. It has been the subject of over 100 published preclinical studies examining its properties in experimental tissue, GI, and cell signaling models.

Lyophilized BPC 157 should be stored at -20°C for long term or 2-8°C for short term. Protect from light and moisture.

**Is BPC 157 a steroid?**  
No. BPC 157 is a peptide, a short chain of amino acids. It is structurally and functionally unrelated to anabolic steroids.

**What is the difference between BPC 157 and TB 500?**
