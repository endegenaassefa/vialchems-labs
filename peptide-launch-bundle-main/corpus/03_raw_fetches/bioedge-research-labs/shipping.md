## Order Processing Time

All orders are processed within **1–3 business days** after payment confirmation. Orders are not processed, shipped, or delivered on weekends or public holidays. 

If we are experiencing a high volume of orders, processing may be delayed slightly. In such cases, we will notify you via email.

## Shipping Locations

BioEdge Research Labs currently ships orders to **domestic and selected international locations** , subject to local laws, regulations, and carrier availability. 

If your location is not supported at checkout, please contact our support team for assistance. 

## Shipping Rates & Delivery Estimates

Shipping charges for your order will be calculated and displayed at checkout. 

Estimated delivery times depend on your location and the shipping method selected: 

**Domestic Shipping**

Estimated delivery time is **3–7 business days**.

**International Shipping**

Estimated delivery time is **7–21 business days**.

Please note that delivery times are estimates and may vary due to customs processing, carrier delays, or unforeseen circumstances beyond our control. 

## Shipment Confirmation & Tracking

Once your order has been shipped, you will receive a **shipment confirmation email** containing your tracking number. Tracking information may take up to 24 hours to become active. 

## Customs, Duties & Taxes

BioEdge Research Labs is not responsible for any customs duties, taxes, or import fees applied to your order. All fees imposed during or after shipping are the responsibility of the customer. 

Customs policies vary by country, so please check with your local customs office for further information. 

## Shipping Delays

While we strive to meet delivery timelines, delays may occasionally occur due to factors such as weather conditions, carrier issues, customs inspections, or high order volumes. 

BioEdge Research Labs is not liable for shipping delays caused by third-party carriers. 

## Incorrect or Incomplete Address

Please ensure that all shipping details are accurate at the time of checkout. BioEdge Research Labs is not responsible for orders delayed, returned, or lost due to incorrect or incomplete address information provided by the customer. 

If an order is returned to us due to an incorrect address, additional shipping charges may apply for reshipment. 

## Lost or Damaged Packages

If your order arrives damaged or is lost in transit, please contact us within **48 hours** of delivery or expected delivery date with relevant details and photos if applicable. We will work with the shipping carrier to resolve the issue as quickly as possible. 

**Damaged package**

Contact support within 48 hours and include relevant details and photos if applicable.

**Lost in transit**

Contact support within 48 hours of the expected delivery date so the issue can be reviewed.

## Order Restrictions

All products sold by BioEdge Research Labs are intended **strictly for laboratory research and development purposes only**. Orders may be subject to verification to ensure compliance with applicable laws and regulations. 

## Changes to This Shipping Policy

We reserve the right to update or modify this Shipping Policy at any time. Any changes will be effective immediately upon posting on this page.
