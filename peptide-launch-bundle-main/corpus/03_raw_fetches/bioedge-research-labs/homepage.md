## Questions We Get The Most

We believe researchers deserve clarity. These are the questions we hear most often about quality control, third-party testing, fulfillment, and compliance.

Are research peptides legal to purchase?

Yes. Research peptides are legal to purchase in the United States when sold and used strictly for laboratory and scientific research purposes. BioEdge Research Labs operates in full compliance with all applicable federal and state regulations. All products are clearly labeled for research use only and are not intended for human or animal consumption.

Why do your products say “Not for Human Consumption”?

This disclaimer indicates that our peptides are research-grade compounds intended for laboratory applications only. They have not been evaluated by the FDA for human safety or efficacy. We maintain strict regulatory compliance — all products are manufactured and labeled for scientific research purposes under Good Manufacturing Practices.

Where are your peptides manufactured?

All BioEdge peptides are produced in U.S.-based GMP-compliant facilities with rigorous quality controls and full documentation.

How do you verify purity?

Every lot undergoes HPLC and mass spectrometry analysis by certified, independent third-party laboratories. Lot-specific Certificates of Analysis are available on every product page.

How quickly do orders ship?

Orders placed before 3 PM EST ship the same business day. Free shipping is available on all domestic orders over $150.

These compounds are handled in a controlled research supply chain, and once a product leaves our facility we can no longer verify its chain of custody or guarantee that it has been stored, transported, or handled under proper conditions.

For safety and compliance reasons, we can only ship out peptides that have remained fully within our controlled environment from start to finish.

Can you provide lab work for your products?

Absolutely! Certificates of Analysis (COA) with high-performance liquid chromatography (HPLC) lab results are available on each product page.

What is your return policy?

Due to the temperature-sensitive storage requirements and the nature of research compounds, we do not accept returns on shipped peptides. Once chain of custody is broken, we cannot verify product integrity and therefore cannot resell or restock returned items. This policy is in place to protect the quality and safety of every product we ship.

What if my order does not arrive?

Contact us immediately if tracking shows delivery but you haven’t received your package. If the carrier confirms non-delivery, we will reship your order at no cost. Your research timeline matters, and we work quickly to resolve any delivery issues. Email us at [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection) for assistance.

How should I store my peptides?

Store lyophilized peptides at -20°C in their original sealed vials. Keep them away from light and humidity. Under proper conditions, lyophilized peptides maintain stability for long-term laboratory use.
