### Published Preclinical Research

Thymosin Beta 4 is one of the most widely studied actin sequestering peptides in the biomedical literature. Key areas of investigation in preclinical animal and in vitro models include:

  * **Wound repair models** — full thickness dermal wound studies in rodent models demonstrated accelerated re-epithelialization and increased collagen deposition (Malinda et al., J Investigative Dermatology, 1999)
  * **Cardiac tissue models** — myocardial infarction models in mice showed enhanced cardiomyocyte migration and survival through integrin linked kinase and Akt signaling activation (Bock-Marquette et al., Nature, 2004)
  * **Angiogenesis and cell migration** — studies in both normal and aged rodent models documented increased blood vessel formation and directional cell migration in controlled experimental settings (Philp et al., Annals of the New York Academy of Sciences, 2004)
  * **Inflammatory regulation** — preclinical evidence describes modulation of NF-κB signaling, reduction of pro-inflammatory cytokines, and decreased inflammatory cell infiltration in experimental tissue models (Goldstein et al., Expert Opinion on Biological Therapy, 2012)



All data referenced on this page is derived from published preclinical animal and in vitro studies. No human clinical trials have established approved uses for TB 500.
