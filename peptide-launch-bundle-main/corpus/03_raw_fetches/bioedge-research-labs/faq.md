## Common questions

Open a question below to view the answer. Only one answer opens at a time to keep the page easy to scan. 

What if I haven’t received a tracking number for my order?

Each weekday, within 24 hours of confirming your payment, your shipping label will be printed, and you will receive an email with your tracking number. Since we don’t ship on Saturdays or Sundays, orders placed after 3 PM on Friday will be shipped on Monday, and you will receive your tracking number by the following Tuesday.

What if there’s a problem with my order?

For any order issues, including missing items, please use the contact form on our website. Fill out the form with:

  * Your Name
  * Email Address
  * Order Number
  * A Detailed Description of the Issue



Our team will respond ASAP, usually within 24 to 48 hours, and work quickly to resolve the issue.

Why is my order on hold?

If you ordered an item that is currently out of stock, your order will be placed on hold until we receive the item. As soon as it arrives, we will ship it to you immediately.

How long will it take to receive my order?

  * First-Class Packages: Arrive in 5 to 7 days.
  * Priority Mail: Arrives in approximately 2 to 4 days.
  * Express Mail: Arrives in approximately 24 to 48 hours.



Holiday Delays: Expect potential delays around holidays, except for Express Mail, which is rarely delayed.

Tracking Your Order: Use your tracking number for real-time updates. You can also sign up for USPS Informed Delivery Service to track your packages.

What is your return policy?

We are not able to accept any returns on peptide products.

These compounds are handled in a controlled research supply chain, and once a product leaves our facility we can no longer verify its chain of custody or guarantee that it has been stored, transported, or handled under proper conditions.

For safety and compliance reasons, we can only ship out peptides that have remained fully within our controlled environment from start to finish.

Can you provide lab work for your products?

Absolutely! Certificates of Analysis (COA) with high-performance liquid chromatography (HPLC) lab results are available on each product page.

Why does my peptide bottle appear empty?

Since BioEdge Peptides does not use fillers like mannitol or lactose, the bottle may appear to contain very little substance.

For example:

5 mg or 10 mg peptides take up very little volume without fillers, so the bottle may look nearly empty. However, rest assured that your full dosage is inside—even if it looks like only a few grains of sand.

Your peptides are ready to be reconstituted and used in research.
