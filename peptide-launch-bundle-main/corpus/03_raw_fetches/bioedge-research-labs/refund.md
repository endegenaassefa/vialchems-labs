At **BioEdge Research Labs** , we are committed to providing high-quality research products and ensuring customer satisfaction. Please read our Refunds & Returns Policy carefully to understand your rights and responsibilities regarding orders. 

Request Window **Within 7 days**

Return or refund requests should be submitted within 7 days of receiving your order.

## Eligibility for Refunds or Returns

All products sold by BioEdge Research Labs are intended **strictly for laboratory research and development purposes**. 

Due to the nature of research products, returns are **limited** and refunds are provided only in specific circumstances. 

**May be eligible**

  * Damaged products received during shipping
  * Incorrect items sent
  * Verified defective or compromised products



**Not eligible**

  * Products that have been used
  * Products that have been partially opened
  * Products that have been improperly handled



## Requesting a Return or Refund

To request a return or refund, please contact our support team **within 7 days** of receiving your order. Your request should include: 

  * Order number
  * Description of the issue
  * Photos of damaged or incorrect items, if applicable



Requests received after 7 days may not be eligible for consideration. 

## Return Process

If your return is approved: 

1 **Support reviews**

Our support team reviews the request details.

2 **Instructions sent**

Approved returns receive return instructions.

3 **Secure packaging**

Products must be packaged to prevent damage.

4 **Return shipping**

Return shipping is generally the customer’s responsibility.

5 **Inspection & refund**

Refunds are processed after the returned items are received and inspected.

  1. Our support team will provide instructions for returning the product.
  2. Products must be securely packaged to prevent damage during shipping.
  3. Return shipping costs are generally the responsibility of the customer, unless the return is due to our error.
  4. Refunds will be processed after we receive and inspect the returned items.



## Refund Method

Approved refunds will be issued via the **original payment method**. The processing time may vary depending on your bank or payment provider. Please allow **5–10 business days** for the refund to appear in your account. 

## Non-Refundable Items

The following are **not eligible** for return or refund: 

  * Products that have been opened or used
  * Products damaged due to misuse, improper storage, or unauthorized modifications
  * Orders placed in error, unless approved by our support team



## Shipping Damage or Loss

If your order is damaged during shipping, please contact us **within 48 hours** of delivery with photos of the damage. We will work with the shipping carrier to resolve the issue and provide a replacement or refund where applicable. 

**Damaged during shipping**

Contact support within 48 hours of delivery and include photos of the damage.

**Replacement or refund**

BioEdge will work with the shipping carrier and provide a replacement or refund where applicable.

## Policy Updates

BioEdge Research Labs reserves the right to update or modify this Refunds & Returns Policy at any time. Any changes will be effective immediately upon posting on this page. 

## Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding refunds or returns, please contact us through our official support email or contact page.
