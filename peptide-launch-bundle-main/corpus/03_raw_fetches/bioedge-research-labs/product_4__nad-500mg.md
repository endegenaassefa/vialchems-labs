### Compound Overview

Nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide (NAD+) is a pyridine nucleotide coenzyme present in all living cells and classified as a redox-active dinucleotide composed of adenine and nicotinamide moieties linked through phosphate groups. In its oxidized form, NAD+ participates in electron transfer reactions central to cellular metabolism and mitochondrial bioenergetics research systems.

In laboratory research settings, NAD+ is evaluated for redox biochemistry characterization, enzymatic cofactor interaction studies, and analytical quantification using chromatographic and mass spectrometry methodologies. Experimental literature describes its classification within metabolic cofactor research models investigated under controlled in vitro and preclinical experimental conditions.
