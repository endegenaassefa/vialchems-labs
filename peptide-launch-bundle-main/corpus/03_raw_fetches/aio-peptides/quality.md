## Quality Control

Pure Peptides. Proven Quality

### **Quality Control for Peptides**

At AIO Peptides, we prioritize the highest standards of quality in every peptide we provide. Our commitment to rigorous quality control ensures that every peptide we sell is of the utmost purity and integrity, designed for accurate and reproducible research results. We are dedicated to providing researchers with products that exceed expectations in both quality and reliability.

#### 

#### **Quality Control Process**

The comprehensive quality control (QC) process begins from the moment raw materials arrive at the supplier’s facility and continues through to the final product testing. Here’s an overview of the process:

  1. **Raw Material Selection**

     * We source raw materials from reputable suppliers who meet stringent industry standards. Each batch of raw materials undergoes testing for purity, identity, and quality before it is used in the peptide synthesis process.

  2. **Peptide Synthesis and Purification**

     * Our peptides are synthesized using state-of-the-art equipment and high-quality reagents. During the synthesis, the supplier employs strict monitoring and in-process controls to ensure optimal yields and purity levels.

     * After synthesis, peptides undergo multiple purification steps, including high-performance liquid chromatography (HPLC), to remove any impurities and achieve the desired purity.

  3. **Quality Assurance Testing**

     * **Mass Spectrometry (MS):** To confirm peptide identity and molecular weight, suppliers use high-resolution mass spectrometry.

     * **High-Performance Liquid Chromatography (HPLC):** Our supplier conducts HPLC analysis to determine the purity of each peptide batch, ensuring it meets or exceeds the 95% purity standard required for research purposes.

     * **Amino Acid Analysis (AAA):** This test verifies the sequence and composition of peptides to guarantee their accuracy.

     * **Endotoxin Testing:** For peptides used in biological research, our supplier ensures that endotoxin levels are within acceptable limits to avoid potential contamination in biological studies.

  4. **Stability and Storage**

     * Stability of our peptides is carefully monitored throughout their shelf life. Proper storage conditions are maintained to preserve the integrity and potency of each product, from production through shipping.




#### **Why Quality Control Matters**

Peptides are powerful tools in research, and even small variations in quality can have significant effects on research outcomes. Whether you’re studying protein interactions, signaling pathways, or designing novel therapeutics, the consistency and accuracy of your reagents are critical. Our supplier’s rigorous QC processes help ensure that the peptides you receive are:

  * **Accurate:** Correct sequence and composition verified by mass spectrometry and amino acid analysis.

  * **Pure:** Over 95% purity, as measured by HPLC.

  * **Reliable:** Consistent quality from batch to batch, with comprehensive testing to verify stability and potency.

  * **Safe:** Free of contaminants, including endotoxins, for your experiments.




#### **Certificates of Analysis**

For transparency and confidence in our products, we provide a detailed **Certificate of Analysis (COA)** for each peptide we offer. The COA includes information such as the peptide sequence, purity percentage, and results from the tests we’ve conducted. This documentation can be crucial for your research and compliance needs.
