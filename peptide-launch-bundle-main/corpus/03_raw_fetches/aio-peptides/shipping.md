  * **Overnight Shipping (FedEx Standard Overnight):** $50 – Delivery within 1 business day (excludes weekends).

  * **International Shipping:** We currently do not offer international shipping at this time.



## Order Tracking

Once your order has been processed, you’ll receive an email with a tracking number and link. If you experience issues tracking your order, contact us at [hello@aiopeptides.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20251111021229/mailto:hello@aiopeptides.com).

## Shipping Issues

If your order arrives damaged, incomplete, or incorrect, please contact us within **48 hours of delivery** at [hello@aiopeptides.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20251111021229/mailto:hello@aiopeptides.com). We’ll quickly resolve it with a replacement or refund.

AIO Peptides is **not responsible** for delays caused by:

  * Weather
  * Carrier issues
  * Other factors beyond our control



## Address Accuracy

Please double-check your shipping address during checkout. We are not responsible for delivery issues due to incorrect or incomplete addresses.

## Delivery Confirmation

If tracking shows your package as delivered but you haven’t received it, please contact the carrier (FedEx or USPS) directly. We are happy to assist with filing a claim, but we are not liable for lost or stolen packages once delivery is confirmed.

## Returns & Exchanges

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. Here’s how returns and exchanges work:

### Return Eligibility

  * Only unopened items are eligible for return.
  * Email [hello@aiopeptides.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20251111021229/mailto:hello@aiopeptides.com) with your order number and reason for return.
  * If approved, we’ll send you return shipping instructions.
  * Please use a trackable shipping method for returns—we are not responsible for lost items in transit.



### Refunds

  * Refunds are processed within 2 business days of receiving your return.
  * Refunds will be issued to your original payment method.
  * Shipping charges are non-refundable.



## Damaged or Defective Items

If you receive a damaged or defective item, email us immediately at [hello@aiopeptides.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20251111021229/mailto:hello@aiopeptides.com) with your order number and clear photos of the issue. We will arrange a return, exchange, or refund. You have 30 days from the date of purchase to contact us about any defective items.

## Final Sale Items

Items marked as “Final Sale” are not eligible for returns or exchanges. These items are clearly indicated on the product page.
