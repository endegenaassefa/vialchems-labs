If you receive an incorrect product or your order arrives damaged during transit, you may request a replacement under the following conditions:

If your tracking information shows “delivered” but you have not received your package, please first check with:

If the carrier officially marks the package as lost, please contact us at **sales@concordiarc.com** so we can assist with a resolution. Orders shipped to an incorrectly entered address are **not eligible** for replacement or refund.

  


### **4\. Order Cancellation Policy**

  


Orders may only be canceled **before fulfillment and shipment**. Once a shipping label has been created or the order has entered the shipping process, cancellations are no longer possible.

  


If a cancellation is approved, funds will be returned to the original payment method within **3–10 business days** , depending on your bank or payment processor.

  


### **5\. Refund Exclusions**

  


The following are **not eligible** for refunds or returns:
