Concordia Research Chems is a US-based supplier of research-grade peptides and chemical reference materials for laboratory and analytical applications. Based in Chicago, Illinois, we provide the research community with high-purity compounds backed by rigorous quality standards.

## Quality Assurance

We partner with Janoshik Analytical for independent third-party testing of our products. Our testing program verifies identity, purity, and composition to ensure every compound meets research-grade standards. Certificates of analysis are available for tested products and we are actively expanding coverage across our full catalog. All peptides are additionally verified via HPLC analysis for purity confirmation.

## Shipping and Fulfillment

All orders ship from our US facility via 2nd Day Air, with same-day processing on orders placed before 2:00 PM CST. We ship exclusively within the continental United States. Orders over $200 qualify for free shipping.

## Research Use Policy

All products sold by Concordia Research Chems are intended exclusively for in vitro laboratory research, analytical testing, and scientific study by qualified researchers. Our materials are not intended for human consumption, medical applications, veterinary use, or household use. Purchasers must confirm their understanding of this policy before completing any order.
