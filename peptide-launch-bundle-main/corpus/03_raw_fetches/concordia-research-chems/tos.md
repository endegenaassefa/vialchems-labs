  * **Account Details:** Login credentials (encrypted) and purchase history

  * **Technical Data:** IP address, browser type, device type, referring pages, and usage analytics

  * **Cookies & Tracking:** Website preferences, session identifiers, cart activity, and saved login settings

We may use collected information for the following purposes:

  * To process and fulfill orders

  * To send promotional emails or newsletters if you opt in




### **3\. Legal Basis for Data Processing**

We process personal data under one or more of the following legal grounds:

  * Your consent

  * Compliance with legal obligations

  * Legitimate business interests, including security and fraud prevention




### **4\. Sharing Your Information**

We do not sell or rent personal information. Limited data may be shared with:

  * Payment processors for secure transaction handling

  * Shipping carriers and logistics providers

We use cookies, tracking pixels, and analytics tools to enhance user experience, remember preferences, monitor traffic, and support marketing efforts. You may disable cookies in your browser settings; however, some website features may not function properly as a result.

### **6\. Data Retention**

We retain personal information only as long as necessary for order processing, legal compliance, fraud prevention, and legitimate business purposes.

### **7\. Data Security**

### **11\. Children’s Privacy**
