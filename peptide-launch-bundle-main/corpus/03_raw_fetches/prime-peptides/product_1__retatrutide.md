Research 

Metabolic Effects in Rodent Models – Animal studies have shown that Retatrutide activates multiple hormonal pathways involved in energy regulation, improving metabolic profiles in rodents exposed to high-calorie diets.

Glucose Homeostasis in Mice – Research in mice demonstrates that Retatrutide enhances insulin sensitivity and stabilizes blood glucose levels, supporting further investigation into its role in glycemic control mechanisms.

Liver Function in Rats – In rat models, Retatrutide has been associated with improved liver enzyme markers and reduced hepatic fat accumulation, suggesting potential liver-supportive effects under metabolic stress.

Mechanism of Action 

Retatrutide works by activating three different hormone receptors: GLP-1, GIP, and glucagon. These hormones help control blood sugar, energy use, and digestion. Together, they tell the body to release insulin, slow down food movement in the stomach, and improve how the body handles fats and sugars.
