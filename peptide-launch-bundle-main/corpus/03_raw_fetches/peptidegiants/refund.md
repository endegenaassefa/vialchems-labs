## 2\. Policy Overview

  * **Return Window:** Within 30 days from the delivery date

  * **Refund Processing:** Within 7 business days after inspection

  * **Return Shipping Costs:** Customer responsibility unless the item is defective or incorrect




* * *

## 3\. Order Cancellations

You may cancel your order within **two (2) hours of purchase** by contacting customer service.

If your order has already shipped, cancellation is no longer possible. You may still request a return once the item arrives (see instructions below).

* * *

## 4\. Return Conditions

### Return Window

Items may be returned within **30 days of delivery**.

### Item Condition Requirements

Returned products must be:

  * Unopened

  * Unused

  * In original manufacturer packaging

  * Sealed and untampered




Due to the nature of **research-use-only peptide products** , opened or used items cannot be returned, except in cases of verified defects.

### Proof of Purchase

A receipt or order number is required for all returns.

* * *

### Non-Returnable Items

We cannot accept returns for:

  * Opened or used peptide products (unless defective upon arrival)

  * Products damaged due to improper storage by the customer

  * Items purchased from unauthorized sellers

  * Custom or special-order items




* * *

## 5\. How to Initiate a Return

### Step 1 — Contact Customer Service

Email **cs@peptidegiants.com** with:

  * Your order number

  * Reason for return

  * Photos (if item is defective, damaged, or incorrect)




### Step 2 — Receive Return Authorization

Our support team will provide:

  * Return authorization number

  * Packaging instructions

  * Return shipping address (if different)




### Step 3 — Package the Item

Ensure:

  * The product is securely packed

  * All original packaging is included

  * Return authorization form is enclosed




### Step 4 — Ship the Item

Use a reliable shipping carrier with tracking.

Return shipping costs are the customer’s responsibility unless the item is defective or incorrect.

* * *

## 6\. Refund Policy

### Refund Approval

Upon receiving your return, we will inspect the item and notify you of approval or denial within **7 business days**.

### Processing Time

Approved refunds will be issued to your original payment method within **7 business days**.

Please note that banks or card providers may require additional time to post funds.

### Confirmation

You will receive an email once your refund has been completed.

If your refund does not appear within the expected timeframe:

  1. Contact your bank or card provider

  2. Email **cs@peptidegiants.com** for further assistance




* * *

## 7\. Exchange Policy

We offer exchanges for:

  * Incorrect items

  * Verified defective items




Contact us within **30 days of delivery** to initiate an exchange.

Peptide Giants will cover return shipping costs for:

  * Wrong items sent

  * Verified defective items




If you ordered the wrong product or quantity, please initiate a return and place a new order.

* * *

## 8\. Exceptions & Special Cases

### Defective or Damaged Items

If your product arrives damaged or defective:

  * Contact us within **48 hours**

  * Provide photos of the issue

  * Include your order number




We will arrange a replacement or refund at no cost to you.

* * *

### Incorrect Product Received

If you receive the wrong item:

  * Notify us immediately

  * We will cover return shipping

  * We will send the correct item promptly




* * *

### Lost Packages

If your package appears lost:

  * Contact us immediately

  * We will initiate a carrier investigation

  * A replacement or resolution will follow based on the carrier’s findings




* * *

## 9\. Processing Timeline Overview

Action | Timeline  
---|---

Inspection after receiving item | 7 business days  
Refund issuance | Within 7 business days after approval  
Exchange shipment | 4–7 delivery days after authorization
