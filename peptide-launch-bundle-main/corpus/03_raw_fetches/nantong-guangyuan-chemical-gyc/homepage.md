## Frequently Asked Questions

What is GYC? __

Nantong Guangyuan Chemical Co., Ltd. (GYC) is a direct manufacturer and exporter based in Nantong, China. We produce and supply 350+ research-grade compounds including peptides, SARMs, injectable oils, oral tablets, and raw materials. All products are independently tested by Janoshik Analytical with certificates of analysis (COA) available. We are a PeptideMeter verified vendor with Verified on GLP1Forum.

How do I place an order? __

All orders must be placed through our website. Browse our [products page](/products), add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. Fill in your shipping details, then choose your payment method — we accept Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Revolut, bank transfer, or cryptocurrency (BTC, ETH, USDT, USDC, SOL, XRP, LTC, BNB). Crypto payments receive a 10% discount. For questions or support, message us on [Telegram](https://t.me/glisa8).

**Standard shipping:** $25 worldwide with full tracking from our Nantong, China warehouse — unless another warehouse is chosen, in which case 2-3 days. We ship to all countries with discreet packaging.  
**Express Priority shipping:** $50 — fully tracked via USPS Priority Express (US), Royal Mail Special Delivery (UK), DHL Express (EU), and equivalent priority couriers worldwide.

What payment methods do you accept? __

We accept **Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Revolut, and bank transfer** through our secure, PCI-compliant payment processor. You pay the exact USD amount shown — your card is charged in dollars just like any regular online purchase. We also accept **cryptocurrency** (BTC, ETH, USDT, USDC, SOL, XRP, LTC, BNB) with **10% off** your order. All payments are processed through 256-bit encrypted channels and your card details are never stored by us.

Every product is independently tested by **Janoshik Analytical** — the industry's gold standard laboratory for purity and potency verification.
