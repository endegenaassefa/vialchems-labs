# MODULE 10: CASE STUDIES

## A. Detailed case studies

### Case Study 1: Prime Peptides

1. Company name  
Prime Peptides. Source: [FDA warning letter](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/prime-vitality-inc-dba-prime-peptides-695156-12102024)

2. Website URL(s)  
Current: `https://primepeptides.co`  
Associated social URLs cited by FDA: `facebook.com/primepeptidesco`, `instagram.com/primepeptidesco`, `youtube.com/@PrimePeptide`  
Source: [FDA warning letter](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/prime-vitality-inc-dba-prime-peptides-695156-12102024)

3. Business entity name and state of registration  
Prime Vitality, Inc. dba Prime Peptides. The FDA letter places the entity at a Santa Barbara, California address. I did not confirm the California registry filing directly in this pass.  
Source: [FDA warning letter](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/prime-vitality-inc-dba-prime-peptides-695156-12102024)

4. Registered agent  
Not confirmed in the official public records reviewed in this pass.

5. Products sold  
FDA cited semaglutide and retatrutide. Current product pages still show retatrutide.  
Sources: [FDA warning letter](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/prime-vitality-inc-dba-prime-peptides-695156-12102024), [Prime retatrutide page](https://primepeptides.co/products/reta)

6. Marketing claims and tactics  
FDA cited website and social media claims tied to weight loss and liver fat reduction. Current site emphasizes testing, secure payment, and technical presentation.  
Sources: [FDA warning letter](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/prime-vitality-inc-dba-prime-peptides-695156-12102024), [Prime testing page](https://primepeptides.co/testing/)

7. Disclaimer language used  
Current product page says products are for laboratory research only, not for human, medical, or veterinary use, and says the buyer must be 21 or older.  
Source: [Prime retatrutide page](https://primepeptides.co/products/reta)

8. Payment methods  
Current product pages say "Secured Payment." Specific processor details were not confirmed in this pass.  
Source: [Prime retatrutide page](https://primepeptides.co/products/reta)

9. Supply chain clues  
Prime publicly leans on third party testing and COAs. Product pages say peptides require reconstitution with bacteriostatic water sold separately.  
Sources: [Prime testing page](https://primepeptides.co/testing/), [Prime retatrutide page](https://primepeptides.co/products/reta)

10. What enforcement action happened  
FDA sent a warning letter dated December 10, 2024 alleging unlawful sale of semaglutide and retatrutide based on website and social media evidence showing intended human use.  
Source: [FDA warning letter](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/prime-vitality-inc-dba-prime-peptides-695156-12102024)

11. How the company responded  
Current public pages appear more disclaimer heavy and more testing focused than the claims FDA quoted in 2024, but the site remains active and still sells peptide products.  
Sources: [FDA warning letter](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/prime-vitality-inc-dba-prime-peptides-695156-12102024), [Prime retatrutide page](https://primepeptides.co/products/reta), [Prime testing page](https://primepeptides.co/testing/)

12. Current status  
Still operating on the reviewed domain as of this reporting pass.  
Sources: [Prime retatrutide page](https://primepeptides.co/products/reta), [Prime testing page](https://primepeptides.co/testing/)

13. Key evidence  
FDA social media citations, current testing page, current retatrutide product page, and BAC water reference.  
Sources: same as above

14. Source links for every claim  
See sources above.

15. Open questions  
Who compounds or fills the vials. What processor handles checkout. Whether the company changed domains or corporate paperwork after the warning letter.

### Case Study 2: USApeptide.com

1. Company name  
USApeptide.com. FDA’s letter named the website rather than a U.S. corporate entity.  
Source: [USApeptide warning letter](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/usapeptidecom-696885-02262025)

2. Website URL(s)  
Current or cited domain: `https://www.usapeptide.com`  
Source: [USApeptide warning letter](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/usapeptidecom-696885-02262025)

3. Business entity name and state of registration  
Not identified in the public records reviewed. FDA listed the recipient as USApeptide.com in Germany.  
Source: [USApeptide warning letter](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/usapeptidecom-696885-02262025)

4. Registered agent  
Not confirmed.

5. Products sold  
Semaglutide and tirzepatide injectable products, plus bacteriostatic water and insulin syringes.  
Source: [USApeptide warning letter](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/usapeptidecom-696885-02262025)

6. Marketing claims and tactics  
FDA said the site used research only disclaimers while still making human use claims for injectable semaglutide and tirzepatide.  
Source: [USApeptide warning letter](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/usapeptidecom-696885-02262025)

7. Disclaimer language used  
FDA quoted "research use only," "not for human consumption," "lab purposes only," and "not intended to diagnose, cure, mitigate, treat or prevent disease."  
Source: [USApeptide warning letter](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/usapeptidecom-696885-02262025)

8. Payment methods  
Not confirmed in this pass.

9. Supply chain clues  
Germany recipient address, bundled bacteriostatic water and insulin syringes, and injectable vial format suggest a direct to consumer international supply chain.  
Source: [USApeptide warning letter](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/usapeptidecom-696885-02262025)

10. What enforcement action happened  
FDA sent a warning letter on February 26, 2025 over unlawful online sale of semaglutide and tirzepatide to U.S. consumers.  
Source: [USApeptide warning letter](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/usapeptidecom-696885-02262025)

11. How the company responded  
Public response not confirmed in this pass.

12. Current status  
Unknown in this pass.

13. Key evidence  
FDA’s quoted disclaimers, Germany recipient detail, and bundled supplies.  
Source: [USApeptide warning letter](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/usapeptidecom-696885-02262025)

14. Source links for every claim

### Case Study 3: Trust Labs and EA WORKS

1. Company name  
Trust Labs.  
Source: [Trust Labs warning letter](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/ea-works-limited-liability-company-dba-trust-labs-715798-09092025)

2. Website URL(s)  
FDA cited `https://coretrustlabs.com`. Current legal pages also tie EA WORKS to `https://onepeptideshop.com` and `https://mypeptideswarehouse.com`.  
Sources: [Trust Labs warning letter](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/ea-works-limited-liability-company-dba-trust-labs-715798-09092025), [OnePeptideShop terms](https://onepeptideshop.com/terms-and-conditions/), [My Peptides Warehouse terms](https://mypeptideswarehouse.com/terms-conditions/)

3. Business entity name and state of registration  
EA WORKS Limited Liability Company, Wyoming.  
Sources: [Trust Labs warning letter](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/ea-works-limited-liability-company-dba-trust-labs-715798-09092025), [Wyoming business record](https://wyobiz.wyo.gov/Business/FilingDetails.aspx?eFNum=040026109250153238100098184023250152046009201137)

4. Registered agent  
Registered Agents Inc., Sheridan, Wyoming.  
Source: [Wyoming business record](https://wyobiz.wyo.gov/Business/FilingDetails.aspx?eFNum=040026109250153238100098184023250152046009201137)

5. Products sold  
FDA cited compounded retatrutide, semaglutide, and tirzepatide products.  
Source: [Trust Labs warning letter](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/ea-works-limited-liability-company-dba-trust-labs-715798-09092025)

6. Marketing claims and tactics  
FDA said the site made false or misleading claims for compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide and sold retatrutide as an unapproved drug.  
Source: [Trust Labs warning letter](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/ea-works-limited-liability-company-dba-trust-labs-715798-09092025)

7. Disclaimer language used  
Current OnePeptideShop and My Peptides Warehouse terms pages are conventional ecommerce legal pages. In the current terms reviewed, they do not openly describe the products as FDA approved medicines.  
Sources: [OnePeptideShop terms](https://onepeptideshop.com/terms-and-conditions/), [My Peptides Warehouse terms](https://mypeptideswarehouse.com/terms-conditions/)

8. Payment methods  
OnePeptideShop terms say it accepts Visa, American Express, Mastercard, Discover, and PayPal.  
Source: [OnePeptideShop terms](https://onepeptideshop.com/terms-and-conditions/)

9. Supply chain clues  
Same Wyoming LLC appears behind multiple peptide retail domains. That suggests a reusable shell or umbrella structure.  
Sources: [Wyoming business record](https://wyobiz.wyo.gov/Business/FilingDetails.aspx?eFNum=040026109250153238100098184023250152046009201137), [OnePeptideShop terms](https://onepeptideshop.com/terms-and-conditions/), [My Peptides Warehouse terms](https://mypeptideswarehouse.com/terms-conditions/)

10. What enforcement action happened  
FDA sent a warning letter dated September 9, 2025.  
Source: [Trust Labs warning letter](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/ea-works-limited-liability-company-dba-trust-labs-715798-09092025)

11. How the company responded  
In this pass, the clearest public response is continuity through multiple active domains tied to the same Wyoming entity.  
Sources: [Trust Labs warning letter](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/ea-works-limited-liability-company-dba-trust-labs-715798-09092025), [OnePeptideShop terms](https://onepeptideshop.com/terms-and-conditions/), [My Peptides Warehouse terms](https://mypeptideswarehouse.com/terms-conditions/)

12. Current status  
At least two related domains tied to the same LLC are currently live enough to display terms pages.  
Sources: [OnePeptideShop terms](https://onepeptideshop.com/terms-and-conditions/), [My Peptides Warehouse terms](https://mypeptideswarehouse.com/terms-conditions/)

13. Key evidence  
FDA warning letter, Wyoming record, and cross domain legal pages.

14. Source links for every claim  
See sources above.

15. Open questions  
Whether coretrustlabs.com, onepeptideshop.com, and mypeptideswarehouse.com shared checkout infrastructure, inventory, or supplier chain. Whether any additional domains are tied to EA WORKS.

## B. Cross case comparison

1. The legal entity and the public brand often diverge.
2. Current sites often tighten disclaimer language after enforcement but keep selling.
3. Bundled bacteriostatic water or injection adjacent language appears across multiple cases.
4. Telehealth and mail order pharmacy layers make some GLP 1 sellers look more formal than classic peptide storefronts, but FDA still treats misleading claims as actionable.
5. Multi domain continuity appears to be a serious reporting clue, especially in the EA WORKS case.
